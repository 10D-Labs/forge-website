## The Tools

- **[1RM Calculator](https://forgetrainer.ai/tools/1rm-calculator)** - Estimate your one-rep max from any set, across seven formulas, with reps-in-reserve support and a load chart.
- **[Barbell Plate Calculator](https://forgetrainer.ai/tools/barbell-plate-calculator)** - Type a target weight and get the exact plates per side, or tap plates on the bar to count the total.
- **[Weightlifting Percentage Chart](https://forgetrainer.ai/tools/weightlifting-percentage-chart)** - Turn your max into working weights from 100% down to 30%, with reps and training zones.
- **[Warm-Up Calculator](https://forgetrainer.ai/tools/warm-up-calculator)** - Get a ready-to-run warm-up ramp from the empty bar to your working weight.
- **[RPE Calculator](https://forgetrainer.ai/tools/rpe-calculator)** - Pair your estimated max with a target RPE and rep count to get your working weight.
- **[Body Fat Calculator](https://forgetrainer.ai/tools/body-fat-calculator)** - Estimate body fat with the US Navy tape method or a quick height-and-weight formula.
- **[Lean Body Mass Calculator](https://forgetrainer.ai/tools/lean-body-mass-calculator)** - Calculate lean mass from body fat percentage or the Boer, Hume, and James formulas, with FFMI.
- **[Training Volume Calculator](https://forgetrainer.ai/tools/training-volume-calculator)** - Enter weekly hard sets per muscle and see which muscles sit below, inside, or past the productive range.
- **[DOTS Calculator](https://forgetrainer.ai/tools/dots-calculator)** - Score your powerlifting total with the modern pound-for-pound formula.
- **[Wilks Calculator](https://forgetrainer.ai/tools/wilks-calculator)** - Score your total with the original Wilks formula and the 2020 update, side by side.
- **[Ideal Body Weight Calculator](https://forgetrainer.ai/tools/ideal-body-weight-calculator)** - Run Devine, Robinson, Miller, and Hamwi together, with honest context for lifters.
- **[Personal Trainer Cost Calculator](https://forgetrainer.ai/tools/personal-trainer-cost-calculator)** - Estimate what a human trainer costs per session, month, and year in your city.
