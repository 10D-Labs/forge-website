## What Forge Is

Forge is an AI personal trainer app for iPhone and Android. It builds a training plan around your goals, schedule, equipment, and injuries, then adapts that plan as you log progress and give feedback. Four trainer personalities handle the coaching: Sergeant Stone for drill-instructor discipline, Maya for patient encouragement, Mike for a casual gym-buddy tone, and Reese for data-driven analysis. You can message your trainer at any hour and get an in-character answer about why the plan looks the way it does.

Every plan is $6.99 a week, $19.99 a month, or $59.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial on the yearly plan. Forge is workout-only by design: no nutrition features and no watch app.
