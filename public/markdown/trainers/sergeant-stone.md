# Sergeant Stone | Forge AI Coach

Tough love. Show up. Put in the work.

You know what you want. Sometimes you need a push to follow through. Stone is Forge's drill-instructor-style AI coach: direct, demanding, and focused on helping you stay consistent.

## What to expect

- Straight answers and clear expectations
- Firm reminders to follow through
- A focus on consistency over perfection

## A good fit if

- You like a direct, no-nonsense tone
- You respond well to a challenge
- You want firm accountability

## Example coaching style

> You showed up when you didn't want to. That's the only thing that matters. Now do it again tomorrow.

Sergeant Stone is an AI character. This example shows the coaching tone.
