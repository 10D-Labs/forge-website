## Start where you are

- New to training: get your exercises and targets ready before each session.
- Returning to training: build a routine around your current experience.
- Short on time: plan around the days and equipment you have.
- Ready for more progress: log your lifts and talk through your next step with your coach.

## Four AI coaching styles

- [Sergeant Stone](https://forgetrainer.ai/trainers/sergeant-stone): Direct coaching for days when you need a firm push.
- [Maya](https://forgetrainer.ai/trainers/maya): Patient encouragement to help you build confidence.
- [Mike](https://forgetrainer.ai/trainers/mike): Easygoing coaching with a sense of humor.
- [Reese](https://forgetrainer.ai/trainers/reese): Focused guidance for people who want to understand their training.

The coaches are AI characters. You can switch coaches in the app.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does Forge do?

Forge builds workout plans around your goals, experience, schedule, and available equipment. You can log your training, track progress, and ask your AI coach questions along the way.

### Can I use it as a beginner?

Yes. Tell your coach your experience level when you get started. Your plan includes exercises with instructions, and you can ask for help understanding your workout.

### Do I need a gym membership?

You can train at a gym or at home. Tell Forge which equipment you have so your coach can plan around it.

### Are the coaches real people?

The four Forge coaches are AI characters with distinct coaching styles. Choose the approach you prefer and switch coaches in the app.
