# Scapula Dips

## Quick answer

A small dip with straight arms and your hands on a bench, raising your body by pushing your shoulders down. It works the traps, with help from the upper back and shoulders. Beginner back exercise using body weight. Targets traps, also works rhomboids, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Traps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Rhomboids, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on the edge of a flat bench, knees bent and feet flat. Place your hands on the edge beside your hips, palms down, fingers forward.
2. Straighten your arms and slide your hips just off the front of the bench.
3. Keep your arms straight and let your body sink as your shoulders rise toward your ears.
4. Push your shoulders down away from your ears to lift your body back up.

## Pro tips

- Keep your arms straight and locked; move only by depressing (pushing down) and elevating (shrugging up) your shoulder blades.
- At the bottom position, depress your shoulders as far as possible to maximize lower trap activation.
- Maintain an upright torso and avoid leaning forward, which would change the exercise mechanics.

## Common mistakes

- Bending the elbows and performing actual dips instead of keeping arms straight and moving only the shoulder blades.
- Not achieving full depression at the bottom, which limits the training effect on the lower traps.
- Shrugging the shoulders too aggressively at the top, which overloads the upper traps.

## FAQ

### What do Scapula Dips work?

They primarily target the lower trapezius and rhomboids through scapular depression and retraction, while also engaging the serratus anterior for stabilization.

### Are Scapula Dips good for posture?

Yes, they strengthen the muscles responsible for pulling the shoulder blades down and back, which directly combats the forward-rounded shoulder posture common from desk work.

### How many Scapula Dips should I do?

Aim for 3 sets of 12-15 reps. Since the range of motion is small, focus on a 2-second hold at the bottom (fully depressed) position to maximize activation.

### What muscles does the Scapula Dips work?

The Scapula Dips primarily targets the traps, with secondary activation in the rhomboids, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Scapula Dips good for beginners?

Yes, the Scapula Dips is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building traps strength.
