# Lying Calf Stretch with Band

## Quick answer

A lying calf stretch with a band looped around one foot, pulling your knee in toward your chest and your toes toward your shin. It stretches the calf. Beginner legs exercise using resistance band. Targets calves, also works hamstrings.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Calves
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Stretching

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your back, legs straight, with a band looped around the ball of one foot. Hold a handle in each hand by your hips.
2. Bend that knee and pull it in toward your chest, using the band to draw your toes back toward your shin.
3. Stop when your thigh points straight up and you feel a stretch in your calf.
4. Hold for the set time, then straighten your leg slowly back to the floor and switch legs.

## Pro tips

- Lie on your back and loop the band around the ball of one foot.
- Pull your knee in toward your chest, then pull your toes toward your shin with the band.
- Slowly straighten the knee as far as you can to deepen the calf stretch.

## Common mistakes

- Pulling too hard and overstretching, which can irritate the Achilles tendon.
- Placing the band on your toes, which stretches the foot instead of the calf.
- Lifting your head and shoulders off the floor to pull.

## FAQ

### Can I use a towel instead of a band?

Yes. A towel, yoga strap or band all work. Use whatever is long enough to hold with your head on the floor.

### Why do this lying down?

Lying down lets you relax completely, which suits people with tight calves or balance problems. A standing wall stretch uses your body weight for a deeper stretch.

### How often should I do this stretch?

Daily if your calves are tight. Hold 30 seconds per leg, 2-3 rounds.

### What muscles does the Lying Calf Stretch with Band work?

The Lying Calf Stretch with Band primarily targets the calves, with secondary activation in the hamstrings. It's classified as a stretching exercise for the legs.

### Is the Lying Calf Stretch with Band good for beginners?

Yes, the Lying Calf Stretch with Band is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building calves strength.
