# Reverse Tabletop Leg Lift

## Quick answer

A face-up tabletop hold on your hands and feet, knees bent and hips level, where you kick one straight leg up at a time. It works the abs, glutes and hamstrings, with the shoulders holding you up. Advanced core exercise using body weight. Targets abs, also works glutes, hamstrings, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on the floor with your knees bent and feet flat. Place your hands flat on the floor behind you, under your shoulders.
2. Lift your hips until your body is flat from shoulders to knees, like a tabletop, with your arms straight.
3. Straighten one leg and kick it up toward the ceiling, keeping your hips up.
4. Lower that foot back to the floor, then lift the other leg. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Sit with your knees bent and hands behind you, then lift your hips until your body is flat like a table.
- Kick one straight leg up without letting your hips drop, then lower it and switch.
- Squeeze the glute of the supporting leg to keep your hips level.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips sag when lifting a leg.
- Lifting the leg so high that the hips twist.
- Placing the hands too far behind the body, which strains the wrists and shoulders.

## FAQ

### What does the Reverse Tabletop Leg Lift work?

The abs, glutes and hamstrings, with the shoulders holding you up.

### My wrists hurt in this position. What should I do?

Turn your fingers outward or toward your feet, or press up on your fists or on parallettes.

### How many reps should I do?

8-10 lifts per leg, holding each for 1-2 seconds.

### What muscles does the Reverse Tabletop Leg Lift work?

The Reverse Tabletop Leg Lift primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the glutes, hamstrings, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Reverse Tabletop Leg Lift good for beginners?

The Reverse Tabletop Leg Lift is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental core movements first.
