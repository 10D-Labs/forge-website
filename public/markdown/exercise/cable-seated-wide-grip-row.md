# Cable Seated Wide Grip Row

## Quick answer

A seated cable row with a wide bar, pulled to your lower chest with your elbows out to the sides. It works the upper back. Beginner back exercise using cable. Targets upper back, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit at the cable row, feet on the footrests and knees slightly bent. Hold the wide bar with your palms facing down.
2. Lean slightly forward from your hips with your back straight.
3. Pull the bar to your lower chest, elbows flared out to the sides, and squeeze your shoulder blades together.
4. Let the bar move slowly forward until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Use a wide overhand grip and pull toward your lower chest with elbows flared at about 60 degrees to target the upper back, rear delts, and rhomboids.
- Squeeze your shoulder blades together hard at peak contraction and hold for a count before slowly extending forward.
- Keep your torso upright with a slight forward lean at the start. Do not rock back and forth to move the weight.

## Common mistakes

- Using a grip that is too narrow, which turns this into a close-grip row targeting the lats and biceps instead of the upper back.
- Pulling with the elbows tight to the body, which engages the lats more than the intended upper back muscles.
- Excessive body rocking to generate momentum, which reduces upper back muscle engagement.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the Cable Seated Wide Grip Row target?

Primarily the upper back muscles including the rhomboids, middle trapezius, and rear deltoids. The wide grip and flared elbows shift focus from the lats to these upper back muscles.

### Wide grip vs close grip cable row: what is the difference?

Wide grip with flared elbows targets the upper back, rhomboids, and rear delts. Close grip with elbows tight to the body targets the lats and middle back. Both are valuable and should be included in a complete back program.

### What muscles does the Cable Seated Wide Grip Row work?

The Cable Seated Wide Grip Row primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Cable Seated Wide Grip Row good for beginners?

Yes, the Cable Seated Wide Grip Row is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building upper back strength.
