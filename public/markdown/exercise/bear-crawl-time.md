# Bear Crawl (Time)

## Quick answer

Crawling forward on your hands and feet with your knees just off the floor. It raises your heart rate and works the abs, shoulders and triceps. Intermediate cardio exercise using body weight. Targets cardiovascular, also works core, shoulders, triceps.

- **Body Part:** Cardio
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Cardiovascular
- **Secondary Muscles:** Core, Shoulders, Triceps
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Cardio

---

## How to perform

1. Get on your hands and knees with your hands under your shoulders and your knees under your hips.
2. Lift your knees slightly off the floor, keeping your back flat.
3. Move your right hand and left foot forward together, then your left hand and right foot.
4. Keep crawling forward at a steady pace, alternating sides.
5. Keep going for the set time.

## Pro tips

- Keep your knees hovering just 1-2 inches off the ground to maximize core engagement.
- Move contralateral limbs together (right hand with left foot) to maintain balance and natural crawling rhythm.
- Look slightly ahead of your hands, not straight down, to maintain a neutral neck position.

## Common mistakes

- Raising the hips too high into a pike position, which takes tension off the core and shifts it to the shoulders.
- Moving the same-side arm and leg simultaneously instead of opposite limbs, which creates an awkward lurch.
- Taking steps that are too large, which destabilizes your position and reduces the core training effect.

## FAQ

### How long should I bear crawl?

Start with 20-30 second intervals for 3-4 sets. As your conditioning improves, work up to 45-60 second intervals.

### Is the bear crawl a good warm-up exercise?

Yes, it activates the core, shoulders, hips, and legs simultaneously, making it an effective full-body warm-up or conditioning exercise.

### What muscles does the bear crawl work?

It targets the core, shoulders, and quads as primary movers, with the glutes, hamstrings, and triceps working as stabilizers. It's a true full-body exercise.

### What muscles does the Bear Crawl (Time) work?

The Bear Crawl (Time) primarily targets the cardiovascular, with secondary activation in the core, shoulders, triceps. It's classified as a cardio exercise for the cardio.

### Is the Bear Crawl (Time) good for beginners?

The Bear Crawl (Time) is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of cardiovascular strength with easier variations first.
