# Single Arm Dip

## Quick answer

A one-arm dip with your hand on a bench beside you and your legs stretched out in front. It works the triceps, with help from the chest and shoulders. Advanced arms exercise using body weight. Targets triceps, also works chest, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Chest, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit next to a flat bench with your side to it and place the hand nearest it on the bench, beside your hip.
2. Stretch your legs out straight in front of you, off the floor, and straighten that arm to lift your hips. Reach your free arm forward.
3. Bend your elbow to lower your hips toward the floor, keeping your side close to the bench.
4. Push through your palm to straighten your arm. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Position your working hand directly under your shoulder for maximum stability and force transfer.
- Keep your legs extended but place one foot slightly in front of the other for balance.
- Lower yourself slowly (3-4 seconds) and don't go deeper than 90 degrees at the elbow to protect the shoulder.

## Common mistakes

- Placing the hand too far behind the body, which puts excessive stress on the shoulder joint.
- Using the legs to Pushup rather than relying on the tricep and chest to do the work.
- Flaring the elbow outward instead of keeping it tracking straight back.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the Single Arm Dip work?

It primarily targets the triceps with significant involvement from the chest and front deltoid. The core also works hard to prevent rotation.

### How do I progress to a Single Arm Dip?

Master standard dips for 20+ reps, then practice with one hand on a higher surface, then use fingertip assistance from the other hand, and finally attempt the full single-arm version.

### Is the Single Arm Dip safe for the shoulder?

It places high stress on the shoulder joint. Only attempt it if you have healthy shoulders and strong standard dip performance. Don't go deeper than 90 degrees at the elbow.

### What muscles does the Single Arm Dip work?

The Single Arm Dip primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the chest, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Single Arm Dip good for beginners?

The Single Arm Dip is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental arms movements first.
