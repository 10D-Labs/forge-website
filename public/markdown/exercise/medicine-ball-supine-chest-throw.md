# Medicine Ball Supine Chest Throw

## Quick answer

A power drill where you lie on the floor and throw a medicine ball straight up from your chest. It works the triceps, chest and shoulders. Intermediate arms exercise using medicine ball. Targets triceps, also works chest, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Medicine Ball
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Chest, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your back on the floor with your knees bent and feet flat.
2. Hold a medicine ball in both hands with your arms straight up over your chest.
3. Lower the ball to your chest, keeping your elbows close to your body.
4. Push the ball up hard, straightening your arms, and throw it as high as you can.
5. Catch it and bring it back down to your chest for the next throw.

## Pro tips

- Start with the ball at your chest and explode upward, releasing the ball as high as possible.
- Catch the ball with soft elbows and immediately absorb the weight back to your chest before the next throw.
- Use a weight that lets you throw explosively for 8-12 reps without losing power.

## Common mistakes

- Using too heavy a ball, which turns the explosive throw into a slow press.
- Not catching the ball with bent elbows, which jars the wrists and shoulders on impact.
- Letting the ball bounce off the chest instead of controlling it down and exploding it back up.

## FAQ

### What weight medicine ball should I use?

Start with 6-10 pounds. The ball should be light enough to throw explosively but heavy enough to provide meaningful resistance. Power output matters more than load.

### What muscles does the supine chest throw work?

It trains the triceps, chest, and shoulders explosively. The throwing motion develops power and rate of force development that standard bench pressing doesn't.

### Can I do this without a partner?

Yes, throw the ball straight up and catch it yourself. Make sure you're in an open space with high ceilings. Some gyms have wall-mounted rebounders designed for this exercise.

### What muscles does the Medicine Ball Supine Chest Throw work?

The Medicine Ball Supine Chest Throw primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the chest, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Medicine Ball Supine Chest Throw good for beginners?

The Medicine Ball Supine Chest Throw is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of triceps strength with easier variations first.
