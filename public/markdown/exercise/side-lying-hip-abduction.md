# Side Lying Hip Abduction

## Quick answer

A side-lying leg raise where you lift your straight top leg up and lower it. It works the outer hips, with help from the glutes. Beginner legs exercise using body weight. Targets abductors, also works glutes, quadriceps.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Abductors
- **Secondary Muscles:** Glutes, Quadriceps
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your side, propped up on your bottom forearm, legs straight and stacked. Put your top hand on the floor in front of you.
2. Keep your top leg straight and lift it until it's about 45 degrees from the floor, without rolling your hips back.
3. Lower it slowly back onto your bottom leg.
4. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Lie on your side with your legs straight and stacked, and rest your head on your lower arm.
- Lift the top leg straight up, leading with the heel, without rolling your hips back.
- Lower the leg slowly instead of letting it drop.

## Common mistakes

- Rolling the hips backward to lift the leg higher, which turns the move into a hip flexor exercise.
- Turning the toes up toward the ceiling instead of keeping them pointed forward.
- Swinging the leg up with momentum.

## FAQ

### What does Side Lying Hip Abduction work?

The muscles on the outside of the hip, mainly the gluteus medius, which helps keep your pelvis level when you walk and run.

### Should I add a resistance band?

Once you can do 20 slow reps per side easily, a band around your ankles or knees adds resistance.

### How high should I lift my leg?

About 30-45 degrees. Going higher usually means rolling the hips or bending at the waist.

### What muscles does the Side Lying Hip Abduction work?

The Side Lying Hip Abduction primarily targets the abductors, with secondary activation in the glutes, quadriceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Side Lying Hip Abduction good for beginners?

Yes, the Side Lying Hip Abduction is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building abductors strength.
