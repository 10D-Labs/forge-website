# Weighted Tricep Dips

## Quick answer

A dip on parallel bars with a weight plate hanging from a belt at your waist. It works the triceps, with help from the chest and shoulders. Advanced arms exercise using weight plate. Targets triceps, also works chest, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Weight Plate
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Chest, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Put on a dip belt with a plate hanging from the chain. Grip the parallel bars of a dip station, palms facing each other.
2. Push up until your arms are straight, and bend your knees so your feet are off the floor.
3. Lean slightly forward and bend your elbows to lower your body until your upper arms are about level with the floor.
4. Push back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Use a dip belt, weight vest, or hold a dumbbell between your feet to add resistance beyond bodyweight.
- Keep your elbows tucked close to your body and your torso upright to maximize tricep emphasis over chest.
- Lower until your upper arms are parallel to the floor, then press up explosively to full lockout.

## Common mistakes

- Leaning too far forward, which shifts the emphasis from triceps to chest.
- Adding too much weight before mastering bodyweight dips with perfect form.
- Not reaching full depth, which limits the stretch on the triceps and reduces the effectiveness of the exercise.

## FAQ

### How much weight should I add for weighted dips?

Start by adding 10-15 pounds and progress in 5-pound increments. You should be able to do at least 12-15 clean bodyweight dips before adding weight.

### Are weighted dips better than close grip bench press for triceps?

Both are excellent compound tricep exercises. Dips allow more natural shoulder movement, while close grip bench press is easier to load progressively. Use both.

### Can weighted dips hurt my shoulders?

Going too deep or adding too much weight can stress the anterior shoulder. Stop at parallel depth, keep your torso upright, and progress weight gradually to minimize shoulder strain.

### What muscles does the Weighted Tricep Dips work?

The Weighted Tricep Dips primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the chest, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Weighted Tricep Dips good for beginners?

The Weighted Tricep Dips is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental arms movements first.
