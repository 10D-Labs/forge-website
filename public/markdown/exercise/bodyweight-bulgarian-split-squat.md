# Bodyweight Bulgarian Split Squat

## Quick answer

A split squat with your back foot up on a box behind you, using just your body weight. It works the glutes and quads, with help from the hamstrings. Advanced legs exercise using body weight. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand a stride in front of a box, facing away. Rest the top of one foot on it behind you, hands on your hips.
2. Bend your front knee and lower straight down until your front thigh is level with the floor.
3. Push through your front foot to stand back up. Do all your reps on one leg, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Rest the top of your back foot on a box or bench behind you and step your front foot forward.
- Lower straight down until your front thigh is about parallel to the floor, keeping your chest tall.
- Drive through your front foot to stand back up, and finish all reps on one leg before switching.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the front knee collapse inward; push it out over your toes.
- Placing the front foot too close to the box, which pushes the knee far forward.
- Not going deep enough; partial reps mostly hit the quads.

## FAQ

### Is a bodyweight Bulgarian Split Squat a good beginner exercise?

Yes. It builds single-leg strength and balance with no equipment beyond a box or bench. Hold a wall for balance at first if you need to.

### How do I make it harder?

Slow the lowering to 3 seconds, pause at the bottom, or hold dumbbells once bodyweight reps get easy.

### How high should the box be?

About knee height. A higher box increases the stretch on the back leg's hip but makes balancing harder.

### What muscles does the Bodyweight Bulgarian Split Squat work?

The Bodyweight Bulgarian Split Squat primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Bodyweight Bulgarian Split Squat good for beginners?

The Bodyweight Bulgarian Split Squat is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental legs movements first.
