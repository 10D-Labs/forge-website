# Band Fixed Back Underhand Pulldown

## Quick answer

A seated pulldown with a band anchored overhead, pulling with your palms facing you. It works the lats, with help from the biceps, upper back and rear shoulders. Beginner back exercise using resistance band. Targets lats, also works biceps, rhomboids, rear deltoids.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Rhomboids, Rear Deltoids
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Anchor a band with two handles high above you and sit on a box beneath it, feet flat on the floor.
2. Reach up and hold a handle in each hand, palms facing you, arms straight.
3. Pull the handles down in front of you, driving your elbows down to your sides, until your hands reach your chest.
4. Let your arms rise slowly until they are straight overhead.

## Pro tips

- Use an underhand grip to increase bicep involvement and target the lower lats more effectively.
- Pull the band all the way to your upper chest and squeeze your shoulder blades together at the bottom.
- Step far enough back from the anchor to maintain tension throughout the entire range of motion.

## Common mistakes

- Standing too close to the anchor point, which provides no tension at the top of the movement and reduces the effective range.
- Pulling with the arms only instead of initiating the movement from the shoulder blades and lats.
- Using a band with too much resistance, which forces you to lean back excessively and use momentum.

## FAQ

### Can band pulldowns replace lat pulldown machines?

They are a solid home-gym alternative. The resistance profile differs (bands get harder at the bottom vs. constant machine tension), but the muscle activation pattern is similar.

### What is the advantage of underhand grip for pulldowns?

Underhand grip increases bicep activation and tends to emphasize the lower lats more, while also allowing a slightly greater range of motion compared to overhand grip.

### What type of band should I use for pulldowns?

Use a medium to heavy loop band. You should be able to complete 10-15 reps with a full range of motion and controlled tempo.

### What muscles does the Band Fixed Back Underhand Pulldown work?

The Band Fixed Back Underhand Pulldown primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the biceps, rhomboids, rear deltoids. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Band Fixed Back Underhand Pulldown good for beginners?

Yes, the Band Fixed Back Underhand Pulldown is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building lats strength.
