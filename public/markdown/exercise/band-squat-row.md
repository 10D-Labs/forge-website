# Band Squat Row

## Quick answer

A deep squat with a band row: you pull the band to your waist as you stand up. It works the glutes, hamstrings and quads, along with the back. Beginner legs exercise using resistance band. Targets glutes, also works hamstrings, quadriceps, back.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Quadriceps, Back
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Anchor the band at waist height. Stand facing it with your feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Hold the handles with your palms facing each other and your arms straight out in front of you.
3. Bend your knees and sit down into a deep squat, keeping your back straight, chest up and arms straight.
4. Stand up, and as you rise, pull the handles in to your waist.
5. Straighten your arms slowly as you squat back down.

## Pro tips

- Sink into a full squat first, then row the band handles to your ribcage at the bottom position before standing up.
- Keep your chest lifted throughout and squeeze your shoulder blades together at the peak of the row.
- Maintain band tension even at the starting position by standing far enough from the anchor point.

## Common mistakes

- Rowing before fully squatting down, which turns it into a standing row rather than a combined movement.
- Rounding the upper back during the squat portion, which compromises both the squat and row form.
- Standing too close to the anchor, resulting in no band tension at the start.

## FAQ

### What makes the squat row a good exercise?

It combines a lower body squat with an upper body row in one movement, making it time-efficient and increasing heart rate for a metabolic training effect.

### Is this a strength exercise or a cardio exercise?

It can be both depending on how you program it. Heavier band with lower reps for strength, lighter band with higher reps for cardio and conditioning.

### What muscles does the Band Squat Row work?

Quads, glutes, and hamstrings from the squat. Lats, rhomboids, and biceps from the row. Core throughout as a stabilizer.

### What muscles does the Band Squat Row work?

The Band Squat Row primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, quadriceps, back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Band Squat Row good for beginners?

Yes, the Band Squat Row is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building glutes strength.
