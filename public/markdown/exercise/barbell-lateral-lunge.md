# Barbell Lateral Lunge

## Quick answer

A side lunge with a barbell across your upper back, bending one knee while the other leg stays straight. It works the glutes, with help from the quads and hamstrings. Intermediate legs exercise using barbell. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and a barbell across your upper back.
2. Take a big step to the side with your right foot, keeping your left foot planted.
3. Bend your right knee to lower your body, keeping your left leg straight.
4. Push off your right foot to return to the start, then do the same stepping out with your left foot.

## Pro tips

- Take a wide step to the side and sit back into the lunging hip while keeping the trailing leg completely straight.
- Keep the barbell stable across your upper back and resist the urge to lean your torso excessively forward.
- Push off the lunging foot powerfully through the heel to return to the starting position.

## Common mistakes

- Not stepping wide enough, which limits the adductor stretch and reduces glute activation.
- Letting the lunging knee cave inward instead of tracking over the toes.
- Leaning the torso too far forward, which shifts the load from the glutes to the lower back.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the Barbell Lateral Lunge target?

Primarily the glutes and adductors (inner thigh) of the lunging leg, with secondary work from the quads, hamstrings, and core stabilizers.

### How is this different from a regular Forward Lunge?

The lateral (side) movement targets the glute medius and adductors more than Forward Lunges, which primarily hit the quads and glute max.

### Should I alternate legs or do all reps on one side?

Either works. Alternating legs gives each side brief recovery between reps. Doing all reps on one side increases fatigue and time under tension for that leg.

### What muscles does the Barbell Lateral Lunge work?

The Barbell Lateral Lunge primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Barbell Lateral Lunge good for beginners?

The Barbell Lateral Lunge is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of glutes strength with easier variations first.
