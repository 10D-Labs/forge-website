# Weighted Cossack Squats

## Quick answer

A wide-stance squat that drops onto one leg at a time while the other stays straight, holding a plate at your chest. It works the glutes, quads and hamstrings. Advanced legs exercise using weight plate. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Weight Plate
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet wider than shoulder-width apart, toes turned out slightly. Hold a weight plate in front of your chest with both hands.
2. Shift your weight to one side and bend that knee, keeping your other leg straight.
3. Go as low as you can while keeping your balance and your chest up.
4. Push through the heel of your bent leg to return to the middle, then go to the other side. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Keep the weight close to your chest (goblet position) to counterbalance your body as you descend to one side.
- Turn the toes of the straight leg upward as you descend to stretch the adductors and protect the knee.
- Only go as deep as your mobility allows with a flat foot on the squatting side.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the heel of the squatting leg come off the ground, which shifts stress to the knee.
- Rounding the back excessively at the bottom instead of keeping the chest up.
- Not going low enough to actually challenge the adductors and hips through their full range.

## FAQ

### What muscles do Cossack squats work?

The squatting leg works the glutes, quads, and adductors through a deep range. The straight leg gets an active adductor stretch. The core works hard to maintain balance throughout.

### How heavy should I go on Weighted Cossack Squats?

Start with 15-25 lbs in a goblet hold. This exercise is more about mobility and single-leg strength than maximal loading.

### Are Cossack squats good for athletes?

Extremely. They build lateral mobility, adductor strength, and single-leg stability, all of which reduce injury risk in sports that involve cutting, pivoting, and lateral movement.

### What muscles does the Weighted Cossack Squats work?

The Weighted Cossack Squats primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Weighted Cossack Squats good for beginners?

The Weighted Cossack Squats is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental legs movements first.
