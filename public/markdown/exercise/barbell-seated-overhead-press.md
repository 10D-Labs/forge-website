# Barbell Seated Overhead Press

## Quick answer

A barbell press from your shoulders to overhead, done sitting on a bench. It works the front of the shoulders, with help from the triceps and upper back. Intermediate shoulders exercise using barbell. Targets shoulders, also works triceps, upper back.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Upper Back
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on a bench with your back straight and your feet flat on the floor.
2. Grip the barbell with your palms facing forward, hands slightly wider than shoulder-width apart. Lift it off the rack to shoulder level, elbows bent.
3. Press the bar overhead until your arms are straight.
4. Lower the bar slowly back to shoulder level.

## Pro tips

- Press the bar in a slight arc up and back so it finishes directly over the crown of your head, not in front of your face.
- Keep your core tight and avoid excessive arching of the lower back; the seat back should support you but not become a decline bench.
- Lower the bar to your upper chest or chin level before pressing, ensuring a full range of motion.

## Common mistakes

- Arching the lower back excessively to turn it into an incline press, which stresses the lumbar spine and reduces shoulder work.
- Pressing the bar forward in front of your head instead of directly overhead, which is less efficient and harder on the shoulders.
- Not lowering the bar enough, doing partial reps that limit shoulder development.

## FAQ

### Is seated overhead press easier than standing?

You can typically press about 10% more weight seated because you don't need to stabilize your entire body. However, standing presses build more core strength and functional stability.

### Should I use a back pad or no back support?

A back pad helps you press more weight safely by preventing excessive arching. No back support forces more core engagement. Use a back pad for heavy work and no pad for core development.

### How should I grip the bar for overhead press?

Slightly wider than shoulder width, with the bar resting in the heel of your palm, not your fingers. Your forearms should be vertical at the bottom position.

### What muscles does the Barbell Seated Overhead Press work?

The Barbell Seated Overhead Press primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the triceps, upper back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Barbell Seated Overhead Press good for beginners?

The Barbell Seated Overhead Press is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of shoulders strength with easier variations first.
