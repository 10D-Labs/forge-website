# Prone Bench Flutter Kicks

## Quick answer

Small up-and-down leg kicks done face down on a bench, legs straight out past the end. Lifting each leg behind you works the glutes. Beginner legs exercise using body weight. Targets glutes, also works hamstrings, lower back.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Lower Back
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie face down on a flat bench, hips at the end, legs straight out behind you. Hold the bench legs by your head.
2. Keep your hips on the bench and your legs straight.
3. Raise one leg a little above your body as the other drops a little below it.
4. Switch the legs in short up-and-down kicks. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Lie face down on a bench with your hips at the edge and your legs straight out past the end, holding the bench for support.
- Lift one leg behind you while the other lowers, keeping the kicks small and controlled.
- Squeeze your glutes on each lift and keep your hips pressed into the bench.

## Common mistakes

- Arching the lower back to lift the legs higher, which strains the spine instead of working the glutes.
- Bending the knees, which shortens the lever and takes work off the glutes.
- Kicking fast and loose instead of lifting each leg with control.

## FAQ

### What do Prone Bench Flutter Kicks work?

Mainly the glutes, with the hamstrings and lower back helping. Lying face down makes them a glute exercise, unlike flutter kicks on your back, which work the abs and hip flexors.

### How long should I do Prone Bench Flutter Kicks?

Start with 3 sets of 20-30 seconds and build up to 45-60 seconds per set. Timed sets work better than counting reps for this exercise.

### What muscles does the Prone Bench Flutter Kicks work?

The Prone Bench Flutter Kicks primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, lower back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Prone Bench Flutter Kicks good for beginners?

Yes, the Prone Bench Flutter Kicks is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building glutes strength.
