# Cable Middle Fly

## Quick answer

A standing cable fly with the pulleys at chest height, sweeping your arms together in front of you. It works the chest, with some help from the shoulders and triceps. Intermediate chest exercise using cable. Targets chest, also works shoulders, triceps.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Triceps
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set both pulleys of a cable machine at chest height. Stand in the middle with one foot slightly in front of the other.
2. Hold a handle in each hand with your arms out to the sides and your palms facing forward.
3. Keep a slight bend in your elbows and lean forward slightly.
4. Bring your arms forward in a wide arc until your hands come together in front of you.
5. Open your arms slowly back out to the sides.

## Pro tips

- Set the pulleys at chest height so the cable pulls directly to the sides, isolating the sternal (middle) fibers of the chest.
- Step forward past the cable columns into a staggered stance to increase the stretch at the starting position.
- Focus on squeezing your pecs together at the front of the movement rather than pushing with your hands.

## Common mistakes

- Setting the pulleys too high or too low, which changes the angle and turns this into a high or low fly instead of a middle chest fly.
- Straightening the elbows completely, which increases elbow joint stress. Keep a consistent slight bend.
- Bringing the hands together too far past center, which reduces tension on the chest and wastes the contraction.

## FAQ

### What is the best pulley height for Cable Middle Flyes?

Set the pulleys at the same height as your chest when standing. This ensures the resistance line is horizontal and targets the sternal (middle) portion of the pectorals.

### How many sets and reps for Cable Middle Flyes?

Cable flyes work best in moderate to high rep ranges. Aim for 3-4 sets of 10-15 reps with a controlled tempo and strong squeeze at the peak.

### What muscles does the Cable Middle Fly work?

The Cable Middle Fly primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the shoulders, triceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Cable Middle Fly good for beginners?

The Cable Middle Fly is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of chest strength with easier variations first.
