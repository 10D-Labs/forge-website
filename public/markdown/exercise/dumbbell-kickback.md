# Dumbbell Kickback

## Quick answer

A one-arm triceps kickback with one knee and one hand braced on a flat bench. It works the triceps. Beginner arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets triceps, also works shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Put one knee and the hand on the same side on a flat bench, with your other foot on the floor.
2. Hold a dumbbell in your free hand, palm facing your body. Raise your upper arm level with your back, forearm hanging straight down.
3. Keep your upper arm still and straighten your elbow to push the dumbbell back until your arm is straight.
4. Bend your elbow slowly to bring the dumbbell back down. Do all your reps with one arm, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Lock your upper arm parallel to the floor and only move from the elbow down to fully isolate the triceps.
- Squeeze hard at full extension and hold for a beat to maximize the peak contraction where the triceps are shortest.
- Hinge forward at the hips at least 45 degrees to keep gravity working against the triceps throughout the full range of motion.

## Common mistakes

- Dropping the upper arm below parallel, which removes tension from the triceps at the most important part of the movement.
- Swinging the dumbbell back using shoulder momentum rather than extending purely from the elbow.
- Using too heavy a weight and only achieving a partial range of motion, never reaching full elbow extension.

## FAQ

### Are Dumbbell Kickbacks effective for building triceps?

Yes, especially for the lateral and long heads. They provide peak contraction at full extension, which complements pressing movements that load the triceps most in the stretched position.

### What weight should I use for tricep kickbacks?

Most people use 10-25 lbs. This is an isolation exercise that relies on strict form, not heavy loads. If you need to swing the weight, go lighter.

### Should I do kickbacks with both arms at once or one at a time?

Both work, but single-arm kickbacks let you brace with the free hand for better stability and stricter form.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Kickback work?

The Dumbbell Kickback primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Kickback good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Kickback is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building triceps strength.
