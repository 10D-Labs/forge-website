# Band Bent Over Kickback

## Quick answer

A kickback done leaning on a wall, pushing one straight leg back against a band anchored low in front of you. It works the glutes, with help from the hamstrings and lower back. Beginner legs exercise using resistance band. Targets glutes, also works hamstrings, lower back.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Lower Back
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Loop a band around one ankle and anchor the other end low, near the floor, in front of you.
2. Face a wall, lean forward and press both hands flat on it with your arms straight. Stand on the other leg.
3. Keeping the banded leg straight, push it back and up until it's almost level with the floor.
4. Bring it slowly forward and down to just in front of your standing foot. Do all your reps on one leg, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Hinge forward at the hips and kick one leg straight back against the band resistance, squeezing the glute at full extension.
- Keep your hips square to the ground and avoid rotating your pelvis as you kick back.
- Pause at the top for a one-second glute squeeze before lowering the leg back down.

## Common mistakes

- Arching the lower back to get the leg higher instead of hinging at the hip and extending from the glute.
- Rotating the hip open to cheat the range of motion, which takes the load off the glute.
- Using momentum to swing the leg instead of controlled glute contraction.

## FAQ

### Do glute kickbacks actually build the glutes?

Yes. Kickbacks isolate the glute max through hip extension. They are effective when combined with compound movements like squats and Deadlifts.

### How heavy should the band be for kickbacks?

Use a medium band that allows 12-15 controlled reps per side. You should feel a strong glute contraction without needing to compensate with your lower back.

### Should you do kickbacks before or after squats?

After squats as an isolation accessory, or before squats as a glute activation warmup with a lighter band.

### What muscles does the Band Bent Over Kickback work?

The Band Bent Over Kickback primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, lower back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Band Bent Over Kickback good for beginners?

Yes, the Band Bent Over Kickback is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building glutes strength.
