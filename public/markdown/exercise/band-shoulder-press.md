# Band Shoulder Press

## Quick answer

An overhead press using a resistance band under your front foot instead of weights. It works the shoulders, with help from the triceps and upper back. Beginner shoulders exercise using resistance band. Targets shoulders, also works triceps, upper back.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Upper Back
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with one foot in front of the other and the middle of a resistance band under your front foot.
2. Hold an end in each hand at shoulder height, elbows bent, palms facing forward.
3. Press your hands straight up until your arms are straight overhead.
4. Lower your hands slowly back to shoulder height.

## Pro tips

- Stand on the center of the band with both feet and press straight overhead, fully extending the arms.
- Keep your core braced and ribs down to prevent your lower back from arching as you press.
- Lower the band to ear level under control before pressing back up.

## Common mistakes

- Arching the lower back excessively to assist the press, which puts pressure on the lumbar spine.
- Not pressing to full lockout overhead, which reduces the range of motion and shoulder engagement.
- Standing on the band unevenly, creating different resistance for each arm.

## FAQ

### Can Band Shoulder Presses replace dumbbell shoulder presses?

For home workouts, yes. The resistance curve is different (harder at the top with bands), but both effectively train the deltoids and triceps.

### What band thickness should you use for shoulder presses?

Start with a medium band. You should be able to press for 8-12 reps with full overhead extension. If you cannot lock out, the band is too thick.

### Should you press in front of your head or behind?

Always press in front of or directly above your head. Behind-the-neck pressing puts the shoulder in a vulnerable position and increases impingement risk.

### What muscles does the Band Shoulder Press work?

The Band Shoulder Press primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the triceps, upper back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Band Shoulder Press good for beginners?

Yes, the Band Shoulder Press is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building shoulders strength.
