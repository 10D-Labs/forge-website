# Weighted Close Grip Chinup

## Quick answer

A chinup with your hands close together, palms facing you, and a weight plate hanging from a belt at your waist. It works the lats, with help from the biceps and forearms. Advanced back exercise using weight plate. Targets lats, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Weight Plate
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Put on a dip belt with a weight plate hanging from the chain.
2. Grab the pullup bar with your palms facing you, hands a few inches apart, and hang with your arms straight and knees bent.
3. Pull yourself up until your chin is above the bar.
4. Lower slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Hang the plate from a dip belt around your waist, not between your feet, for stability.
- Use a close underhand grip to work the biceps and lower lats.
- Pull until your chin clears the bar, squeezing your lats hard at the top of each rep.

## Common mistakes

- Kipping or swinging to get the chin over the bar, which is riskier with added weight.
- Gripping so narrow that it strains the wrists.
- Adding weight before you can do at least 8-10 clean bodyweight Chinups.

## FAQ

### How much weight should I add to Chinups?

Start with 5-10 lbs and add weight in small steps (2.5-5 lbs). You should be able to complete 5-8 clean reps with the added weight.

### Is close grip better than wide grip for Chinups?

Close grip brings in more biceps and allows a longer range of motion. Wide grip puts more emphasis on the upper lats. Both have value.

### When should I start adding weight to Chinups?

Once you can do 3 sets of 10-12 bodyweight Chinups with full range of motion and good form.

### What muscles does the Weighted Close Grip Chinup work?

The Weighted Close Grip Chinup primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Weighted Close Grip Chinup good for beginners?

The Weighted Close Grip Chinup is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental back movements first.
