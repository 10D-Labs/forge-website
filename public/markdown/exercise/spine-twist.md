# Spine Twist

## Quick answer

A seated twist with straight legs and your arms held in front of your chest. It works the abs and the obliques. Beginner core exercise using body weight. Targets abs, also works obliques, lower back.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Obliques, Lower Back
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Mobility

---

## How to perform

1. Sit tall on the floor with your legs straight in front of you, toes pointing up.
2. Fold your arms in front of your chest at shoulder height, elbows out.
3. Keep your hips and legs still and twist your upper body to one side as far as you can.
4. Turn back through the middle and twist to the other side. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Sit tall with your spine lengthened before initiating the twist; imagine growing an inch taller before each rotation.
- Rotate from the thoracic spine (mid-back), not the lower back, to protect the lumbar discs.
- Keep both sit bones firmly planted on the ground throughout the twist to prevent hip shifting.

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the back before twisting, which compresses the spine and limits rotation range.
- Forcing the twist with the arms instead of rotating through the core muscles.
- Lifting one hip off the floor during the rotation, which means the twist is coming from the hips rather than the spine.

## FAQ

### Is the Spine Twist good for back pain?

Gentle spinal twists can help relieve tension and improve thoracic mobility, but people with disc issues should consult a professional before performing rotational exercises.

### How far should I twist in a Spine Twist?

Rotate only as far as you can while keeping both hips grounded and maintaining a tall posture. Do not force the range of motion.

### What muscles does the Spine Twist work?

It primarily targets the obliques and deep spinal rotators, while also stretching the erector spinae and improving thoracic spine mobility.

### What muscles does the Spine Twist work?

The Spine Twist primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the obliques, lower back. It's classified as a mobility exercise for the core.

### Is the Spine Twist good for beginners?

Yes, the Spine Twist is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building abs strength.
