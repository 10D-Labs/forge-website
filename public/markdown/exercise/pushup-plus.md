# Pushup Plus

## Quick answer

A pushup with an extra push at the top that spreads your shoulder blades apart. It works the chest and the muscles that hold your shoulder blades steady. Intermediate chest exercise using body weight. Targets chest, also works triceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Start at the top of a pushup, hands a little wider than your shoulders and body straight from head to heels.
2. Bend your elbows, keeping them close to your sides, until your chest is just above the floor.
3. Push back up until your arms are straight.
4. Push your upper back toward the ceiling so your shoulder blades spread apart.
5. Let your shoulder blades come back together, then lower into the next pushup.

## Pro tips

- Perform a full Pushup, then at the top, push extra far so your upper back rounds and your shoulder blades protract fully.
- The 'plus' portion is the key; really push the floor away and feel your serratus anterior engage around your ribcage.
- Keep your core braced throughout so the extra push comes from protraction, not from piking the hips.

## Common mistakes

- Skipping the plus (protraction) at the top, which turns it into a regular Pushup and misses the serratus anterior work.
- Piking the hips during the plus phase instead of keeping the body straight and protracting only the shoulder blades.
- Not achieving full protraction because of rushing; hold the top position for 1-2 seconds to ensure full scapular spread.

## FAQ

### What is the Pushup Plus?

It is a standard Pushup with an added scapular protraction at the top. After locking out your arms, you push further to spread your shoulder blades apart, activating the serratus anterior.

### Is the Pushup Plus good for shoulder health?

Yes, it is one of the best exercises for strengthening the serratus anterior, a muscle critical for shoulder blade stability and preventing shoulder impingement.

### How do I know if I am doing the plus portion correctly?

Your upper back should visibly round at the top of the movement, and you should feel a deep contraction around your ribcage (serratus anterior). Your shoulder blades should be fully spread apart.

### What muscles does the Pushup Plus work?

The Pushup Plus primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Pushup Plus good for beginners?

The Pushup Plus is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of chest strength with easier variations first.
