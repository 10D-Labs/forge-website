# Cable Pull Through

## Quick answer

A cable exercise where you bend forward from the hips with a rope between your legs, then stand back up tall. It works the glutes, plus the hamstrings and lower back. Intermediate legs exercise using cable. Targets glutes, also works hamstrings, lower back.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Lower Back
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand facing away from a low pulley with a rope attachment, feet shoulder-width apart. Reach between your legs and hold the rope with both hands.
2. Step forward until the cable is tight.
3. Push your hips back and lower your upper body until it's level with the floor, keeping your back straight.
4. Squeeze your glutes and hamstrings to pull yourself back up to standing.

## Pro tips

- Hinge at the hips with a soft knee bend, pushing your glutes back toward the cable machine. This is a hip hinge, not a squat.
- At the top, drive your hips forward aggressively and squeeze your glutes as hard as possible. Your torso should be completely vertical at lockout.
- Keep the rope between your legs and close to your body. Your arms should stay relaxed and act only as hooks.

## Common mistakes

- Squatting the movement instead of hinging, which turns this into a squat-pull hybrid and removes hamstring and glute emphasis.
- Pulling with the arms or upper back instead of driving the hips forward to move the weight.
- Not standing far enough from the machine, which reduces the range of motion at the bottom of the hinge.

## FAQ

### Are Cable Pull Throughs a good substitute for Romanian Deadlifts?

They target similar muscles (glutes and hamstrings) but with less spinal loading. They are an excellent alternative for people who want to work the posterior chain without heavy axial loading.

### Where should I feel Cable Pull Throughs?

Primarily in the glutes and hamstrings. If you feel it in your lower back, you are likely rounding your spine or not hinging properly at the hips.

### What muscles does the Cable Pull Through work?

The Cable Pull Through primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, lower back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Cable Pull Through good for beginners?

The Cable Pull Through is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of glutes strength with easier variations first.
