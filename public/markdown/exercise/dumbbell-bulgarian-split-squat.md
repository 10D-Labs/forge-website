# Dumbbell Bulgarian Split Squat

## Quick answer

A split squat with your rear foot raised on a bench and a dumbbell in each hand. It works the quads and glutes of the front leg. Intermediate legs exercise using dumbbell. Targets quads, also works glutes, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Quads
- **Secondary Muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand a stride in front of a flat bench, facing away from it, with a dumbbell in each hand at your sides, palms facing your body.
2. Reach one foot back and rest the top of it on the bench.
3. Bend your front knee and lower straight down until your front thigh is about level with the floor.
4. Push through your front foot to stand back up. Do all your reps on one leg, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Rest your back foot on a bench and lower until your front thigh is about parallel to the floor.
- Keep your front shin as vertical as possible by placing the front foot far enough forward from the bench.
- Drive up through the whole front foot, with a little extra pressure through the heel to bring in the glutes.

## Common mistakes

- Placing the front foot too close to the bench, which pushes the front knee far forward and increases knee stress.
- Leaning the torso too far forward, which loads the lower back instead of the legs.
- Letting the front knee collapse inward on the way down.

## FAQ

### Is a Bulgarian split squat the same as a regular split squat?

No. A regular split squat keeps both feet on the floor. In the Bulgarian version your rear foot rests on a bench, which puts more of your weight on the front leg.

### How far should my front foot be from the bench?

About 2-3 feet, depending on your height. At the bottom, your front shin should be roughly vertical with the knee over the ankle.

### Why do Bulgarian Split Squats instead of regular squats?

They build strength one leg at a time, help correct imbalances, and challenge balance more than squats. They are also easier on the lower back because the load is lighter.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Bulgarian Split Squat work?

The Dumbbell Bulgarian Split Squat primarily targets the quads, with secondary activation in the glutes, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Dumbbell Bulgarian Split Squat good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Bulgarian Split Squat is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of quads strength with easier variations first.
