# Dumbbell Lying Neutral Grip Tricep Extension

## Quick answer

A lying triceps extension with a dumbbell in each hand and your palms facing each other, lowering the weights beside your head. It works the triceps. Beginner arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets triceps, also works chest, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Chest, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your back on a flat bench, feet flat on the floor.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand straight up over your shoulders, palms facing each other.
3. Keep your elbows pointing up and bend them to lower the dumbbells down beside your head.
4. Straighten your elbows to raise the dumbbells back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Lie on a bench holding a dumbbell in each hand above your chest, palms facing each other.
- Bend only at the elbows and lower the dumbbells beside your head, keeping your upper arms still.
- Straighten your arms back to the start and squeeze the triceps at the top.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the upper arms drift back and forth, which turns the extension into a pullover and takes work off the triceps.
- Letting the elbows flare out wide, which reduces triceps work and can strain the elbows.
- Dropping the dumbbells quickly toward your head instead of lowering them under control.

## FAQ

### Why use a neutral grip for lying Tricep Extensions?

Palms facing each other is easier on the wrists and elbows for many people than a palms-down grip, and it lets the dumbbells pass beside your head.

### Is this the same as a skull crusher?

It is a dumbbell version of the same movement. A skull crusher usually uses a barbell or EZ bar with a palms-down grip.

### How heavy should I go?

Use a weight you can control for 10-15 reps. The dumbbells travel close to your head, so end the set when your form starts to slip.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Lying Neutral Grip Tricep Extension work?

The Dumbbell Lying Neutral Grip Tricep Extension primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the chest, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Lying Neutral Grip Tricep Extension good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Lying Neutral Grip Tricep Extension is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building triceps strength.
