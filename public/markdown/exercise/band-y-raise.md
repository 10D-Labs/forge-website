# Band Y-raise

## Quick answer

A raise where you lift straight arms up and out against a band until your body forms a Y. It works the shoulders, with help from the upper back. Beginner shoulders exercise using resistance band. Targets shoulders, also works traps, rhomboids.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Traps, Rhomboids
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand on a resistance band with your feet shoulder-width apart. Hold an end in each hand in front of your thighs, palms facing in.
2. Keep your arms straight and lift them up and out until they are overhead and your body makes a Y shape.
3. Lower your arms slowly back to your thighs.

## Pro tips

- Raise your arms in a Y shape (about 30 degrees from vertical) to target the lower traps and posterior delts.
- Keep a slight bend in the elbows and lead with the thumbs pointing upward.
- Squeeze the shoulder blades together at the top of the Y position for a one-second hold.

## Common mistakes

- Raising the arms straight out to the side (T shape) instead of angled forward in a Y, which shifts the emphasis away from the lower traps.
- Shrugging the shoulders up instead of keeping them depressed and retracted throughout.
- Using a band that is too thick, which prevents achieving full range of motion overhead.

## FAQ

### What muscles do Y-raises target?

Primarily the lower trapezius, posterior deltoids, and serratus anterior. These are critical muscles for shoulder stability and overhead function.

### Are Y-raises a rehab exercise or a strength exercise?

Both. They are widely used in shoulder rehab and prehab programs, but also valuable as a warmup or accessory exercise for anyone who presses overhead.

### How often should you do Band Y-raises?

3-5 times per week as part of a shoulder warmup routine. 2-3 sets of 12-15 reps with a light band is sufficient.

### What muscles does the Band Y-raise work?

The Band Y-raise primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the traps, rhomboids. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Band Y-raise good for beginners?

Yes, the Band Y-raise is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building shoulders strength.
