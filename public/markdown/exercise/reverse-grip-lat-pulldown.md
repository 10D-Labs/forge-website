# Reverse Grip Lat Pulldown

## Quick answer

A plate-loaded lever pulldown done with your palms facing you. It works the lats, with help from the biceps and upper back. Beginner back exercise using machine. Targets lats, also works biceps, rhomboids, rear deltoids.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Rhomboids, Rear Deltoids
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit in the lever pulldown machine with your thighs under the pad and your feet flat.
2. Reach up and hold the handles with your palms facing you.
3. Pull the handles down, bringing your elbows down to your sides, until your hands reach your shoulders.
4. Let the handles rise slowly until your arms are straight overhead.

## Pro tips

- Use an underhand (supinated) grip slightly narrower than shoulder width to maximize bicep and lower lat involvement.
- Pull the bar to your upper chest while arching your upper back slightly and lifting your chest.
- Focus on driving your elbows straight down and behind you rather than pulling the bar with your hands.

## Common mistakes

- Gripping too wide with a reverse grip, which puts the wrists in an uncomfortable position and reduces bicep activation.
- Leaning back excessively to pull the weight, which turns the pulldown into a body-english row.
- Not achieving a full stretch at the top by keeping tension in the arms instead of fully extending overhead.

## FAQ

### Is the reverse grip pulldown better for biceps than a regular pulldown?

Yes, the underhand grip increases bicep activation compared to an overhand grip, making it a good exercise for training both the lats and biceps simultaneously.

### Does the reverse grip pulldown hit the lower lats?

Yes, the supinated grip and narrower hand position tend to emphasize the lower lats more than a wide overhand pulldown.

### What grip width should I use for reverse grip pulldowns?

Shoulder width or slightly narrower. This allows the wrists to stay in a comfortable position while maximizing the range of motion and muscle engagement.

### What muscles does the Reverse Grip Lat Pulldown work?

The Reverse Grip Lat Pulldown primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the biceps, rhomboids, rear deltoids. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Reverse Grip Lat Pulldown good for beginners?

Yes, the Reverse Grip Lat Pulldown is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building lats strength.
