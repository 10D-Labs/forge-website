# Feet Elevated Pushup

## Quick answer

A pushup with your feet up on a bench, which shifts more of your weight onto your hands. It works the upper chest, shoulders and triceps. Intermediate chest exercise using body weight. Targets chest, also works triceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Place your feet on a flat bench and your hands flat on the floor, about shoulder-width apart. Straighten your arms and body.
2. Bend your elbows to lower your chest until it's just above the floor, keeping your body straight from head to heels.
3. Push back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Place your feet on a bench or elevated surface with your hands on the floor. The higher your feet, the more this targets the upper chest and front delts.
- Keep your core braced and body in a straight line from head to heels. Do not let your hips sag or pike up.
- Lower your chest all the way to the floor with your elbows at a 45-degree angle from your torso, then press up.

## Common mistakes

- Placing the feet too high before building enough shoulder and chest strength, which can strain the shoulders.
- Letting the hips sag, which stresses the lower back and reduces chest work.
- Flaring the elbows straight out to the sides (90 degrees), which puts extra stress on the shoulder joints.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the Feet Elevated Pushup target?

Raising your feet shifts emphasis to the upper chest and the front of the shoulders. The higher the feet, the more shoulder-dominant the exercise becomes.

### How high should my feet be for Feet Elevated Pushups?

A standard bench (16-18 inches) gives a good balance of upper chest work. A higher surface increases shoulder involvement. Start lower and progress gradually.

### What muscles does the Feet Elevated Pushup work?

The Feet Elevated Pushup primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Feet Elevated Pushup good for beginners?

The Feet Elevated Pushup is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of chest strength with easier variations first.
