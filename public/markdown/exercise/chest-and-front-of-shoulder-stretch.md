# Chest and Front of Shoulder Stretch

## Quick answer

A standing stretch that lifts a band from your hips up and over to behind your head. It stretches the chest and the front of the shoulders. Beginner chest exercise using resistance band. Targets chest, also works shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Stretching

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart. Hold a band in front of your hips with your hands wide apart, palms facing your thighs.
2. Lift the band up and over your head, bending your elbows as it rises.
3. Bring it down behind your head until your elbows point out to the sides and bend at about a right angle.
4. Reverse the path slowly to bring the band back to your hips.

## Pro tips

- Interlace your fingers behind your back and gently lift your arms while squeezing your shoulder blades together.
- Keep your chest lifted and avoid rounding the upper back during the stretch.
- Hold for 20-30 seconds and breathe deeply to allow the chest and shoulder muscles to fully lengthen.

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the upper back forward, which counteracts the chest stretch you are trying to achieve.
- Forcing the arms too high behind the back, which can strain the shoulder joint.
- Holding the breath instead of breathing steadily, which prevents the muscles from relaxing into the stretch.

## FAQ

### When is the best time to do this stretch?

After any chest or shoulder workout, or at the end of a long day of sitting. It counteracts the forward-rounded posture from desk work.

### How long should you hold the stretch?

20-30 seconds for 2-3 sets. For significant flexibility improvements, hold for 45-60 seconds.

### Can this stretch help with posture?

Yes. Tight pecs and anterior delts pull the shoulders forward. Regular stretching lengthens these muscles and allows the back muscles to pull the shoulders into a better position.

### What muscles does the Chest and Front of Shoulder Stretch work?

The Chest and Front of Shoulder Stretch primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the shoulders. It's classified as a stretching exercise for the chest.

### Is the Chest and Front of Shoulder Stretch good for beginners?

Yes, the Chest and Front of Shoulder Stretch is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building chest strength.
