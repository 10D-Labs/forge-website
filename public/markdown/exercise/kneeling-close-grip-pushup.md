# Kneeling Close Grip Pushup

## Quick answer

A pushup from your knees with your hands close together, easier than a full close-grip pushup. It works the triceps, with help from the chest and shoulders. Beginner arms exercise using body weight. Targets triceps, also works chest, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Chest, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Get on your hands and knees with your knees hip-width apart and your hands close together under your chest.
2. Bend your elbows to lower your chest until it's just above the floor, keeping your elbows close to your sides.
3. Push through your palms to straighten your arms.

## Pro tips

- Place your hands directly under your shoulders or slightly narrower to maximize tricep activation.
- Keep your elbows tucked close to your ribs, pointing backward, not flaring outward.
- Lower your chest between your hands and press back up by fully extending your arms for a complete tricep contraction.

## Common mistakes

- Placing the hands too far forward, which shifts the load to the shoulders and away from the triceps.
- Letting the hips sag or pike up, breaking the straight line from knees to shoulders.
- Not fully extending the arms at the top, which cuts the tricep lockout and reduces muscle activation.

## FAQ

### Do Kneeling Close Grip Pushups work the triceps?

Yes, the close hand placement shifts significant emphasis to the triceps compared to a standard Pushup, making it one of the best bodyweight tricep exercises for beginners.

### How do I progress from kneeling to full Close Grip Pushups?

Once you can do 3 sets of 15 Kneeling Close Grip Pushups with good form, start attempting full Close Grip Pushups from your toes, even if you can only do a few.

### Where exactly should I place my hands for Close Grip Pushups?

Place your hands shoulder-width apart or slightly narrower, directly under your chest. You do not need to touch your hands together as that can strain the wrists.

### What muscles does the Kneeling Close Grip Pushup work?

The Kneeling Close Grip Pushup primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the chest, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Kneeling Close Grip Pushup good for beginners?

Yes, the Kneeling Close Grip Pushup is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building triceps strength.
