# Kettlebell Swing

## Quick answer

A kettlebell swung from between your legs up to shoulder height by driving your hips forward. It works the glutes, with the hamstrings and abs helping. Intermediate legs exercise using kettlebell. Targets glutes, also works hamstrings, core.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Core
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and your toes turned slightly out, holding a kettlebell in both hands with straight arms.
2. Bend your knees slightly and push your hips back, swinging the kettlebell back between your legs with your back flat.
3. Drive your hips forward to swing the kettlebell up to shoulder height, arms straight.
4. Let it swing back down between your legs and go straight into the next swing.

## Pro tips

- This is a hip hinge, not a squat. Your knees should bend slightly, but the power comes from snapping the hips forward.
- At the top of the swing, squeeze your glutes hard and brace your abs as if you're about to be punched. The bell should float to shoulder height.
- Let the Kettlebell Swing back between your legs on each rep by hinging your hips back. Your forearms should contact your inner thighs.

## Common mistakes

- Squatting the swing by bending the knees too much instead of hinging at the hips.
- Using the arms to lift the kettlebell rather than projecting it forward with hip power.
- Hyperextending the lower back at the top instead of finishing in a tall, braced standing position.

## FAQ

### How heavy should my kettlebell be for swings?

Most men should start with 16 kg and progress to 24-32 kg. Most women should start with 12 kg and progress to 16-24 kg. The kettlebell should be heavy enough that you must use your hips, not your arms.

### Are Kettlebell Swings cardio or strength?

Both. They're a unique hybrid that builds posterior chain strength (glutes, hamstrings, back) while elevating heart rate significantly. A 20-minute swing session can burn as many calories as running.

### How many swings should I do per workout?

For beginners, start with 50-75 total swings in sets of 10-15. Intermediate trainees can aim for 100-200 swings. The Simple & Sinister standard is 100 one-arm swings in 5 minutes.

### What muscles does the Kettlebell Swing work?

The Kettlebell Swing primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, core. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Kettlebell Swing good for beginners?

The Kettlebell Swing is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of glutes strength with easier variations first.
