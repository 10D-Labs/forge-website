# Dumbbell Lunge

## Quick answer

A forward lunge holding a dumbbell in each hand, alternating legs. It works the glutes, plus the quads, hamstrings and calves. Intermediate legs exercise using dumbbell. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a dumbbell in each hand.
2. Step forward with your right foot and bend both knees to lower your body into a lunge. Keep your back straight and your chest up.
3. Push through your right heel to step back to standing, then lunge with your left leg. Keep alternating legs.

## Pro tips

- Take a long enough stride that your front knee stays directly over your ankle at the bottom, not pushed past your toes.
- Keep your torso upright and eyes forward throughout the lunge to maintain balance and protect your lower back.
- Push through the heel of your front foot to drive back to standing, which emphasizes the glutes over the quads.

## Common mistakes

- Taking too short a step, which forces the knee past the toes and puts excessive stress on the knee joint.
- Leaning the torso forward excessively, which shifts the load off the legs and strains the lower back.
- Letting the back knee slam into the ground instead of lowering it with control just above the floor.

## FAQ

### Are Dumbbell Lunges better than Barbell Lunges?

Dumbbells distribute load more evenly, are easier to balance, and put less compressive force on the spine. Barbell Lunges allow heavier loads for max strength.

### Should I do Walking Lunges or stationary lunges with dumbbells?

Walking Lunges add a balance and coordination challenge. Stationary (alternating in place) lunges are better for beginners learning the pattern.

### How deep should I go on Dumbbell Lunges?

Lower until your back knee is 1-2 inches from the ground and your front thigh is roughly parallel to the floor. Going deeper adds glute activation but requires good mobility.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Lunge work?

The Dumbbell Lunge primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Dumbbell Lunge good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Lunge is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of glutes strength with easier variations first.
