# Staircase Calf Raise

## Quick answer

A calf raise on the edge of a stair, so your heels can travel further than they do on flat floor. It works the calves. Beginner legs exercise using body weight. Targets calves, also works ankles, feet.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Calves
- **Secondary Muscles:** Ankles, Feet
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand on the edge of a stair or sturdy step with your toes on it and your heels hanging off.
2. Hold a railing or wall for balance if you need to.
3. Raise your heels as high as you can, lifting your weight onto the balls of your feet.
4. Lower your heels slowly back down.

## Pro tips

- Let your heels drop below the step at the bottom to get a full stretch on the Achilles tendon and calf muscle.
- Pause for 2 seconds at the top of each rep and squeeze hard to maximize the peak contraction.
- Perform the exercise slowly, taking 2 seconds up and 3 seconds down, to keep the calves under tension longer.

## Common mistakes

- Bouncing at the bottom of the movement, which uses the Achilles tendon like a spring instead of working the calf muscle.
- Not allowing the heels to drop fully below the step, which limits the range of motion and reduces the stretch benefit.
- Leaning forward and shifting weight to the toes of the wrong part of the foot; press through the balls of your feet.

## FAQ

### How often should I do Staircase Calf Raises?

Calves recover quickly and respond well to frequency. You can train them 3-4 times per week with 3-4 sets of 15-20 reps.

### Is one leg at a time better for calf raises?

Single-leg calf raises are more challenging and help correct imbalances between legs. Start with both legs and progress to single-leg once you can do 20+ reps comfortably.

### Why aren't my calves growing from calf raises?

Calves need high volume, full range of motion, and slow eccentrics. Most people bounce through reps too quickly and don't let the heel drop below the step for a full stretch.

### What muscles does the Staircase Calf Raise work?

The Staircase Calf Raise primarily targets the calves, with secondary activation in the ankles, feet. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Staircase Calf Raise good for beginners?

Yes, the Staircase Calf Raise is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building calves strength.
