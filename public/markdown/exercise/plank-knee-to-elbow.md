# Plank Knee to Elbow

## Quick answer

A move from the top of a pushup where you bring one knee up toward the same-side elbow, then switch sides. It works the glutes and abs, with your chest, shoulders and arms holding you up. Advanced legs exercise using body weight. Targets glutes, also works core, chest, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Core, Chest, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Start at the top of a pushup, arms straight, hands slightly wider than shoulder-width apart and feet together.
2. Keeping your arms straight, bring your right knee forward toward your right elbow, keeping the foot off the floor.
3. Return your right foot to the start.
4. Bring your left knee toward your left elbow, then return it. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Start at the top of a pushup with your hands under your shoulders and your body in a straight line.
- Bring one knee up toward the same-side elbow, then return it and switch sides.
- Keep your hips low and level as the knee comes forward.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips pike up when bringing the knee to the elbow.
- Only bringing the knee partway instead of reaching toward the elbow.
- Letting the hips sag as you tire.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the Plank Knee to Elbow work?

The abs and obliques drive the knee forward, the glutes help steady the hips, and the chest, shoulders and arms hold you up.

### How is it different from a Mountain Climber?

Mountain Climbers drive the knee straight toward the chest, often fast. Here the knee comes out to the side toward the elbow, slowly, which works the obliques more.

### What muscles does the Plank Knee to Elbow work?

The Plank Knee to Elbow primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the core, chest, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Plank Knee to Elbow good for beginners?

The Plank Knee to Elbow is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental legs movements first.
