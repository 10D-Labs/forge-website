# Kettlebell Two Arm Row

## Quick answer

A bent-over row with a kettlebell in each hand, pulling both up together. It works the upper back. Intermediate back exercise using kettlebell. Targets upper back, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and knees slightly bent, holding a kettlebell in each hand, palms facing your body.
2. Bend forward at your hips, keeping your back straight.
3. Pull the kettlebells up toward your chest, drawing your shoulder blades together.
4. Lower the kettlebells slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Hinge at the hips until your torso is about 45 degrees to the floor, and maintain this angle throughout the set.
- Grip the kettlebell handles with a neutral grip and pull them toward your hip creases, not your chest.
- Squeeze your shoulder blades together hard at the top and hold for a beat before lowering.

## Common mistakes

- Standing too upright, which turns the row into a shrug-like movement instead of a back exercise.
- Rounding the lower back to reach the starting position instead of hinging properly at the hips.
- Swinging the kettlebells to build momentum rather than pulling with controlled back engagement.

## FAQ

### Can kettlebell rows replace dumbbell rows?

Yes, for most purposes. The thicker kettlebell handle also builds grip strength. However, very heavy loads are easier to manage with dumbbells since kettlebells max out at lower weights in most gyms.

### Should my knees be bent during the row?

Yes, keep a slight bend in the knees (15-20 degrees) to protect your lower back and allow a deeper hip hinge.

### How do I avoid lower back pain during bent-over kettlebell rows?

Brace your core like you are about to be punched, maintain a flat back, and do not let your torso drop below 45 degrees. If your back rounds, the weight is too heavy.

### What muscles does the Kettlebell Two Arm Row work?

The Kettlebell Two Arm Row primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Kettlebell Two Arm Row good for beginners?

The Kettlebell Two Arm Row is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of upper back strength with easier variations first.
