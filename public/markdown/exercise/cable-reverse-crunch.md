# Cable Reverse Crunch

## Quick answer

A reverse crunch with a low cable strapped to your ankles, curling your hips off the floor against the pull. It works the abs, with help from the front of the hips. Intermediate core exercise using cable. Targets abs, also works hip flexors.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hip Flexors
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Strap your ankles to a low pulley and lie on your back, feet toward the machine, arms at your sides, palms down.
2. Lift your legs so your thighs point straight up and your knees are bent, lower legs level with the floor.
3. Curl your hips off the floor, pulling your knees in toward your chest.
4. Lower your hips slowly back to the floor, keeping your knees bent.

## Pro tips

- Attach the cable to your ankles or hold it between your feet to add resistance to the pelvic curl.
- Keep your upper body stable on the mat and let only your pelvis and lower body move.
- Use light resistance and focus on the quality of the pelvic curl rather than loading heavy.

## Common mistakes

- Using too much cable weight, which causes the hip flexors to take over from the abs.
- Pulling with the arms to assist the crunch instead of keeping the upper body stable and driving with the abs.
- Not curling the pelvis off the mat far enough, which limits lower ab activation.

## FAQ

### How is a Cable Reverse Crunch different from a regular Reverse Crunch?

The cable adds external resistance throughout the movement, making the abs work harder, especially at the top where bodyweight Reverse Crunches lose tension.

### What cable height should you use?

Use a low pulley setting. Lie facing away from the machine with the cable attached to your ankles, creating resistance as you curl your pelvis upward.

### What is a good weight to start with for Cable Reverse Crunches?

Start with 10-20 pounds. The movement is challenging even with light weight because the cable maintains constant tension.

### What muscles does the Cable Reverse Crunch work?

The Cable Reverse Crunch primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the hip flexors. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Cable Reverse Crunch good for beginners?

The Cable Reverse Crunch is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of abs strength with easier variations first.
