# Dumbbell Single Arm Seated Neutral Wrist Curl

## Quick answer

A seated wrist exercise with your forearm on your thigh, tilting a dumbbell up and down with your thumb on top. It works the forearms. Beginner arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets forearms, also works biceps, brachialis.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Forearms
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Brachialis
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on the end of a flat bench, feet flat, and lean forward. Rest one forearm along your thigh with your hand just past your knee.
2. Hold a dumbbell in that hand, palm facing in and thumb on top, with the dumbbell tipped forward and down.
3. Keeping your forearm on your thigh, lift the top of the dumbbell up with your wrist until it stands upright.
4. Lower it slowly back to the tipped position. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Rest your forearm on your thigh with a neutral grip (thumb up) and curl the wrist upward in a hammer-like motion.
- Only your wrist should move, keeping the forearm completely stationary on the thigh.
- Use a light weight and high reps (15-25) since the forearm muscles involved are small and prone to overuse injury.

## Common mistakes

- Moving the entire forearm instead of isolating the wrist curl movement.
- Using too heavy a weight, which forces compensatory movement from the elbow.
- Positioning the wrist too far back on the thigh, which reduces the effective range of motion.

## FAQ

### What does a neutral grip wrist curl target?

It targets the brachioradialis and the wrist extensors and flexors in a neutral position, which is different from the standard palm-up or palm-down wrist curl.

### How is this different from a regular wrist curl?

A regular wrist curl uses a supinated (palm up) or pronated (palm down) grip. The neutral grip targets the radial deviation muscles of the forearm.

### Is this exercise good for grip strength?

It contributes to overall forearm development but is not the most effective grip exercise. Farmer's carries, dead hangs, and plate pinches are better for pure grip strength.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Single Arm Seated Neutral Wrist Curl work?

The Dumbbell Single Arm Seated Neutral Wrist Curl primarily targets the forearms, with secondary activation in the biceps, brachialis. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Single Arm Seated Neutral Wrist Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Single Arm Seated Neutral Wrist Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building forearms strength.
