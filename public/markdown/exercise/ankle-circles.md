# Ankle Circles

## Quick answer

A standing warm-up where you turn one lifted foot in slow circles. It loosens the ankle and works the calves and the small muscles that keep the ankle steady. Beginner legs exercise using body weight. Targets calves, also works ankle stabilizers.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Calves
- **Secondary Muscles:** Ankle Stabilizers
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Mobility

---

## How to perform

1. Stand tall with your hands on your hips and your weight on one leg.
2. Lift your other foot just off the floor.
3. Turn that ankle in slow circles, moving only your foot. Do all your circles on one foot, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Make the circles as large as possible to move the ankle through its full range of motion in every direction.
- Perform the circles slowly and deliberately, spending extra time in any direction that feels restricted.
- Do 10-15 circles in each direction per ankle for a thorough warm-up.

## Common mistakes

- Moving the entire leg from the knee instead of isolating the rotation at the ankle joint.
- Making tiny circles that do not challenge the full range of motion.
- Rushing through the circles without paying attention to stiff or painful points in the range.

## FAQ

### Why are Ankle Circles important?

They warm up the ankle joint, improve mobility, and can help prevent sprains by taking the joint through its full range before weight-bearing exercise.

### When should I do Ankle Circles?

Before any lower body workout, running, or sport that involves footwork. They are also helpful first thing in the morning if you have stiff ankles.

### Can Ankle Circles help with ankle stiffness after a sprain?

Yes, gentle Ankle Circles are one of the first mobility exercises recommended during ankle sprain rehabilitation to restore range of motion.

### What muscles does the Ankle Circles work?

The Ankle Circles primarily targets the calves, with secondary activation in the ankle stabilizers. It's classified as a mobility exercise for the legs.

### Is the Ankle Circles good for beginners?

Yes, the Ankle Circles is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building calves strength.
