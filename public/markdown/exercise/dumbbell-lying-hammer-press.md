# Dumbbell Lying Hammer Press

## Quick answer

A flat-bench dumbbell press with your palms facing each other. It works the chest, with help from the triceps and shoulders. Beginner chest exercise using dumbbell. Targets chest, also works triceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie flat on a bench with a dumbbell in each hand, arms straight up and palms facing each other.
2. Lower the dumbbells to the sides of your chest, until your elbows are bent to 90 degrees.
3. Press them back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Keep your palms facing each other (hammer grip) for the entire press to shift emphasis toward the inner chest and triceps.
- Lower the dumbbells to the sides of your chest with elbows at roughly 45 degrees, not flared out wide.
- Press the dumbbells straight up and slightly inward at the top to maximize chest contraction with the neutral grip.

## Common mistakes

- Flaring elbows out to 90 degrees, which increases shoulder stress and reduces the benefit of the neutral grip.
- Rotating the wrists during the press so palms face forward, negating the hammer grip advantage.
- Bouncing the dumbbells off the chest at the bottom instead of pausing for a controlled reversal.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between a hammer press and a regular dumbbell press?

The hammer (neutral) grip keeps palms facing each other, which reduces shoulder stress, recruits more triceps, and emphasizes the inner chest compared to a pronated grip press.

### Is the hammer press easier on the shoulders?

Yes. The neutral grip places the shoulder joint in a more natural position, making it a good alternative for lifters with shoulder pain during standard bench pressing.

### Can I build chest with the hammer press?

Absolutely. The chest still works hard as the primary mover. The slightly different grip angle does not reduce chest activation significantly.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Lying Hammer Press work?

The Dumbbell Lying Hammer Press primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Dumbbell Lying Hammer Press good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Lying Hammer Press is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building chest strength.
