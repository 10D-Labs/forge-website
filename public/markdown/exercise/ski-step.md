# Ski Step

## Quick answer

A cardio drill where you stand with your hands on your hips and switch your feet forward and back past each other, like skis. It raises your heart rate and works the quads, hamstrings, glutes and calves. Beginner cardio exercise using body weight. Targets cardiovascular, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, glutes, calves.

- **Body Part:** Cardio
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Cardiovascular
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Cardio

---

## How to perform

1. Stand tall with your hands on your hips. Put one foot about a step ahead of the other, knees slightly bent and back heel up.
2. Bring your feet together under your hips.
3. Keep moving them past each other so the back foot ends up in front and the front foot behind.
4. Switch back the same way, staying light on your feet. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Land on one foot at a time with a slight knee bend and immediately push off to the opposite side.
- Swing your arms in opposition to your legs, just like actual skiing, to maintain rhythm and balance.
- Stay low throughout the movement; the lower your athletic stance, the more your legs and glutes work.

## Common mistakes

- Landing with locked-out knees, which increases impact stress and reduces power for the next hop.
- Standing too upright, which reduces the athletic base and glute engagement.
- Moving too quickly before establishing good landing mechanics on each side.

## FAQ

### What's the difference between Ski Steps and Skater Hops?

They're very similar movements. Ski Steps tend to emphasize quicker, smaller side-to-side movements, while Skater Hops focus on maximal lateral distance per jump.

### Are Ski Steps a good cardio exercise?

Yes, they elevate the heart rate quickly and build lateral agility. They're commonly used in HIIT circuits and sports conditioning programs.

### How long should I do Ski Steps?

30-60 seconds per set for conditioning, or 20-30 reps total. Start on the lower end and build up as your coordination and endurance improve.

### What muscles does the Ski Step work?

The Ski Step primarily targets the cardiovascular, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, glutes, calves. It's classified as a cardio exercise for the cardio.

### Is the Ski Step good for beginners?

Yes, the Ski Step is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building cardiovascular strength.
