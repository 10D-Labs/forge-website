# Calf Push Stretch with Hands Against Wall

## Quick answer

A standing calf stretch with your toes propped up against a wall, leaning in with your hands on the wall. It stretches the calf, with some stretch in the hamstrings. Beginner legs exercise using body weight. Targets calves, also works hamstrings.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Calves
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Stretching

---

## How to perform

1. Stand facing a wall and put your palms flat on it at chest height.
2. Set one heel on the floor by the wall with the ball of that foot up against the wall, leg straight. Step the other foot back.
3. Bend your elbows and lean your hips and chest toward the wall until you feel a stretch in the front leg's calf.
4. Hold for the set time, then ease back slowly and switch legs.

## Pro tips

- Press into the wall with both hands and drive the back heel firmly into the ground for maximum calf stretch.
- Experiment with the distance between your back foot and the wall to find the position that gives the deepest comfortable stretch.
- Keep your hips square to the wall and avoid rotating your torso to one side.

## Common mistakes

- Placing the back foot too close to the wall, which does not create enough stretch on the calf.
- Bending the back knee, which reduces the gastrocnemius stretch and shifts it to the soleus.
- Pushing too hard into the wall with the arms instead of using the body weight to lean forward and stretch the calf.

## FAQ

### How is this different from the regular wall calf stretch?

It is essentially the same movement with slightly different hand positioning. The key stretch mechanics are identical: straight back knee, heel on the ground, lean forward.

### Can I stretch both calves at the same time?

No, this stretch works one calf at a time. The staggered stance is what creates the stretch. You must switch legs to stretch both sides.

### My heel keeps lifting. What should I do?

Move your back foot closer to the wall until you can keep the heel flat. Gradually increase the distance as your calf flexibility improves over weeks.

### What muscles does the Calf Push Stretch with Hands Against Wall work?

The Calf Push Stretch with Hands Against Wall primarily targets the calves, with secondary activation in the hamstrings. It's classified as a stretching exercise for the legs.

### Is the Calf Push Stretch with Hands Against Wall good for beginners?

Yes, the Calf Push Stretch with Hands Against Wall is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building calves strength.
