# Smith Machine Incline Tricep Extension

## Quick answer

A lying triceps extension on an incline bench in a Smith machine, lowering the bar toward your forehead. It works the triceps. Intermediate arms exercise using smith machine. Targets triceps, also works shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Smith Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set an incline bench in a Smith machine with the bar at shoulder height. Sit back against the pad, feet flat on the floor.
2. Hold the bar with your palms facing away from you, hands a little wider than your shoulders.
3. Straighten your arms to lift the bar off the hooks and hold it over your chest.
4. Keep your elbows close to your head and bend them to lower the bar slowly toward your forehead.
5. Straighten your arms to push the bar back up over your chest.

## Pro tips

- Set the incline bench inside the Smith machine and position the bar so it's directly above your forehead at full extension.
- Lower the bar by bending at the elbows only, keeping your upper arms stationary and perpendicular to the floor.
- The Smith machine's fixed path ensures consistent form; focus on slow, controlled reps.

## Common mistakes

- Positioning the bench too far forward or back, which misaligns the bar path with the natural extension arc.
- Flaring the elbows wide during the extension, reducing tricep isolation.
- Using too much weight and letting the bar drop quickly, relying on the Smith machine's safety to catch it.

## FAQ

### Why use the Smith machine for Tricep Extensions?

The fixed bar path eliminates the need for stabilization, allowing you to focus entirely on tricep contraction. It's also safer for going to failure since you can rack the bar at any point.

### What incline angle is best?

A 30-45 degree incline provides a good stretch on the long head of the triceps. The angle can be adjusted based on comfort.

### How does this compare to free-weight lying extensions?

The Smith machine removes the stabilizer challenge, which some view as a disadvantage. However, it allows you to push closer to failure safely, which can be valuable for hypertrophy.

### What muscles does the Smith Machine Incline Tricep Extension work?

The Smith Machine Incline Tricep Extension primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Smith Machine Incline Tricep Extension good for beginners?

The Smith Machine Incline Tricep Extension is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of triceps strength with easier variations first.
