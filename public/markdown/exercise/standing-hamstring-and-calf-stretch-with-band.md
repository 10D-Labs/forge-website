# Standing Hamstring and Calf Stretch with Band

## Quick answer

A standing stretch where you fold forward from the hips with straight legs, holding a band looped under your feet. It stretches the hamstrings and calves. Beginner legs exercise using resistance band. Targets hamstrings, also works calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Hamstrings
- **Secondary Muscles:** Calves
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Stretching

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with both feet on the middle of the band and hold one end in each hand.
2. Keep your legs straight and hinge forward from your hips, back straight, sliding your hands down to about knee height.
3. Stop when you feel a stretch in the backs of your thighs and calves.
4. Hold for the set time, then stand back up slowly.

## Pro tips

- Stand on the middle of the band with your feet together and hold one end in each hand.
- Fold forward from the hips with your legs straight, letting the band guide your hands toward your feet.
- Keep your back long and pull gently on the band to deepen the stretch in your hamstrings and calves.

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the back to reach lower instead of hinging at the hips.
- Locking the knees hard; keep them straight but soft.
- Bouncing at the bottom instead of holding the stretch.

## FAQ

### What can I use instead of a band?

A yoga strap or a long towel works the same way.

### Can I do this stretch sitting down?

Yes. Sitting with your legs straight and the band around your feet stretches the same muscles and lets you relax more.

### How does the band help?

It bridges the gap between your hands and feet, so you can keep your back straight instead of rounding over to reach.

### What muscles does the Standing Hamstring and Calf Stretch with Band work?

The Standing Hamstring and Calf Stretch with Band primarily targets the hamstrings, with secondary activation in the calves. It's classified as a stretching exercise for the legs.

### Is the Standing Hamstring and Calf Stretch with Band good for beginners?

Yes, the Standing Hamstring and Calf Stretch with Band is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building hamstrings strength.
