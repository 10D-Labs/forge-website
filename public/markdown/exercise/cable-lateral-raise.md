# Cable Lateral Raise

## Quick answer

A lateral raise with two low cables, crossed so each hand holds the opposite one, lifting your arms out to shoulder height. It works the sides of the shoulders. Intermediate shoulders exercise using cable. Targets shoulders, also works traps, triceps.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Traps, Triceps
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set both pulleys of a cable machine at the bottom. Stand between them with your feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Hold the left handle in your right hand and the right handle in your left so the cables cross, palms facing down.
3. Keep your arms straight and raise them out to the sides until they're level with the floor.
4. Lower your arms slowly back down.

## Pro tips

- Position the cable at the lowest setting and stand sideways to the machine so the cable crosses behind or in front of your body for the best resistance curve.
- Lead the movement with your elbow, not your hand. Think about pushing your elbow up toward the ceiling.
- Stop at shoulder height. Going higher recruits the upper traps and reduces lateral delt isolation.

## Common mistakes

- Shrugging the shoulder up as you raise the arm, which activates the upper traps instead of the lateral delts.
- Using too much weight and swinging the cable up with body momentum rather than a controlled Lateral Raise.
- Raising the arm in front of the body instead of directly to the side, which shifts emphasis to the anterior delt.

## FAQ

### Should I do Cable Lateral Raises standing or seated?

Standing allows for slightly more weight and a natural body position. Seated removes the ability to use momentum, making it stricter and better for isolation.

### Cable Lateral Raise vs Dumbbell Lateral Raise: which is better?

Cable Lateral Raises provide constant tension throughout the range of motion, including at the bottom where dumbbells provide zero resistance. Cables are generally superior for lateral delt hypertrophy.

### What muscles does the Cable Lateral Raise work?

The Cable Lateral Raise primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the traps, triceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Cable Lateral Raise good for beginners?

The Cable Lateral Raise is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of shoulders strength with easier variations first.
