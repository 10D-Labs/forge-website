# Dumbbell Standing Single Arm Reverse Curl

## Quick answer

A one-arm curl with your palm facing down instead of up. It works the biceps and the muscles on top of the forearm. Beginner arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand tall with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a dumbbell in one hand with your arm straight, close to your body, palm facing behind you.
2. Keep your upper arm still and curl the dumbbell up toward your shoulder, keeping your palm facing down as it rises.
3. Lower it slowly until your arm is straight. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Hold the dumbbell with an overhand grip (palm facing down) and curl it up while keeping the upper arm stationary.
- Focus on the top portion of your forearm (brachioradialis) doing the work rather than flipping your wrist during the curl.
- Keep the wrist rigid and in line with the forearm throughout to avoid wrist strain.

## Common mistakes

- Rotating the wrist to a palms-up position during the curl, which turns this into a standard curl.
- Using body swing to get the weight up, especially since the reverse grip is mechanically weaker than a supinated grip.
- Going too heavy, leading to poor form and potential wrist strain.

## FAQ

### What is the benefit of doing reverse curls one arm at a time?

Single arm allows you to focus on each forearm individually, correct strength imbalances, and use the free hand for bracing if needed.

### Will single arm reverse curls build bigger forearms?

Yes. The reverse grip emphasizes the brachioradialis, which is the most visible forearm muscle and a major contributor to forearm size.

### How many reps for single arm reverse curls?

10-15 reps per arm. Use lighter weight than standard curls and focus on controlled form throughout each rep.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Standing Single Arm Reverse Curl work?

The Dumbbell Standing Single Arm Reverse Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Standing Single Arm Reverse Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Standing Single Arm Reverse Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building biceps strength.
