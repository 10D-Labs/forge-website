# Walking High Knees Lunge

## Quick answer

Lunges that move forward, with a high knee lift before each step. It raises your heart rate and works the quads, hamstrings, glutes and calves. Beginner cardio exercise using body weight. Targets cardiovascular, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, glutes, calves.

- **Body Part:** Cardio
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Cardiovascular
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Cardio

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart.
2. Lift your right knee toward your chest as high as you can, balancing on your left leg.
3. Step forward with your right foot and lower into a lunge, both knees bent to 90 degrees.
4. Push off your right foot and lift your left knee toward your chest, then step forward into a lunge on that side.
5. Keep alternating legs as you move forward at a steady pace.

## Pro tips

- Drive each knee up explosively before stepping into the lunge to keep your heart rate elevated.
- Maintain an upright torso throughout both the high knee and lunge phases.
- Keep a steady rhythm and avoid pausing between the high knee and the lunge step.

## Common mistakes

- Rushing the high knee and not driving it high enough, reducing the hip flexor and cardio benefit.
- Losing balance on the transition from high knee to lunge because of too-fast tempo.
- Letting the front knee cave inward during the lunge portion.

## FAQ

### What makes this different from regular Walking Lunges?

The high knee between each lunge adds a balance challenge, elevates the heart rate more, and engages the hip flexors actively. It's more of a dynamic cardio movement.

### How far should I walk?

20-30 yards per set is a good starting point. Focus on smooth, controlled transitions rather than covering distance quickly.

### Is this exercise good for a warm-up?

Excellent warm-up movement. It activates the glutes, quads, hip flexors, and core while gradually raising your heart rate and body temperature.

### What muscles does the Walking High Knees Lunge work?

The Walking High Knees Lunge primarily targets the cardiovascular, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, glutes, calves. It's classified as a cardio exercise for the cardio.

### Is the Walking High Knees Lunge good for beginners?

Yes, the Walking High Knees Lunge is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building cardiovascular strength.
