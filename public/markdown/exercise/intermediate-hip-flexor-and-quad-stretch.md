# Intermediate Hip Flexor and Quad Stretch

## Quick answer

A face-down stretch using a band over your shoulder to pull your heel toward your glutes. It stretches the quads on the front of the thigh. Intermediate legs exercise using resistance band. Targets quads, also works hip flexors.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Quads
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hip Flexors
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Stretching

---

## How to perform

1. Lie face down with your legs straight and a band looped around one ankle.
2. Bend that knee, run the band over your shoulder, and hold it with both hands in front of your head.
3. Pull the band to draw your heel toward your glutes until the front of your thigh stretches. Keep your hips on the floor.
4. Hold for the set time, then ease off slowly until your shin points straight up and switch legs.

## Pro tips

- Use the resistance band to gently assist pulling your heel closer to your glutes for a deeper quad stretch than you could achieve by hand alone.
- Keep your standing leg's knee slightly bent to maintain balance and protect the supporting knee.
- Tuck your pelvis slightly under (posterior tilt) to intensify the hip flexor stretch component.

## Common mistakes

- Arching the lower back excessively, which compresses the lumbar spine and reduces the hip flexor stretch.
- Pulling the foot too aggressively with the band, overshooting your current flexibility and straining the quad.
- Leaning forward at the waist instead of staying upright, which shifts the stretch away from the hip flexor.

## FAQ

### Why use a resistance band for this stretch?

The band provides gentle, consistent assistance to pull the heel closer to the glute, allowing a deeper stretch than many people can achieve by reaching back with their hand alone.

### What does this stretch help with?

It targets both the quadriceps and hip flexors, which become chronically shortened from sitting. Regular stretching here improves posture and reduces anterior Pelvic Tilt.

### Can I do this stretch without a band?

Yes, just grab your ankle with your hand. The band simply makes it easier for people with limited shoulder mobility or very tight quads to reach their foot.

### What muscles does the Intermediate Hip Flexor and Quad Stretch work?

The Intermediate Hip Flexor and Quad Stretch primarily targets the quads, with secondary activation in the hip flexors. It's classified as a stretching exercise for the legs.

### Is the Intermediate Hip Flexor and Quad Stretch good for beginners?

The Intermediate Hip Flexor and Quad Stretch is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of quads strength with easier variations first.
