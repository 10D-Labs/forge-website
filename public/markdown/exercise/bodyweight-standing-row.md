# Bodyweight Standing Row

## Quick answer

A standing row where you lean back from a fixed bar and pull your body toward it. It works the upper back, with help from the biceps and shoulders. Beginner back exercise using body weight. Targets upper back, also works biceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand facing a fixed bar at about chest height, such as a rung on wall bars, with your feet shoulder-width apart near its base.
2. Hold the bar with your palms facing down and lean back until your arms are straight, keeping your body straight.
3. Pull your chest toward the bar, drawing your shoulder blades together.
4. Lower yourself slowly back until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Lean back until your arms are fully extended and your body is at an angle that challenges you for 10-15 reps.
- Pull your chest toward the bar or handles by driving your elbows back, not by bending at the hips.
- Squeeze your shoulder blades together hard at the top of each rep and hold for one second.

## Common mistakes

- Standing too upright, which makes the exercise too easy and provides little training benefit for the back muscles.
- Bending at the hips during the pull instead of keeping the body rigid like a plank.
- Only pulling with the arms and not engaging the upper back muscles to retract the shoulder blades.

## FAQ

### How do I adjust difficulty for Bodyweight Standing Rows?

Lean back further to make it harder or stand more upright to make it easier. Foot position relative to the anchor point determines the body angle and difficulty.

### What muscles do Bodyweight Standing Rows work?

They target the upper back (rhomboids, traps), lats, and biceps, similar to machine or dumbbell rows but using your body weight as resistance.

### Are bodyweight rows a good Pullup alternative?

Yes, they work the same muscle groups at a lower intensity and are one of the best progression exercises for people working toward their first Pullup.

### What muscles does the Bodyweight Standing Row work?

The Bodyweight Standing Row primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the biceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Bodyweight Standing Row good for beginners?

Yes, the Bodyweight Standing Row is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building upper back strength.
