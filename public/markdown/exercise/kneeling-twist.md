# Kneeling Twist

## Quick answer

A machine twist done kneeling: your hips turn the pad side to side while your upper body stays braced on the handles. It works the abs and the obliques at your sides. Intermediate core exercise using machine. Targets abs, also works obliques, lower back.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Obliques, Lower Back
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Kneel on the machine's turning pad with your upper body facing forward. Hold the handles in front of your chest.
2. Keep your upper body still and braced on the handles.
3. Twist your hips to turn the pad to one side as far as is comfortable.
4. Return slowly to the middle, then twist to the other side. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Rotate from the thoracic spine (mid-back), not the lumbar spine. Your hips should stay relatively fixed while your upper body twists.
- Use a controlled tempo. Twist to one side for 2 seconds, hold for 1 second, return for 2 seconds.
- Keep your abs braced throughout the entire set, not just during the twist.

## Common mistakes

- Twisting from the lower back instead of the mid-back, which can strain the lumbar spine.
- Using momentum to swing from side to side rather than controlling the rotation with the obliques.
- Gripping the handles too tightly, which tenses the upper body and reduces the focus on the core.

## FAQ

### Is the Kneeling Twist safe for the lower back?

It's generally safe when you rotate from the thoracic spine with controlled weight. If you have disc issues, avoid this exercise or use very light weight and limited range of motion.

### What muscles does the Kneeling Twist target?

Primarily the obliques (internal and external) and to a lesser extent the rectus abdominis. It also engages the hip stabilizers and spinal erectors isometrically.

### What muscles does the Kneeling Twist work?

The Kneeling Twist primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the obliques, lower back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Kneeling Twist good for beginners?

The Kneeling Twist is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of abs strength with easier variations first.
