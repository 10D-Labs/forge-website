# Cable Incline Bench Pullover

## Quick answer

A straight-arm pullover done sitting back on an incline bench, pulling a high cable bar from overhead to straight out in front of you. It works the lats, with help from the triceps, rear shoulders and chest. Intermediate back exercise using cable. Targets lats, also works triceps, rear deltoids, chest.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Rear Deltoids, Chest
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set an incline bench to about 60 degrees with its backrest toward a high pulley with a straight bar attached. Sit back, feet flat.
2. Reach up and grip the bar with your hands about shoulder-width apart, palms facing away from you, arms straight overhead.
3. Keeping your arms straight, pull the bar down and forward in an arc until your arms point straight ahead at shoulder height.
4. Let the bar rise slowly back overhead, keeping your arms straight the whole time.

## Pro tips

- Keep your arms nearly straight and pull the bar in an arc from overhead to straight out in front of you.
- Sit back against the incline bench and keep your back on the pad so your lats do the work.
- Pause briefly with the bar in front of you, then let it travel back overhead slowly for a full stretch.

## Common mistakes

- Bending the elbows a lot, which turns the pull into a triceps or rowing movement.
- Lifting your back off the bench to start each rep, which uses momentum instead of the lats.
- Using too much weight and losing control of the bar on the way back overhead.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between a Cable Incline Bench Pullover and a straight arm pulldown?

Both are straight-arm lat exercises. The bench version supports your back and starts with your arms overhead, which gives a long stretch and makes it harder to swing.

### When should I do Cable Incline Bench Pullovers in my workout?

Use them either at the start of a back workout to warm up the lats, or at the end to finish them off after heavy rows and pulldowns.

### What muscles does the Cable Incline Bench Pullover work?

The Cable Incline Bench Pullover primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the triceps, rear deltoids, chest. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Cable Incline Bench Pullover good for beginners?

The Cable Incline Bench Pullover is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of lats strength with easier variations first.
