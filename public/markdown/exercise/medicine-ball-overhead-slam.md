# Medicine Ball Overhead Slam

## Quick answer

A power move where you lift a medicine ball overhead and throw it hard into the floor. It works the upper back, shoulders and abs. Intermediate back exercise using medicine ball. Targets upper back, also works shoulders, core.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Medicine Ball
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Core
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a medicine ball above your head with both hands.
2. Keep your back straight and your abs tight.
3. Bend your knees slightly and slam the ball down hard onto the floor in front of you, driving with your whole body.
4. Catch the ball on the bounce and lift it back above your head.

## Pro tips

- Rise up on your toes as you bring the ball overhead, then slam down aggressively by engaging your lats, core, and hip flexors.
- Hinge at the hips and bend your knees as you slam to use your entire posterior chain, not just your arms.
- Use a dead ball (no bounce) for safety, or stand clear of the rebound if using a rubber ball.

## Common mistakes

- Keeping the legs stiff and slamming with only the arms, which greatly reduces power output.
- Rounding the lower back during the slam instead of hinging at the hips with a flat spine.
- Standing too close to the slam point and getting hit by the rebound.

## FAQ

### What muscles do medicine ball slams work?

Primarily the lats, core, and shoulders. Secondarily the hip flexors, quads, and forearms. It is a full-body power exercise disguised as an upper body movement.

### How heavy should the medicine ball be?

Start with 10-15 pounds. You should be able to slam it aggressively for all prescribed reps. If the ball barely reaches the floor, it is too heavy.

### Are medicine ball slams good for cardio?

Excellent. High-rep ball slams (15-20 reps) with minimal rest drive your heart rate up quickly, making them an effective conditioning tool.

### What muscles does the Medicine Ball Overhead Slam work?

The Medicine Ball Overhead Slam primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the shoulders, core. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Medicine Ball Overhead Slam good for beginners?

The Medicine Ball Overhead Slam is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of upper back strength with easier variations first.
