# Dumbbell Single Arm Seated Hammer Curl

## Quick answer

A seated one-arm curl with your palm facing in, thumb up, and your back supported. It works the biceps and the forearms. Beginner arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on an upright bench with your back against the pad and your feet flat on the floor.
2. Hold a dumbbell in one hand with your arm hanging at your side, palm facing in (thumb up).
3. Keep your elbow at your side and curl the dumbbell up toward your shoulder, palm still facing in.
4. Lower it slowly until your arm is straight. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Brace your working elbow against the inside of your thigh for extra stabilization, similar to a concentration curl position.
- Keep the dumbbell in a neutral position (thumb up) throughout the entire movement.
- Focus on a slow, controlled curl and an even slower lowering phase for maximum brachialis engagement.

## Common mistakes

- Rotating the wrist at the top, converting it into a regular supinated curl and losing the hammer curl emphasis.
- Lifting the elbow off the thigh during the curl, which allows the shoulder to contribute.
- Using excessive weight and compromising the strict form that makes the seated single-arm version effective.

## FAQ

### Why do hammer curls one arm at a time?

Single-arm work lets you focus entirely on one bicep/brachialis, improving the mind-muscle connection and allowing you to identify and correct strength imbalances between arms.

### Can I rest my elbow on my thigh?

Yes, bracing the elbow on the inner thigh adds stability and turns this into a hammer concentration curl, which maximizes isolation of the brachialis.

### How does this compare to standing hammer curls?

The seated position removes all body swing and momentum, forcing the brachialis and brachioradialis to do all the work. You'll likely use less weight but get a better contraction.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Single Arm Seated Hammer Curl work?

The Dumbbell Single Arm Seated Hammer Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Single Arm Seated Hammer Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Single Arm Seated Hammer Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building biceps strength.
