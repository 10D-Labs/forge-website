# Smith Split Squat

## Quick answer

A split squat with a Smith machine bar across your upper back, feet staying in place as you lower and rise. It works the glutes and quads, with help from the hamstrings. Advanced legs exercise using smith machine. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Smith Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set a Smith machine bar at shoulder height. Step under it so it rests across your upper back.
2. Hold the bar with your hands wider than your shoulders, palms facing forward, and unhook it.
3. Step one foot forward and the other back into a long stance, back heel off the floor.
4. Bend both knees to lower straight down until your back knee nearly touches the floor.
5. Push back up without moving your feet. Do all your reps on one leg, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Set your feet in a long split stance under the bar and keep them in place for the whole set.
- Keep the bar on your upper back, not on your neck, and stay upright.
- Lower straight down until your back knee nearly touches the floor, then push through your front foot to rise.

## Common mistakes

- Setting the feet too close together, which puts extra pressure on the front knee.
- Letting the front knee collapse inward.
- Leaning the torso forward excessively, which loads the lower back.

## FAQ

### Is the Smith machine split squat safe for the knees?

It can be, because the fixed bar helps you balance. Adjust your foot position until the movement feels smooth and your front knee tracks over your toes.

### Should I do all reps on one leg before switching?

Yes. Finish all reps on one leg, then switch. Start with your weaker leg and match the reps on the stronger one.

### How deep should I go?

Lower until your back knee nearly touches the floor and your front thigh is about parallel to the ground.

### What muscles does the Smith Split Squat work?

The Smith Split Squat primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Smith Split Squat good for beginners?

The Smith Split Squat is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental legs movements first.
