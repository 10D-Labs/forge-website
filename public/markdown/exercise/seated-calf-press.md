# Seated Calf Press

## Quick answer

A calf raise done on a seated leg press, pushing the footplate with your toes while your legs stay straight. It works the calves. Beginner legs exercise using machine. Targets calves, also works soleus.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Calves
- **Secondary Muscles:** Soleus
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit in a leg press machine with your back against the pad and hold the handles at your sides.
2. Place the balls of your feet on the bottom edge of the footplate, heels off, and straighten your legs.
3. Keep your legs straight and point your toes to push the footplate away.
4. Let your heels come back slowly toward you until you feel a stretch in your calves.

## Pro tips

- Position your toes on the lower edge of the platform so your heels can drop fully for a complete stretch.
- Press through the big toe side of your foot to emphasize the medial gastrocnemius and soleus.
- Pause at the top of each rep for 1-2 seconds with a hard squeeze to maximize the contraction.

## Common mistakes

- Bouncing at the bottom of the movement, which uses the Achilles tendon elasticity instead of muscular contraction.
- Using too short a range of motion, which limits both the stretch and contraction phases of the exercise.
- Placing the knees too far under the pad, which changes the ankle angle and reduces calf activation.

## FAQ

### Does the Seated Calf Press work the soleus or gastrocnemius?

The seated position with bent knees primarily targets the soleus muscle, which sits underneath the gastrocnemius and contributes significantly to overall calf size.

### Should I do both seated and Standing Calf Raises?

Yes, doing both is ideal. Standing variations target the gastrocnemius, while seated variations emphasize the soleus. Both muscles need training for complete calf development.

### How many sets per week for calf growth?

Most people need 12-16 sets per week of direct calf work, split across 2-3 sessions, to see noticeable growth.

### What muscles does the Seated Calf Press work?

The Seated Calf Press primarily targets the calves, with secondary activation in the soleus. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Seated Calf Press good for beginners?

Yes, the Seated Calf Press is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building calves strength.
