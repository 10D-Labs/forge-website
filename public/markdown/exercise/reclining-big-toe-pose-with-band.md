# Reclining Big Toe Pose with Band

## Quick answer

A lying hamstring stretch: loop a strap around one foot and use it to raise your straight leg toward your chest. It stretches the hamstrings on the back of the thigh. Beginner legs exercise using resistance band. Targets hamstrings, also works calves, glutes.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Hamstrings
- **Secondary Muscles:** Calves, Glutes
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Stretching

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your back with your legs straight and your arms by your sides.
2. Loop a band or strap around the ball of your right foot and hold an end in each hand.
3. Raise your right leg slowly toward your chest, keeping your knee straight and your toes pulled back toward you.
4. Hold for the set time, then lower your leg slowly to the floor and switch legs.

## Pro tips

- Keep both hips flat on the ground; if one hip lifts, you've pulled the leg too far.
- Loop the band around the ball of the foot, not the arch, and keep the leg straight.
- Walk your hands up the band to gradually deepen the stretch as your hamstring relaxes.

## Common mistakes

- Pulling the leg too far and lifting the opposite hip off the ground.
- Tensing the shoulders and neck while pulling; keep your upper body relaxed on the floor.
- Holding your breath; breathe steadily and use each exhale to deepen the stretch.

## FAQ

### What can I use if I don't have a band?

A yoga strap or a towel works. Choose something long enough to hold while keeping your head and shoulders on the ground.

### How long should I hold this pose?

Hold for 30-60 seconds per leg for general flexibility, or up to 2-3 minutes per side for deeper work.

### Is this stretch good for runners?

Yes. Runners often have tight hamstrings, and this stretch is easy to do lying down after a run.

### What muscles does the Reclining Big Toe Pose with Band work?

The Reclining Big Toe Pose with Band primarily targets the hamstrings, with secondary activation in the calves, glutes. It's classified as a stretching exercise for the legs.

### Is the Reclining Big Toe Pose with Band good for beginners?

Yes, the Reclining Big Toe Pose with Band is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building hamstrings strength.
