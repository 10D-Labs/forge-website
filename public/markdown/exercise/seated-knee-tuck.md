# Seated Knee Tuck

## Quick answer

A seated knee tuck on the edge of a bench, pulling your knees in toward your chest and stretching your legs back out. It works the abs, with help from the front of the hips. Beginner core exercise using body weight. Targets abs, also works hip flexors.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hip Flexors
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on the edge of a flat bench with your legs stretched out in front of you.
2. Hold the sides of the bench just behind your hips and lean back slightly.
3. Bend your knees and pull them in toward your chest, bringing your upper body forward to meet them.
4. Straighten your legs back out and lean back to the start.

## Pro tips

- Sit on the edge of a bench, hold the sides behind you and lean back slightly with your legs out straight.
- Pull your knees in toward your chest while bringing your chest slightly forward to meet them.
- Stretch your legs back out slowly, keeping your feet off the floor between reps.

## Common mistakes

- Swinging the legs in with momentum instead of pulling them in with the abs.
- Leaning back too far, which takes tension off the abs.
- Letting the feet drop to the floor between reps.

## FAQ

### What do Seated Knee Tucks work?

The abs, with help from the hip flexors at the front of the hips.

### How do I make Seated Knee Tucks harder?

Hold a light dumbbell between your feet, slow the tempo, or straighten your legs more on each rep.

### Can I do them without a bench?

Yes. Sit on the floor with your hands behind you. The bench just gives your legs more room to straighten.

### What muscles does the Seated Knee Tuck work?

The Seated Knee Tuck primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the hip flexors. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Seated Knee Tuck good for beginners?

Yes, the Seated Knee Tuck is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building abs strength.
