# Band Standing Crunch

## Quick answer

A crunch done standing, pulling against a band anchored high behind you with your hands by your head. It works the abs, with help from the obliques. Beginner core exercise using resistance band. Targets abs, also works obliques.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Obliques
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Anchor the band high behind you. Stand facing away from it with your feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Hold the band with both hands beside your head, elbows bent.
3. Tighten your abs and crunch forward, bringing your chest toward your knees.
4. Return slowly to standing tall.

## Pro tips

- Stand facing away from the anchor and hold the band at your chest, then crunch your ribcage down toward your pelvis.
- Focus on rounding the thoracic spine to contract the abs rather than just bending at the hips.
- Exhale forcefully at the bottom of each crunch to maximize abdominal contraction.

## Common mistakes

- Hinging at the hips instead of flexing the spine, which works the hip flexors instead of the abs.
- Pulling the band with the arms instead of keeping the hands fixed and crunching with the torso.
- Standing too close to the anchor point, resulting in insufficient tension.

## FAQ

### Are standing crunches as effective as lying crunches?

Yes, and they can be better because the cable or band provides constant tension throughout, whereas lying crunches lose tension at certain points.

### Can you do this exercise without a band?

You would need a cable machine instead. Without any resistance, a standing crunch provides minimal load for the abs.

### How do you progress the Band Standing Crunch?

Use a thicker band, stand further from the anchor point, or slow down the eccentric to 3-4 seconds.

### What muscles does the Band Standing Crunch work?

The Band Standing Crunch primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the obliques. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Band Standing Crunch good for beginners?

Yes, the Band Standing Crunch is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building abs strength.
