# Band Shrug

## Quick answer

A shrug against a resistance band under your feet, lifting your shoulders toward your ears. It works the traps, the muscles along the top of your shoulders. Beginner back exercise using resistance band. Targets traps, also works shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Traps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand on the middle of a resistance band with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding an end in each hand.
2. Let your arms hang straight and relaxed at your sides.
3. Shrug your shoulders straight up toward your ears as high as you can.
4. Lower your shoulders slowly.

## Pro tips

- Stand on the band and shrug your shoulders straight up toward your ears, not rolling them forward or backward.
- Hold the top position for 2 seconds to maximize upper trap contraction.
- Keep your arms completely straight and let the traps do all the lifting.

## Common mistakes

- Rolling the shoulders in circles, which does not increase trap activation and can stress the shoulder joint.
- Bending the elbows to assist the shrug, which recruits the biceps and reduces trap isolation.
- Not holding the top position long enough, rushing through reps with no peak contraction.

## FAQ

### Are shrugs necessary if you do Deadlifts?

Deadlifts do train the traps isometrically, but shrugs target the upper traps through their full range of motion. If trap development is a priority, include shrugs.

### Should you shrug straight up or roll the shoulders?

Straight up only. Shoulder rolling adds no benefit and increases the risk of shoulder impingement. Just elevate and depress.

### How heavy should Band Shrugs be?

Use a medium to thick band. The traps are strong muscles, so you need sufficient resistance. You should feel a strong contraction at the top for 2 seconds.

### What muscles does the Band Shrug work?

The Band Shrug primarily targets the traps, with secondary activation in the shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Band Shrug good for beginners?

Yes, the Band Shrug is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building traps strength.
