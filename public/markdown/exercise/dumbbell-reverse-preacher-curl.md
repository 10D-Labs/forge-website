# Dumbbell Reverse Preacher Curl

## Quick answer

A preacher curl done with your palms facing down instead of up, both arms at once. It works the biceps and puts more of the work on the forearms. Intermediate arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on a preacher bench with your feet flat and the backs of your upper arms on the pad.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand, arms straight, palms facing down.
3. Keep your upper arms on the pad and curl both dumbbells up until your forearms are upright and your palms face away from you.
4. Lower slowly until your arms are straight again.

## Pro tips

- Press your upper arms firmly against the preacher pad with a palms-up (underhand) grip and curl from a fully extended position.
- Control the lowering phase for 2-3 seconds to avoid jerking the tendons at the bottom of the range of motion.
- Squeeze the biceps at the top and hold for one second before lowering to maximize the peak contraction.

## Common mistakes

- Dropping the weight too fast at the bottom, which puts dangerous stress on the biceps tendon in its most vulnerable stretched position.
- Lifting the elbows off the preacher pad to cheat past the sticking point midway through the curl.
- Using too much weight for the strict preacher position, leading to half reps that miss the full stretch.

## FAQ

### What is a reverse Preacher Curl?

It is a Preacher Curl performed with the arms on the opposite side of the pad (the flat side), which changes the resistance curve and emphasizes different parts of the bicep contraction.

### Is the reverse Preacher Curl harder than a regular Preacher Curl?

The difficulty depends on the pad angle. The key difference is where in the range of motion the exercise is hardest, which shifts depending on which side of the pad you use.

### Can Preacher Curls help build the bicep peak?

Yes. Preacher Curls isolate the biceps, particularly the short head, which contributes to the bicep peak when the arm is flexed.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Reverse Preacher Curl work?

The Dumbbell Reverse Preacher Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Reverse Preacher Curl good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Reverse Preacher Curl is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of biceps strength with easier variations first.
