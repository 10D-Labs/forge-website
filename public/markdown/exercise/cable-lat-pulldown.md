# Cable Lat Pulldown

## Quick answer

A seated pulldown, pulling a long bar from a high cable down to your upper chest. It works the lats, plus the biceps, upper back and backs of the shoulders. Beginner back exercise using cable. Targets lats, also works biceps, rhomboids, rear deltoids.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Rhomboids, Rear Deltoids
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Attach a long straight bar to a high pulley. Sit on the bench facing the machine, feet flat on the floor and knees slightly bent.
2. Grip the bar with your palms facing away from you, hands slightly wider than shoulder-width apart.
3. Lean back slightly with your chest up and a slight arch in your lower back.
4. Pull the bar down to your upper chest, leading with your elbows and squeezing your shoulder blades together.
5. Let the bar rise slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Depress your shoulder blades down and back before initiating the pull. This engages the lats from the very start of the rep.
- Pull the bar to your upper chest, not behind your neck. Behind-the-neck pulldowns place the shoulder in a vulnerable position.
- Lean back about 15-20 degrees and puff your chest up to create a better line of pull for the lats.

## Common mistakes

- Leaning too far back and turning the pulldown into a cable row, which shifts emphasis from the lats to the mid-back.
- Pulling with the biceps by not engaging the shoulder blades first. If your forearms burn out before your lats, this is the issue.
- Using too wide a grip, which reduces range of motion and can strain the shoulders.

## FAQ

### What grip width is best for Cable Lat Pulldowns?

A grip slightly wider than shoulder width (about 1.5x shoulder width) provides the best balance of lat stretch and range of motion. Too wide limits range, too narrow shifts emphasis to the biceps.

### Can lat pulldowns replace Pullups?

Lat pulldowns work the same muscles and are excellent for building strength when you cannot yet do Pullups. However, Pullups also challenge core stability and coordination, so progress toward them over time.

### What muscles does the Cable Lat Pulldown work?

The Cable Lat Pulldown primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the biceps, rhomboids, rear deltoids. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Cable Lat Pulldown good for beginners?

Yes, the Cable Lat Pulldown is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building lats strength.
