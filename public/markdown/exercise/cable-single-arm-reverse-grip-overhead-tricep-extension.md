# Cable Single Arm Reverse Grip Overhead Tricep Extension

## Quick answer

A one-arm overhead tricep extension with a low cable behind you and your palm facing up. It works the triceps on the back of the upper arm. Intermediate arms exercise using cable. Targets triceps, also works shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Attach a single handle to the low pulley and stand with your back to the machine. Hold the handle in one hand, palm facing up.
2. Raise that elbow so it points at the ceiling, with the handle behind your head and the cable running down behind you.
3. Keep your upper arm still and straighten your elbow to press the handle straight up overhead.
4. Bend your elbow slowly to lower the handle back behind your head. Do all your reps on one arm, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Face away from the machine and hold the handle with an underhand (supinated) grip overhead.
- Keep your upper arm close to your head and extend the forearm forward by straightening the elbow.
- Control the return slowly; the overhead reverse grip creates an excellent stretch on the long head.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the elbow drift away from the head, which reduces the stretch on the tricep long head.
- Arching the lower back under the overhead load instead of bracing the core.
- Using too much weight, which forces compensatory movement through the shoulder joint.

## FAQ

### What does the reverse grip do for overhead Tricep Extensions?

The underhand grip slightly changes the angle of pull on the tricep, with some lifters finding it easier on the elbows and better for feeling the long head contraction.

### Is this exercise good for the long head of the triceps?

Yes, the overhead position stretches the long head maximally, and doing it single-arm allows full focus on each tricep. The long head makes up the majority of the tricep mass.

### Can I do this facing the machine?

This exercise is designed to be performed facing away from the machine. Facing the machine would turn it into a pushdown-style movement with different mechanics.

### What muscles does the Cable Single Arm Reverse Grip Overhead Tricep Extension work?

The Cable Single Arm Reverse Grip Overhead Tricep Extension primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Cable Single Arm Reverse Grip Overhead Tricep Extension good for beginners?

The Cable Single Arm Reverse Grip Overhead Tricep Extension is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of triceps strength with easier variations first.
