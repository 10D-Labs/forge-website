# Dumbbell Lying Hamstring Curl

## Quick answer

A hamstring curl done lying face down on a bench, holding a dumbbell between your feet. It works the hamstrings on the back of the thigh. Beginner legs exercise using dumbbell. Targets hamstrings, also works glutes.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Hamstrings
- **Secondary Muscles:** Glutes
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie face down on a flat bench with your knees just past the end, and hold the bench near your head.
2. Hold a dumbbell upright between your feet, legs straight.
3. Keep your hips pressed into the bench and bend your knees to curl your heels up until your lower legs point straight up.
4. Lower slowly until your legs are straight.

## Pro tips

- Secure the dumbbell between your feet firmly before starting so it does not slip during the curling motion.
- Curl your heels toward your glutes in a smooth arc, squeezing the hamstrings hard at the top.
- Lower the dumbbell slowly over 2-3 seconds to load the hamstrings eccentrically, which is where most growth stimulus occurs.

## Common mistakes

- Lifting the hips off the bench during the curl, which uses hip flexor momentum instead of isolating the hamstrings.
- Not securing the dumbbell properly between the feet, causing it to slip and creating a safety hazard.
- Using a jerky, fast motion instead of a controlled curl, which reduces hamstring activation and risks straining the knee.

## FAQ

### How do I hold the dumbbell for lying hamstring curls?

Place the dumbbell vertically between your feet, squeezing it between the arches of both feet. Start with a light weight until you are comfortable with the grip.

### Can Dumbbell Lying Hamstring Curls replace a leg curl machine?

They work the same muscle but the machine provides more consistent resistance. Dumbbell curls are a solid alternative when a machine is unavailable.

### Why do my calves cramp during lying hamstring curls?

Your calves are assisting the hamstrings during knee flexion. Point your toes away from your body (plantarflex) to reduce calf involvement and cramping.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Lying Hamstring Curl work?

The Dumbbell Lying Hamstring Curl primarily targets the hamstrings, with secondary activation in the glutes. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Dumbbell Lying Hamstring Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Lying Hamstring Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building hamstrings strength.
