# Lunge with Twist

## Quick answer

A forward lunge with a twist of your upper body toward the front leg. It works the abs, with the quads, glutes and hamstrings helping, and trains balance. Intermediate core exercise using body weight. Targets abs, also works quadriceps, glutes, hamstrings.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Glutes, Hamstrings
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Step forward with your right foot and lower into a lunge.
3. Twist your upper body to the right as you lower, bringing your left elbow toward your right knee.
4. Turn back to face forward and push off your right foot to stand up.
5. Do the same with your left foot forward, twisting to the left. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Complete the lunge first, then add the twist once you're stable in the bottom position.
- Twist toward the front leg side, driving the rotation from the obliques and thoracic spine.
- Keep your front knee stable and tracking over your toes while you rotate; don't let it cave inward.

## Common mistakes

- Twisting before the lunge is stable, which throws off balance and reduces core engagement.
- Rotating from the lower back instead of the mid-back, which can cause lumbar strain.
- Taking too short a step, which causes the knee to push past the toes and limits the depth of the lunge.

## FAQ

### What does the Lunge with Twist target?

The lunge works the quads, glutes, and hamstrings while the twist engages the obliques and core. It's a compound movement that builds lower body strength with rotational stability.

### Can I hold a weight during this exercise?

Yes, holding a medicine ball or light dumbbell at chest height increases the core challenge during the rotation. Start with bodyweight and add load as you master the movement.

### Is this a good exercise for athletes?

Excellent. The combination of lunging and rotating trains the body for rotational sports like tennis, golf, and baseball, where power transfers through the core during movement.

### What muscles does the Lunge with Twist work?

The Lunge with Twist primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, glutes, hamstrings. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Lunge with Twist good for beginners?

The Lunge with Twist is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of abs strength with easier variations first.
