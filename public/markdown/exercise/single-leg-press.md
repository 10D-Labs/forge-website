# Single Leg Press

## Quick answer

A leg press done one leg at a time on a 45-degree machine. It works the glutes, quads and hamstrings and can help even out strength differences between your legs. Intermediate legs exercise using machine. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit in a 45-degree leg press and put one foot in the middle of the footplate.
2. Rest your other foot to the side, or let it hover near the platform.
3. Release the safety handles and lower the weight by bending your knee toward your chest.
4. Push through your heel to straighten your leg, without locking your knee.
5. Do all your reps on one leg, then switch.
6. Lock the safety handles back in place when you finish.

## Pro tips

- Place your foot in the center of the platform at roughly hip height to balance quad and glute activation.
- Lower the weight until your knee reaches about 90 degrees of flexion, then drive through the heel to press back up.
- Start with about 40-50% of your bilateral Leg Press weight to account for the single-leg stability demand.

## Common mistakes

- Placing the foot too high on the platform, which over-emphasizes the glutes and hamstrings at the expense of the quads.
- Letting the knee cave inward during the press, which stresses the medial knee ligaments.
- Lowering the weight too deep, allowing the lower back to round off the pad.

## FAQ

### Why do Single Leg Press instead of bilateral?

It identifies and corrects strength imbalances between legs, increases the training stimulus per leg without loading as much total weight, and improves single-leg stability.

### Is Single Leg Press safe for the knees?

Yes, if the knee tracks over the toes and you do not go excessively deep. The machine supports your body and eliminates balance concerns, making it safer than Single Leg Squats for many people.

### Should I lock out my knee at the top?

No. Keep a slight bend at the top to maintain tension on the muscles and protect the knee joint from Hyperextension.

### What muscles does the Single Leg Press work?

The Single Leg Press primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Single Leg Press good for beginners?

The Single Leg Press is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of glutes strength with easier variations first.
