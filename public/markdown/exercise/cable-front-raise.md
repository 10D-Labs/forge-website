# Cable Front Raise

## Quick answer

A one-arm front raise, lifting a low cable handle from your thigh to shoulder height. It works the front of the shoulders. Beginner shoulders exercise using cable. Targets shoulders, also works triceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your back to a low pulley, feet shoulder-width apart and knees slightly bent, so the cable runs between your legs.
2. Hold the handle in one hand, arm straight and in front of your thigh.
3. Keep your arm straight and raise the handle in front of you to shoulder height, palm facing down.
4. Lower it slowly back in front of your thigh.

## Pro tips

- Stand with the cable running between your legs from a low pulley behind you to keep constant tension on the front delts throughout the entire range.
- Stop the raise at shoulder height. Going higher shifts the work from the anterior deltoid to the upper traps.
- Use a thumbs-up or neutral grip to reduce Internal Shoulder Rotation stress while still hitting the front delt effectively.

## Common mistakes

- Swinging the torso backward to generate momentum, especially as fatigue sets in. Keep your torso completely still.
- Locking the elbows fully straight, which places unnecessary stress on the elbow joint. Maintain a very slight bend.
- Raising the cable too high above shoulder level, which recruits the traps and reduces front delt isolation.

## FAQ

### Are Cable Front Raises better than Dumbbell Front Raises?

Yes, cables maintain constant tension through the entire rep, while dumbbells provide almost no tension at the bottom of the movement when your arms hang at your sides.

### Should I do Cable Front Raises with one arm or two?

Single-arm Cable Front Raises let you focus on one shoulder at a time and correct imbalances. Two-arm versions are faster but can encourage compensating with the stronger side.

### What muscles does the Cable Front Raise work?

The Cable Front Raise primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the triceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Cable Front Raise good for beginners?

Yes, the Cable Front Raise is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building shoulders strength.
