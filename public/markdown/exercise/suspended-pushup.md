# Suspended Pushup

## Quick answer

A pushup with your hands in suspension trainer handles or rings just above the floor, so you have to keep the handles steady. It works the chest, triceps and shoulders. Advanced chest exercise using rings. Targets chest, also works triceps, shoulders, core.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Rings
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders, Core
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set a suspension trainer or a pair of rings so the handles hang a few inches above the floor. Hold one in each hand, palms facing down.
2. Walk your feet back into a pushup position, arms straight under your shoulders and body straight from head to heels.
3. Bend your elbows and lower your chest between the handles until it's level with your hands. Don't let the handles drift apart.
4. Push back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Keep the straps close to your body and your hands directly under your shoulders for the most stable pressing position.
- Engage your core hard to prevent the straps from swaying during the press.
- Lower your chest between the handles for a deeper stretch than a regular Pushup provides.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the straps swing outward during the descent, which puts the shoulder in a compromised position.
- Not going deep enough to take advantage of the extra range of motion the suspension trainer allows.
- Placing the feet too close to the anchor point, which makes the exercise too easy.

## FAQ

### Are Suspended Pushups harder than regular Pushups?

Yes. The instability of the suspension trainer forces your chest, shoulders, and core to work much harder to stabilize the movement. Most people can do significantly fewer Suspended Pushups than regular ones.

### Where should I place my feet relative to the anchor point?

The further your feet are from the anchor point, the more horizontal your body becomes and the harder the exercise. Start with your feet about 4-5 feet from the anchor and adjust to your strength level.

### Can Suspended Pushups replace regular bench press?

They're a supplement, not a replacement. Suspended Pushups build stability and muscle activation that bench pressing doesn't, but you can't load them as heavily for pure strength development.

### What muscles does the Suspended Pushup work?

The Suspended Pushup primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders, core. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Suspended Pushup good for beginners?

The Suspended Pushup is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental chest movements first.
