# Cable Preacher Curl

## Quick answer

A preacher-bench curl using a low cable, so your upper arms stay fixed on the pad and the cable pulls the whole way. It works the biceps. Beginner arms exercise using cable. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Attach a bar to a low pulley and set the preacher pad at chest height in front of it.
2. Sit with your upper arms resting on the pad and grip the bar with your palms facing up. Keep your back straight and your elbows in.
3. Curl the bar up toward your shoulders.
4. Lower it slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Press the back of your upper arms firmly into the preacher pad throughout the entire rep. If your arms lift off, the weight is too heavy.
- Lower the cable slowly for a full 2-3 second eccentric to take advantage of the stretch position, where the bicep is most vulnerable to growth.
- Stop just short of full elbow lockout at the bottom to keep tension on the biceps and protect the elbow joint.

## Common mistakes

- Lifting the elbows off the preacher pad at the top of the curl, which brings the front delts into the movement.
- Fully extending the elbows at the bottom, which places excessive stress on the bicep tendon and elbow joint.
- Leaning back to curl the weight up instead of keeping the torso pressed against the pad.

## FAQ

### What is the advantage of Cable Preacher Curls over Dumbbell Preacher Curls?

The cable provides constant tension throughout the range of motion, including at the top where dumbbells lose resistance due to gravity. This means more time under tension for the biceps.

### Are Preacher Curls good for the bicep peak?

Preacher Curls emphasize the long head of the biceps in the stretched position, which contributes to overall bicep size. For peak development, combine them with exercises like concentration curls.

### What muscles does the Cable Preacher Curl work?

The Cable Preacher Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Cable Preacher Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Cable Preacher Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building biceps strength.
