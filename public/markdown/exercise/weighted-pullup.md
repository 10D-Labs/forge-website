# Weighted Pullup

## Quick answer

A pullup with a weight plate hung from a belt around your hips for extra load. It works the lats, the large muscles down the sides of your back, plus the biceps and forearms. Advanced back exercise using weight plate. Targets lats, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Weight Plate
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Put on a dip belt (a belt that goes around your hips) and hang a weight plate from it.
2. Grab a pullup bar with your palms facing away from you, hands slightly wider than shoulder-width apart, and hang with your arms straight.
3. Pull yourself up, bringing your elbows down toward your sides, until your chin is above the bar.
4. Lower yourself slowly until your arms are straight again.

## Pro tips

- Start each rep from a dead hang with fully extended arms to ensure full range of motion.
- Use a dip belt for adding weight rather than holding a dumbbell between your feet, which limits loading.
- Initiate the pull by depressing and retracting your shoulder blades before bending your elbows.

## Common mistakes

- Kipping or using momentum to get the chin over the bar, which defeats the purpose of Weighted Pullups.
- Adding too much weight too soon, which causes form breakdown and limits range of motion.
- Not fully extending at the bottom, performing partial reps that shortchange lat development.

## FAQ

### How much weight should you add to Pullups as a beginner?

Start with 5-10 pounds once you can do 3 sets of 10-12 strict bodyweight Pullups. Add weight in 5-pound increments.

### Are Weighted Pullups better than lat pulldowns for back development?

Weighted Pullups generally recruit more stabilizer muscles and allow progressive overload in a functional movement pattern. They are superior for most trainees who can perform them.

### What grip width is best for Weighted Pullups?

A grip slightly wider than shoulder width provides the best balance of lat engagement and range of motion. Too wide limits ROM and increases shoulder stress.

### What muscles does the Weighted Pullup work?

The Weighted Pullup primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Weighted Pullup good for beginners?

The Weighted Pullup is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental back movements first.
