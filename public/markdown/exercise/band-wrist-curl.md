# Band Wrist Curl

## Quick answer

A wrist curl with your palms facing up, curling your hands against a band under your feet. It works the forearms. Beginner arms exercise using resistance band. Targets forearms, also works biceps, triceps.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Forearms
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Triceps
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on a box, bench or chair with your feet flat on the floor and the middle of a resistance band under your feet.
2. Hold the band with both hands, palms facing up, and rest your forearms on your thighs.
3. Bend your wrists to curl your hands up.
4. Lower your hands slowly.

## Pro tips

- Rest your forearms on your thighs with palms facing up and curl your wrists upward against the band tension.
- Squeeze the forearm flexors at the top of each curl for a one-second hold.
- Lower slowly rather than letting the wrists drop to maintain tension on the muscles.

## Common mistakes

- Lifting the forearms off the thighs during the curl, which allows the biceps to assist.
- Rolling the band in the fingers instead of curling from the wrist joint.
- Using a band that is too thick for the small forearm muscles.

## FAQ

### Are wrist curls necessary for grip strength?

They target the wrist flexors specifically, which contribute to grip. However, exercises like farmer carries and Deadlifts also build grip in a more functional way.

### How many sets and reps should you do for wrist curls?

2-3 sets of 15-25 reps. The forearm muscles respond well to higher rep ranges and benefit from sustained time under tension.

### Can wrist curls help prevent wrist injuries?

Yes. Stronger wrist flexors and extensors provide more joint stability, which helps prevent strains during pressing and pulling movements.

### What muscles does the Band Wrist Curl work?

The Band Wrist Curl primarily targets the forearms, with secondary activation in the biceps, triceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Band Wrist Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Band Wrist Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building forearms strength.
