# Barbell Wide Grip Upright Row

## Quick answer

An upright row with your hands set wide, lifting a barbell from your thighs to your upper chest with your elbows leading. It works the sides of the shoulders, plus the upper back and biceps. Intermediate shoulders exercise using barbell. Targets shoulders, also works traps, biceps.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Traps, Biceps
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart. Hold a barbell in front of your thighs with your palms facing you, hands wider than shoulder-width and arms straight.
2. Lift the bar straight up toward your chin until it reaches your upper chest, leading with your elbows. Keep your back straight.
3. Lower the bar slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Use a grip wider than shoulder width (about 1.5x) and lead with the elbows, pulling the bar up along your body.
- The wider grip reduces the impingement risk compared to a narrow grip and shifts more emphasis to the lateral delts.
- Stop the pull when your elbows reach shoulder height and control the descent.

## Common mistakes

- Pulling the bar too high past shoulder level, which compresses the supraspinatus tendon.
- Using too much body swing to compensate for the grip width reducing your mechanical advantage.
- Shrugging the shoulders up instead of pulling with the deltoids.

## FAQ

### Is the wide grip upright row better for the shoulders than narrow grip?

Yes, the wider grip reduces internal rotation of the shoulder, decreasing impingement risk. It also shifts more work to the lateral deltoids and away from the traps.

### Can wide grip upright rows replace Lateral Raises?

They can partially replace them since both target the lateral deltoids. However, Lateral Raises isolate the side delts more purely. Using both provides the most complete side delt development.

### What's the ideal grip width?

About 1.5 times shoulder width. This is wide enough to reduce impingement risk but not so wide that the range of motion becomes too short to be effective.

### What muscles does the Barbell Wide Grip Upright Row work?

The Barbell Wide Grip Upright Row primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the traps, biceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Barbell Wide Grip Upright Row good for beginners?

The Barbell Wide Grip Upright Row is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of shoulders strength with easier variations first.
