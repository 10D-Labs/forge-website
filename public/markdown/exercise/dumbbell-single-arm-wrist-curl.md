# Dumbbell Single Arm Wrist Curl

## Quick answer

A seated one-arm wrist curl with your palm facing up and your forearm resting on your thigh. It works the forearm muscles that bend your wrist. Beginner arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets forearms, also works biceps, brachialis.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Forearms
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Brachialis
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on a bench or chair with your feet flat on the floor.
2. Hold a dumbbell in one hand with your palm facing up and rest that forearm on your thigh.
3. Let your wrist bend back so the dumbbell rolls down toward your fingers.
4. Curl your wrist back up, bringing the dumbbell toward your forearm. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Rest your forearm on your thigh with your palm facing up and only your wrist hanging off the knee for a stable setup.
- Let the dumbbell roll down to your fingertips at the bottom, then curl it back up to maximize forearm flexor range of motion.
- Use slow, deliberate reps with a 1-second squeeze at the top to build forearm strength effectively.

## Common mistakes

- Lifting the forearm off the thigh during the curl, which adds elbow flexion and reduces wrist-specific work.
- Using too heavy a weight that forces jerky, incomplete reps instead of full range of motion curls.
- Rushing through reps without pausing at the top, missing the peak contraction of the forearm flexors.

## FAQ

### How heavy should I go on wrist curls?

Start light, around 5-15 lbs. The wrist flexors are small muscles and overloading them can strain the tendons. Focus on 15-25 reps with controlled form.

### Can wrist curls improve my grip strength?

Yes. Wrist curls strengthen the forearm flexors that are primary contributors to grip strength, which transfers to Deadlifts, Pullups, and other pulling movements.

### Should I do wrist curls one arm at a time?

Single arm allows you to focus on form and address imbalances. It is especially useful if one forearm is noticeably weaker than the other.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Single Arm Wrist Curl work?

The Dumbbell Single Arm Wrist Curl primarily targets the forearms, with secondary activation in the biceps, brachialis. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Single Arm Wrist Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Single Arm Wrist Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building forearms strength.
