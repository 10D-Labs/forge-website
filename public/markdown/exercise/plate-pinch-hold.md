# Plate Pinch Hold

## Quick answer

A grip hold where you pinch a weight plate between your fingers and thumb at your side, one hand at a time. It works the forearms and grip. Beginner arms exercise using weight plate. Targets forearms, also works biceps, triceps.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Weight Plate
- **Target Muscle:** Forearms
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Triceps
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand tall with your feet hip-width apart. Pinch a weight plate by its top edge in one hand, palm facing your thigh.
2. Keep your fingers on the outer face of the plate and your thumb on the side facing your leg.
3. Let your arm hang straight at your side and squeeze hard so the plate doesn't slip.
4. Hold for the set time, then switch hands.

## Pro tips

- Pinch a smooth weight plate between your fingers and thumb, with the plate's flat sides against them, and hold it at your side.
- Keep your arm straight and your shoulder down; only the hand should be working.
- Hold for a set time, such as 20-40 seconds, then switch hands.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with a plate that is too heavy and dropping it within a few seconds.
- Curling the wrist or resting the plate against your leg.
- Holding over your feet instead of a clear area where a dropped plate is safe.

## FAQ

### What does the Plate Pinch Hold work?

Your pinch grip: the thumb and the forearm muscles that close your fingers. It is a different kind of grip from holding a bar.

### How often should I train grip strength?

2-3 times per week is enough. Grip muscles recover fairly quickly but can be overworked if trained hard every day.

### How do I progress?

Hold longer, use a heavier plate, or pinch two plates together with their smooth sides out.

### What muscles does the Plate Pinch Hold work?

The Plate Pinch Hold primarily targets the forearms, with secondary activation in the biceps, triceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Plate Pinch Hold good for beginners?

Yes, the Plate Pinch Hold is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building forearms strength.
