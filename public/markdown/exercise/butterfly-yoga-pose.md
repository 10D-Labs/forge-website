# Butterfly Yoga Pose

## Quick answer

A seated stretch with the soles of your feet together and your knees out to the sides. It stretches the inner thighs and loosens the hips. Beginner legs exercise using body weight. Targets adductors, also works hamstrings, groin.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Adductors
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Groin
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Stretching

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on the floor with your legs straight out in front of you.
2. Bend your knees and bring the soles of your feet together, letting your knees drop out to the sides.
3. Hold your ankles or feet with your hands.
4. Lean forward over your feet and gently press your knees toward the floor until you feel a stretch in your inner thighs.
5. Hold for the set time, then sit up, bring your knees back up and straighten your legs.

## Pro tips

- Sit on the edge of a folded towel or yoga block to tilt your pelvis forward, making it easier to keep your spine straight.
- Use your elbows to gently press your knees toward the floor rather than forcing them down with your hands.
- Focus on lengthening your spine upward before folding forward; a tall Spine Stretches the adductors more effectively.

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the upper back and collapsing the chest forward, which limits the hip stretch and compresses the spine.
- Forcing the knees down aggressively, which can strain the groin muscles or inner knee ligaments.
- Holding your breath during the stretch; breathe deeply and let each exhale deepen the stretch naturally.

## FAQ

### How long should I hold the butterfly pose?

Hold for 30-60 seconds per set, breathing deeply. For deeper flexibility work, hold for 2-3 minutes as part of a yin yoga or mobility routine.

### Why can't I get my knees to the floor in butterfly pose?

Tight adductors, hip flexors, or limited hip joint mobility are usually the cause. Consistent practice over weeks will improve your range. Don't force it.

### Does butterfly pose help with hip pain?

It can relieve tightness-related hip pain by opening the adductors and hip rotators. However, if you have structural hip issues like impingement, consult a professional before performing this stretch.

### What muscles does the Butterfly Yoga Pose work?

The Butterfly Yoga Pose primarily targets the adductors, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, groin. It's classified as a stretching exercise for the legs.

### Is the Butterfly Yoga Pose good for beginners?

Yes, the Butterfly Yoga Pose is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building adductors strength.
