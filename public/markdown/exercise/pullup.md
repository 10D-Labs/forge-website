# Pullup

## Quick answer

A bodyweight exercise, hanging from a bar with your palms facing away and pulling your chest up toward it. It works the lats, with help from the biceps and forearms. Advanced back exercise using body weight. Targets lats, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Hang from a pullup bar with your palms facing away from you and your arms straight.
2. Pull your shoulder blades together and tighten your abs.
3. Bend your elbows to pull your chest up toward the bar.
4. Lower yourself slowly until your arms are straight again.

## Pro tips

- Initiate the movement by depressing and retracting your shoulder blades before bending your elbows.
- Pull until your chin clears the bar, then lower yourself all the way to a dead hang on every rep.
- Keep your core tight and legs still; swinging wastes energy and reduces back muscle engagement.

## Common mistakes

- Not going through the full range of motion (partial reps from halfway up or not lowering to a dead hang).
- Kipping or swinging the legs to generate momentum, which reduces lat and bicep activation.
- Craning the neck forward to get the chin 'over' the bar without actually pulling high enough.

## FAQ

### How do I get my first Pullup?

Start with dead hangs, then progress to negative Pullups (jump up and lower slowly), then Band Assisted Pullups. Most people can achieve their first Pullup within 4-8 weeks of consistent training.

### What's the difference between a Pullup and a Chinup?

Pullups use an overhand (pronated) grip and emphasize the lats more. Chinups use an underhand (supinated) grip and involve more bicep activation. Chinups are generally easier for beginners.

### How many Pullups should I be able to do?

For general fitness, 8-12 strict Pullups is a solid benchmark. For athletic performance, 15-20 is excellent. If you can do 20+, start adding weight.

### What muscles does the Pullup work?

The Pullup primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Pullup good for beginners?

The Pullup is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental back movements first.
