# Barbell Skull Crusher

## Quick answer

A lying triceps exercise on a flat bench, bending your elbows to lower a barbell behind your head, then straightening them. It works the triceps. Intermediate arms exercise using barbell. Targets triceps, also works shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie flat on a bench with your head at the end and your feet flat on the floor.
2. Hold the barbell straight up over your chest, hands shoulder-width apart and palms facing your feet.
3. Keep your upper arms still and bend your elbows to lower the bar slowly past your forehead to behind your head.
4. Straighten your arms to lift the bar back up over your chest.

## Pro tips

- Angle your upper arms slightly back toward your head (not straight up) so there's tension on the triceps even at the top of the rep.
- Lower the bar to your forehead or just behind it with a controlled 2-3 second eccentric.
- Keep your elbows shoulder-width apart; don't let them flare out or squeeze together.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the elbows flare wide, which shifts stress to the shoulders and increases elbow joint strain.
- Holding the upper arms perpendicular to the floor, which creates a dead spot at the top where the triceps aren't working.
- Using too much weight and turning the exercise into a close-grip press by involving the chest and shoulders.

## FAQ

### Why are they called skull crushers?

Because the bar is lowered toward the forehead (skull). Despite the intimidating name, they're perfectly safe when performed with controlled weight and proper form.

### Should I lower the bar to my forehead or behind my head?

Behind the head (toward the bench) gives a deeper stretch on the long head of the triceps. To the forehead is the standard version. Both are effective; behind the head is slightly more demanding.

### Do skull crushers hurt your elbows?

They can if you use too much weight or lock out aggressively. Use moderate weight, control every rep, and try an EZ curl bar if straight bar causes elbow pain. Stop if you feel sharp pain.

### What muscles does the Barbell Skull Crusher work?

The Barbell Skull Crusher primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Barbell Skull Crusher good for beginners?

The Barbell Skull Crusher is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of triceps strength with easier variations first.
