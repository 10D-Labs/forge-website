# Dumbbell Standing Rear Delt Row

## Quick answer

A row done standing upright, pulling the dumbbells up and back along your sides with your elbows out. It works the rear shoulders and upper back. Intermediate shoulders exercise using dumbbell. Targets shoulders, also works trapezius, rhomboids.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Trapezius, Rhomboids
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand tall with your feet hip-width apart and a dumbbell in each hand at your sides, palms facing your thighs.
2. Keep your torso upright and pull the dumbbells up and back along your sides until they reach your waist, elbows pointing out and back.
3. Lower them slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Stand upright holding dumbbells in front of your thighs, palms facing you.
- Lead with your elbows and pull the dumbbells up and back along your sides, keeping your elbows out wide.
- Pause at the top and squeeze your shoulder blades back before lowering.

## Common mistakes

- Pulling the elbows close to your body, which shifts the work to the lats and biceps.
- Shrugging the weights up with the traps.
- Swinging the torso to get the dumbbells moving.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Standing Rear Delt Row work?

The rear shoulders and the upper back, with help from the biceps.

### How is it different from an upright row?

An upright row pulls the weight up the front of your body to chest height. This row pulls up and back along your sides with the elbows out, which puts more work on the rear shoulders.

### How heavy should I go?

Light to moderate. If you need to swing or shrug to move the weight, it is too heavy.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Standing Rear Delt Row work?

The Dumbbell Standing Rear Delt Row primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the trapezius, rhomboids. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Dumbbell Standing Rear Delt Row good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Standing Rear Delt Row is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of shoulders strength with easier variations first.
