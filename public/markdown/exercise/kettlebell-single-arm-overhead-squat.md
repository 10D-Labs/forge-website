# Kettlebell Single Arm Overhead Squat

## Quick answer

A one-arm squat with a kettlebell held straight overhead the whole time. It works the legs and glutes, while your shoulder and core keep the bell steady. Intermediate legs exercise using kettlebell. Targets quads, also works glutes, hamstrings, core, front delts.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Target Muscle:** Quads
- **Secondary Muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings, Core, Front Delts
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet a little wider than shoulder-width apart.
2. Press a kettlebell straight overhead with one hand and lock your arm. Let your other arm hang at your side.
3. Keep the kettlebell steady over your shoulder as you push your hips back and bend your knees to squat down as low as you can.
4. Stand back up with the kettlebell still overhead.

## Pro tips

- Lock the kettlebell straight overhead with the bell resting on the back of your forearm before you start to squat.
- Keep your eyes on the bell or straight ahead and your arm vertical the whole time.
- Squat only as deep as you can while keeping the bell steady and your chest up.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the arm drift forward as you squat, which makes the bell harder to control and strains the shoulder.
- Bending the elbow, which puts the shoulder at risk.
- Going too heavy before your shoulder and hip mobility can handle the position.

## FAQ

### What does the Kettlebell Single Arm Overhead Squat work?

The legs and glutes do the squatting, while the shoulder, upper back and core work hard to keep the bell steady overhead.

### How do I get ready for this exercise?

You should be able to hold a kettlebell steady overhead for 15+ seconds per side and do a deep bodyweight squat with your arms raised. Start with a light bell and build up.

### What muscles does the Kettlebell Single Arm Overhead Squat work?

The Kettlebell Single Arm Overhead Squat primarily targets the quads, with secondary activation in the glutes, hamstrings, core, front delts. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Kettlebell Single Arm Overhead Squat good for beginners?

The Kettlebell Single Arm Overhead Squat is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of quads strength with easier variations first.
