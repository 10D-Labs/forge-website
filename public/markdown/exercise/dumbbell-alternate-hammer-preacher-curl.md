# Dumbbell Alternate Hammer Preacher Curl

## Quick answer

A preacher curl done one arm at a time with your palms facing each other, thumbs up. It works the biceps and forearms. Intermediate arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit at a preacher bench with your upper arms on the pad and a dumbbell in each hand.
2. Straighten your arms and turn your palms to face each other (thumbs up).
3. Keeping your upper arm on the pad, curl one dumbbell up to shoulder height.
4. Lower it slowly until your arm is straight, then curl the other arm. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Maintain the hammer (neutral) grip throughout; your palms should face each other, not rotate during the curl.
- Press your chest against the preacher pad to prevent your body from rocking as you alternate arms.
- Curl one arm at a time and fully complete the rep before starting the other arm to keep the tempo controlled.

## Common mistakes

- Rotating the wrist during the curl, changing it from a hammer curl to a standard curl mid-rep.
- Pulling the upper arm off the pad to help lift the weight, which removes the isolation benefit.
- Alternating too quickly without fully completing each rep, which shortens the range of motion.

## FAQ

### Why alternate arms on the preacher bench?

Alternating lets you focus on one arm at a time for better mind-muscle connection, and the brief rest between sides allows slightly more weight per arm.

### What does the hammer grip do on Preacher Curls?

The neutral grip targets the brachialis and brachioradialis more than a standard supinated curl, building arm thickness rather than peak.

### Can I use this to fix arm strength imbalances?

Yes, alternating single-arm work is one of the best ways to identify and correct imbalances. Start with your weaker arm and match the reps with the stronger arm.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Alternate Hammer Preacher Curl work?

The Dumbbell Alternate Hammer Preacher Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Alternate Hammer Preacher Curl good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Alternate Hammer Preacher Curl is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of biceps strength with easier variations first.
