# Resistance Band Seated Chest Press

## Quick answer

A seated chest press with a resistance band wrapped behind your back. It works the chest, with help from the shoulders and triceps. Beginner chest exercise using resistance band. Targets chest, also works shoulders, triceps.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Triceps
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on a bench or chair with your back straight and your feet flat on the floor.
2. Wrap a resistance band behind your back and hold a handle in each hand, palms facing down, elbows bent to 90 degrees.
3. Push your hands straight forward until your arms are straight.
4. Let your hands come back slowly until your elbows are bent to 90 degrees again.

## Pro tips

- Wrap the band around your back at chest height and press forward with both hands simultaneously.
- Focus on squeezing the chest at the end of each press, bringing your hands close together at full extension.
- Maintain an upright seated posture and do not lean forward as you press.

## Common mistakes

- Positioning the band too high on the back (around the neck), which changes the pressing angle and can cause discomfort.
- Not extending the arms fully, which leaves out the peak contraction where bands provide the most resistance.
- Letting the band pull the arms back too quickly instead of controlling the eccentric for 2-3 seconds.

## FAQ

### Can band Chest Presses replace bench press?

They are a useful supplement or alternative for home training, but the resistance curve and maximum load are different from a Barbell Bench Press. Use both when possible.

### Where should I position the band for Chest Press?

Wrap it around your mid-back at chest level. It should sit across your shoulder blades, not on your neck.

### How do I make band Chest Presses harder?

Use a thicker band, shorten the band by wrapping it around your hands, or use two bands simultaneously.

### What muscles does the Resistance Band Seated Chest Press work?

The Resistance Band Seated Chest Press primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the shoulders, triceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Resistance Band Seated Chest Press good for beginners?

Yes, the Resistance Band Seated Chest Press is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building chest strength.
