# Cable Upright Row

## Quick answer

An upright row with a bar on a low cable pulley, pulled from your thighs up to your chin. It works the sides of the shoulders, with help from the traps and biceps. Intermediate shoulders exercise using cable. Targets shoulders, also works traps, biceps.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Traps, Biceps
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand facing a low cable pulley with a bar attached, feet shoulder-width apart and knees slightly bent.
2. Hold the bar in front of your thighs with your palms facing you.
3. Keep your back straight and pull the bar straight up toward your chin, leading with your elbows.
4. Lower the bar slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Pull the cable straight up along your body, leading with your elbows, until the attachment reaches chin height. Your elbows should always be higher than your hands.
- Use a grip slightly wider than shoulder width to reduce impingement risk. A very narrow grip pinches the shoulder joint.
- Keep the cable close to your body throughout the pull. If it drifts forward, you lose mechanical advantage.

## Common mistakes

- Using too narrow a grip, which increases the risk of shoulder impingement by forcing the shoulder into excessive internal rotation.
- Pulling the cable too high above chin level, which puts the shoulder in a vulnerable position.
- Shrugging at the top instead of leading with the elbows, which turns this into a shrug rather than a delt and trap exercise.

## FAQ

### Are Cable Upright Rows bad for your shoulders?

Upright rows can cause shoulder impingement if performed with a too-narrow grip or pulled too high. Use a wider-than-shoulder-width grip, stop at chin height, and avoid the exercise if you have existing shoulder impingement.

### What muscles do Cable Upright Rows work?

Primarily the lateral deltoids and upper trapezius. Secondary muscles include the biceps and forearms. The exercise is one of the few that effectively hits both the delts and traps simultaneously.

### What muscles does the Cable Upright Row work?

The Cable Upright Row primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the traps, biceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Cable Upright Row good for beginners?

The Cable Upright Row is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of shoulders strength with easier variations first.
