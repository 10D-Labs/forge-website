# Cable Palm Rotational Row

## Quick answer

A one-arm row from a low cable where your palm turns from down to up as you pull. It works the upper back, with help from the biceps and forearms. Intermediate back exercise using cable. Targets upper back, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Attach a single handle to a low pulley and stand facing the machine with your feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Hold the handle in one hand, palm facing down, and step back until the cable is tight.
3. Bend your knees into a half squat and lean forward slightly, keeping your back straight.
4. Pull the handle toward your waist, turning your palm to face up as you pull.
5. Let the handle go back slowly, turning your palm down again, until your arm is straight.

## Pro tips

- Start the pull with your palm facing down and rotate to palm-up as the handle reaches your torso for full biceps and lat engagement.
- Initiate the row by retracting your shoulder blade first, then bending the elbow to complete the pull.
- Keep a slight bend in your knees and a strong hip hinge to protect your lower back throughout the set.

## Common mistakes

- Rotating the palm too early in the movement, which turns it into a biceps curl rather than a back exercise.
- Standing too close to the cable stack, which reduces the effective range of motion.
- Rounding the upper back to reach forward at the start position instead of maintaining a proud chest.

## FAQ

### What does the palm rotation add to a regular cable row?

The rotation engages the supinator and biceps more fully while also hitting different fibers of the upper back through varying the pulling angle during the rep.

### Should I use heavy weight on this exercise?

Use moderate weight that allows you to control the rotation smoothly. This is a technique-focused exercise where the rotation quality matters more than load.

### Can I do this exercise seated?

Yes. A seated version on a low cable row works well and removes the need to stabilize your hips, letting you focus purely on the pulling and rotation mechanics.

### What muscles does the Cable Palm Rotational Row work?

The Cable Palm Rotational Row primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Cable Palm Rotational Row good for beginners?

The Cable Palm Rotational Row is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of upper back strength with easier variations first.
