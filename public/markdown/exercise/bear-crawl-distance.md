# Bear Crawl (Distance)

## Quick answer

Crawling forward on your hands and feet with your knees just off the floor. It raises your heart rate and works the abs, shoulders and triceps. Intermediate cardio exercise using body weight. Targets cardiovascular, also works core, shoulders, triceps.

- **Body Part:** Cardio
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Cardiovascular
- **Secondary Muscles:** Core, Shoulders, Triceps
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Cardio

---

## How to perform

1. Get on your hands and knees with your hands under your shoulders and your knees under your hips.
2. Lift your knees slightly off the floor, keeping your back flat.
3. Move your right hand and left foot forward together, then your left hand and right foot.
4. Keep crawling forward at a steady pace, alternating sides.
5. Keep going for the set distance.

## Pro tips

- Keep your knees hovering just 1-2 inches off the ground for maximum core activation.
- Move opposite hand and foot together (right hand, left foot) for a natural, stable crawling pattern.
- Take small, controlled steps rather than large lunging strides.

## Common mistakes

- Raising the hips too high into a pike position, which reduces the core and shoulder demand.
- Moving same-side limbs together instead of contralateral (opposite) pairs.
- Losing the flat back position as fatigue sets in, letting the back round or sag.

## FAQ

### How far should I bear crawl?

Start with 10-20 yards per set. As your conditioning improves, work up to 30-50 yards. The distance should challenge your coordination and core endurance without breaking form.

### What makes the distance version different from the time version?

The distance version gives you a specific target to reach, which often pushes you to maintain pace. The time version lets you control intensity by adjusting speed throughout.

### Can bear crawls replace traditional cardio?

For short, intense conditioning sessions, yes. Bear crawls elevate heart rate significantly while building strength. They won't replace longer steady-state cardio for endurance training.

### What muscles does the Bear Crawl (Distance) work?

The Bear Crawl (Distance) primarily targets the cardiovascular, with secondary activation in the core, shoulders, triceps. It's classified as a cardio exercise for the cardio.

### Is the Bear Crawl (Distance) good for beginners?

The Bear Crawl (Distance) is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of cardiovascular strength with easier variations first.
