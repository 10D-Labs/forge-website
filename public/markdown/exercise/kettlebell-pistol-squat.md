# Kettlebell Pistol Squat

## Quick answer

A one-leg squat holding a kettlebell at your chest, with the other leg held straight out in front. It works the glutes, quads, hamstrings and calves, and takes real strength, balance and flexibility. Advanced legs exercise using kettlebell. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a kettlebell in front of your chest with both hands.
2. Lift your left foot and stretch that leg out in front of you, level with the floor.
3. Squat down slowly on your right leg, keeping your right foot flat and your left leg out in front.
4. Push through your right heel to stand back up.
5. Do all your reps on one leg, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Hold the kettlebell at chest height as a counterbalance. The weight in front of you actually makes the Pistol Squat easier by shifting your center of gravity forward.
- Actively push your non-working leg forward and flex the foot to keep it off the ground throughout the descent.
- Sit back and down slowly, keeping your heel planted on the working leg. Use a 3-4 second descent.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the heel of the working leg lift off the ground, which indicates insufficient ankle mobility.
- Allowing the non-working leg to touch the ground at the bottom, reducing the single-leg demand.
- Collapsing forward at the bottom instead of keeping the chest up and core braced.

## FAQ

### How do I build up to a Kettlebell Pistol Squat?

Progress through: box-assisted Pistol Squats, Pistol Squats holding a doorframe or TRX for support, bodyweight Pistol Squats to a bench, full bodyweight pistols, then add the kettlebell.

### Is the Kettlebell Pistol Squat easier or harder than a bodyweight pistol?

Surprisingly, it can be easier for some people because the kettlebell acts as a counterbalance that helps you stay upright. The extra load does increase strength demands on the working leg, but the balance challenge decreases.

### What muscles does the Kettlebell Pistol Squat work?

The Kettlebell Pistol Squat primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Kettlebell Pistol Squat good for beginners?

The Kettlebell Pistol Squat is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental legs movements first.
