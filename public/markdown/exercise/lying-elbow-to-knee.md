# Lying Elbow to Knee

## Quick answer

A crunch with one ankle crossed over the other knee, twisting your opposite elbow toward it. It works the abs and the obliques. Beginner core exercise using body weight. Targets abs, also works hip flexors, lower back.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hip Flexors, Lower Back
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your back with your knees bent and feet flat. Cross one ankle over the other knee.
2. Place your hands behind your head with your elbows pointing out.
3. Curl your head and shoulders off the floor and twist to bring your opposite elbow toward the raised knee.
4. Lower slowly back to the floor. Do all your reps on one side, then switch legs.

## Pro tips

- Focus on rotating your ribcage, not just moving your elbow. Your shoulder should come off the ground toward the opposite knee.
- Keep your lower back pressed into the floor throughout the movement to protect your spine.
- Extend the straight leg fully and hover it just above the ground to maximize ab and hip flexor engagement.

## Common mistakes

- Pulling on the neck with the hands to bring the elbow forward, which strains the neck and reduces ab activation.
- Moving only the elbow toward the knee without actually rotating the torso, which turns it into an arm movement instead of a core exercise.
- Letting the extended leg touch the ground between reps, which releases tension on the abs.

## FAQ

### Is the Lying Elbow to Knee the same as a Bicycle Crunch?

They are very similar. The Lying Elbow to Knee is typically performed at a slower, more controlled pace with a pause at each side, while Bicycle Crunches use a continuous pedaling motion.

### Does the Lying Elbow to Knee work obliques?

Yes, the rotational component heavily targets the obliques in addition to the rectus abdominis.

### How many reps should I do for Lying Elbow to Knee?

Aim for 10-15 reps per side (20-30 total). Focus on controlled rotation rather than speed.

### What muscles does the Lying Elbow to Knee work?

The Lying Elbow to Knee primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the hip flexors, lower back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Lying Elbow to Knee good for beginners?

Yes, the Lying Elbow to Knee is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building abs strength.
