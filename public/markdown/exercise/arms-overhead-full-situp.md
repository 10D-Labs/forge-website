# Arms Overhead Full Situp

## Quick answer

A full situp with straight legs that starts with your arms overhead and ends folded forward, hands at your toes. It works the abs, with help from the front of the hips and the lower back. Intermediate core exercise using body weight. Targets abs, also works hip flexors, lower back.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hip Flexors, Lower Back
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie flat on your back with your legs straight and together and your arms straight on the floor above your head.
2. Swing your arms up and forward as you sit up, peeling your back off the floor.
3. Keep your legs straight on the floor and fold forward over them until your hands reach your toes.
4. Roll back down slowly until your back and arms are flat on the floor again.

## Pro tips

- Extend your arms straight overhead throughout the entire sit-up to create the longest possible lever arm.
- Engage your abs first, then curl up sequentially: head, shoulders, upper back, then lower back leaving the floor.
- Lower yourself back down vertebra by vertebra rather than dropping flat.

## Common mistakes

- Bending the arms during the ascent, which shortens the lever arm and makes the exercise easier.
- Using momentum by throwing the arms forward to help sit up, which bypasses the ab work.
- Anchoring the feet under something heavy, which lets the hip flexors dominate the movement.

## FAQ

### Why is the arms overhead sit-up so much harder?

Extending the arms overhead dramatically increases the lever arm and shifts the center of mass further from the pivot point (hips), requiring significantly more ab strength to curl up.

### Is the arms overhead sit-up safe?

It is safe for people with good core strength and no lower back issues. Beginners should build up with standard sit-ups first before attempting this advanced variation.

### How many arms overhead sit-ups should I be able to do?

If you can do 10-15 with good form and no momentum, you have strong abs. Many fit people struggle with 5-8 reps the first time they try this variation.

### What muscles does the Arms Overhead Full Situp work?

The Arms Overhead Full Situp primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the hip flexors, lower back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Arms Overhead Full Situp good for beginners?

The Arms Overhead Full Situp is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of abs strength with easier variations first.
