# Dumbbell Preacher Hammer Curl

## Quick answer

A preacher curl done with your palms facing each other, thumbs up, both arms at once, with the pad keeping your upper arms still. It works the biceps and the forearms. Beginner arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on a preacher bench with your feet flat and the backs of your upper arms on the pad.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand, arms straight, palms facing each other (thumbs up).
3. Keep your upper arms on the pad and your palms facing each other the whole time.
4. Curl both dumbbells up until your forearms are upright, close to your shoulders.
5. Lower slowly until your arms are straight again.

## Pro tips

- Keep the neutral (hammer) grip throughout with palms facing each other to target the brachialis and brachioradialis alongside the biceps.
- Press your upper arms firmly into the preacher pad and resist the urge to lift them during the curl.
- Lower the dumbbells slowly through the full range until your arms are nearly straight to maximize the stretch on the brachialis.

## Common mistakes

- Lifting the elbows off the preacher pad at the top of the curl, which allows momentum and reduces isolation.
- Rotating the wrists to a supinated (palms-up) position, which turns this into a standard Preacher Curl and reduces brachialis involvement.
- Cutting the range of motion short by not lowering fully, which misses the most effective stretched portion of the exercise.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between a preacher hammer curl and a regular Preacher Curl?

The hammer (neutral) grip shifts more work to the brachialis and brachioradialis muscles, which contribute to arm thickness and a bigger bicep peak.

### Will preacher hammer curls make my arms wider?

Yes. The brachialis sits underneath the biceps and when developed pushes the biceps up, creating wider-looking arms from the front.

### Can I do preacher hammer curls one arm at a time?

Yes, and single arm versions allow better concentration and help identify left-right strength differences.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Preacher Hammer Curl work?

The Dumbbell Preacher Hammer Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Preacher Hammer Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Preacher Hammer Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building biceps strength.
