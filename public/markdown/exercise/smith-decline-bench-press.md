# Smith Decline Bench Press

## Quick answer

A Smith machine bench press on a decline bench, with your head lower than your hips. It works the lower chest, with help from the triceps and shoulders. Intermediate chest exercise using smith machine. Targets chest, also works triceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Smith Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set a decline bench under the Smith machine at the angle you want. Lie on it with your feet secured under the foot pads.
2. Grip the bar with your palms facing toward your feet, hands slightly wider than shoulder-width.
3. Unhook the bar and lower it slowly to your lower chest, keeping your elbows tucked in.
4. Press the bar back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Set the decline angle to 15-30 degrees; anything steeper puts too much blood pressure in your head.
- Position the bench so the bar naturally lowers to your lower chest line.
- Keep your shoulder blades pinched and your feet secured firmly under the foot pads.

## Common mistakes

- Setting the decline angle too steep, which reduces chest activation and increases discomfort.
- Letting the bar drift toward the neck instead of targeting the lower pecs.
- Not securing the feet under the pads, which can cause you to slide on the bench during the press.

## FAQ

### Does the decline bench press target the lower chest?

Yes. The decline angle shifts emphasis to the lower (sternal) fibers of the pectoralis major. It's one of the best exercises for lower chest development.

### Is the Smith Decline Bench Press safe to do alone?

Safer than free weight decline because the safety hooks can catch the bar at any point. However, always set the safety stops before your set, especially when training to failure.

### What angle should I use for decline bench press?

15-30 degrees of decline is the sweet spot. Beyond 30 degrees, the front deltoids take over and blood pools in the head, causing discomfort.

### What muscles does the Smith Decline Bench Press work?

The Smith Decline Bench Press primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Smith Decline Bench Press good for beginners?

The Smith Decline Bench Press is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of chest strength with easier variations first.
