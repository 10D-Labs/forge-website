# Hands Elevated Pushup

## Quick answer

A pushup with your hands up on a step, which makes it easier than a floor pushup. It works the chest, mainly the lower part, plus the shoulders and triceps. Beginner chest exercise using body weight. Targets chest, also works triceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Place your hands flat on a step, a bit wider than your shoulders, and walk your feet back until your body is straight.
2. Keep your body straight and bend your elbows to lower your chest to the edge of the step.
3. Push back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Place your hands on a step or bench, slightly wider than your shoulders, with your feet on the floor.
- Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels. Don't let your hips pike up or sag down.
- Lower your chest to the edge of the step with your elbows at about 45 degrees from your body.

## Common mistakes

- Piking the hips up high, which shortens the range of motion and takes work off the chest.
- Placing the hands too far forward instead of under the shoulders.
- Stopping several inches short of the step instead of lowering all the way.

## FAQ

### Are Hands Elevated Pushups easier than regular Pushups?

Yes. With your hands higher than your feet, your arms push less of your body weight. That makes them a good way to build up to floor Pushups.

### How do I progress to floor Pushups?

Start at a height where you can do 3 sets of 10-15 good reps, then use a lower step every week or two until your hands are on the floor.

### What muscles does the Hands Elevated Pushup work?

The Hands Elevated Pushup primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Hands Elevated Pushup good for beginners?

Yes, the Hands Elevated Pushup is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building chest strength.
