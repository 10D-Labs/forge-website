# Dumbbell Alternate Bicep Curl

## Quick answer

A standing dumbbell curl done one arm at a time, palms facing forward, with your upper arms kept still. It works the biceps and also the forearms. Beginner arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand tall with a dumbbell in each hand, arms straight at your sides, palms facing forward.
2. Keep your upper arms still and curl one dumbbell up to shoulder level.
3. Lower it slowly until your arm is straight, then curl the other arm. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Alternate arms each rep so one arm rests while the other works. This allows you to maintain better form over more total reps than simultaneous curls.
- Supinate your wrist (rotate palm upward) as you curl to maximize bicep peak contraction. Start with palms facing your sides and rotate as you lift.
- Keep your torso completely still. If your upper body sways, the weight is too heavy.

## Common mistakes

- Swinging the body side to side with each alternating curl to generate momentum.
- Not fully extending the arm at the bottom between reps, which limits the stretch and reduces range of motion.
- Curling both dumbbells at the same time instead of alternating, which reduces the rest benefit and form focus.

## FAQ

### Is alternating Bicep Curls better than doing both arms at once?

Alternating allows more focus on each arm individually, enables heavier weights due to brief rest periods between arms, and helps identify strength imbalances. Both methods are effective.

### Should I rotate my wrist during Dumbbell Bicep Curls?

Yes, starting with a neutral grip and rotating to a supinated grip (palms up) as you curl engages the bicep through its full function, including forearm supination. This produces a stronger peak contraction.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Alternate Bicep Curl work?

The Dumbbell Alternate Bicep Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Alternate Bicep Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Alternate Bicep Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building biceps strength.
