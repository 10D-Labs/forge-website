# Barbell Lunge

## Quick answer

A forward lunge with a barbell across your upper back, alternating legs. It works the glutes, plus the quads, hamstrings and calves. Intermediate legs exercise using barbell. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and a barbell resting across your upper back.
2. Step forward with your right foot, keeping your upper body upright.
3. Bend your right knee to lower your body until your right thigh is level with the floor.
4. Push through your right heel to step back to standing, then lunge with your left leg. Keep alternating legs.

## Pro tips

- Take a long enough step that your front shin stays vertical or only slightly angled forward at the bottom of the lunge.
- Keep your torso upright and avoid leaning forward; the barbell should stay directly over your hips.
- Drive back to standing through your front heel, not your toes, to maximize glute activation.

## Common mistakes

- Taking too short a step, which causes the front knee to shoot past the toes and overloads the knee joint.
- Wobbling side to side because the feet are too narrow; maintain a hip-width stance as if walking on two rails.
- Leaning forward with the torso, which places excessive stress on the lower back with the barbell loaded.

## FAQ

### Are Barbell Lunges better than Dumbbell Lunges?

Barbell Lunges allow heavier loading and train core stability differently (weight on the back vs. at the sides). Dumbbell Lunges are more balance-friendly for beginners. Both are effective.

### Should I do Walking Lunges or stationary lunges with a barbell?

Stationary (alternating) lunges are safer with a barbell because the bar on your back makes balance challenging during Walking Lunges. Use dumbbells for Walking Lunges.

### How deep should I lunge with a barbell?

Lower until your back knee is about 1-2 inches from the ground and your front thigh is parallel to the floor. Full range of motion produces the best glute and quad development.

### What muscles does the Barbell Lunge work?

The Barbell Lunge primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Barbell Lunge good for beginners?

The Barbell Lunge is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of glutes strength with easier variations first.
