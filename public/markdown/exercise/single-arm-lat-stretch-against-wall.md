# Single Arm Lat Stretch Against Wall

## Quick answer

A standing stretch with your bent arm raised and your elbow against a wall, leaning in. It stretches the lats down the side of your back, plus the shoulder and triceps. Beginner back exercise using body weight. Targets lats, also works shoulders, triceps.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Triceps
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Stretching

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with one side close to a wall, about a foot away, feet hip-width apart.
2. Raise the arm nearest the wall, bend the elbow, and rest your elbow and upper arm on the wall with your hand behind your head.
3. Lean your body toward the wall until you feel a stretch down the side of your back.
4. Hold for the set time, then ease back out slowly and switch sides.

## Pro tips

- Stand side-on to a wall, raise your bent arm and rest your elbow against the wall above your head.
- Lean your body gently toward the wall until you feel the stretch along the side of your back.
- Hold for 20-30 seconds while breathing slowly, then switch sides.

## Common mistakes

- Leaning too hard and forcing the shoulder into pain.
- Arching the lower back instead of keeping your ribs down.
- Holding your breath, which keeps the muscles tense.

## FAQ

### What does the Single Arm Lat Stretch Against Wall stretch?

The lats down the side of your back, plus the shoulder and triceps of the raised arm.

### When should I do this stretch?

After back or overhead workouts, or any time your upper back feels tight from sitting.

### How do I make the stretch deeper?

Step your feet a little further from the wall, or turn your chest slightly toward the floor.

### What muscles does the Single Arm Lat Stretch Against Wall work?

The Single Arm Lat Stretch Against Wall primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the shoulders, triceps. It's classified as a stretching exercise for the back.

### Is the Single Arm Lat Stretch Against Wall good for beginners?

Yes, the Single Arm Lat Stretch Against Wall is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building lats strength.
