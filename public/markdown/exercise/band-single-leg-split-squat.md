# Band Single Leg Split Squat

## Quick answer

A split squat with your back foot up on a box and a band under your front foot, held at your sides. It works the quads, with the glutes and hamstrings helping. Intermediate legs exercise using resistance band. Targets quads, also works glutes, hamstrings.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Quads
- **Secondary Muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand a big step in front of a box and rest the top of your back foot on it.
2. Stand on the middle of a band with your front foot and hold a handle in each hand at your sides, palms facing in.
3. Bend your front knee and lower straight down until your front thigh is about level with the floor.
4. Push through your front foot to stand back up. Do all your reps on one leg, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Place the band around your ankles and take a wide split stance with your front foot flat and back foot on the toes.
- Lower straight down until the back knee nearly touches the floor, keeping the front knee over the toes.
- Drive up through the front heel to return to standing, keeping tension on the band throughout.

## Common mistakes

- Stepping too narrow, which limits depth and makes balance difficult.
- Leaning the torso too far forward over the front knee instead of staying upright.
- Letting the front knee cave inward, especially with the band pulling at the ankle.

## FAQ

### What does the band add to a split squat?

The band around the ankles adds lateral resistance that forces the hip abductors (glute med) to work harder to keep the knees tracking properly.

### How deep should you go in a split squat?

Lower until your back knee is 1-2 inches from the ground or your front thigh is parallel to the floor. Full range of motion is key for muscle development.

### Are Split Squats as effective as back squats?

For single-leg strength and muscle development, yes. They also identify and correct imbalances that bilateral squats can mask.

### What muscles does the Band Single Leg Split Squat work?

The Band Single Leg Split Squat primarily targets the quads, with secondary activation in the glutes, hamstrings. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Band Single Leg Split Squat good for beginners?

The Band Single Leg Split Squat is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of quads strength with easier variations first.
