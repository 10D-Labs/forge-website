# Band Jack Knife Situp

## Quick answer

A sit-up where you pull your knees to your chest as you sit up, against a band anchored past your feet. It works the abs and the hip flexors at the front of the hips. Intermediate core exercise using resistance band. Targets abs, also works hip flexors.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hip Flexors
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Loop a band around both ankles, anchored low past your feet. Lie on your back with your legs straight and arms at your sides.
2. Sit up and pull your knees in toward your chest at the same time, lifting your feet off the floor.
3. Reach your arms forward beside your legs and balance on your hips at the top.
4. Lower slowly back down until you're lying flat with your legs straight.

## Pro tips

- Hold the band overhead and simultaneously lift your legs and arms to meet in the middle, forming a V shape.
- Keep the band taut throughout the movement by holding tension even at the bottom position.
- Lower both your arms and legs slowly and do not let them crash to the floor.

## Common mistakes

- Bending the knees to bring the feet closer instead of keeping the legs straight for maximum ab challenge.
- Only reaching halfway up instead of fully closing the V shape at the top.
- Letting the arms and legs fall freely on the eccentric instead of controlling the descent.

## FAQ

### How does the band add difficulty to a jack knife situp?

The band creates resistance in the overhead position, so your abs must work harder to pull both your upper and lower body together against the tension.

### Can beginners do Band Jack Knife Situps?

It is an intermediate exercise. Beginners should master regular Tuck Crunches and bodyweight V-ups before adding band resistance.

### How many reps should you do?

Aim for 3 sets of 8-12 reps with controlled form. If you cannot maintain straight legs and full range of motion, reduce reps or use a lighter band.

### What muscles does the Band Jack Knife Situp work?

The Band Jack Knife Situp primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the hip flexors. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Band Jack Knife Situp good for beginners?

The Band Jack Knife Situp is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of abs strength with easier variations first.
