# Dumbbell Arnold Press

## Quick answer

A seated dumbbell shoulder press that turns your palms from facing you to facing forward as you press. It works the front of the shoulders, with help from the triceps and upper chest. Intermediate shoulders exercise using dumbbell. Targets shoulders, also works triceps, upper chest.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Upper Chest
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on a bench with a back rest. Hold a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder height, elbows bent, palms facing you.
2. Press the dumbbells up until your arms are straight, turning your wrists as you go so your palms face forward at the top.
3. Lower the dumbbells slowly back to your shoulders, turning your palms to face you again.

## Pro tips

- Start with palms facing you at the bottom and rotate smoothly so palms face forward at the top; the rotation should happen through the middle third of the press.
- Keep your elbows slightly in front of your body at the bottom position to protect the shoulder joint.
- Use a controlled 2-second rotation on the way up and down rather than rushing through the twist.

## Common mistakes

- Rotating the wrists too early or too late in the movement, which defeats the purpose of the Arnold press and reduces front delt activation.
- Arching the lower back excessively to compensate for heavy weight, which risks spinal injury.
- Locking out the elbows aggressively at the top, which shifts tension from the shoulders to the joints.

## FAQ

### What makes the Arnold press different from a regular shoulder press?

The Arnold press adds a rotation component that activates all three heads of the deltoid more evenly, particularly the front delt, compared to a standard overhead press.

### Did Arnold Schwarzenegger invent the Arnold press?

Arnold Schwarzenegger popularized this variation during his bodybuilding career. He used it to develop fuller, rounder shoulders by incorporating the rotation.

### Should I do the Arnold press seated or standing?

Seated with back support is recommended for most people because it isolates the shoulders better and prevents using leg drive to cheat the weight up.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Arnold Press work?

The Dumbbell Arnold Press primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the triceps, upper chest. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Dumbbell Arnold Press good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Arnold Press is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of shoulders strength with easier variations first.
