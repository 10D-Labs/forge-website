# Single Leg Donkey Calf Raise

## Quick answer

A one-leg calf raise done bent forward with your hands on a bench for balance. It works the calves. Intermediate legs exercise using body weight. Targets calves, also works hamstrings, glutes.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Calves
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Glutes
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand a long step from the end of a flat bench. Bend forward and rest your hands on it, palms down, arms straight.
2. Bend one knee and lift that foot behind you so all your weight is on the other foot.
3. Rise up onto the ball of your standing foot, lifting your heel as high as you can.
4. Lower your heel slowly back to the floor. Do all your reps on one leg, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Lean forward at the hips to about a 90-degree angle with your torso parallel to the floor, supporting yourself with your hands.
- Let the heel of the working leg drop as low as possible before driving up on the toes for full range.
- Stand on a raised surface to increase the available range of motion at the bottom of the movement.

## Common mistakes

- Not leaning forward enough, which turns it into a regular Standing Calf Raise and changes the muscle emphasis.
- Using the non-working leg to push off the ground subtly, reducing the single-leg challenge.
- Rushing through the bottom stretch instead of pausing to load the calf at its most lengthened position.

## FAQ

### Why is the Donkey Calf Raise position different from standing?

The forward-leaned hip position pre-stretches the gastrocnemius more than standing upright, which increases muscle activation and can produce greater hypertrophy stimulus.

### How do I add weight to this exercise?

Wear a weighted vest, have a partner sit on your lower back (traditional Donkey Calf Raise method), or hold a dumbbell in one hand while bracing with the other.

### Is this exercise good for runners?

Excellent. Single-leg calf strength is critical for running since each stride is essentially a single-leg calf raise. This exercise closely mimics that demand.

### What muscles does the Single Leg Donkey Calf Raise work?

The Single Leg Donkey Calf Raise primarily targets the calves, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, glutes. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Single Leg Donkey Calf Raise good for beginners?

The Single Leg Donkey Calf Raise is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of calves strength with easier variations first.
