# Potty Squat with Support

## Quick answer

A deep bodyweight squat done holding onto a support for balance, which helps while you learn to squat. It works the glutes. Beginner legs exercise using body weight. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, toes turned out slightly, holding the top of an incline bench or another steady support for balance.
2. Push your hips back and bend your knees to lower into a deep squat, keeping your chest up and back straight.
3. Push through your heels to stand back up.

## Pro tips

- Hold the support lightly for balance, not to pull yourself up. Your legs should do all the work.
- Sit as deep as your mobility allows, working to increase depth over time.
- Focus on keeping your heels on the ground and your knees tracking over your toes.

## Common mistakes

- Relying too heavily on the support to pull yourself up, which removes the leg strengthening benefit.
- Leaning forward excessively instead of sitting straight down between the feet.
- Losing heel contact with the floor, which indicates tight ankles that need mobility work.

## FAQ

### Why use support for a Potty Squat?

The support helps with balance while you build the ankle, hip, and core mobility needed for an unsupported deep squat. It is an excellent progression tool.

### How do I transition from supported to unsupported Potty Squats?

Gradually reduce the amount of support by using a lighter grip, then just fingertips, then one hand, until you can balance independently.

### What support should I use for Potty Squats?

A doorframe, sturdy chair back, or squat rack upright all work well. The support should be stable and at about waist height.

### What muscles does the Potty Squat with Support work?

The Potty Squat with Support primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Potty Squat with Support good for beginners?

Yes, the Potty Squat with Support is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building glutes strength.
