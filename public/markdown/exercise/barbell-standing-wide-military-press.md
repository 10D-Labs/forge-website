# Barbell Standing Wide Military Press

## Quick answer

A standing barbell press from the front of your shoulders to overhead, with your hands a little wider than shoulder-width. It works the front of the shoulders, with help from the triceps and upper back. Intermediate shoulders exercise using barbell. Targets shoulders, also works triceps, upper back.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Upper Back
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart. Hold a barbell with your palms facing forward, hands a little wider than your shoulders.
2. Bring the bar up to the front of your shoulders, with your elbows slightly in front of the bar.
3. Press the bar overhead until your arms are fully straight.
4. Lower the bar slowly back to the front of your shoulders.

## Pro tips

- Use a grip about 4-6 inches wider than shoulder width to maximize deltoid recruitment over tricep involvement.
- Tuck your chin slightly as the bar passes your face, then push your head through once the bar clears to stack the weight over your spine.
- Control the eccentric by taking 2-3 seconds to lower the bar back to your shoulders.

## Common mistakes

- Gripping so wide that wrist pain develops; your forearms should remain roughly vertical at the bottom of the press.
- Letting the bar drift forward during the press, which puts the shoulders in a mechanically weaker position.
- Using leg drive to push past sticking points, which turns the strict press into a push press and reduces shoulder stimulus.

## FAQ

### What is the benefit of a wide grip Military Press?

The wider grip increases lateral and anterior deltoid activation while shortening the range of motion slightly. It targets the outer shoulders more than a close grip variation.

### How wide should I grip for the wide Military Press?

Place your hands about 4-6 inches outside shoulder width. Your forearms should be close to vertical when the bar is at shoulder height.

### Can I do the wide grip Military Press seated?

Yes, a seated version removes the need for core stabilization and lets you focus purely on pressing strength, but standing builds more functional total-body stability.

### What muscles does the Barbell Standing Wide Military Press work?

The Barbell Standing Wide Military Press primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the triceps, upper back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Barbell Standing Wide Military Press good for beginners?

The Barbell Standing Wide Military Press is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of shoulders strength with easier variations first.
