# Smith Chair Squat

## Quick answer

A squat on a Smith machine, with the bar across your upper back on fixed rails. It works the quads, with help from the glutes, hamstrings and calves. Beginner legs exercise using smith machine. Targets quads, also works glutes, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Smith Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Quads
- **Secondary Muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set the Smith machine bar at a comfortable height and step under it so it rests across your upper back.
2. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, toes turned slightly out.
3. Keep your chest up as you bend your knees and hips to lower yourself slowly.
4. Go down until your thighs are level with the floor, or as low as you comfortably can.
5. Push through your heels to stand back up.

## Pro tips

- Use a chair or bench behind you as a depth cue: squat until your glutes lightly touch it, then stand up.
- Keep your chest up and weight in your heels throughout the entire squat.
- The Smith machine bar should rest on your upper traps, not on your neck.

## Common mistakes

- Sitting fully onto the chair and relaxing at the bottom, which loses all muscle tension.
- Leaning too far forward because the bar is positioned too low on the back.
- Not squatting deep enough; the chair should be at a height that puts your thighs at least parallel.

## FAQ

### What is the purpose of the chair in a Smith Chair Squat?

The chair serves as a depth guide and safety net, ensuring consistent squat depth and giving beginners confidence. It teaches proper squat mechanics in a controlled environment.

### Is the Smith Chair Squat good for beginners?

Yes. The combination of the fixed bar path and the chair depth cue makes it one of the most beginner-friendly squat variations. It builds confidence before transitioning to free weight squats.

### How do I progress from the Smith Chair Squat?

First remove the chair and do regular Smith Squats. Then transition to goblet squats, and finally barbell back squats. Each step introduces more balance and stability demands.

### What muscles does the Smith Chair Squat work?

The Smith Chair Squat primarily targets the quads, with secondary activation in the glutes, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Smith Chair Squat good for beginners?

Yes, the Smith Chair Squat is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building quads strength.
