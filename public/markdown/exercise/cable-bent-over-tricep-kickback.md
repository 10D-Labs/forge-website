# Cable Bent Over Tricep Kickback

## Quick answer

A bent-over cable move where you pull a nearly straight arm back past your hip, one arm at a time. It works the triceps and the backs of the shoulders. Beginner arms exercise using cable. Targets triceps, also works shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Attach a handle to a low pulley and hold it in one hand, palm facing in. Face the machine and step back, opposite foot forward.
2. Bend forward at the hips to about 45 degrees with your knees slightly bent. Rest your free hand on your front thigh.
3. Keeping your arm nearly straight, pull the handle back past your hip as far as you can.
4. Let your arm swing slowly forward until your hand is in front of your thigh. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Set the pulley low, hinge forward and hold the handle with your arm hanging straight down.
- Keep the arm nearly straight and pull it back past your hip, squeezing at the end.
- Return the handle slowly to the start before the next rep.

## Common mistakes

- Standing too upright, which limits how far back the arm can travel.
- Bending the elbow a lot and turning the move into a row.
- Using too much weight and swinging the torso to move it.

## FAQ

### What does the straight-arm cable kickback work?

The triceps, especially the long head that crosses the shoulder, and the backs of the shoulders.

### What is the best cable height?

The lowest pulley position, so the cable pulls forward and down as you move your arm back.

### How many reps should I do?

12-20 reps per arm with moderate weight. Focus on the squeeze rather than heavy loads.

### What muscles does the Cable Bent Over Tricep Kickback work?

The Cable Bent Over Tricep Kickback primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Cable Bent Over Tricep Kickback good for beginners?

Yes, the Cable Bent Over Tricep Kickback is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building triceps strength.
