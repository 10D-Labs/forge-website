# Cable Seated Rear Lateral Raise

## Quick answer

A rear raise done sitting on the end of a bench, bent forward, with each hand pulling the low cable from the opposite side. It works the backs of the shoulders and the upper back. Intermediate shoulders exercise using cable. Targets shoulders, also works traps, rhomboids.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Traps, Rhomboids
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on the end of a flat bench placed between two low pulleys, one on each side, with your feet flat on the floor.
2. Lean forward until your chest rests over your thighs.
3. Hold the left handle in your right hand and the right handle in your left, arms hanging by your ankles, palms facing back.
4. Keeping a slight bend in your elbows, raise your arms out to the sides until they're level with your shoulders.
5. Lower slowly until your hands are back by your ankles.

## Pro tips

- Sit facing the cable machine and lean forward slightly from the hips to align the movement with the rear deltoid muscle fibers.
- Keep your arms nearly straight with just a slight elbow bend, and raise them out to the sides in a wide arc, not backward.
- Use lighter weight than standing raises since the seated position eliminates momentum, making each rep stricter.

## Common mistakes

- Sitting too upright, which turns this into a Lateral Raise for the middle delts rather than a rear delt exercise.
- Bending the elbows too much during the raise, which shortens the lever arm and recruits the biceps.
- Shrugging the shoulders during the raise, which shifts tension to the upper traps.

## FAQ

### Why do cable rear Lateral Raises seated instead of standing?

Sitting removes lower body momentum and prevents cheating, which ensures the rear deltoids do all the work. This results in better isolation and more effective muscle activation.

### What angle should I lean forward for seated rear Lateral Raises?

Lean forward about 30-45 degrees from vertical. This positions your torso so the Lateral Raise path aligns with the rear deltoid fibers for maximum activation.

### What muscles does the Cable Seated Rear Lateral Raise work?

The Cable Seated Rear Lateral Raise primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the traps, rhomboids. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Cable Seated Rear Lateral Raise good for beginners?

The Cable Seated Rear Lateral Raise is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of shoulders strength with easier variations first.
