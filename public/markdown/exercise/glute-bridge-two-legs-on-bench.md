# Glute Bridge Two Legs on Bench

## Quick answer

A glute bridge with your heels up on a bench and your back on the floor. It works the glutes and hamstrings. Beginner legs exercise using body weight. Targets glutes, also works hamstrings, core.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Core
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your back on the floor with your heels resting on a flat bench, knees bent, arms flat at your sides with palms down.
2. Press your heels into the bench and lift your hips until your body is straight from shoulders to knees.
3. Lower your hips slowly back to the floor.

## Pro tips

- Position your upper back against the bench edge at the bottom of your shoulder blades for the best leverage.
- Drive through both heels equally and squeeze your glutes hard at the top for a full 2-second hold.
- Keep your chin slightly tucked to maintain a neutral spine throughout the movement.

## Common mistakes

- Placing the back too high on the bench, which limits range of motion and shifts work away from the glutes.
- Hyperextending the lower back at the top instead of stopping when the hips are in line with the shoulders and knees.
- Pushing through the toes instead of the heels, which shifts the load to the quads.

## FAQ

### Why use a bench for glute bridges?

The elevated position increases the range of motion compared to floor bridges, allowing a deeper hip drop and greater glute stretch at the bottom.

### How do I make this exercise harder without weights?

Slow down the tempo (3 seconds down, 2-second pause, 2 seconds up), add a resistance band above the knees, or progress to the single-leg version.

### Where should my feet be positioned?

Feet should be flat on the floor, about hip-width apart. When your hips are at the top, your shins should be roughly vertical.

### What muscles does the Glute Bridge Two Legs on Bench work?

The Glute Bridge Two Legs on Bench primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, core. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Glute Bridge Two Legs on Bench good for beginners?

Yes, the Glute Bridge Two Legs on Bench is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building glutes strength.
