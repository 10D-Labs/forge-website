# Dumbbell Supported Squat

## Quick answer

A squat holding one dumbbell at your chest, sitting back until you touch a knee-high pad behind you. It works the quads, with help from the glutes and hamstrings. Beginner legs exercise using dumbbell. Targets quads, also works glutes, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Quads
- **Secondary Muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand on the footplate of the support stand with the roller in front of your ankles and the knee-high pad right behind you.
2. Hold one dumbbell upright against your chest, both palms cupped under its top end.
3. Keep your chest up, push your hips back and bend your knees until your thighs are level with the floor and you touch the pad.
4. Push through your feet to stand back up.

## Pro tips

- Let the dumbbells hang naturally at your sides rather than holding them in front, which changes the loading pattern.
- Sit back into the squat as if sitting into a chair, keeping your weight in your heels and midfoot.
- Keep your chest up and eyes forward to maintain a neutral spine throughout the descent and ascent.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the knees shoot forward over the toes excessively, which shifts the load from the glutes and quads to the knees.
- Rounding the lower back at the bottom of the squat, which is dangerous under load.
- Not squatting deep enough; aim for thighs at least parallel to the floor for full muscle activation.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between a Dumbbell Supported Squat and a goblet squat?

In a supported squat, dumbbells hang at your sides. In a goblet squat, one dumbbell is held at chest height. The goblet squat is better for learning squat form; the supported squat allows heavier loading.

### Can Dumbbell Squats replace barbell squats?

For beginners and intermediate lifters, Dumbbell Squats are an effective alternative. However, barbell squats allow much heavier loading, which becomes necessary for advanced strength development.

### How deep should I squat with dumbbells?

Aim for at least parallel (thighs parallel to the floor). If your mobility allows, going slightly below parallel increases glute activation.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Supported Squat work?

The Dumbbell Supported Squat primarily targets the quads, with secondary activation in the glutes, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Dumbbell Supported Squat good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Supported Squat is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building quads strength.
