# Inverted Row with Feet on Bench

## Quick answer

An inverted row under a Smith machine bar with your heels up on a bench, which makes it harder than with your feet on the floor. It works the upper back, with help from the biceps and forearms. Beginner back exercise using body weight. Targets upper back, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set a Smith machine bar at about waist height and place a flat bench where your heels will rest when you lie under the bar.
2. Lie under the bar and grab it with your palms facing toward your feet, hands a little wider than your shoulders.
3. Rest your heels on the bench and hang with your arms straight and your body straight from head to heels.
4. Pull your chest up to the bar, keeping your body straight.
5. Lower slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Set a Smith machine bar at about waist height, hang under it and rest your heels on a bench so your body is straight.
- Pull your chest up to the bar, driving your elbows back and squeezing your shoulder blades together.
- Lower yourself with control until your arms are straight.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips sag toward the floor.
- Only pulling halfway up instead of bringing your chest to the bar.
- Reaching for the bar with your chin instead of pulling with your back.

## FAQ

### Why put my feet on a bench?

Raising your feet puts more of your body weight on your arms, so the row is harder than with your feet on the floor.

### Are inverted rows a good step toward Pullups?

Yes. They build the same upper back and lat muscles at a lower difficulty.

### How do I know I'm ready for Pullups?

If you can do 3 sets of 12-15 rows with your feet on a bench, you likely have the strength to start practicing Pullups.

### What muscles does the Inverted Row with Feet on Bench work?

The Inverted Row with Feet on Bench primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Inverted Row with Feet on Bench good for beginners?

Yes, the Inverted Row with Feet on Bench is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building upper back strength.
