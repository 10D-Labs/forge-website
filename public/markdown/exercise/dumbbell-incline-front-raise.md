# Dumbbell Incline Front Raise

## Quick answer

A front raise done sitting back on an incline bench, so your arms start from behind your body. It works the front of the shoulders. Intermediate shoulders exercise using dumbbell. Targets shoulders, also works trapezius, triceps.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Trapezius, Triceps
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit back on an incline bench set to about 45 degrees, feet flat. Let a dumbbell hang in each hand, palms facing back.
2. Keeping your arms straight, raise both dumbbells in front of you until your arms are about level with the floor.
3. Lower slowly until your arms hang straight down again.

## Pro tips

- Press your back firmly into the incline bench so the shoulders do the lifting, not momentum.
- Raise the dumbbells in a controlled arc, pausing briefly at the top before lowering.
- Keep a slight bend in the elbows throughout to protect the joint while keeping tension on the shoulders.

## Common mistakes

- Using too much weight and arching the back off the bench to get the dumbbells up.
- Locking the elbows hard at the top, which moves tension from the shoulders to the joints.
- Dropping the dumbbells instead of lowering them under control.

## FAQ

### How is an Incline Front Raise different from a standing front raise?

Sitting back on an incline bench starts your arms behind your body, so the front of the shoulders works through a longer range. The bench also stops you from swinging.

### How heavy should I go on Dumbbell Incline Front Raises?

Start lighter than you use for standing front raises. The longer range from the incline makes the same weight feel heavier.

### Can I do incline front raises with a shoulder injury?

The back support can help, but check with a physical therapist first. Raising your arms from behind your body may still bother some shoulder problems.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Incline Front Raise work?

The Dumbbell Incline Front Raise primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the trapezius, triceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Dumbbell Incline Front Raise good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Incline Front Raise is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of shoulders strength with easier variations first.
