# EZ Bar Standing Wide Grip Bicep Curl

## Quick answer

A standing curl with an EZ bar, hands set wider than your shoulders. It works the biceps, with help from the forearms. Beginner arms exercise using ez bar. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** EZ Bar
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand up straight with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding an EZ bar with your palms facing up and your hands wider than your shoulders.
2. Keep your elbows close to your sides and your upper arms still.
3. Curl the bar up toward your shoulders.
4. Lower the bar slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Position your hands on the wide angles of the EZ bar to target the short (inner) head of the biceps.
- Keep your elbows pinned to your sides throughout the entire curl; they should not drift forward.
- Lower the bar slowly for a 2-3 second eccentric to maximize muscle tension and growth stimulus.

## Common mistakes

- Swinging the body to generate momentum, which takes tension off the biceps and risks lower back strain.
- Allowing the elbows to move forward during the curl, which shifts the load from the biceps to the front delts.
- Using an excessively wide grip that causes wrist pain; the EZ bar angles should position the wrists comfortably.

## FAQ

### Does a wide grip on the EZ bar hit different parts of the biceps?

Yes, a wide grip emphasizes the short (inner) head of the biceps, while a narrow grip targets the long (outer) head. Using both grips provides more complete bicep development.

### Is the EZ bar easier on the wrists than a straight bar?

Yes, the angled grip of the EZ bar reduces wrist strain by placing the forearms in a more natural, semi-supinated position during curls.

### How much should I be able to EZ Bar Curl?

As a general benchmark, most intermediate lifters can curl about 30-40% of their body weight on the EZ bar for 8-10 reps with strict form.

### What muscles does the EZ Bar Standing Wide Grip Bicep Curl work?

The EZ Bar Standing Wide Grip Bicep Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the EZ Bar Standing Wide Grip Bicep Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the EZ Bar Standing Wide Grip Bicep Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building biceps strength.
