# Smith Leg Press

## Quick answer

A leg press done lying on the floor, pushing a Smith machine bar up with the soles of your feet. It works the glutes, quads and hamstrings. Beginner legs exercise using smith machine. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Smith Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set a Smith machine bar low enough to reach with your feet when you lie under it with your knees bent.
2. Lie on your back, hips under the bar, arms flat on the floor. Put the soles of your feet on the bar, hip-width apart.
3. Press the bar up until your legs are straight, then unhook it.
4. Bend your knees and lower the bar slowly until your knees come in toward your chest.
5. Press the bar back up until your legs are straight.

## Pro tips

- Position your feet shoulder-width apart on the footplate and press through the full foot.
- Don't lock your knees at the top; keep a slight bend to maintain muscle tension.
- Lower the sled until your thighs are about parallel, then press back up explosively.

## Common mistakes

- Lowering the sled too far, causing the lower back to round off the pad.
- Locking the knees at full extension, which places all the load on the joint.
- Placing feet too low on the footplate, increasing knee shear forces.

## FAQ

### Is the Smith Leg Press the same as a regular Leg Press?

The concept is similar, but a Smith machine Leg Press may have a different angle and feel. A dedicated Leg Press machine is generally preferred for safety and ergonomics.

### What foot position targets glutes more?

A higher foot placement on the platform with a wider stance shifts more emphasis to the glutes and hamstrings. A lower, narrower position is more quad-dominant.

### How much weight should I start with?

Start with just the sled to learn the movement pattern. Add weight in small increments. Most beginners can handle 1-2 plates per side as a starting working weight.

### What muscles does the Smith Leg Press work?

The Smith Leg Press primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Smith Leg Press good for beginners?

Yes, the Smith Leg Press is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building glutes strength.
