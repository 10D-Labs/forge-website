# Band Twisting Overhead Press

## Quick answer

A one-arm band press that drives up on a diagonal from a low anchor, twisting your torso as you go. It works the front of the shoulders, with help from the triceps and core. Intermediate shoulders exercise using resistance band. Targets shoulders, also works triceps, upper back.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Upper Back
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Anchor the band near the floor to one side of you. Stand with your feet wide.
2. Hold the band in the hand nearer the anchor, at your shoulder, palm facing forward.
3. Press the band up on a diagonal, turning your torso away from the anchor, until your arm is straight overhead.
4. Lower it slowly back to your shoulder.

## Pro tips

- Press the band overhead while rotating your torso in the same direction to combine shoulder and core work.
- Keep your hips facing forward and only rotate through the upper body.
- Alternate the twist direction with each press to train both sides evenly.

## Common mistakes

- Rotating the hips and feet along with the torso, which removes the core anti-rotation challenge.
- Not pressing to full lockout overhead, which reduces shoulder engagement.
- Using too thick a band, which causes form breakdown on the pressing and twisting components.

## FAQ

### What is the benefit of adding a twist to an overhead press?

The twist recruits the obliques and core rotators, turning a pure shoulder exercise into a full-body functional movement that mimics real-world pressing patterns.

### Is this exercise suitable for people with shoulder issues?

The twist component adds complexity. If you have shoulder problems, master the standard Band Shoulder Press first and consult a professional before adding rotation.

### How many reps should you do?

8-12 reps per side for 2-3 sets. Each press-and-twist counts as one rep on that side.

### What muscles does the Band Twisting Overhead Press work?

The Band Twisting Overhead Press primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the triceps, upper back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Band Twisting Overhead Press good for beginners?

The Band Twisting Overhead Press is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of shoulders strength with easier variations first.
