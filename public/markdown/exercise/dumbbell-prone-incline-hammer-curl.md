# Dumbbell Prone Incline Hammer Curl

## Quick answer

A curl done lying chest down on an incline bench, with your palms facing each other. Lying on the bench cuts out swinging, so the biceps and forearms do the work. Intermediate arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set an incline bench to 45 degrees and lie face down on it with a dumbbell in each hand.
2. Let your arms hang straight down toward the floor, elbows slightly bent, palms facing each other (thumbs up).
3. Keep your upper arms still and curl the dumbbells up to your shoulders.
4. Lower them slowly until your arms hang straight down again.

## Pro tips

- Lie chest-down on a 45-degree incline bench and let your arms hang straight down for a full stretch before curling.
- The prone position eliminates all body swing, so focus purely on squeezing the brachialis at the top.
- Keep your elbows pointed at the floor and stationary; only your forearms should move.

## Common mistakes

- Lifting the chest off the bench during the curl, which introduces momentum and reduces isolation.
- Swinging the elbows back to help lift the weight instead of keeping them fixed.
- Using too much weight since the prone position removes all cheating ability; start lighter than you think.

## FAQ

### Why do hammer curls face-down on an incline?

The prone position eliminates all body English and momentum. With gravity pulling straight down and your chest braced against the bench, every rep is pure brachialis and bicep contraction.

### What angle should the bench be?

A 45-degree incline works best. Steeper angles make it harder to maintain contact with the bench; shallower angles reduce the gravitational challenge.

### How does this compare to regular hammer curls?

You'll use significantly less weight because you can't cheat at all. The trade-off is much higher quality muscle activation and better brachialis development.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Prone Incline Hammer Curl work?

The Dumbbell Prone Incline Hammer Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Prone Incline Hammer Curl good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Prone Incline Hammer Curl is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of biceps strength with easier variations first.
