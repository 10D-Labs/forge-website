# Barbell Incline Close Grip Bench Press

## Quick answer

An incline bench press with your hands closer than shoulder-width. It works the triceps, with help from the upper chest and shoulders. Intermediate arms exercise using barbell. Targets triceps, also works chest, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Chest, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set an incline bench to 45 degrees and lie back on it with your feet flat on the floor.
2. Grip the bar with your hands slightly closer than shoulder-width and lift it off the rack.
3. Lower the bar slowly until it touches your chest, keeping your elbows close to your body.
4. Press the bar back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Set the bench to 30-45 degrees and use a grip slightly narrower than shoulder width.
- Tuck your elbows at about 30 degrees from your torso as you lower the bar to your upper chest.
- Press the bar up and slightly back toward the rack, locking out your elbows fully at the top.

## Common mistakes

- Gripping too narrow, which overloads the wrists and can cause them to buckle under heavy weight.
- Flaring the elbows wide, which shifts work from the triceps to the chest and front deltoids.
- Bouncing the bar off the chest instead of pausing and pressing with control.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the incline close grip bench press target?

It primarily targets the triceps, with significant upper chest and front deltoid involvement. The incline angle increases upper chest work compared to flat close grip pressing.

### How close should my grip be?

Hands about shoulder-width apart or slightly narrower. Going closer than that puts excessive stress on the wrists with minimal additional tricep benefit.

### Can I substitute this for dips?

Yes, the incline close grip bench press is an excellent alternative for people who find dips hard on their shoulders. It hits the same muscles with more controlled mechanics.

### What muscles does the Barbell Incline Close Grip Bench Press work?

The Barbell Incline Close Grip Bench Press primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the chest, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Barbell Incline Close Grip Bench Press good for beginners?

The Barbell Incline Close Grip Bench Press is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of triceps strength with easier variations first.
