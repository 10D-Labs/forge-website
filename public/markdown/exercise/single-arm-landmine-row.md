# Single Arm Landmine Row

## Quick answer

A one-arm row where you pull up the loaded end of a barbell while the other end stays on the floor. It works the upper back, with help from the biceps and forearms. Intermediate back exercise using barbell. Targets upper back, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Anchor one end of a barbell in a landmine or corner and load the other end. Stand beside the loaded end, facing away from the anchor.
2. Push your hips back, bend your knees and lean forward until your chest is angled toward the floor, back flat.
3. Grab the bar just behind the plates with one hand, palm facing your body, arm straight.
4. Pull the bar up toward your lower chest, driving your elbow back.
5. Lower it slowly until your arm is straight. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Stagger your stance with the opposite foot forward for stability, and brace your non-working hand on your knee or a bench.
- Pull the barbell (or the end of the barbell in a landmine setup) toward your hip, not your chest, for maximum lat engagement.
- Keep your torso at roughly 45 degrees throughout and resist rotating toward the working arm.

## Common mistakes

- Rotating the torso to heave the weight up, which uses momentum instead of back strength.
- Standing too upright, which reduces the horizontal pull angle and turns it into a shrug.
- Using the bicep to curl the weight rather than driving the elbow back with the lat and rhomboid.

## FAQ

### Why use a barbell for single arm rows instead of a dumbbell?

A barbell or landmine setup allows for heavier loading and a slightly different wrist position. The thicker grip also challenges forearm strength more than a dumbbell handle.

### How many sets and reps are ideal?

3-4 sets of 8-12 reps per side works well for hypertrophy. For strength, use heavier weight for 5-6 reps per side. Always train both sides equally.

### What muscles does the Single Arm Landmine Row work?

The Single Arm Landmine Row primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Single Arm Landmine Row good for beginners?

The Single Arm Landmine Row is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of upper back strength with easier variations first.
