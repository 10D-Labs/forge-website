# Dumbbell Fly

## Quick answer

A chest exercise lying on a flat bench, opening your arms wide with a dumbbell in each hand and bringing them back together. It works the chest. Intermediate chest exercise using dumbbell. Targets chest, also works shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie flat on a bench with a dumbbell in each hand, palms facing each other.
2. Hold the dumbbells straight up over your chest with a slight bend in your elbows.
3. Keep that bend and lower your arms out to the sides in a wide arc until you feel a stretch in your chest.
4. Bring the dumbbells back up over your chest along the same arc.

## Pro tips

- Lie flat on a bench with dumbbells pressed up, palms facing each other. Lower in a wide arc with a slight, fixed elbow bend until you feel a deep stretch across your chest.
- Think about hugging a large tree as you bring the dumbbells back together. The movement should be a sweeping arc, not a press.
- Do not let the dumbbells touch at the top. Stop when they are 2-3 inches apart to maintain chest tension.

## Common mistakes

- Bending the elbows too much and turning the fly into a press. The elbows should have a slight bend that stays constant.
- Going too heavy, which forces a pressing motion and reduces the chest isolation benefit.
- Not going deep enough to stretch the chest. The stretch at the bottom is where the most muscle fiber recruitment occurs.

## FAQ

### Are Dumbbell Flyes good for chest growth?

Yes, flyes isolate the chest by removing tricep involvement and provide a deep stretch under load, which is a strong stimulus for muscle growth. They complement pressing movements for complete chest development.

### Should I do Dumbbell Flyes before or after bench press?

Most people do flyes after pressing to isolate and finish the chest when the triceps are fatigued. Some use light flyes before pressing to pre-activate the chest. After pressing is more common and effective.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Fly work?

The Dumbbell Fly primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Dumbbell Fly good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Fly is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of chest strength with easier variations first.
