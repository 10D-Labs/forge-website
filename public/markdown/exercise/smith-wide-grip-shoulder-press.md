# Smith Wide Grip Shoulder Press

## Quick answer

A seated shoulder press in a Smith machine, with your hands set wider than your shoulders. It works the front of the shoulders. Beginner shoulders exercise using smith machine. Targets shoulders, also works triceps, upper back.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Smith Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Upper Back
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set the seat in the Smith machine and sit with your back against the pad, feet flat on the floor and shoulder-width apart.
2. Grab the bar with your palms facing forward, hands a little wider than shoulder-width apart.
3. Unhook the bar and hold it at shoulder level with your elbows bent.
4. Press the bar up until your arms are straight overhead.
5. Lower it slowly back to shoulder level.

## Pro tips

- Set a bench under the Smith machine so the bar lowers to about chin height, and sit tall with your back against the pad.
- Grip the bar wider than your shoulders and press straight up.
- Lower the bar to just below chin height for a full range of motion.

## Common mistakes

- Arching the lower back off the pad to push heavier weight.
- Setting the bench so far forward or back that the bar path hits your face or drifts away from your shoulders.
- Gripping so wide that your shoulders feel pinched at the bottom.

## FAQ

### What does the wide grip change?

A wider grip shortens the range of motion and brings in more of the side of the shoulders. If it bothers your shoulders, bring your hands in a little.

### Is the Smith machine shoulder press effective?

Yes, for building pressing strength. The fixed path reduces balance demands, so pair it with dumbbell or barbell presses for complete shoulder work.

### How much weight should I use compared to a barbell overhead press?

Most people can lift a bit more on the Smith machine because the bar path is fixed. Start near your barbell press weight and adjust from there.

### What muscles does the Smith Wide Grip Shoulder Press work?

The Smith Wide Grip Shoulder Press primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the triceps, upper back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Smith Wide Grip Shoulder Press good for beginners?

Yes, the Smith Wide Grip Shoulder Press is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building shoulders strength.
