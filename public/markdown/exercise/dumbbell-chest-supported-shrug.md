# Dumbbell Chest Supported Shrug

## Quick answer

A shrug done lying chest down on an incline bench, which stops your body from swinging. It works the traps, the muscles between your neck and shoulders. Beginner back exercise using dumbbell. Targets traps, also works shoulders, rhomboids.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Traps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Rhomboids
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set an incline bench to about 45 degrees. Lie chest down on it with your head over the top and your feet on the floor.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand with your arms hanging straight down, palms facing each other.
3. Keeping your arms straight, raise your shoulders toward your ears as high as you can.
4. Lower your shoulders slowly back down.

## Pro tips

- Lie chest down on a bench set to about 45 degrees with the dumbbells hanging straight down, then shrug your shoulders up toward your ears.
- Let the bench do the bracing: with your chest supported, the traps have to do all the lifting.
- Hold the top for 2-3 seconds and think about pulling your shoulder blades up and slightly together.

## Common mistakes

- Using too much weight and barely moving the shoulders. Lying on the bench makes the shrug harder, so use less weight than on standing shrugs.
- Bending the elbows during the shrug, which turns it into a partial row.
- Lifting your chest off the pad to help the weight up instead of moving only the shoulders.

## FAQ

### Why do shrugs chest down on a bench?

The bench supports your chest and stops your body from swinging, so the traps do all the work. Leaning forward also lets the middle traps help more than in a standing shrug.

### What angle should the bench be for Chest Supported Shrugs?

About 30-45 degrees, lying face down with your chest on the pad and your arms hanging straight down.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Chest Supported Shrug work?

The Dumbbell Chest Supported Shrug primarily targets the traps, with secondary activation in the shoulders, rhomboids. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Dumbbell Chest Supported Shrug good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Chest Supported Shrug is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building traps strength.
