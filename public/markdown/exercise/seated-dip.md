# Seated Dip

## Quick answer

A machine version of the dip: you stay seated and press handles down at your sides. It works the triceps, with help from the chest and shoulders. Beginner arms exercise using machine. Targets triceps, also works chest, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Chest, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set the seat so your feet are flat on the floor and your knees are bent at about 90 degrees.
2. Grab the handles at your sides with your palms facing down and your elbows bent.
3. Press the handles down until your arms are straight. Stay seated; only the handles move.
4. Let the handles rise slowly back to the start.

## Pro tips

- Keep your back close to the pad and avoid leaning forward to keep tension on the triceps rather than the chest.
- Lower until your upper arms are parallel to the floor but not deeper, as excessive depth shifts stress to the shoulder joint.
- Squeeze your triceps hard at the top of each rep and hold for a one-count before lowering again.

## Common mistakes

- Using momentum by bouncing out of the bottom position instead of pressing with controlled force.
- Setting the seat too low, which forces the shoulders into an impinged position at the bottom of the rep.
- Flaring the elbows out wide, which turns this into more of a chest exercise and reduces tricep activation.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the Seated Dip machine work?

The Seated Dip machine primarily targets the triceps, with secondary work on the chest and front deltoids. Keeping your elbows tucked emphasizes the triceps more.

### How is the Seated Dip different from bodyweight dips?

The machine version lets you control the exact load, making it easier for beginners and allowing advanced lifters to isolate the triceps without stabilizer fatigue limiting their sets.

### How much weight should I use on the Seated Dip machine?

Start with a weight you can control for 10-12 reps with good form. Most beginners start around 40-60% of their body weight and progress from there.

### What muscles does the Seated Dip work?

The Seated Dip primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the chest, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Seated Dip good for beginners?

Yes, the Seated Dip is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building triceps strength.
