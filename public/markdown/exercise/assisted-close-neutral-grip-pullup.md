# Assisted Close Neutral Grip Pullup

## Quick answer

A pullup on an assisted machine, kneeling on a pad that takes some of your weight, with your hands close and palms facing each other. It works the lats and biceps. Beginner back exercise using machine. Targets lats, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set the assistance weight on an assisted pullup machine; more weight makes it easier. Kneel on the pad.
2. Grab the close parallel handles with your palms facing each other and start with your arms straight.
3. Pull yourself up until your chin is above your hands.
4. Lower slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Use a neutral (palms facing each other) grip to reduce wrist and shoulder strain while working the lats and biceps.
- Pull your elbows down toward your hips rather than thinking about pulling yourself up; this cue activates the lats more effectively.
- Control the descent for at least 2-3 seconds to build strength through the full range of motion.

## Common mistakes

- Bouncing off the knee pad at the bottom instead of starting each rep from a controlled hang.
- Only pulling halfway up instead of getting the chin clearly above the handles.
- Letting the body drop quickly on the way down instead of controlling the lowering phase.

## FAQ

### Are close grip Pullups easier than Wide Grip Pullups?

Yes, for most people. The close neutral grip lets the biceps help more and puts the lats in a stronger position, making it easier to complete reps than with a wide overhand grip.

### What muscles do Assisted Close Neutral Grip Pullups target?

Primarily the lats, with heavy involvement from the biceps, brachialis and forearms. The close neutral grip also works the lower traps and rhomboids.

### How do I progress to unassisted close grip Pullups?

Reduce the counterweight on the assisted machine a little at a time over weeks. Add negative Pullups (slow descents) and Inverted Rows to build pulling strength.

### What muscles does the Assisted Close Neutral Grip Pullup work?

The Assisted Close Neutral Grip Pullup primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Assisted Close Neutral Grip Pullup good for beginners?

Yes, the Assisted Close Neutral Grip Pullup is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building lats strength.
