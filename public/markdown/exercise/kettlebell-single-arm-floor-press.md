# Kettlebell Single Arm Floor Press

## Quick answer

A one-arm press done lying on the floor, pushing a kettlebell straight up over your chest. It works the middle of the chest, plus the triceps and shoulders. Intermediate chest exercise using kettlebell. Targets chest, also works triceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your back on the floor with your legs straight.
2. Hold a kettlebell in one hand, arm straight up over your chest, palm facing toward your feet.
3. Bend your elbow to lower the kettlebell until it's just above your chest, keeping your upper arm close to your body.
4. Push it back up until your arm is straight. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Let your elbow touch the floor gently at the bottom of each rep to establish a consistent range of motion.
- Keep the kettlebell handle diagonal across your palm so the bell rests on the back of your forearm, reducing wrist strain.
- Bend the non-working side knee and plant that foot flat to stabilize your hips during the press.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the elbow flare out to 90 degrees instead of keeping it at about 45 degrees from the torso.
- Bouncing the elbow off the floor to generate momentum rather than pressing from a dead stop.
- Gripping the handle too tightly with the wrist cocked back, which fatigues the forearm prematurely.

## FAQ

### What is the advantage of a floor press over a bench press?

The floor limits your range of motion, reducing shoulder stress at the bottom. It also eliminates leg drive, making it a purer upper body pressing exercise.

### Why use a kettlebell instead of a dumbbell for floor presses?

The kettlebell's offset center of mass challenges your stabilizers more and the bell resting on your forearm provides a proprioceptive cue to keep your wrist neutral.

### Is this exercise safe for people with shoulder problems?

Generally yes. The reduced range of motion means the shoulder never enters the vulnerable end range that aggravates many shoulder issues.

### What muscles does the Kettlebell Single Arm Floor Press work?

The Kettlebell Single Arm Floor Press primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Kettlebell Single Arm Floor Press good for beginners?

The Kettlebell Single Arm Floor Press is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of chest strength with easier variations first.
