# Band Close Grip Pushup

## Quick answer

A pushup with your hands under your shoulders and a band across your upper back, pinned under each hand for extra resistance. It works the triceps, with the chest and shoulders helping. Intermediate arms exercise using resistance band. Targets triceps, also works chest, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Chest, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lay a band across your upper back and hold one end flat on the floor under each hand.
2. Get into a pushup position with your hands under your shoulders, palms flat, and your body straight from head to heels.
3. Bend your elbows to lower your chest almost to the floor, keeping your elbows close to your sides.
4. Push back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Place your hands directly under your shoulders or even closer together to maximize tricep activation.
- Keep the band taut across your upper back to add resistance at the top of the Pushup where you are strongest.
- Lower your chest all the way to the floor and press up explosively against the band resistance.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the band slip off the upper back mid-set, which removes the added resistance.
- Flaring the elbows out wide, which shifts the emphasis from triceps to chest.
- Sagging the hips, which reduces core engagement and puts stress on the lower back.

## FAQ

### Where should the band sit on your back for banded Pushups?

Across the upper back, just below the shoulder blades. Loop each end around your thumbs or palms to secure it in place.

### What resistance band should you use for Close Grip Pushups?

Start with a light band. Even a thin band adds significant resistance at the top of the Pushup. Progress to thicker bands as you get stronger.

### Are Close Grip Pushups better for triceps than regular Pushups?

Yes. The narrow hand position forces the triceps to do more of the pressing work. The closer your hands, the more tricep-dominant the movement becomes.

### What muscles does the Band Close Grip Pushup work?

The Band Close Grip Pushup primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the chest, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Band Close Grip Pushup good for beginners?

The Band Close Grip Pushup is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of triceps strength with easier variations first.
