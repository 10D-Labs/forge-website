# Smith Incline Reverse Grip Press

## Quick answer

An incline press on a Smith machine with your palms facing toward you instead of away. It works the upper chest, with help from the triceps and shoulders. Intermediate chest exercise using smith machine. Targets chest, also works triceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Smith Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set an incline bench to about 45 degrees in a Smith machine, with the bar over your upper chest. Lie back, feet flat on the floor.
2. Grip the bar about shoulder-width apart with your palms facing toward your face, then unhook it.
3. Lower the bar to your upper chest, keeping your elbows close to your sides.
4. Press it back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Use a shoulder-width underhand grip and lower the bar to your upper chest area.
- Keep your wrists straight and your elbows close to your body throughout the movement.
- Start with 50-60% of your standard incline press weight to account for the unfamiliar grip.

## Common mistakes

- Using too wide a grip, which makes the wrist position uncomfortable and potentially dangerous.
- Not properly securing the bar in the safety hooks before unracking with the reverse grip.
- Letting the elbows flare out wide, which negates the benefit of the reverse grip.

## FAQ

### What does the reverse grip add to the incline press?

The supinated grip significantly increases upper chest fiber activation compared to a standard overhand grip. EMG studies show up to 30% more upper pec activation with a reverse grip.

### Is this exercise hard on the wrists?

It can be if your wrist mobility is limited. Warm up thoroughly and start light. The Smith machine is much safer for this variation than a free barbell since the fixed path prevents the bar from rolling.

### Can I use this as my main chest exercise?

It works best as an accessory movement for upper chest development. Use it after your primary pressing exercise, not as a replacement for it.

### What muscles does the Smith Incline Reverse Grip Press work?

The Smith Incline Reverse Grip Press primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Smith Incline Reverse Grip Press good for beginners?

The Smith Incline Reverse Grip Press is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of chest strength with easier variations first.
