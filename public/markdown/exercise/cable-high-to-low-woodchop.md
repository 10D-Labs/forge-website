# Cable High to Low Woodchop

## Quick answer

A cable chop from high to low, pulling a handle diagonally down across your body as you turn. It works the abs and obliques. Intermediate core exercise using cable. Targets abs, also works obliques, lower back.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Obliques, Lower Back
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set a cable pulley at its highest point with a single handle. Stand side-on to the machine, feet wider than your shoulders.
2. Reach up and hold the handle with both hands, one wrapped over the other, arms straight toward the pulley.
3. Keeping your arms straight, pull the handle down and across your body to outside your far hip, turning your shoulders and bending your knees.
4. Let the handle rise slowly back to the start. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Set the pulley high, stand side-on to the machine and hold the handle with both hands above your shoulder.
- Pull the handle down and across your body toward the opposite hip, turning your torso as you go.
- Let your back foot pivot so your hips can follow, and control the return to the top.

## Common mistakes

- Pulling with the arms instead of turning through the torso.
- Using too much weight to control the turn.
- Letting the handle snap back to the top instead of returning it slowly.

## FAQ

### What does the Cable High to Low Woodchop work?

The obliques and the rest of the abs, through a turning, chopping movement.

### Is there a low to high version?

Yes. The low to high woodchop starts with the pulley low and lifts the handle up across your body. Many programs use both.

### How should I program woodchops?

10-15 reps per side for 2-3 sets. Keep the weight moderate and focus on a smooth, controlled turn.

### What muscles does the Cable High to Low Woodchop work?

The Cable High to Low Woodchop primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the obliques, lower back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Cable High to Low Woodchop good for beginners?

The Cable High to Low Woodchop is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of abs strength with easier variations first.
