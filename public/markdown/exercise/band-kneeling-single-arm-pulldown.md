# Band Kneeling Single Arm Pulldown

## Quick answer

A one-arm pulldown done kneeling on one knee, pulling a band from a high anchor down to your side. It works the lats, with help from the biceps and upper back. Beginner back exercise using resistance band. Targets lats, also works biceps, rhomboids.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Rhomboids
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Attach a resistance band to a sturdy anchor above head height.
2. Kneel on one knee facing the anchor and hold the band in one hand, palm facing down.
3. Reach that arm up toward the anchor, keeping your elbow slightly bent.
4. Pull the band down to your side, bringing your elbow in toward your ribs.
5. Let your arm rise slowly back up. Do all your reps on one arm, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Kneel on one or both knees and pull the band from overhead down to your side, driving the elbow toward your hip.
- Initiate the pull by depressing the shoulder blade on the working side before bending the elbow.
- Squeeze the lat at the bottom for a one-second hold before releasing the band back up.

## Common mistakes

- Pulling with the bicep instead of starting the movement with the lat by depressing the shoulder blade.
- Leaning away from the anchor point, which turns it into a row instead of a pulldown.
- Using too thick a band, which causes form breakdown and reduces mind-muscle connection with the lat.

## FAQ

### Why do this exercise single arm?

Single arm work allows you to focus on each lat individually, correct muscle imbalances, and achieve a greater range of motion compared to bilateral pulldowns.

### What is the advantage of kneeling during pulldowns?

Kneeling removes the ability to use leg drive or body momentum, forcing the lat to do all the work. It also reduces lower back involvement.

### How do you adjust resistance on a band pulldown?

Choke up on the band (grip it shorter) to increase tension, or use a thicker band. Move closer to the anchor point to decrease tension.

### What muscles does the Band Kneeling Single Arm Pulldown work?

The Band Kneeling Single Arm Pulldown primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the biceps, rhomboids. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Band Kneeling Single Arm Pulldown good for beginners?

Yes, the Band Kneeling Single Arm Pulldown is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building lats strength.
