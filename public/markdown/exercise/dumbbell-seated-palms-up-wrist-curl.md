# Dumbbell Seated Palms Up Wrist Curl

## Quick answer

A seated wrist curl with a dumbbell in each hand, palms facing up and forearms resting on your thighs. It works the forearm muscles that bend your wrist. Beginner arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets forearms, also works biceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Forearms
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on a bench with your feet flat on the floor, holding a dumbbell in each hand with your palms facing up.
2. Rest your forearms on your thighs so your wrists hang just past your knees.
3. Curl your wrists up as far as you can.
4. Lower your wrists slowly back down.

## Pro tips

- Rest your forearms fully on your thighs with palms facing up and wrists hanging off the knees for full wrist range of motion.
- Let the dumbbells roll to your fingertips at the bottom, then curl wrists and fingers back up for maximum forearm flexor activation.
- Squeeze the forearms at the top of each curl and hold briefly before lowering.

## Common mistakes

- Using partial range of motion by not letting the wrists fully extend and the dumbbells roll to the fingertips.
- Going too heavy and jerking the wrists upward, which strains the tendons.
- Lifting the forearms off the thighs during the curl, adding bicep engagement.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between seated wrist curls and over-bench wrist curls?

The movement is identical. The thigh version is more convenient and requires no bench, while the bench version provides a firmer surface and slightly more wrist range of motion.

### Will wrist curls help my Deadlift grip?

Yes. Wrist curls strengthen the forearm flexors that are the primary muscles responsible for grip strength during pulling movements.

### Should I do wrist curls at the beginning or end of my workout?

End. Fatiguing your forearms early will compromise your grip on every other exercise in the session.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Seated Palms Up Wrist Curl work?

The Dumbbell Seated Palms Up Wrist Curl primarily targets the forearms, with secondary activation in the biceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Seated Palms Up Wrist Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Seated Palms Up Wrist Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building forearms strength.
