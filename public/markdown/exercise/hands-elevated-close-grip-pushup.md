# Hands Elevated Close Grip Pushup

## Quick answer

A pushup with your hands close together on a bar at hip height. The close hands put more of the work on the triceps, with help from the chest and shoulders. Beginner arms exercise using body weight. Targets triceps, also works chest, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Chest, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set a Smith machine bar at about hip height and leave it locked. Grip it with your hands closer than shoulder-width, palms facing down.
2. Walk your feet back until your body is straight from head to heels and your arms are straight.
3. Bend your elbows, keeping them close to your sides, and lower your chest to the bar.
4. Push back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Place your hands close together on a bar at about hip height, directly under your shoulders or slightly narrower.
- Keep your elbows tucked tight to your sides throughout the push to keep the work on the triceps.
- Lower your chest to the bar with control, then press back up to straight arms.

## Common mistakes

- Flaring the elbows out wide, which shifts the work from the triceps to the chest and shoulders.
- Letting the hips sag, which stresses the lower back.
- Placing the hands too wide apart, turning this into a regular Hands Elevated Pushup.

## FAQ

### What muscles does a Hands Elevated Close Grip Pushup target?

Mainly the triceps, with help from the chest, the front of the shoulders and the core. The close hands make the triceps do most of the pressing.

### How do I make Hands Elevated Close Grip Pushups harder?

Lower the bar a little at a time until you can do them on the floor. You can also slow the lowering to 3 seconds or pause with your chest at the bar.

### What muscles does the Hands Elevated Close Grip Pushup work?

The Hands Elevated Close Grip Pushup primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the chest, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Hands Elevated Close Grip Pushup good for beginners?

Yes, the Hands Elevated Close Grip Pushup is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building triceps strength.
