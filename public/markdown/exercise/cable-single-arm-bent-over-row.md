# Cable Single Arm Bent Over Row

## Quick answer

A one-arm cable row from a low pulley, leaning forward with one foot in front of the other. It works the upper back. Intermediate back exercise using cable. Targets upper back, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Attach a handle to a low cable pulley and stand facing it with one foot in front of the other.
2. Bend your knees slightly and lean forward from your hips, keeping your back straight.
3. Grab the handle with one hand, palm facing in toward your body, arm straight.
4. Pull the handle toward your hip, keeping your elbow close to your side.
5. Straighten your arm slowly. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Place your non-working hand on your knee or a bench for support and keep your torso parallel to the ground throughout.
- Pull the handle toward your lower chest, keeping your elbow tight to your ribs, and rotate your torso slightly at the top for extra range of motion.
- Let your shoulder blade protract fully at the bottom of each rep to get a complete lat stretch before retracting on the pull.

## Common mistakes

- Rotating the torso excessively to heave the weight up rather than using a controlled pull from the back muscles.
- Standing too upright, which turns this into a cable row rather than a Bent Over Row and reduces lat engagement.
- Pulling to the hip instead of the lower chest, which limits upper back and rhomboid involvement.

## FAQ

### Why do single arm rows instead of two-arm rows?

Single arm rows let you focus on one side at a time, correct muscle imbalances, and achieve a greater range of motion since there is no barbell or bar limiting the pull.

### Should I use a cable or dumbbell for Single Arm Bent Over Rows?

Cables provide constant tension and a different resistance curve. Dumbbells allow for heavier loads. Both are effective and can be alternated in your program.

### What muscles does the Cable Single Arm Bent Over Row work?

The Cable Single Arm Bent Over Row primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Cable Single Arm Bent Over Row good for beginners?

The Cable Single Arm Bent Over Row is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of upper back strength with easier variations first.
