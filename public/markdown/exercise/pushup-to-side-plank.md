# Pushup to Side Plank

## Quick answer

A pushup followed by a turn into a side plank with your top arm reaching up. It works the abs, plus the chest, shoulders and triceps, and tests your balance. Intermediate core exercise using body weight. Targets abs, also works shoulders, chest, triceps.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Chest, Triceps
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Start at the top of a pushup with your hands shoulder-width apart and your body in a straight line.
2. Bend your elbows to lower your body toward the floor, then push back up.
3. Shift your weight onto your left hand and turn your body to the right, reaching your right arm up to the ceiling.
4. Hold the side plank for a few seconds, then come back to the pushup position.
5. Do another pushup and turn to the other side. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Rotate your entire body as one unit when transitioning to the side plank; don't just lift your arm.
- Stack your feet or stagger them for the side plank and fully extend the top arm toward the ceiling.
- Maintain a tight core throughout the transition; the moment between Pushup and plank is where form breaks down.

## Common mistakes

- Rushing the side plank rotation and not holding it long enough to get the anti-rotation core benefit.
- Letting the hips drop during the side plank phase instead of keeping the body in a straight line.
- Placing the support hand too far forward during the side plank, which makes the position unstable.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the Pushup to Side Plank work?

The Pushup targets chest, triceps, and shoulders. The side plank adds obliques, glute medius, and shoulder stabilizers. It's one of the most complete bodyweight core and upper body exercises.

### How many reps should I do of Pushup to Side Plank?

Start with 5-6 per side (10-12 total). The alternating rotation is demanding on the core, so quality matters more than quantity.

### Can I do this exercise as a beginner?

If you can do 10+ standard Pushups and hold a side plank for 20+ seconds, you're ready. If not, master those two movements individually first.

### What muscles does the Pushup to Side Plank work?

The Pushup to Side Plank primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the shoulders, chest, triceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Pushup to Side Plank good for beginners?

The Pushup to Side Plank is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of abs strength with easier variations first.
