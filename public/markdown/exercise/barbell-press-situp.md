# Barbell Press Situp

## Quick answer

A situp on a decline bench where you press a barbell from your chest to straight overhead as you sit up. It works the abs, with the shoulders doing the press. Advanced core exercise using barbell. Targets abs, also works shoulders, chest.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Chest
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie back on a decline bench with your feet hooked under the pads at the high end.
2. Hold a barbell on your chest, hands shoulder-width apart, palms facing your feet.
3. Sit all the way up and press the bar overhead as you rise, until you're upright with your arms straight above your head.
4. Lower slowly back down, bringing the bar back to your chest as you lie back.

## Pro tips

- Start with the bar on your chest, perform the situp first, then press the bar overhead at the top of the situp.
- Anchor your feet securely under something heavy or have a partner hold them down.
- Use a light bar and focus on coordinating the situp and press as one fluid movement.

## Common mistakes

- Pressing the bar up before completing the situp, which makes balancing nearly impossible.
- Using too much weight and failing to complete the situp portion, compromising the core training benefit.
- Arching the lower back excessively at the top of the movement during the press.

## FAQ

### What muscles do Barbell Press Situps work?

They combine a situp (abs and hip flexors) with an overhead press (shoulders, triceps), making it a compound core and upper body exercise. The abs work hardest to stabilize during the press at the top.

### Is this exercise safe for beginners?

It's an advanced exercise. Beginners should master both regular situps and overhead pressing separately before combining them. Start with just a light bar or even a broomstick.

### Can I substitute this for regular situps and overhead press?

It's better used as a finisher or a conditioning exercise than a replacement for dedicated situp or pressing work, since the loads will be limited by whichever movement is weaker.

### What muscles does the Barbell Press Situp work?

The Barbell Press Situp primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the shoulders, chest. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Barbell Press Situp good for beginners?

The Barbell Press Situp is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental core movements first.
