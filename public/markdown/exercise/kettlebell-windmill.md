# Kettlebell Windmill

## Quick answer

A side bend with a kettlebell held straight overhead while your free hand slides down your leg toward the floor. It works the abs and obliques, and your shoulder works to hold the weight steady. Advanced core exercise using kettlebell. Targets abs, also works obliques, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Obliques, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet wider than shoulder-width apart, holding a kettlebell straight overhead in your right hand.
2. Turn your left foot out about 45 degrees and keep your right foot pointing forward.
3. Keep your right arm straight and your eyes on the kettlebell as you bend your upper body to the left.
4. Slide your left hand down your left leg toward the floor, going as low as you can.
5. Push through your right foot to stand back up. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Turn both feet about 45 degrees away from the overhead arm and push the hip on the kettlebell side outward as you hinge.
- Keep your eyes locked on the kettlebell overhead throughout the entire movement for shoulder stability.
- Reach your free hand down your inner leg toward the floor, loading the stretch on your hamstrings and obliques.

## Common mistakes

- Looking at the ground instead of keeping eyes on the overhead kettlebell, which destabilizes the shoulder.
- Bending forward (flexion) instead of hinging laterally (lateral flexion), which turns it into a Deadlift.
- Letting the overhead arm bend or the kettlebell drift behind or in front of the shoulder line.

## FAQ

### What does the Kettlebell Windmill stretch and strengthen?

It stretches the hamstrings and hip adductors while strengthening the obliques, shoulder stabilizers, and thoracic spine mobility. It's one of the most complete single-exercise mobility and stability drills.

### How low should I go in the windmill?

Go as low as your hamstring flexibility allows while keeping your eyes on the kettlebell and maintaining a straight overhead arm. For most people, touching the opposite ankle or shin is a good target.

### What muscles does the Kettlebell Windmill work?

The Kettlebell Windmill primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the obliques, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Kettlebell Windmill good for beginners?

The Kettlebell Windmill is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental core movements first.
