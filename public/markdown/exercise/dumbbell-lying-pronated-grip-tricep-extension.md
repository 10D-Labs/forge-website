# Dumbbell Lying Pronated Grip Tricep Extension

## Quick answer

A lying triceps extension with a dumbbell in each hand and your palms facing toward your feet. It works the triceps on the back of the upper arm. Intermediate arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets triceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your back on a flat bench with your feet flat on the floor.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand with straight arms above your forehead, palms facing toward your feet.
3. Keep your upper arms still, elbows pointing up, and bend your elbows to lower the dumbbells back beside your head.
4. Straighten your elbows slowly to raise the dumbbells back above your forehead.

## Pro tips

- Keep your palms facing toward your feet throughout the extension.
- Lie on a bench and lower the dumbbells toward your forehead by bending only at the elbows, keeping your upper arms still.
- Squeeze the triceps fully at the top before starting each rep's lowering phase.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the elbows flare out to the sides, which reduces triceps work and can stress the shoulders.
- Lifting your hips or arching your back to move a weight that is too heavy.
- Using momentum to swing the dumbbells up rather than extending strictly from the elbows.

## FAQ

### How does the palms-down grip change the Tricep Extension?

Many lifters feel the palms-down grip more in the outer part of the triceps. It also keeps the dumbbells in front of your forehead rather than beside your head.

### Can I do this with a barbell instead?

Yes. A barbell or EZ bar version is the classic skull crusher. Dumbbells let each arm work on its own.

### How heavy should I go on palms-down extensions?

Start with a moderate weight you can control for 10-15 reps. The grip feels awkward to many people at first, so put form ahead of weight.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Lying Pronated Grip Tricep Extension work?

The Dumbbell Lying Pronated Grip Tricep Extension primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Lying Pronated Grip Tricep Extension good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Lying Pronated Grip Tricep Extension is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of triceps strength with easier variations first.
