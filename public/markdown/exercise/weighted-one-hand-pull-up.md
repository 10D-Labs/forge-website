# Weighted One Hand Pull Up

## Quick answer

An advanced one-arm pullup done holding a dumbbell in your free hand for extra weight. It works the lats, the large muscles down the sides of your back, plus the biceps and forearms. Advanced back exercise using dumbbell. Targets lats, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Hold a dumbbell in one hand and grab a pullup bar with the other, palm facing away from you.
2. Hang with your arm straight and the dumbbell hanging at your side.
3. Bend your elbow to pull yourself up until your chin is above the bar.
4. Lower yourself slowly until your arm is straight again.

## Pro tips

- Train unweighted one-arm Pullups for at least 3 solid reps before adding any external load.
- Grip the bar with your thumb wrapped around it and squeeze as hard as possible to recruit more lat fibers.
- Initiate the pull by depressing your shoulder blade first, then drive your elbow down toward your hip.

## Common mistakes

- Using excessive body rotation or kipping to compensate for the added weight, which reduces the training effect.
- Gripping the bar with only the fingers instead of a full grip, which limits force production and risks a fall.
- Neglecting grip and forearm training, which becomes the limiting factor long before lat strength does.

## FAQ

### How much weight should I use for a Weighted One Hand Pull Up?

Start with just 2.5-5 lbs. This is already one of the most demanding bodyweight exercises, so even small increments represent massive relative load increases.

### How do I train toward a weighted one-arm Pullup?

Progress from weighted two-arm Pullups, to unweighted one-arm negatives, to full one-arm Pullups, and only then add external load.

### Is the Weighted One Hand Pull Up safe for elbows?

The elbow joint bears enormous force during this movement. Warm up thoroughly, progress slowly, and stop immediately if you feel any elbow pain.

### What muscles does the Weighted One Hand Pull Up work?

The Weighted One Hand Pull Up primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Weighted One Hand Pull Up good for beginners?

The Weighted One Hand Pull Up is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental back movements first.
