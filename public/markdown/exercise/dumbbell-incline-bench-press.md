# Dumbbell Incline Bench Press

## Quick answer

A dumbbell press on a bench set to a 45-degree incline. It works the upper chest, with help from the shoulders and triceps. Intermediate chest exercise using dumbbell. Targets chest, also works shoulders, triceps.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Triceps
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set an incline bench to 45 degrees. Sit with your back flat against it and your feet flat on the floor.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder height, palms facing forward, and press them up until your arms are straight.
3. Lower the dumbbells slowly to the sides of your chest, until your elbows are bent to about 90 degrees.
4. Press the dumbbells back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Set the bench at 30-45 degrees. Lower the dumbbells to the sides of your upper chest, just below the collarbone, to target the upper pec fibers.
- Retract and depress your shoulder blades into the bench before pressing. This stabilizes the shoulder joint and increases chest engagement.
- Press the dumbbells up and slightly inward so they converge at the top above your upper chest.

## Common mistakes

- Setting the bench angle too high (above 45 degrees), which turns this into a shoulder press rather than an upper Chest Press.
- Letting the dumbbells drift over the lower chest or belly during the press, which negates the incline angle.
- Not retracting the shoulder blades, which destabilizes the shoulders and reduces chest involvement.

## FAQ

### What angle is best for incline Dumbbell Bench Press?

30-45 degrees is the sweet spot. 30 degrees emphasizes the upper chest while still engaging the mid-chest. 45 degrees shifts more work to the upper chest and front delts. Above 45 degrees becomes a shoulder press.

### Should I do incline or flat bench press first?

If upper chest development is a priority, do incline first while you are fresh. If overall chest size is the goal, start with flat bench since you can handle more weight. Many programs alternate the order.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Incline Bench Press work?

The Dumbbell Incline Bench Press primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the shoulders, triceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Dumbbell Incline Bench Press good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Incline Bench Press is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of chest strength with easier variations first.
