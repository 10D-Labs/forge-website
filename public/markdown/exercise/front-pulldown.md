# Front Pulldown

## Quick answer

A seated pulldown on a lever machine, pulling handles from overhead down to your chest. It works the lats, the wide muscles along the sides of your back. Beginner back exercise using machine. Targets lats, also works biceps, rhomboids, rear deltoids.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Rhomboids, Rear Deltoids
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Adjust the seat so your knees fit under the pads and your feet are flat on the floor.
2. Grab the handles with your palms facing away from you, hands slightly wider than shoulder-width.
3. Sit upright with your chest lifted, shoulders back and a slight arch in your lower back.
4. Pull the handles down toward your chest.
5. Let the handles rise slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Lean back slightly (about 10-15 degrees) and pull the bar to your upper chest, not behind your neck.
- Initiate the pull by depressing your shoulder blades (pulling them down) before bending your elbows.
- Squeeze your lats at the bottom and control the return for 2-3 seconds, feeling the stretch at the top.

## Common mistakes

- Leaning too far back and turning the pulldown into a rowing motion.
- Pulling with the biceps instead of initiating with the lats by depressing the shoulder blades first.
- Using too much weight and only pulling halfway down, missing the peak lat contraction.

## FAQ

### Should I pull behind the neck or to the front?

Pull to the front (upper chest). Behind-the-neck pulldowns put the shoulders in a vulnerable externally rotated and abducted position under load, increasing injury risk without additional muscle benefit.

### How wide should I grip the pulldown bar?

Slightly wider than shoulder width (about 1.5x shoulder width) is optimal for lat engagement. Too wide reduces range of motion; too narrow shifts the work to the biceps and lower traps.

### What muscles does the Front Pulldown work?

The Front Pulldown primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the biceps, rhomboids, rear deltoids. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Front Pulldown good for beginners?

Yes, the Front Pulldown is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building lats strength.
