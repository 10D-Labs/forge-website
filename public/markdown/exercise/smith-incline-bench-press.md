# Smith Incline Bench Press

## Quick answer

A Smith machine bench press on a bench set to a 30 to 45 degree incline. It works the upper chest, with help from the shoulders and triceps. Beginner chest exercise using smith machine. Targets chest, also works shoulders, triceps.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Smith Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Triceps
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set a bench under the Smith machine to a 30 to 45 degree incline.
2. Sit with your back flat against the pad and your feet flat on the floor.
3. Grip the bar with your palms facing toward your feet, hands slightly wider than shoulder-width.
4. Unhook the bar and lower it slowly to your upper chest, keeping your elbows slightly tucked in.
5. Press the bar back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Set the incline to 30-45 degrees; steeper angles shift too much work to the front delts.
- Position the bench so the bar lowers to your upper chest, just below the collarbone.
- Drive the bar up and slightly back so it finishes over your upper chest, not your face.

## Common mistakes

- Setting the incline too steep (60+ degrees), which turns it into an overhead press rather than a chest exercise.
- Positioning the bench incorrectly so the bar path doesn't align with the upper chest.
- Arching the back excessively, which flattens the incline angle and defeats the purpose of the exercise.

## FAQ

### What incline angle is best for upper chest development?

30 degrees is the sweet spot for most people. 45 degrees also works but involves more front deltoid. Stay between 30-45 degrees for the best upper chest emphasis.

### Is the Smith Incline Bench Press safe for shoulders?

The fixed bar path can be problematic if the bench isn't positioned correctly. Make sure the bar tracks naturally to your upper chest and your shoulders don't feel impinged at the bottom.

### Should I use a wider or narrower grip on incline?

Use the same grip width as your flat bench press or slightly narrower. A grip that's too wide can stress the shoulder joint, especially at the bottom of the incline press.

### What muscles does the Smith Incline Bench Press work?

The Smith Incline Bench Press primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the shoulders, triceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Smith Incline Bench Press good for beginners?

Yes, the Smith Incline Bench Press is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building chest strength.
