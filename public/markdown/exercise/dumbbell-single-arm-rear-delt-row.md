# Dumbbell Single Arm Rear Delt Row

## Quick answer

A one-arm row braced on a bench, pulling with your elbow out wide instead of close to your side. It works the rear shoulders and the upper back. Intermediate shoulders exercise using dumbbell. Targets shoulders, also works trapezius, rhomboids.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Trapezius, Rhomboids
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Kneel with one knee and the same-side hand on a flat bench, other foot on the floor, back flat and level.
2. Hold a dumbbell in your free hand, arm hanging straight down, palm facing back toward your legs.
3. Pull the dumbbell up with your elbow pointing out to the side, until your upper arm is level with your back.
4. Lower slowly until your arm is straight. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Flare your elbow out to the side at about 90 degrees rather than pulling it straight back, which targets the rear delt instead of the lat.
- Squeeze your shoulder blade toward your spine at the top and hold for one second.
- Brace one hand and knee on a bench with a flat back, so your torso is close to parallel with the floor.

## Common mistakes

- Pulling the elbow back close to the body like a standard row, which works the lat instead of the rear delt.
- Raising your torso as you pull, which turns the row into more of an upright pull.
- Using too much weight and twisting the torso to get the dumbbell up.

## FAQ

### How is a rear delt row different from a regular dumbbell row?

The elbow flares out wide at about 90 degrees instead of pulling straight back. This shifts the work from the lats to the rear delts and upper back.

### What weight should I use for rear delt rows?

About 50-60% of your regular dumbbell row weight. The rear delts are smaller muscles that can't handle the same loads as the lats.

### Can I do rear delt rows on an incline bench?

Yes. Lying chest down on an incline bench takes strain off the lower back and keeps the movement strict.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Single Arm Rear Delt Row work?

The Dumbbell Single Arm Rear Delt Row primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the trapezius, rhomboids. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Dumbbell Single Arm Rear Delt Row good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Single Arm Rear Delt Row is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of shoulders strength with easier variations first.
