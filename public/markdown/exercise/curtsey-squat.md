# Curtsey Squat

## Quick answer

A squat where one leg steps back and across behind the other, like a curtsy. It works the glutes, with help from the quads and hamstrings. Beginner legs exercise using body weight. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and your hands clasped together.
2. Step your right foot back and across, behind your left leg.
3. Bend both knees to lower your body, as if curtsying. Keep your torso upright and your weight on your front foot.
4. Push through your front foot to return to the start, then step your left foot back behind your right leg.

## Pro tips

- Step diagonally behind and across your body, landing on the ball of your back foot.
- Keep your front knee tracking over your second toe; don't let it collapse inward.
- Push back to the starting position through the heel of your front foot.

## Common mistakes

- Not stepping far enough behind and across, which reduces the glute medius activation that makes this exercise unique.
- Letting the front knee cave inward as you descend, which puts stress on the knee ligaments.
- Leaning the torso too far forward instead of staying upright throughout the movement.

## FAQ

### What muscles do Curtsey Squats target differently than regular squats?

The cross-behind step specifically targets the gluteus medius and hip adductors in ways that standard squats don't, because of the diagonal movement pattern.

### Are Curtsey Squats bad for knees?

They can stress the knee if your front knee caves inward or if you have existing knee instability. Keep the front knee tracking outward and don't go deeper than comfortable.

### How many reps should I do?

10-12 per leg for 2-3 sets. Start without weight to master the balance and movement pattern before adding dumbbells.

### What muscles does the Curtsey Squat work?

The Curtsey Squat primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Curtsey Squat good for beginners?

Yes, the Curtsey Squat is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building glutes strength.
