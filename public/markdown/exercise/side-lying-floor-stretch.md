# Side Lying Floor Stretch

## Quick answer

A stretch lying on your side, reaching your top arm overhead while your top knee drops forward. It stretches the lats along the side of your back, and the obliques. Beginner back exercise using body weight. Targets lats, also works obliques, glutes.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Obliques, Glutes
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Stretching

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your side, legs straight and stacked, bottom arm stretched along the floor past your head. Rest your top hand on your hip.
2. Reach your top arm over your head to meet your bottom hand.
3. At the same time, bend your top knee and let it drop forward to the floor, until you feel a stretch along your side.
4. Hold for the set time, then straighten your top leg, bring your arm back to your hip and switch sides.

## Pro tips

- Reach your top arm as far overhead as possible while keeping it in line with your body to maximize the lat and oblique stretch.
- Breathe deeply into the stretched side to expand the ribcage and increase the stretch effect.
- Keep your hips stacked (do not roll forward or backward) to maintain proper alignment during the stretch.

## Common mistakes

- Rolling the torso forward or backward instead of staying purely on the side, which changes the stretch target.
- Not holding the stretch long enough to get a meaningful release (minimum 20 seconds per side).
- Tensing the muscles being stretched instead of consciously relaxing into the position.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the Side Lying Floor Stretch target?

It primarily stretches the lats, obliques, and intercostal muscles along the side of the ribcage. It can also provide a mild glute stretch depending on leg positioning.

### When should I do this stretch?

It works well as a cooldown after back or core workouts, or as a morning mobility routine to loosen up the lateral trunk muscles.

### I do not feel much stretch. How can I make it deeper?

Reach your top arm further overhead and actively push your bottom hip toward the floor. You can also bend your top knee and place that foot in front of you for more stability and a deeper reach.

### What muscles does the Side Lying Floor Stretch work?

The Side Lying Floor Stretch primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the obliques, glutes. It's classified as a stretching exercise for the back.

### Is the Side Lying Floor Stretch good for beginners?

Yes, the Side Lying Floor Stretch is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building lats strength.
