# Kettlebell Single Arm Snatch

## Quick answer

An explosive one-arm move that swings a kettlebell from between your legs to straight overhead in one motion. It works the shoulders, plus the upper back, forearms and abs. Advanced shoulders exercise using kettlebell. Targets shoulders, also works trapezius, forearms, core.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Trapezius, Forearms, Core
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a kettlebell in one hand between your legs.
2. Bend your knees slightly and push your hips back, lowering the kettlebell toward the floor.
3. Straighten your hips and knees fast and use that drive to swing the kettlebell up toward your shoulder.
4. Turn your hand as it reaches shoulder height and punch the kettlebell straight up until your arm is straight overhead.
5. Bring the kettlebell back down between your legs to start the next rep.

## Pro tips

- Drive from the hips first. The arm stays loose like a rope until the kettlebell reaches chest height, then punch through overhead.
- Insert your hand around the kettlebell at the top rather than letting it flip over and bang your wrist.
- On the downswing, guide the bell back between your legs by hinging at the hips. Don't let it pull you forward.

## Common mistakes

- Using the arm to muscle the bell overhead instead of projecting it with hip drive, which leads to early fatigue and shoulder strain.
- Letting the bell swing out in a wide arc at the top instead of keeping it close and punching straight up.
- Not taming the bell on the descent, letting it pull the body forward and rounding the back.

## FAQ

### How do I stop the kettlebell from bruising my forearm during snatches?

The bell shouldn't flip over your hand. At the top of the pull, insert your hand around the bell as it weightlessly transitions, so it lands gently against your forearm. Practice with a light bell until the timing is smooth.

### What's a good goal for kettlebell snatches?

The Simple & Sinister standard is 100 snatches in 5 minutes with a 24 kg (men) or 16 kg (women) kettlebell, switching hands every 10 reps. This is a challenging but achievable goal for intermediate trainees.

### What muscles does the Kettlebell Single Arm Snatch work?

The Kettlebell Single Arm Snatch primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the trapezius, forearms, core. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Kettlebell Single Arm Snatch good for beginners?

The Kettlebell Single Arm Snatch is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental shoulders movements first.
