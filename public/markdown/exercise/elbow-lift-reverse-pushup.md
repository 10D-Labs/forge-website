# Elbow Lift Reverse Pushup

## Quick answer

A face-up lift where you press your elbows down into two raised pads to raise your body into a straight line. It works the upper back, with help from the shoulders. Beginner back exercise using body weight. Targets upper back, also works triceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set two padded stools of the same height side by side, a little wider than your shoulders apart.
2. Lie face up between them with your legs straight and heels on the floor. Rest one elbow on each pad, forearms pointing up.
3. Press your elbows down into the pads to lift your chest and hips until your body is straight from heels to shoulders.
4. Lower slowly back to the start.

## Pro tips

- Press through your palms evenly and focus on squeezing your upper back at the top of each rep.
- Keep your elbows tucked at about 45 degrees to protect your shoulders while maximizing upper back engagement.
- Exhale as you press up and hold the top position for 2 seconds to increase time under tension on the upper back.

## Common mistakes

- Flaring the elbows out wide, which shifts the load from the upper back to the shoulders and increases shoulder strain.
- Using momentum to Pushup rather than controlled muscle contraction, reducing the effectiveness on the target muscles.
- Letting the hips sag or pike up instead of maintaining a rigid torso throughout the movement.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the Elbow Lift Reverse Pushup work?

It primarily targets the upper back muscles including the rhomboids and mid-traps, with secondary work on the triceps and shoulders.

### How is an Elbow Lift Reverse Pushup different from a regular Pushup?

The reverse Pushup starts face down and emphasizes pressing the upper body up while focusing on squeezing the upper back, whereas a regular Pushup targets the chest and triceps with a lowering-then-pressing motion.

### Can beginners do the Elbow Lift Reverse Pushup?

Yes, it is a beginner-friendly movement. Start with partial range of motion and build up as your upper back strength improves.

### What muscles does the Elbow Lift Reverse Pushup work?

The Elbow Lift Reverse Pushup primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Elbow Lift Reverse Pushup good for beginners?

Yes, the Elbow Lift Reverse Pushup is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building upper back strength.
