# Dynamic Chest Stretch

## Quick answer

Arm swings that cross your arms in front of you, then open them wide to the sides. They loosen up and stretch the chest and the fronts of the shoulders. Beginner chest exercise using body weight. Targets chest, also works shoulders, triceps.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Triceps
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Mobility

---

## How to perform

1. Stand tall with your feet hip-width apart. Raise your arms straight out in front of you at shoulder height, crossing one over the other.
2. Swing both arms open wide to your sides at shoulder height, palms facing forward, until you feel a stretch across your chest.
3. Swing them back in front of you until they cross again. Keep the swings smooth, without stopping.

## Pro tips

- Swing your arms across your body and back in a controlled rhythmic motion, gradually increasing the range of motion.
- Keep your core engaged and avoid rotating your torso; the movement should come from the shoulder joints.
- Perform 10-15 reps of each arm pattern as part of your upper body warmup.

## Common mistakes

- Swinging too aggressively at the start before the muscles are warm, which risks straining the pec or shoulder.
- Holding the stretch statically when this is meant to be a dynamic warmup movement.
- Rounding the upper back, which limits the chest stretch and reduces the warmup benefit.

## FAQ

### When should you do a Dynamic Chest Stretch?

Before any pressing workout (bench press, Pushups, overhead press) as part of your warmup. Dynamic stretches before exercise are better than static stretches for performance.

### Is this better than a doorway stretch?

They serve different purposes. Dynamic stretches are better pre-workout for warming up. Static stretches like the doorway stretch are better post-workout for improving flexibility.

### How many reps of the Dynamic Chest Stretch should you do?

10-15 arm crosses, gradually increasing range of motion. You should feel a gentle stretch in the chest and front of the shoulders by the last few reps.

### What muscles does the Dynamic Chest Stretch work?

The Dynamic Chest Stretch primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the shoulders, triceps. It's classified as a mobility exercise for the chest.

### Is the Dynamic Chest Stretch good for beginners?

Yes, the Dynamic Chest Stretch is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building chest strength.
