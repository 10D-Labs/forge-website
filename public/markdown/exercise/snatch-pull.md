# Snatch Pull

## Quick answer

A barbell pull from the floor with a wide grip, finishing up on your toes with a shrug instead of catching the bar overhead. It works the quads, hamstrings and glutes. Advanced legs exercise using barbell. Targets quads, also works hamstrings, glutes, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Quads
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with feet hip-width apart and the bar over your toes. Squat down and grip it much wider than your shoulders, palms facing you.
2. Keep your back flat and your arms straight as you stand up, pushing through your feet and keeping the bar close to your legs.
3. As the bar passes your thighs, push your hips forward, rise onto your toes and shrug your shoulders up.
4. Lower the bar slowly back to the floor by bending your hips and knees.

## Pro tips

- Use a wide (snatch) grip with the bar starting on the floor and your hips lower than in a Deadlift.
- Accelerate the bar aggressively through the second pull (hip extension phase), shrugging powerfully at the top.
- Keep the bar close to your body throughout the entire pull; it should almost brush your thighs.

## Common mistakes

- Pulling with the arms too early instead of driving through the legs and hips first.
- Letting the hips rise faster than the shoulders off the floor, which turns it into a stiff-leg pull.
- Not achieving full hip extension before shrugging, which limits power output.

## FAQ

### What is the Snatch Pull used for?

The Snatch Pull is a training exercise for the snatch that develops the pulling mechanics (first and second pull) without catching the bar overhead. It builds explosive hip power and upper back strength.

### What's the difference between a Snatch Pull and a clean pull?

The grip width is wider on a Snatch Pull (wide/snatch grip vs shoulder-width for clean pull), and the pulling mechanics are slightly different. The Snatch Pull starts with hips lower and has a longer range of motion.

### How heavy should I go on Snatch Pulls?

Use 90-110% of your snatch max. The goal is explosive speed and proper pulling mechanics, not maximal weight. If the bar speed slows significantly, the weight is too heavy.

### What muscles does the Snatch Pull work?

The Snatch Pull primarily targets the quads, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, glutes, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Snatch Pull good for beginners?

The Snatch Pull is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental legs movements first.
