# Barbell Wide Reverse Grip Bench Press

## Quick answer

A flat bench press with a wide grip and your palms facing your head. It works the upper chest, with help from the triceps and shoulders. Advanced chest exercise using barbell. Targets chest, also works triceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie flat on a bench with your feet flat on the floor and your back against the bench.
2. Grip the bar wide, hands wider than your shoulders, with your palms facing your head.
3. Lift the bar off the rack and hold it straight above your chest with your arms straight.
4. Lower the bar slowly until it touches your chest, keeping your elbows close to your sides and your wrists straight.
5. Press the bar back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Use a grip wider than shoulder width with palms facing you, and always have a spotter present.
- Lower the bar to the lower chest and focus on squeezing the pecs at the top of the press.
- Keep wrists straight and firmly locked throughout the movement to prevent the bar from rolling.

## Common mistakes

- Going too wide with the reverse grip, which severely compromises grip security and wrist health.
- Attempting heavy weight before being comfortable with the movement pattern and reverse grip.
- Not using a spotter, which is negligent for any reverse-grip barbell pressing.

## FAQ

### Why go wide with a reverse grip?

The wide reverse grip increases chest stretch at the bottom and shifts more emphasis to the pec fibers. It combines the benefits of wide-grip pressing with the upper-chest bias of reverse grip.

### Is this exercise riskier than a standard bench press?

Yes. The reverse grip combined with a wide grip is less secure. A spotter is mandatory, and you should use significantly less weight than your standard bench.

### Who should do this exercise?

Advanced lifters looking for a novel chest stimulus. Beginners and intermediates should build a strong foundation with standard bench press variations first.

### What muscles does the Barbell Wide Reverse Grip Bench Press work?

The Barbell Wide Reverse Grip Bench Press primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Barbell Wide Reverse Grip Bench Press good for beginners?

The Barbell Wide Reverse Grip Bench Press is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental chest movements first.
