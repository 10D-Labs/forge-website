# Kettlebell Alternating Press on Floor

## Quick answer

A floor press where you lie flat and press two kettlebells up one at a time. It works the middle of the chest, plus the shoulders and triceps. Intermediate chest exercise using kettlebell. Targets chest, also works shoulders, triceps.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Triceps
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your back on the floor with your legs straight.
2. Hold a kettlebell in each hand, arms straight up over your chest, palms facing toward your feet.
3. Lower one kettlebell toward your shoulder while the other arm stays straight up.
4. Press it back up as you lower the other one. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Press your lower back firmly into the floor to create a stable base and prevent arching during the press.
- Bend your legs and plant your feet flat to engage the glutes and stabilize your body while pressing.
- Lower the kettlebell until your elbow touches the floor, then press back up. The floor limits your range of motion and protects the shoulders.

## Common mistakes

- Bouncing the elbows off the floor to generate momentum instead of pressing from a dead stop.
- Not keeping the non-pressing arm locked out overhead, which destabilizes the whole movement.
- Flaring the elbows too wide, which stresses the shoulder joint. Keep them at about 45 degrees.

## FAQ

### Why press on the floor instead of a bench?

The floor limits the range of motion at the bottom, making it shoulder-friendly and eliminating the bounce. It also forces a dead-stop press from the bottom, building starting strength.

### Can I use one kettlebell instead of two?

Yes. A single kettlebell floor press is a great unilateral variation that also challenges anti-rotation core stability. Just press one side at a time.

### What muscles does the Kettlebell Alternating Press on Floor work?

The Kettlebell Alternating Press on Floor primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the shoulders, triceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Kettlebell Alternating Press on Floor good for beginners?

The Kettlebell Alternating Press on Floor is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of chest strength with easier variations first.
