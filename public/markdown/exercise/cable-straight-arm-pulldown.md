# Cable Straight Arm Pulldown

## Quick answer

A standing cable pulldown with straight arms, sweeping a bar from a high pulley down to your thighs. It works the lats. Beginner back exercise using cable. Targets lats, also works shoulders, biceps.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Biceps
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Attach a straight bar to a high pulley. Stand facing the machine with your feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Grip the bar with your palms facing down and your arms straight.
3. Keep your arms straight and pull the bar down to your thighs.
4. Let the bar rise slowly back up without bending your elbows.

## Pro tips

- Stand about two feet from the machine, hinge forward slightly at the hips, and pull the bar in an arc from overhead to your thighs while keeping your arms completely straight.
- Focus on driving the movement with your lats, not your arms. Imagine pushing the bar down with your elbows locked.
- Control the return slowly, feeling the stretch in your lats as the bar rises back to shoulder height.

## Common mistakes

- Bending the elbows during the pull, which recruits the triceps and reduces lat isolation.
- Standing too far from the machine, which limits the range of motion and changes the resistance angle.
- Using too much weight and rocking the body to move the bar, rather than keeping the torso stationary.

## FAQ

### What is the Cable Straight Arm Pulldown good for?

It isolates the lats without bicep involvement since the arms stay straight. It is excellent as a lat activation drill before heavy rows or pulldowns, or as a finishing exercise.

### Where should I feel the Cable Straight Arm Pulldown?

You should feel a strong stretch in your lats at the top and a deep contraction at the bottom near your thighs. If you feel it in your triceps, you are bending your elbows.

### What muscles does the Cable Straight Arm Pulldown work?

The Cable Straight Arm Pulldown primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the shoulders, biceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Cable Straight Arm Pulldown good for beginners?

Yes, the Cable Straight Arm Pulldown is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building lats strength.
