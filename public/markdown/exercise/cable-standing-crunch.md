# Cable Standing Crunch

## Quick answer

A crunch done standing with your back to a high cable, curling your upper body down while holding a rope at your head. It works the abs, with help from the obliques. Intermediate core exercise using cable. Targets abs, also works obliques.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Obliques
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Attach a rope to a high pulley. Stand with your back to the machine and your feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Hold the rope with both hands beside your head, elbows bent, with the rope running behind your head.
3. Use your abs to crunch your upper body forward and down, bringing your elbows toward your knees.
4. Rise back up slowly to standing tall.

## Pro tips

- Hinge at the waist and crunch your ribcage down toward your pelvis, not just bow forward with a flat back.
- Keep the rope behind your head and your hands fixed in position so the abs do all the work.
- Focus on rounding the spine (thoracic flexion) to maximally contract the rectus abdominis.

## Common mistakes

- Bending at the hips instead of flexing the spine, which turns it into a hip hinge and works the lower back.
- Pulling the rope with the arms instead of keeping the hands stationary and crunching with the torso.
- Using too much weight and relying on gravity to drop down rather than controlling the movement with the abs.

## FAQ

### What cable height is best for standing cable crunches?

Set the pulley at the top position. Use a rope attachment and hold the ends behind your head at ear level.

### How much weight should you use for Cable Standing Crunches?

Start with 30-50 pounds and focus on 12-15 slow, controlled reps. The goal is spinal flexion, not heavy loading.

### Are cable crunches bad for your spine?

When done correctly with moderate weight and controlled reps, they are safe. Avoid going excessively heavy or performing hundreds of reps, which increases disc loading.

### What muscles does the Cable Standing Crunch work?

The Cable Standing Crunch primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the obliques. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Cable Standing Crunch good for beginners?

The Cable Standing Crunch is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of abs strength with easier variations first.
