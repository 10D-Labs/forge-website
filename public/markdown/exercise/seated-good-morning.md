# Seated Good Morning

## Quick answer

A forward bend done sitting at a low cable: holding the handle with straight arms, you fold forward from the hips and sit back up. It works the glutes, hamstrings and lower back. Intermediate legs exercise using cable. Targets glutes, also works hamstrings, lower back.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Lower Back
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on a cable row bench facing the low pulley, feet on the foot plates, knees bent. Hold the handle in both hands, palms facing each other, arms straight.
2. Keep your arms straight and your back flat as you fold forward from your hips until your chest is close to your thighs.
3. Pull your upper body back up from the hips until you're sitting tall, leaning slightly back.

## Pro tips

- Hinge forward from the hips, not by rounding the spine, pushing your chest toward your thighs.
- Keep a slight arch in your lower back throughout the movement to protect the spine.
- Control the descent and use your glutes and hamstrings to pull yourself back upright.

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the lower back at the bottom of the movement, which puts dangerous shear force on the spine.
- Going too deep too quickly before the hamstrings have the flexibility to handle the range of motion.
- Using the machine's assistance to swing up rather than engaging the posterior chain muscles.

## FAQ

### What's the benefit of a Seated Good Morning versus standing?

The seated position eliminates lower body momentum and isolates the hip hinge pattern. It focuses the work entirely on the hamstrings, glutes, and spinal erectors.

### Is this exercise safe for the lower back?

When performed with proper form (neutral spine, controlled tempo), it strengthens the lower back. The seated position actually reduces risk compared to standing because there's less compressive force.

### How deep should I go?

Lean forward until you feel a strong stretch in your hamstrings, which is typically when your torso is at about 45-60 degrees from vertical. Don't force depth beyond what your flexibility allows.

### What muscles does the Seated Good Morning work?

The Seated Good Morning primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, lower back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Seated Good Morning good for beginners?

The Seated Good Morning is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of glutes strength with easier variations first.
