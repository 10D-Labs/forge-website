# Ski Ergometer (Distance)

## Quick answer

Cardio on a ski machine, pulling two handles down from overhead as you bend at the hips, like poling in cross-country skiing. It works your heart and lungs, plus the shoulders, forearms and abs. Beginner cardio exercise using cardio machine. Targets cardiovascular, also works shoulders, forearms, core.

- **Body Part:** Cardio
- **Equipment:** Cardio Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Cardiovascular
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Forearms, Core
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Cardio

---

## How to perform

1. Stand facing the ski machine and hold a handle in each hand, arms reaching up overhead.
2. Pull the handles down hard while bending forward at your hips.
3. Let your arms swing back as you finish the pull.
4. Stand back up and reach overhead again, keeping a steady rhythm.
5. Keep going for the set distance.

## Pro tips

- Initiate each pull with a powerful hip hinge, snapping your hips back as your arms drive the handles down.
- Extend fully overhead between each pull to maximize the range of motion and stretch the lats.
- Keep your core braced throughout the entire movement; the power comes from the hip hinge, not the arms.

## Common mistakes

- Pulling with the arms only and not using the hip hinge, which limits power output and exhausts the arms quickly.
- Standing too upright and not hinging at the hips, reducing the lat and posterior chain engagement.
- Not extending the arms fully overhead between pulls, which shortens the stroke and reduces efficiency.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the ski ergometer work?

Primarily the lats, shoulders, core, and hip extensors. It's one of the best upper body cardio machines because it targets the pulling muscles that rowers and bikes miss.

### What pace should I maintain?

For steady-state work, aim for a pace you can sustain for the entire distance. For intervals, alternate between hard 30-second efforts and easy 30-second recovery pulls.

### Is the ski erg good for runners?

It's an excellent cross-training tool for runners because it provides cardiovascular conditioning without any impact, while also building upper body and core strength.

### What muscles does the Ski Ergometer (Distance) work?

The Ski Ergometer (Distance) primarily targets the cardiovascular, with secondary activation in the shoulders, forearms, core. It's classified as a cardio exercise for the cardio.

### Is the Ski Ergometer (Distance) good for beginners?

Yes, the Ski Ergometer (Distance) is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building cardiovascular strength.
