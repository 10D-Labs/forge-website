# Smith Bench Press

## Quick answer

A bench press on a Smith machine, where the bar runs on fixed rails. It works the chest, with help from the triceps and shoulders. Beginner chest exercise using smith machine. Targets chest, also works triceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Smith Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie flat on a bench under the Smith machine bar, with the bar over your chest and your feet flat on the floor.
2. Grip the bar with your palms facing toward your feet, hands slightly wider than shoulder-width.
3. Unhook the bar and lower it until it touches your chest, keeping your elbows tucked in.
4. Press the bar back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Position the bench so the bar path travels straight down to your mid-chest, not your neck or belly.
- Keep your shoulder blades squeezed together and your back slightly arched for a stable pressing position.
- Lower the bar to touch your chest on every rep for full range of motion.

## Common mistakes

- Positioning the bench too far forward or back relative to the bar path, causing the bar to track over the neck or stomach.
- Flattening the back completely against the bench instead of maintaining a slight natural arch.
- Bouncing the bar off the chest to generate momentum.

## FAQ

### Is the Smith machine bench press as effective as free weight?

The fixed bar path means less stabilizer activation, but it allows you to focus entirely on pressing power. It's a solid accessory to the Barbell Bench Press, not a full replacement.

### Where should I position the bench under the Smith machine?

Lie down and position yourself so the bar naturally lowers to your mid-chest (nipple line) when your elbows are at about 45 degrees. The bar should not travel to your neck or lower ribs.

### Is the Smith machine bench press safer for solo training?

Yes. The built-in safety hooks allow you to re-rack the bar at any point during the lift, making it safer for training to failure without a spotter.

### What muscles does the Smith Bench Press work?

The Smith Bench Press primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Smith Bench Press good for beginners?

Yes, the Smith Bench Press is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building chest strength.
