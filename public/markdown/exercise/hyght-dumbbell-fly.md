# Hyght Dumbbell Fly

## Quick answer

A fly on an incline bench where the dumbbells sweep from beside your hips up to meet above your head. It works the chest and the front of the shoulders. Intermediate chest exercise using dumbbell. Targets chest, also works shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on a bench set to a steep incline, back against the pad and feet flat on the floor.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand with your arms hanging down and out beside your hips, palms facing in, elbows slightly bent.
3. Keeping that bend, raise the dumbbells out and up in a wide arc until they meet above your head, palms facing each other.
4. Lower them slowly along the same arc back beside your hips.

## Pro tips

- Keep a slight bend in your elbows throughout the entire arc and do not change the elbow angle as you lower.
- Lower the dumbbells in a wide arc until you feel a stretch across your chest, then reverse by squeezing the pecs.
- Think about hugging a large barrel as you bring the dumbbells back together at the top.

## Common mistakes

- Straightening the arms at the bottom of the fly, which puts excessive stress on the elbow and shoulder joints.
- Turning the fly into a press by bending the elbows too much, which recruits the triceps and reduces chest isolation.
- Using too heavy a weight, which forces a shorter range of motion and reduces the chest stretch at the bottom.

## FAQ

### What is a Hyght Dumbbell Fly?

It is a chest fly variation that uses a specific elbow angle and arc to target the chest fibers more effectively. The movement emphasizes a deep stretch at the bottom and a strong squeeze at the top.

### Are dumbbell flies good for chest growth?

Yes, flies provide a unique stimulus by stretching the chest under load, which is different from pressing exercises. They are best used as an accessory exercise after compound pressing movements.

### How much weight should I use for dumbbell flies?

Use significantly less weight than your Dumbbell Bench Press, typically 30-50% of your pressing weight. The long lever arm means even light dumbbells provide substantial chest tension.

### What muscles does the Hyght Dumbbell Fly work?

The Hyght Dumbbell Fly primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Hyght Dumbbell Fly good for beginners?

The Hyght Dumbbell Fly is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of chest strength with easier variations first.
