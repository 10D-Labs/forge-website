# Dumbbell Stepup Lunge

## Quick answer

A step-up done with your back foot resting on a bench behind you, lifting your front foot onto a second bench into a lunge. It works the quads, with help from the glutes and hamstrings. Intermediate legs exercise using dumbbell. Targets quads, also works glutes, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Quads
- **Secondary Muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set two flat benches in a line with about a stride's gap between them. Stand in the gap facing the front bench.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand at your sides, palms facing your thighs. Reach one foot back and rest its top on the rear bench.
3. Lift your standing foot off the floor and place it flat on the front bench, so you're in a lunge with both feet up.
4. Step that foot back down to the floor. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Drive through the heel of the foot on the step, not the toes, to engage the glutes and quads fully.
- Keep your torso upright throughout both the step-up and lunge portions of the movement.
- Control the step-down phase rather than dropping; the eccentric is where much of the muscle-building benefit happens.

## Common mistakes

- Pushing off the back foot to assist the step-up, which reduces the training effect on the front leg.
- Using a step that is too high, which causes the torso to lean forward excessively and strains the lower back.
- Letting the knee of the stepping leg collapse inward, which puts dangerous stress on the knee joint.

## FAQ

### What is the ideal step height for dumbbell step-up lunges?

A height where your thigh is parallel to the ground when your foot is on the step, typically 12-18 inches depending on your leg length.

### Are step-up lunges better than regular lunges?

They combine the benefits of step-ups (single-leg strength, balance) with lunges (hip flexor stretch, quad loading). The combination is more challenging and functional than either exercise alone.

### How much weight should I use for dumbbell step-up lunges?

Start with light dumbbells (10-20 lbs per hand) and focus on balance and form. Increase weight only when you can complete 10 reps per leg with perfect control.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Stepup Lunge work?

The Dumbbell Stepup Lunge primarily targets the quads, with secondary activation in the glutes, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Dumbbell Stepup Lunge good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Stepup Lunge is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of quads strength with easier variations first.
