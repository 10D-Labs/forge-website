# Dumbbell Standing Alternate Shoulder Press

## Quick answer

A standing overhead press, pushing up one dumbbell at a time while the other waits at your shoulder. It works the front of the shoulders, with the triceps and abs helping. Intermediate shoulders exercise using dumbbell. Targets shoulders, also works triceps, core.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Core
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder height.
2. Press one dumbbell straight overhead while the other stays at your shoulder.
3. Lower it back to your shoulder, then press the other one. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Press one dumbbell overhead while holding the other at shoulder height. This alternating pattern challenges your core to resist leaning.
- Brace your core hard to keep your torso from leaning toward the non-pressing side.
- Press in a slight arc outward and upward, not straight up, to follow the natural shoulder pressing path.

## Common mistakes

- Leaning the torso away from the pressing arm, which makes up for weak shoulders and reduces core work.
- Pressing too quickly and losing the alternating rhythm, which reduces the stability demand.
- Arching the lower back as fatigue sets in, which stresses the spine.

## FAQ

### What is the benefit of alternating dumbbell presses over a regular shoulder press?

Pressing one side at a time makes your core resist leaning, so you train stability alongside shoulder strength. It also gives each shoulder a brief rest between reps.

### Can I do alternating shoulder presses seated?

Yes. Sitting takes the legs out of the movement. A bench with no back support keeps more of the core challenge.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Standing Alternate Shoulder Press work?

The Dumbbell Standing Alternate Shoulder Press primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the triceps, core. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Dumbbell Standing Alternate Shoulder Press good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Standing Alternate Shoulder Press is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of shoulders strength with easier variations first.
