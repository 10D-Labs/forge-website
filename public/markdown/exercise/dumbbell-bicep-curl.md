# Dumbbell Bicep Curl

## Quick answer

A standing curl with a dumbbell in each hand and your palms facing forward. It works the biceps, with help from the forearms. Beginner arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand tall with a dumbbell in each hand, arms hanging straight and palms facing forward.
2. Keep your upper arms still and curl both dumbbells up to shoulder level.
3. Lower them slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Stand with dumbbells at your sides, palms forward, and curl both simultaneously. Keep your elbows pinned to your sides throughout.
- Squeeze your biceps hard at the top and lower for a full 2-3 seconds to maximize the eccentric contraction.
- Do not swing the dumbbells. If you need momentum, the weight is too heavy. Choose a load where you can control every inch of the rep.

## Common mistakes

- Swinging the torso backward to curl the weight, which uses momentum and can strain the lower back.
- Letting the elbows drift forward at the top, which uses the front delts to hold the weight and reduces bicep tension.
- Dropping the weight quickly on the way down instead of controlling the eccentric.

## FAQ

### How much should I be able to curl?

As a rough guideline, most people can strict curl about 15-20% of their body weight per dumbbell. A 150 lb person might curl 20-30 lb dumbbells. Focus on form over weight.

### Should I do dumbbell curls standing or seated?

Standing allows heavier weights but makes it easier to cheat with body swing. Seated (especially incline seated) isolates the biceps more strictly. Use both for complete development.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Bicep Curl work?

The Dumbbell Bicep Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Bicep Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Bicep Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building biceps strength.
