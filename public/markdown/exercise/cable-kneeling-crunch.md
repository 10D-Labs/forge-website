# Cable Kneeling Crunch

## Quick answer

A crunch done kneeling at a cable machine, pulling a rope down as you curl your chest toward your thighs. It works the abs, with help from the obliques. Intermediate core exercise using cable. Targets abs, also works obliques.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Obliques
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Attach a rope to a high pulley and kneel facing the machine.
2. Hold the rope with both hands and pull it down so your hands sit beside your head.
3. Keeping your hips still, round your back and crunch your chest down toward your thighs.
4. Rise slowly back to the start.

## Pro tips

- Curl your spine by thinking about bringing your rib cage toward your pelvis. Do not just hinge at the hips, which turns it into a hip flexor exercise.
- Keep the rope behind your head in a fixed position throughout the rep. Your hands should not move relative to your head.
- Exhale forcefully as you crunch down to maximize rectus abdominis contraction.

## Common mistakes

- Sitting back onto the heels during the crunch, which uses the hip flexors and body weight to pull the cable rather than the abs.
- Pulling with the arms by moving the rope away from the head, which turns this into a Pullover instead of a crunch.
- Using too much weight and only achieving a small range of motion rather than fully curling the spine.

## FAQ

### How do I make Cable Kneeling Crunches harder?

Slow down the eccentric (return) phase to 3-4 seconds, add a 2-second hold at the bottom, or use a slight twist to engage the obliques.

### Why do I feel Cable Kneeling Crunches in my hip flexors instead of my abs?

You are likely hinging at the hips instead of curling your spine. Focus on rounding your upper back and pulling your ribs toward your pelvis. Keep your hips locked in position.

### What muscles does the Cable Kneeling Crunch work?

The Cable Kneeling Crunch primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the obliques. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Cable Kneeling Crunch good for beginners?

The Cable Kneeling Crunch is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of abs strength with easier variations first.
