# Kettlebell Front Squat

## Quick answer

A squat with a kettlebell held at each shoulder. It works the glutes and quads, and holding the weight in front keeps your upper body upright. Intermediate legs exercise using kettlebell. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, toes turned slightly out, holding a kettlebell in each hand up at your shoulders.
2. Keeping your chest up, sit your hips down and back as if into a chair.
3. Lower until your thighs are level with the floor or lower, as far as you can comfortably go.
4. Push through your heels to stand back up.

## Pro tips

- Hold the kettlebell in the goblet position (both hands on the horns) or in a double rack position with elbows tight to your body.
- Keep your elbows up and chest tall throughout the squat to prevent the weight from pulling you forward.
- Push your knees out over your toes and sit your hips down between your feet, not behind them.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the elbows drop and the chest cave forward, which shifts the load onto the lower back.
- Rising on the toes at the bottom of the squat, indicating limited ankle mobility. Place small plates under your heels if needed.
- Not squatting to parallel depth, which limits glute and quad development.

## FAQ

### How is a Kettlebell Front Squat different from a goblet squat?

A Kettlebell Front Squat can use one or two kettlebells in a rack position (at the shoulders), while a goblet squat holds a single kettlebell by the horns at chest level. The rack position allows heavier loading and challenges upper back stability more.

### What muscles does the Kettlebell Front Squat target?

Primarily the quads, glutes, and core, with significant upper back engagement from holding the weight in the front-loaded position.

### What muscles does the Kettlebell Front Squat work?

The Kettlebell Front Squat primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Kettlebell Front Squat good for beginners?

The Kettlebell Front Squat is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of glutes strength with easier variations first.
