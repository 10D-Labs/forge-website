# Hands Elevated Scapula Pushup

## Quick answer

A small straight-arm push with your hands on a bench, moving only your shoulder blades. It works the serratus muscles along the sides of your ribs, with help from the shoulders. Beginner core exercise using body weight. Targets serratus, also works triceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Serratus
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Place your hands on a flat bench, shoulder-width apart, palms down. Walk your feet back until your body is straight from head to heels.
2. Keep your arms straight and let your chest sink toward the bench, squeezing your shoulder blades together.
3. Push the bench away to spread your shoulder blades apart, rounding your upper back slightly.

## Pro tips

- Place your hands on a bench and keep your arms locked straight; only your shoulder blades should move.
- At the top, push the bench away and round your upper back slightly to spread your shoulder blades apart.
- Then let your chest sink between your shoulders so your shoulder blades come together, keeping your arms straight.

## Common mistakes

- Bending the elbows and turning it into a regular Pushup.
- Not pushing all the way apart at the top, which shortens the movement.
- Sagging the hips, which strains the lower back.

## FAQ

### What is a Scapula Pushup?

A Pushup position where your arms stay straight and only your shoulder blades move, spreading apart and squeezing together. It trains the serratus anterior along your ribs.

### Why do it with my hands on a bench?

Raising your hands makes it easier, so you can learn the shoulder blade movement before doing it on the floor.

### Why is serratus anterior strength important?

The serratus holds your shoulder blades against your ribs during pushing and overhead movements. Weakness can lead to the shoulder blade sticking out and shoulder pain.

### What muscles does the Hands Elevated Scapula Pushup work?

The Hands Elevated Scapula Pushup primarily targets the serratus, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Hands Elevated Scapula Pushup good for beginners?

Yes, the Hands Elevated Scapula Pushup is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building serratus strength.
