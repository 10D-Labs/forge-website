# T-Bar Reverse Grip Row

## Quick answer

A standing, bent-over row on a T-bar machine, holding the handles with your palms facing forward. It works the upper back, with help from the biceps and forearms. Intermediate back exercise using machine. Targets upper back, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand on the T-bar row's foot plates facing the weight, knees slightly bent. Lean forward, back flat, and hold the handles, palms facing forward.
2. Pull the handles up toward your belly, driving your elbows back past your body.
3. Lower the handles slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Use an underhand grip to increase lat and lower trap engagement compared to a standard overhand T-Bar Row.
- Keep your chest against the pad and drive your elbows back past your ribcage for maximum contraction.
- Lower the weight until your arms are fully extended to get a complete lat stretch between reps.

## Common mistakes

- Gripping too wide on the handles, which reduces the mechanical advantage of the reverse grip.
- Shrugging the shoulders up during the pull instead of keeping them depressed and retracted.
- Using momentum by bouncing the chest off the pad with each rep.

## FAQ

### Why use a reverse grip on the T-Bar Row?

The reverse (underhand) grip allows the elbows to stay tighter to the body, which shifts emphasis toward the lower lats and increases biceps involvement.

### Is the reverse grip harder on the wrists?

It can be if you grip too tightly or use a bar that is too thick. Start with moderate weight and ensure your wrists stay neutral throughout the pull.

### Should I alternate between overhand and reverse grip?

Yes. Overhand grip emphasizes the upper back and rear delts, while reverse grip targets the lower lats more. Using both creates more complete back development.

### What muscles does the T-Bar Reverse Grip Row work?

The T-Bar Reverse Grip Row primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the T-Bar Reverse Grip Row good for beginners?

The T-Bar Reverse Grip Row is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of upper back strength with easier variations first.
