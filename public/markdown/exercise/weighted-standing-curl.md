# Weighted Standing Curl

## Quick answer

A curl done with one weight plate held by its top edge in both hands, palms facing you. It works the biceps and forearms. Beginner arms exercise using weight plate. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Weight Plate
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand tall with your feet hip-width apart. Hold a weight plate upright in front of your thighs by its top edge, palms facing you.
2. Keep your elbows at your sides and curl the plate up to chest height.
3. Lower it slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Pin your elbows to your sides and do not let them drift forward as you curl the weight up.
- Squeeze the biceps hard at the top for a one-second hold before lowering.
- Lower the weight for a 2-3 second count to maximize eccentric tension on the biceps.

## Common mistakes

- Swinging the torso backward to cheat the weight up, which loads the lower back instead of the biceps.
- Moving the elbows forward during the curl, which turns it into a front raise and reduces bicep isolation.
- Using too much weight and performing half reps that never reach full contraction or full extension.

## FAQ

### Should you fully extend your arms at the bottom of a curl?

Yes. Full extension ensures a complete range of motion and maximizes the stretch on the biceps, which is important for growth.

### Are standing curls better than seated curls?

Standing curls allow more weight but also more cheating. Seated curls provide better isolation. Use both in your programming.

### How heavy should you curl?

Use a weight that allows 8-12 controlled reps without body English. If you need to swing, the weight is too heavy.

### What muscles does the Weighted Standing Curl work?

The Weighted Standing Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Weighted Standing Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Weighted Standing Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building biceps strength.
