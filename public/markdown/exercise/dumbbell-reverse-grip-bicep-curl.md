# Dumbbell Reverse Grip Bicep Curl

## Quick answer

A curl done with your palms facing down instead of up, both arms at once. It works the biceps and the forearms. Beginner arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand tall with a dumbbell in each hand, arms hanging straight, palms facing behind you.
2. Keep your upper arms still and curl both dumbbells up to shoulder level, palms facing down.
3. Lower them slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Keep your palms facing down (overhand grip) throughout the entire curl to target the brachioradialis and forearm extensors.
- Use a lighter weight than standard curls since the reverse grip puts the biceps in a weaker position and relies more on the brachioradialis.
- Keep your wrists straight and rigid throughout to avoid wrist strain under the reverse grip load.

## Common mistakes

- Rotating the wrists to a palms-up position during the curl, which turns this into a regular Bicep Curl.
- Using the same heavy weight as standard curls, leading to poor form and potential wrist strain.
- Bending the wrists backward during the curl, which puts excessive stress on the wrist extensors.

## FAQ

### What does the reverse grip Bicep Curl work?

Primarily the brachioradialis (outer forearm) and brachialis (under the biceps), with less emphasis on the biceps brachii than a standard curl.

### Will reverse curls build bigger forearms?

Yes. The reverse grip heavily targets the brachioradialis, which is the most visible forearm muscle from the front and a key contributor to forearm size.

### How much less weight should I use for reverse curls?

About 40-50% less than your standard Bicep Curl weight. The reverse grip puts the biceps at a mechanical disadvantage.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Reverse Grip Bicep Curl work?

The Dumbbell Reverse Grip Bicep Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Reverse Grip Bicep Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Reverse Grip Bicep Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building biceps strength.
