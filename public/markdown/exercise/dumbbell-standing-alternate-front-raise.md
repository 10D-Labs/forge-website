# Dumbbell Standing Alternate Front Raise

## Quick answer

A front raise done one arm at a time, lifting a dumbbell straight out in front of you to shoulder height. It works the front of the shoulders. Beginner shoulders exercise using dumbbell. Targets shoulders, also works traps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Traps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand tall with a dumbbell in each hand, arms hanging in front of your thighs, palms facing your thighs.
2. Keeping your arm straight and your body still, raise one dumbbell in front of you until your arm is level with the floor.
3. Lower it slowly back to your thigh, then raise the other arm. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Raise one dumbbell straight out in front of you to shoulder height, then lower it before raising the other.
- Keep your body completely still and upright while raising one arm; do not lean back or shift your weight.
- Lower the working arm fully before raising the other side so each rep gets full range of motion.

## Common mistakes

- Leaning back as you lift, using momentum instead of shoulder strength.
- Raising the dumbbell well above shoulder height, which brings in the traps.
- Shrugging the shoulder on the raising side instead of keeping it down and letting the front delt lift.

## FAQ

### Is alternating better than raising both arms at once?

Alternating lets you focus on one shoulder at a time and makes strict form easier, since only one arm is working.

### Do I need to go lighter with alternating front raises?

You can usually use the same weight as for two-arm front raises. Some people can go slightly heavier because each arm rests briefly between reps.

### Can alternating raises help fix uneven shoulders?

Yes. You can add reps or sets on the weaker side to address imbalances more easily than with two-arm raises.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Standing Alternate Front Raise work?

The Dumbbell Standing Alternate Front Raise primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the traps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Dumbbell Standing Alternate Front Raise good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Standing Alternate Front Raise is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building shoulders strength.
