# Dumbbell Single Arm Bent Over Row

## Quick answer

A one-arm dumbbell row with one knee and hand braced on a bench. It works the upper back, with help from the biceps and forearms. Beginner back exercise using dumbbell. Targets upper back, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Put one knee and the same-side hand on a flat bench, other foot on the floor. Hold a dumbbell in your free hand, palm facing in.
2. Keep your back flat and let the dumbbell hang straight down with your arm straight.
3. Pull the dumbbell up toward your chest, keeping your elbow close to your body.
4. Lower it slowly until your arm is straight. Do all your reps, then switch sides.

## Pro tips

- Place your non-working hand and knee on a bench for support, keeping your back flat and parallel to the floor.
- Pull the dumbbell toward your lower rib cage, driving your elbow straight back. Your elbow should pass your torso at the top.
- Let the dumbbell hang at full arm extension at the bottom to get a deep lat stretch, then initiate the pull with the shoulder blade.

## Common mistakes

- Rotating the torso to heave the weight up, which uses momentum instead of back muscle contraction.
- Pulling the dumbbell toward the shoulder instead of the lower rib cage, which shifts emphasis to the rear delt and bicep.
- Rounding the back, especially at full arm extension, which puts the lumbar spine at risk.

## FAQ

### Should I use a bench for single arm dumbbell rows?

Using a bench with one hand and one knee provides stability and allows you to focus entirely on the working side. You can also do them with just one hand on a rack, both feet on the ground.

### How heavy should I go on single arm dumbbell rows?

Single arm rows are a strength exercise that tolerates heavier loads. Most intermediate lifters use 50-80+ lb dumbbells. Go as heavy as you can while maintaining a flat back and controlled form.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Single Arm Bent Over Row work?

The Dumbbell Single Arm Bent Over Row primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Dumbbell Single Arm Bent Over Row good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Single Arm Bent Over Row is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building upper back strength.
