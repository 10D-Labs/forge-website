# Band Straight Back Stiff Leg Deadlift

## Quick answer

A deadlift standing on a band, bending forward from the hips with a flat back and nearly straight legs. It works the glutes, along with the hamstrings and lower back. Beginner legs exercise using resistance band. Targets glutes, also works hamstrings, lower back.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Lower Back
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand on the middle of a band, feet hip-width apart, holding a handle in each hand with your arms straight and palms facing your legs.
2. With a slight bend in your knees, push your hips back and lean forward until the handles are just below your knees. Keep your back flat.
3. Drive your hips forward to stand up tall, keeping your arms straight.

## Pro tips

- Focus on maintaining a perfectly flat back as you hinge forward, avoiding any rounding of the upper or lower spine.
- Push your hips back as far as possible and feel a deep Hamstring Stretch before reversing the movement.
- Drive your hips forward explosively at the top and squeeze the glutes to full lockout.

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the back despite the exercise name emphasizing a straight back, which defeats the purpose.
- Not pushing the hips back far enough, which limits the Hamstring Stretch and hip hinge depth.
- Bouncing out of the bottom position instead of pausing briefly in the stretched position.

## FAQ

### How is this different from a regular stiff leg Deadlift?

The 'straight back' cue emphasizes maintaining a rigid flat spine throughout. A standard stiff leg Deadlift may allow some upper back rounding. The straight-back version is stricter.

### What muscles benefit most from this exercise?

Primarily the glutes and hamstrings through hip extension, with significant isometric work for the erector spinae to maintain the flat back position.

### Is the band version suitable for beginners?

Yes. The band provides a lighter and more forgiving resistance than a barbell, making it a great way to learn the hip hinge pattern with proper spinal alignment.

### What muscles does the Band Straight Back Stiff Leg Deadlift work?

The Band Straight Back Stiff Leg Deadlift primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, lower back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Band Straight Back Stiff Leg Deadlift good for beginners?

Yes, the Band Straight Back Stiff Leg Deadlift is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building glutes strength.
