# Barbell Bench Press

## Quick answer

A press done lying on a flat bench, lowering a barbell to your chest and pushing it back up. It works the chest, with help from the triceps and shoulders. Intermediate chest exercise using barbell. Targets chest, also works triceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie flat on a bench with your back against it and your feet flat on the floor.
2. Grip the bar with your palms facing your feet, hands slightly wider than shoulder-width apart.
3. Lift the bar off the rack and hold it over your chest with your arms straight.
4. Lower the bar slowly until it touches your chest, keeping your elbows tucked in toward your sides.
5. Push the bar back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Plant your feet firmly, retract your shoulder blades, and create a slight arch in your upper back to stabilize your pressing platform.
- Lower the bar to your lower chest (nipple line) with a controlled 2-3 second descent, keeping your elbows at about 45 degrees from your torso.
- Drive the bar up and slightly back toward the rack so it finishes over your shoulder joint, not over your face or stomach.

## Common mistakes

- Flaring the elbows out to 90 degrees, which puts excessive stress on the shoulder joint and reduces pressing power.
- Bouncing the bar off the chest to generate momentum instead of pausing briefly at the bottom.
- Lifting the hips off the bench during heavy reps, which compromises the setup and can strain the lower back.

## FAQ

### How wide should my grip be on bench press?

For most people, hands 1.5 times shoulder width works well. Your forearms should be vertical when the bar is at your chest. Wider targets more chest, narrower shifts to triceps.

### Should I touch the bar to my chest on every rep?

Yes, unless you have a shoulder injury preventing it. Full range of motion produces better chest development and strength. A light touch, not a bounce, is the standard.

### How do I bench press without a spotter?

Use a power rack with safety pins set just below your chest height. Alternatively, learn the 'roll of shame' where you roll the bar down your torso to your hips and sit up. Never bench heavy without either a spotter or safeties.

### What muscles does the Barbell Bench Press work?

The Barbell Bench Press primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Barbell Bench Press good for beginners?

The Barbell Bench Press is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of chest strength with easier variations first.
