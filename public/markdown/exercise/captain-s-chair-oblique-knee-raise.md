# Captain's Chair Oblique Knee Raise

## Quick answer

A knee raise in a captain's chair where your knees come up toward one side instead of straight ahead. It works the abs and the obliques on the sides of your waist. Advanced core exercise using body weight. Targets abs, also works obliques, hip flexors.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Obliques, Hip Flexors
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Support yourself in a captain's chair with your back against the pad and your forearms on the arm pads.
2. Hold the handles with your palms facing each other and let your legs hang down.
3. Bend your knees and lift them toward your chest, turning your hips so your knees point toward one side.
4. Lower your legs slowly back down.
5. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Prop yourself up on your forearms in the captain's chair with your back against the pad.
- Lift your bent knees up toward one side of your chest, then lower and switch sides.
- Move slowly enough that the obliques, not a swing, bring the knees up.

## Common mistakes

- Swinging the legs from side to side with momentum.
- Letting the shoulders shrug up toward the ears.
- Dropping the legs quickly instead of lowering them under control.

The abs and the obliques on the sides of your waist, with help from the hip flexors.

### How do I make it harder?

Lift your knees higher, slow down the tempo, or straighten your legs a little more on each rep.

### What muscles does the Captain's Chair Oblique Knee Raise work?

The Captain's Chair Oblique Knee Raise primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the obliques, hip flexors. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Captain's Chair Oblique Knee Raise good for beginners?

The Captain's Chair Oblique Knee Raise is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental core movements first.
