# Weighted Squat Between Benches

## Quick answer

A squat with a weight plate hanging from a belt between your legs, standing on two benches so the plate can drop below your feet. It works the glutes and quads. Intermediate legs exercise using weight plate. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Weight Plate
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Place two flat benches side by side with a gap between them. Put on a dip belt with a weight plate hanging from it.
2. Stand with one foot on each bench, the plate hanging in the gap, and hold your arms straight out in front at shoulder height.
3. Bend your knees and lower your hips until your thighs are level with the floor, letting the plate sink between the benches.
4. Push through your feet to stand back up. Don't let your knees cave in.

## Pro tips

- Stand with one foot on each bench and let the plate hang from a belt between your legs.
- Squat down so the plate drops below the level of the benches, keeping your chest up.
- Push your knees out over your toes and drive through your whole foot to stand.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the knees cave inward on the way up.
- Rising onto the toes, which often means the stance is too wide for the benches.
- Letting the plate swing instead of lowering it in a straight line.

## FAQ

### Why squat between benches?

Standing on benches lets the weight drop below your feet, so you can squat deep without the plate hitting the floor. The belt keeps the load off your spine.

### What do I need for this exercise?

Two sturdy benches or boxes of the same height, a dip belt and a weight plate.

### How deep should I squat?

As deep as you can while keeping your back flat and your heels down.

### What muscles does the Weighted Squat Between Benches work?

The Weighted Squat Between Benches primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Weighted Squat Between Benches good for beginners?

The Weighted Squat Between Benches is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of glutes strength with easier variations first.
