# Dumbbell Incline Fly

## Quick answer

A chest fly on a 45-degree incline bench, opening the dumbbells out to the sides in a wide arc. It works the upper chest. Intermediate chest exercise using dumbbell. Targets chest, also works shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set an incline bench to 45 degrees. Sit on it with a dumbbell in each hand, palms facing each other.
2. Lie back and press the dumbbells up until they are directly above your chest.
3. Lower the dumbbells out to the sides in a wide arc until you feel a stretch in your chest.
4. Bring them back up along the same arc until they are above your chest again.

## Pro tips

- Set the bench at 30-45 degrees. With a slight, fixed elbow bend, lower the dumbbells in a wide arc until you feel a deep stretch across the upper chest.
- Bring the dumbbells together above your upper chest, not your face. The angle should match the incline to target the clavicular pec fibers.
- Use lighter weight than flat flyes since the incline angle is a weaker position for the chest.

## Common mistakes

- Setting the bench too high, which turns this into a front raise instead of a fly and shifts tension to the shoulders.
- Bending the elbows too much, which converts the fly into an incline press.
- Not going deep enough to stretch the upper chest, which limits the muscle-building stimulus.

## FAQ

### Do incline flyes target the upper chest?

Yes, the 30-45 degree angle shifts emphasis to the clavicular (upper) head of the pectoralis major. The fly motion isolates the chest by removing tricep involvement.

### Should I do incline flyes and incline press in the same workout?

Yes, they complement each other. The incline press is a compound movement for strength and load, while the incline fly is an isolation movement for stretch and contraction. Do the press first, then finish with flyes.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Incline Fly work?

The Dumbbell Incline Fly primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Dumbbell Incline Fly good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Incline Fly is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of chest strength with easier variations first.
