# Arms Apart Circular Toe Touch

## Quick answer

A standing move that sweeps straight arms down to your toes and back up overhead in a wide arc. It works the glutes and stretches the hamstrings. Intermediate legs exercise using body weight. Targets glutes, also works hamstrings, quadriceps, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Quadriceps, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Balance

---

## How to perform

1. Stand tall with your feet hip-width apart and your arms straight overhead, hands shoulder-width apart, palms facing forward.
2. Keep your arms straight and bend forward at the hips, sweeping your hands down toward your toes.
3. Rise back up, reaching your straight arms out in front of you in a wide arc until they're overhead again.
4. At the top, lean back a little with your arms overhead, then fold forward again for the next rep.

## Pro tips

- Reach across to the opposite foot with one hand while keeping your arms wide and extended to increase the rotational stretch.
- Lift the back leg straight and in line with your torso as you reach down, creating a T-shape with your body.
- Move slowly and focus on balance; this is as much a stability exercise as it is a stretch.

## Common mistakes

- Bending the standing knee too much, which reduces the Hamstring Stretch and balance challenge.
- Rounding the back to reach the toes instead of hinging at the hips with a flat back.
- Rushing through the movement without achieving a stable, balanced position at the bottom.

## FAQ

### What does the Arms Apart Circular Toe Touch work?

It works balance, hamstring flexibility, hip mobility, and core stability. The reaching component adds a rotational stretch through the torso.

### Is this exercise good for athletes?

Yes, the single-leg balance, rotational reach, and hamstring flexibility demands make it an excellent dynamic warm-up drill for athletes in any sport.

### How many reps should I do per side?

Aim for 8-10 controlled reps per side. The quality of each reach and the stability of the standing leg matter more than the number of reps.

### What muscles does the Arms Apart Circular Toe Touch work?

The Arms Apart Circular Toe Touch primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, quadriceps, calves. It's classified as a balance exercise for the legs.

### Is the Arms Apart Circular Toe Touch good for beginners?

The Arms Apart Circular Toe Touch is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of glutes strength with easier variations first.
