# Kettlebell Arnold Press

## Quick answer

A one-arm overhead press with a kettlebell, turning your palm from facing you to facing forward as you press. It works the front of the shoulders, with help from the triceps and upper back. Intermediate shoulders exercise using kettlebell. Targets shoulders, also works triceps, upper back.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Upper Back
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a kettlebell in one hand at shoulder height, palm facing you.
2. Press the kettlebell overhead, turning your palm to face forward as your arm straightens.
3. Lower it slowly back to your shoulder, turning your palm to face you again.

## Pro tips

- Start with palms facing you at shoulder height, then rotate your palms outward as you press overhead. The rotation should be smooth and continuous.
- Finish with the kettlebells directly overhead with biceps near your ears and palms facing forward.
- Use a controlled tempo on the way down, reversing the rotation as the kettlebells return to shoulder height.

## Common mistakes

- Rushing the rotation and pressing before the palms have turned forward, which reduces front deltoid engagement.
- Arching the lower back excessively at the top instead of keeping the core braced and ribcage down.
- Using kettlebells that are too heavy, which causes the form to break down during the rotation phase.

## FAQ

### What's special about the Arnold press compared to a regular press?

The Arnold press incorporates a rotation that engages all three deltoid heads (front, lateral, and rear) through a larger range of motion, while a standard press primarily targets the front and lateral deltoids.

### Does the Arnold press work with kettlebells as well as dumbbells?

Yes, but the offset center of gravity of the kettlebell adds a stabilization challenge for the wrists and forearms. Start lighter than you would with dumbbells until you're comfortable with the grip.

### What muscles does the Kettlebell Arnold Press work?

The Kettlebell Arnold Press primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the triceps, upper back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Kettlebell Arnold Press good for beginners?

The Kettlebell Arnold Press is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of shoulders strength with easier variations first.
