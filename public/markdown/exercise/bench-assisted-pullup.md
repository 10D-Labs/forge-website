# Bench Assisted Pullup

## Quick answer

A pullup with one foot on a bench, so your leg can take some of your weight. It works the lats, with help from the biceps and forearms. Beginner back exercise using body weight. Targets lats, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Place a flat bench beside you under a pullup bar.
2. Grab the bar with your palms facing away from you, hands a little wider than your shoulders. Rest one foot on the bench, knee bent.
3. Pull yourself up until your chin is above the bar, pushing through the foot on the bench only as much as you need.
4. Lower slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Set a bench under the pullup bar and rest one foot on it while you hang.
- Pull with your arms and push down with the foot only as much as you need to finish each rep.
- Use less help from your leg over time until you can do full Pullups.

## Common mistakes

- Doing most of the work with the leg instead of the arms and back.
- Not pulling high enough to get your chin over the bar.
- Dropping quickly on the way down instead of lowering with control.

## FAQ

### What is a Bench Assisted Pullup?

A Pullup with one foot on a bench, so your leg can take some of your weight. It is a simple way to practice Pullups without a machine or band.

### How do Bench Assisted Pullups help me get to full Pullups?

They train the full Pullup movement with less load. As you get stronger, push less with your leg until you no longer need it.

### How high should the bench be?

High enough that you can reach the bar with your foot on the bench and a slight bend in the knee.

### What muscles does the Bench Assisted Pullup work?

The Bench Assisted Pullup primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Bench Assisted Pullup good for beginners?

Yes, the Bench Assisted Pullup is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building lats strength.
