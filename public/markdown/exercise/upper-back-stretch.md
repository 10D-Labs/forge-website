# Upper Back Stretch

## Quick answer

A standing stretch where you push your clasped hands forward at shoulder height and round your upper back. It stretches the upper back and the backs of the shoulders. Beginner back exercise using body weight. Targets upper back, also works shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Stretching

---

## How to perform

1. Stand tall with your feet hip-width apart and your arms at your sides.
2. Raise both arms straight in front of you to shoulder height and lace your fingers together.
3. Push your hands forward and let your upper back round, so your shoulder blades spread apart.
4. Hold for the set time, then let go and lower your arms back to your sides.

## Pro tips

- Interlace your fingers and push your palms away from your body while rounding your upper back to maximize the stretch between the shoulder blades.
- Drop your chin toward your chest as you push forward to add a stretch along the posterior neck and upper traps.
- Hold the stretch for a full 20-30 seconds and focus on breathing into the area between your shoulder blades.

## Common mistakes

- Lifting the arms overhead without rounding the upper back, which stretches the shoulders but misses the upper back entirely.
- Holding the stretch for only a few seconds, which is not long enough for the muscle fibers to release.
- Tensing the shoulders up toward the ears during the stretch instead of keeping them relaxed.

## FAQ

### When should I do the Upper Back Stretch?

It is ideal after long periods of sitting, as a warm-up before upper body workouts, or as part of a daily mobility routine to combat desk posture.

### Can this help with shoulder blade pain?

Yes. Tightness in the rhomboids and mid-traps between the shoulder blades is common, and this stretch directly addresses that area.

### What other stretches pair well with this?

Pair it with a doorway chest stretch and a Neck Side Stretch for a complete posture-correction routine targeting the entire upper back and shoulder girdle.

### What muscles does the Upper Back Stretch work?

The Upper Back Stretch primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the shoulders. It's classified as a stretching exercise for the back.

### Is the Upper Back Stretch good for beginners?

Yes, the Upper Back Stretch is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building upper back strength.
