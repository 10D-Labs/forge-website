# Cable Judo Flip

## Quick answer

A standing crunch facing away from a cable, with a rope pulled over one shoulder as you bend forward. It works the abs, with help from the obliques. Intermediate core exercise using cable. Targets abs, also works obliques, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Obliques, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Attach a rope to a pulley set at about head height. Stand facing away from the machine, feet shoulder-width apart, knees slightly bent.
2. Pull the rope over one shoulder and hold both ends by your chin, palms facing you, elbows in front of your chest.
3. Keeping your hands by your chin, bend forward at the waist and crunch down until your upper body is about level with the floor.
4. Rise slowly back to standing. Do all your reps with the rope over one shoulder, then switch shoulders.

## Pro tips

- Generate the rotation from your core and hips, not your arms. Your arms are just transmitting the force your trunk creates.
- Pivot your back foot as you rotate to allow full hip turnover and protect your knees from twisting forces.
- Start with light weight to master the rotational pattern before adding load. This is a power movement that requires coordination.

## Common mistakes

- Pulling with the arms instead of rotating through the core, which turns this into a cable row and misses the abs entirely.
- Keeping the feet planted flat and not pivoting, which limits rotational range and puts stress on the lower back and knees.
- Moving too fast without control, losing the mind-muscle connection with the obliques and core.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the Cable Judo Flip work?

Primarily the rectus abdominis and obliques through the rotational movement, with secondary work from the shoulders as they guide the cable.

### Is the Cable Judo Flip good for athletes?

Yes, the rotational power pattern directly transfers to sports that involve throwing, swinging, or grappling. It builds anti-rotation strength and explosive core power.

### What muscles does the Cable Judo Flip work?

The Cable Judo Flip primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the obliques, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Cable Judo Flip good for beginners?

The Cable Judo Flip is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of abs strength with easier variations first.
