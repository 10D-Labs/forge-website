# Weighted Svend Press

## Quick answer

A standing press that squeezes a weight plate between your palms and pushes it straight out from your chest. It works the chest, with help from the shoulders and triceps. Intermediate chest exercise using weight plate. Targets chest, also works shoulders, triceps.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Weight Plate
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Triceps
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart. Hold a weight plate in front of your chest, squeezed between your palms.
2. Keep squeezing and press the plate straight out in front of you until your arms are straight.
3. Bring the plate slowly back to your chest.

## Pro tips

- Press the plates together as hard as possible throughout the entire movement to maintain chest activation.
- Extend your arms fully at the end of each rep and squeeze the chest for a one-second hold.
- Keep the plates at chest height and press straight forward, not upward.

## Common mistakes

- Not squeezing the plates together hard enough, which eliminates the isometric chest contraction that makes this exercise effective.
- Letting the plates drift below chest height, shifting the load to the front delts.
- Using plates that are too heavy, which causes the arms to fatigue before the chest.

## FAQ

### What is the point of the Svend press?

It creates constant isometric tension in the chest through the squeezing action, combined with a pressing motion. It is excellent as a finisher or warmup for chest day.

### How much weight should you use for the Svend press?

Start with two 5-pound plates squeezed together. Even 10 total pounds is challenging because the isometric squeeze is the primary stimulus.

### Can the Svend press replace bench press?

No. The Svend press is a supplementary movement best used for muscle activation or as a finisher, not as a primary strength builder.

### What muscles does the Weighted Svend Press work?

The Weighted Svend Press primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the shoulders, triceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Weighted Svend Press good for beginners?

The Weighted Svend Press is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of chest strength with easier variations first.
