# Machine Lateral Raise

## Quick answer

A shoulder raise done sitting at a machine, lifting your bent arms out to the sides against pads. It works the side of the shoulders, with help from the upper back. Beginner shoulders exercise using machine. Targets shoulders, also works traps, upper back.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Traps, Upper Back
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Adjust the seat height and sit with your back against the pad.
2. Bend your elbows and rest your arms against the side pads, holding the handles with your palms facing down.
3. Raise your elbows out to the sides until your upper arms are level with the floor. Keep your elbows bent the whole time.
4. Lower your arms slowly back to your sides.

## Pro tips

- Adjust the machine so the pivot point aligns with your shoulder joint, and the pads rest against your outer forearms or elbows.
- Raise your arms to shoulder height (parallel to the floor) and no higher to maximize lateral deltoid engagement without impingement.
- Lower the weight slowly over 3 seconds. The eccentric phase is where much of the muscle-building stimulus occurs.

## Common mistakes

- Shrugging the traps up during the raise instead of keeping shoulders depressed and letting the deltoids do the lifting.
- Raising the arms above shoulder height, which shifts the work to the traps and can cause shoulder impingement.
- Using too much weight and only achieving a partial range of motion.

## FAQ

### Are machine Lateral Raises better than Dumbbell Lateral Raises?

Machine Lateral Raises provide constant tension throughout the range (including the bottom, where dumbbells provide almost no tension). Dumbbells allow a more natural arc. Use both for complete shoulder development.

### How do I target the lateral deltoid specifically?

Lead with your elbows, keep a slight forward lean, and focus on raising your arms directly to the sides. Avoid letting your hands drift forward, which engages the front deltoids.

### What muscles does the Machine Lateral Raise work?

The Machine Lateral Raise primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the traps, upper back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Machine Lateral Raise good for beginners?

Yes, the Machine Lateral Raise is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building shoulders strength.
