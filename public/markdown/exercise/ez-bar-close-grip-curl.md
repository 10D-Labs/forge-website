# EZ Bar Close Grip Curl

## Quick answer

A standing EZ bar curl with your hands close together and palms facing forward. It works the biceps, with the forearms helping. Beginner arms exercise using ez bar. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** EZ Bar
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand tall with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding an EZ bar with your palms facing forward and your hands close together.
2. Keep your elbows close to your sides and your upper arms still.
3. Curl the bar up toward your shoulders.
4. Lower it slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Grip the inner narrow angles of the EZ bar to shift emphasis toward the long head (outer biceps) and brachialis.
- Pin your elbows to your sides and only allow your forearms to move; any elbow drift forward means your front delts are helping.
- Squeeze hard at the top for one full second before lowering to maximize the peak contraction.

## Common mistakes

- Swinging the torso backward to generate momentum, which takes work away from the biceps.
- Letting the bar drop quickly on the eccentric rather than controlling the descent for 2-3 seconds.
- Gripping too tightly, which fatigues the forearms before the biceps are fully worked.

## FAQ

### What's the difference between close grip and wide grip EZ Bar Curls?

Close grip targets the long (outer) head of the biceps and the brachialis more, while wide grip shifts emphasis to the short (inner) head of the biceps.

### How much weight should I use for EZ Bar Close Grip Curls?

Start with about 60-70% of what you'd use on a standard EZ Bar Curl. The close grip reduces mechanical advantage, so lighter weight with stricter form is more effective.

### What muscles does the EZ Bar Close Grip Curl work?

The EZ Bar Close Grip Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the EZ Bar Close Grip Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the EZ Bar Close Grip Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building biceps strength.
