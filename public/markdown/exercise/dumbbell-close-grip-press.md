# Dumbbell Close Grip Press

## Quick answer

A dumbbell bench press with the dumbbells pressed together, palms facing each other and elbows tucked in. It works the triceps, with help from the chest and shoulders. Intermediate arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets triceps, also works chest, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Chest, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your back on a flat bench with your feet flat on the floor. Hold two dumbbells above your chest, arms straight, palms facing each other, dumbbells pressed together.
2. Keep the dumbbells touching and your elbows close to your sides as you lower them to the middle of your chest.
3. Press them back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Hold the dumbbells with a neutral grip (palms facing each other) and keep them touching or very close together throughout the press. This narrow position shifts the load to the triceps.
- Lower the dumbbells to the center of your chest with your elbows tight to your sides, then press straight up.
- Squeeze the dumbbells together at the top of each rep to maximize the tricep and inner chest contraction.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the dumbbells drift apart during the press, which turns this into a standard bench press and reduces tricep emphasis.
- Flaring the elbows outward, which shifts the load to the chest and shoulders and away from the triceps.
- Using too much weight and compensating by arching the back excessively.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Close Grip Press work?

Primarily the triceps with significant inner chest and anterior deltoid involvement. The close grip and neutral hand position shift the emphasis from the pecs to the triceps compared to a standard bench press.

### Dumbbell Close Grip Press vs close grip barbell bench: which is better?

Dumbbells allow a neutral grip which is easier on the wrists, and the pressing-together action engages the inner chest. The barbell version allows heavier loads. Both are excellent tricep builders.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Close Grip Press work?

The Dumbbell Close Grip Press primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the chest, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Close Grip Press good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Close Grip Press is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of triceps strength with easier variations first.
