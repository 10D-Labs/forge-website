# Dumbbell Incline Rear Lateral Raise

## Quick answer

A rear raise done chest-down on an incline bench, lifting the dumbbells out to the sides. It works the back of the shoulders, with help from the upper back. Intermediate shoulders exercise using dumbbell. Targets shoulders, also works traps, rhomboids.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Traps, Rhomboids
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set an incline bench to 45 degrees. Lean your chest against the pad with your feet on the floor behind it.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand, arms hanging straight down, palms facing each other.
3. Raise your arms out to the sides, elbows slightly bent, until they're level with the floor.
4. Lower them slowly back down.

## Pro tips

- Press your chest firmly into the incline bench to eliminate any cheating and isolate the rear delts completely.
- Think about driving your elbows toward the ceiling rather than lifting the dumbbells, which cues better rear delt activation.
- Squeeze your shoulder blades together at the top of the movement for a one-second hold to maximize rhomboid and rear delt engagement.

## Common mistakes

- Lifting the chest off the bench to use body momentum, which turns this into a Bent Over Row and reduces rear delt isolation.
- Pulling with the biceps instead of leading with the elbows, which shifts work away from the posterior delts.
- Raising the arms too high past parallel, which recruits the upper traps and takes tension off the rear delts.

## FAQ

### Why do rear Lateral Raises on an incline bench instead of standing bent over?

The bench supports your chest and eliminates momentum, which forces strict isolation of the rear delts and removes lower back strain from the bent-over position.

### What angle should the incline bench be at for rear Lateral Raises?

Set it to 30-45 degrees. Lower angles increase the range of motion and stretch on the rear delts.

### How many sets and reps for incline rear Lateral Raises?

Rear delts respond well to higher reps. Aim for 3-4 sets of 12-20 reps with lighter weight and strict form.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Incline Rear Lateral Raise work?

The Dumbbell Incline Rear Lateral Raise primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the traps, rhomboids. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Dumbbell Incline Rear Lateral Raise good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Incline Rear Lateral Raise is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of shoulders strength with easier variations first.
