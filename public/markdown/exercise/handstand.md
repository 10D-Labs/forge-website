# Handstand

## Quick answer

Balancing upside down on your hands after kicking up from standing, with no wall for support. It works the triceps and shoulders, and your abs help you hold the balance. Advanced arms exercise using body weight. Targets triceps, also works shoulders, core.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Core
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Balance

---

## How to perform

1. Stand in an open space with your arms straight overhead.
2. Step one foot forward and reach down to place your hands on the floor, shoulder-width apart, fingers pointing forward.
3. Kick your back leg up and bring the other to meet it, until you are upside down over your hands with no wall behind you.
4. Hold for the set time, keeping your body in a straight line. Push through straight arms and use your shoulders and abs to stay up.
5. Lower your legs slowly back to the floor.

## Pro tips

- Focus on pressing through your fingertips to make micro-balance adjustments rather than trying to hold a rigid position.
- Stack your joints: wrists under shoulders, shoulders over hips, hips over ankles, creating one straight line.
- Practice facing the wall (stomach toward wall) to build the correct body alignment before attempting freestanding holds.

## Common mistakes

- Arching the lower back (banana shape) instead of tucking the ribs and squeezing the glutes for a straight line.
- Looking at the ground directly below instead of slightly forward between your hands, which throws off alignment.
- Kicking up with too much force and consistently overshooting, rather than developing a controlled kick-up.

## FAQ

### How long does it take to learn a freestanding Handstand?

Most adults need 3-12 months of consistent practice (15-20 minutes daily) to hold a freestanding Handstand for 10+ seconds.

### Should I practice Handstands against a wall?

Yes, but face the wall (chest-to-wall) rather than back-to-wall. Chest-to-wall builds correct alignment, while back-to-wall reinforces the arched banana shape.

### How long should I hold a Handstand?

A 30-second freestanding hold is a solid intermediate benchmark. Focus on consistent 10-15 second holds first before chasing longer times.

### What muscles does the Handstand work?

The Handstand primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the shoulders, core. It's classified as a balance exercise for the arms.

### Is the Handstand good for beginners?

The Handstand is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental arms movements first.
