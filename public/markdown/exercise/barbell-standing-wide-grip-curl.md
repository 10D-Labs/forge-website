# Barbell Standing Wide Grip Curl

## Quick answer

A standing barbell curl with your hands set wider than your shoulders. It works the biceps, with help from the forearms. Beginner arms exercise using barbell. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a barbell with your palms facing up and your hands wider than your shoulders.
2. Keep your back straight and your elbows close to your sides.
3. Curl the bar up toward your shoulders without moving your upper arms.
4. Lower the bar slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Position your hands about 4-6 inches outside shoulder width to emphasize the short (inner) head of the biceps.
- Pin your elbows to your sides and keep your upper arms completely still throughout the curl.
- Squeeze at the top for a full second before lowering to maximize the peak contraction.

## Common mistakes

- Swinging the body to generate momentum, which is the most common cheat on standing Barbell Curls.
- Gripping so wide that the wrists are angled uncomfortably, causing forearm pain.
- Letting the elbows drift forward at the top, which shifts tension to the front deltoids.

## FAQ

### What does a wide grip target compared to a close grip curl?

The wide grip emphasizes the short (inner) head of the biceps, which contributes to bicep width. A close grip targets the long (outer) head, which builds the peak.

### Is the wide grip Barbell Curl safe for the wrists?

For most people, yes. If you experience wrist discomfort, try an EZ bar with a wide grip instead, which angles the wrists into a more natural position.

### How heavy should I go?

Use a weight you can curl for 8-12 reps with zero body swing. If you need to lean back or rock to complete a rep, the weight is too heavy.

### What muscles does the Barbell Standing Wide Grip Curl work?

The Barbell Standing Wide Grip Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Barbell Standing Wide Grip Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Barbell Standing Wide Grip Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building biceps strength.
