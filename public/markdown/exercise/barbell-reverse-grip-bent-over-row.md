# Barbell Reverse Grip Bent Over Row

## Quick answer

A bent-over barbell row with your palms facing forward instead of toward your legs. It works the upper back, with help from the biceps and forearms. Intermediate back exercise using barbell. Targets upper back, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart and hold a barbell with your hands shoulder-width apart, palms facing forward (away from your legs).
2. Bend your knees slightly and lean forward from your hips with a flat back, arms hanging straight.
3. Pull the bar up to your lower stomach, keeping your elbows close to your sides.
4. Lower it slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Use an underhand (supinated) grip, which shifts more work to the lower lats and biceps compared to an overhand row.
- Pull the bar to your lower stomach or belt line, keeping your elbows close to your body.
- Maintain a 45-60 degree torso angle and don't let your back round or rise during the set.

## Common mistakes

- Using the same weight as overhand rows and compensating with body swing, since the underhand grip is slightly stronger.
- Flaring the elbows wide, which shifts the emphasis away from the lower lats and toward the upper back.
- Rounding the lower back under heavy loads because the underhand grip tempts people to go heavier.

## FAQ

### Does underhand grip change which back muscles are worked?

Yes. The underhand grip recruits the lower lats and biceps more than the overhand version. It also allows a slightly longer range of motion. Overhand rows emphasize the upper back and rear delts more.

### Is the reverse grip row better for biceps?

It does involve the biceps more than an overhand row. However, the biceps are secondary movers. If your primary goal is bicep growth, direct curl work is more effective.

### Can I use the reverse grip row as my primary back exercise?

Yes. Dorian Yates famously built one of the most developed backs in bodybuilding history using underhand barbell rows as a primary back exercise. It's a proven mass builder.

### What muscles does the Barbell Reverse Grip Bent Over Row work?

The Barbell Reverse Grip Bent Over Row primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Barbell Reverse Grip Bent Over Row good for beginners?

The Barbell Reverse Grip Bent Over Row is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of upper back strength with easier variations first.
