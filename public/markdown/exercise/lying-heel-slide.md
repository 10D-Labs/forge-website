# Lying Heel Slide

## Quick answer

A lying move where you slide one heel toward your glutes and back out, bending and straightening the knee. It works the hamstrings on the back of the thigh. Intermediate legs exercise using body weight. Targets hamstrings, also works glutes, quadriceps.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Hamstrings
- **Secondary Muscles:** Glutes, Quadriceps
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Mobility

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your back on the floor with your legs straight and your arms resting at your sides.
2. Slide one heel along the floor toward your glutes, bending your knee until it points up at the ceiling.
3. Keep the other leg straight and flat on the floor.
4. Slide the heel slowly back out until your leg is straight. Do all your reps on one leg, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Lie on your back with your legs straight and slide one heel along the floor toward your glutes.
- Keep your heel in contact with the floor the whole time and move slowly.
- Straighten the leg back out under control before switching sides.

## Common mistakes

- Lifting the heel off the floor instead of sliding it.
- Arching the lower back as the knee bends.
- Rushing through the reps instead of moving slowly.

## FAQ

### What do Lying Heel Slides work?

They work the hamstrings as the knee bends, and they help restore knee movement. They are common in rehab after knee injuries.

### What can I use to make the heel slide easier?

A towel or sock under your heel on a smooth floor reduces friction.

### How many reps should I do?

10-15 slow reps per leg, 2-3 sets. If you are recovering from an injury, follow your physical therapist's plan.

### What muscles does the Lying Heel Slide work?

The Lying Heel Slide primarily targets the hamstrings, with secondary activation in the glutes, quadriceps. It's classified as a mobility exercise for the legs.

### Is the Lying Heel Slide good for beginners?

The Lying Heel Slide is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of hamstrings strength with easier variations first.
