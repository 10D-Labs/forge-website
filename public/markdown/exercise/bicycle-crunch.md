# Bicycle Crunch

## Quick answer

A crunch where you twist one elbow toward the opposite knee while your legs pedal in the air. It works the abs, especially the obliques at your sides, and the front of your hips. Beginner core exercise using body weight. Targets abs, also works hip flexors.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hip Flexors
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your back with your hands behind your head.
2. Lift your legs off the floor with your knees bent to 90 degrees.
3. Bring your right elbow toward your left knee as you straighten your right leg.
4. Switch sides, bringing your left elbow toward your right knee as you straighten your left leg. Keep alternating in a pedaling motion.

## Pro tips

- Rotate your entire torso so your shoulder blade lifts off the ground, not just your elbow; the elbow should follow the rotation, not lead it.
- Extend the straight leg fully and keep it hovering 6-12 inches off the ground to maintain tension on your lower abs.
- Slow down the pedaling motion to a 2-second rotation per side instead of speed-cycling through reps.

## Common mistakes

- Pulling the head forward with the hands instead of rotating the thoracic spine, which strains the neck and reduces oblique activation.
- Moving the elbows toward the knees without actually rotating the torso, turning it into a neck exercise.
- Pedaling too fast and using momentum, which eliminates the time under tension that makes this exercise effective.

## FAQ

### Are Bicycle Crunches better than regular crunches?

Bicycle Crunches activate the rectus abdominis and obliques more than standard crunches according to ACE research. They're a more complete ab exercise because they combine rotation with flexion.

### How many Bicycle Crunches should I do per set?

Aim for 12-20 reps per side (24-40 total touches). If you can easily do more than 20 per side with good form, slow down the tempo rather than adding more reps.

### Why does my neck hurt during Bicycle Crunches?

You're likely pulling your head forward with your hands. Lightly touch your fingertips behind your ears instead of clasping your hands, and focus on lifting your shoulder blade off the ground through torso rotation.

### What muscles does the Bicycle Crunch work?

The Bicycle Crunch primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the hip flexors. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Bicycle Crunch good for beginners?

Yes, the Bicycle Crunch is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building abs strength.
