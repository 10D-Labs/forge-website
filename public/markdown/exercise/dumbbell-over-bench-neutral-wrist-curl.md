# Dumbbell Over Bench Neutral Wrist Curl

## Quick answer

A wrist exercise done kneeling at a bench with your forearms resting on it, tipping upright dumbbells forward and back with your thumbs up. It works the forearms. Beginner arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Forearms

- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Kneel behind a flat bench and rest your forearms across it, hands past the far edge. Hold the dumbbells upright, palms facing each other (thumbs up).
2. Keeping your forearms on the bench, bend your wrists to let the dumbbells tip forward and down toward the floor.
3. Tilt your wrists back up until the dumbbells stand upright again.

## Pro tips

- Drape your forearms over the bench edge with a neutral (thumbs-up) grip and let your wrists hang freely for full range.
- Curl the dumbbells upward by flexing the wrists, squeezing at the top to engage the brachioradialis.
- Keep your forearms pressed firmly against the bench surface throughout to isolate the wrist and forearm muscles.

## Common mistakes

- Allowing the forearms to lift off the bench, which introduces bicep curling instead of pure wrist flexion.
- Using a grip that rotates to palms-up or palms-down, losing the neutral position that targets the brachioradialis.
- Jerking the weight up with fast reps instead of using controlled movements that keep tension on the forearm.

## FAQ

### What does the neutral grip wrist curl target differently?

The neutral (hammer) grip shifts emphasis to the brachioradialis along the outer forearm, compared to palms-up curls that hit the inner forearm flexors.

### Why use a bench instead of your thigh for wrist curls?

The bench provides a firmer, flatter surface that keeps your forearms more stable and allows for a greater wrist range of motion.

### How many sets and reps for over-bench neutral wrist curls?

2-3 sets of 15-20 reps. Forearm muscles benefit from higher rep ranges and short rest periods (30-60 seconds).

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Over Bench Neutral Wrist Curl work?

The Dumbbell Over Bench Neutral Wrist Curl primarily targets the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Over Bench Neutral Wrist Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Over Bench Neutral Wrist Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building forearms strength.
