# Cable Upper Row

## Quick answer

A seated cable row pulled high to your upper chest with your elbows out wide. It works the upper back and rear shoulders, with help from the biceps. Beginner back exercise using cable. Targets upper back, also works biceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Attach a short straight bar to the low pulley and sit on the bench facing the machine, feet flat on the floor.
2. Hold the bar with your palms facing down, hands close together, arms straight in front of you.
3. Keep your torso still and pull the bar up toward your upper chest, lifting your elbows out to the sides until they're level with your shoulders.
4. Return slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Attach the cable at chest height and pull toward your upper chest or collarbone to emphasize the upper back and rear delts.
- Squeeze your shoulder blades together and hold for a count at peak contraction before releasing.
- Stand far enough from the stack that the cable has tension even at full arm extension.

## Common mistakes

- Pulling the bar to the stomach instead of the upper chest, which turns it into a standard row rather than an upper back-focused movement.
- Leaning too far back and using momentum to pull the weight instead of keeping the torso upright.
- Using a grip that is too narrow, reducing the emphasis on the rear delts and upper traps.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between a Cable Upper Row and a Face Pull?

A Cable Upper Row pulls to the chest with elbows below shoulder height, targeting the mid and upper back. A Face Pull pulls to the face with elbows at shoulder height, targeting the rear delts more.

### What attachment works best for Cable Upper Rows?

A straight bar or wide-grip lat bar works best because it allows a wide grip that activates the upper back muscles more effectively than a V-bar or rope.

### Where should this fit in a back workout?

Use it as a secondary or finishing movement after your heavy compound rows and Pullups. Two to three sets of 12-15 reps is effective.

### What muscles does the Cable Upper Row work?

The Cable Upper Row primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the biceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Cable Upper Row good for beginners?

Yes, the Cable Upper Row is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building upper back strength.
