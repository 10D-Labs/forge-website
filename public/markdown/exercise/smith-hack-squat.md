# Smith Hack Squat

## Quick answer

A squat holding a Smith machine bar behind your legs with straight arms. It works the glutes and quads. Intermediate legs exercise using smith machine. Targets quads, also works glutes, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Smith Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Quads
- **Secondary Muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your back to a Smith machine bar set just below knee height, feet hip-width apart.
2. Squat down and grip the bar behind you, hands just outside your hips, palms facing back. Unhook it.
3. Stand up tall with your arms straight until the bar is behind your upper thighs.
4. Bend your knees and lower your hips straight down until your thighs are level with the floor. Keep your chest up.
5. Push through your feet to stand back up.

## Pro tips

- Place your feet slightly forward of the bar to shift the emphasis to the quads rather than the glutes.
- Keep your core braced and your torso upright throughout the movement.
- Descend until your thighs are at least parallel to the floor for full quad activation.

## Common mistakes

- Placing the feet directly under the bar (as in a regular squat), which doesn't take advantage of the Hack Squat angle.
- Leaning forward excessively, which loads the lower back instead of the quads.
- Locking the knees at the top of each rep, which stresses the joint under load.

## FAQ

### How is the Smith Hack Squat different from a regular Smith Squat?

In the Smith Hack Squat, your feet are positioned forward of the bar and the emphasis shifts heavily to the quads. In a regular Smith Squat, the feet are under the bar for a more balanced squat pattern.

### Where should I place my feet for the Smith Hack Squat?

Position your feet about 12-18 inches in front of the bar line, shoulder-width apart. The further forward, the more quad-dominant the exercise becomes.

### Is the Smith Hack Squat good for people with back problems?

The guided bar path and the ability to position feet forward can reduce lower back stress compared to free weight squats. It's often better tolerated by people with minor back issues.

### What muscles does the Smith Hack Squat work?

The Smith Hack Squat primarily targets the quads, with secondary activation in the glutes, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Smith Hack Squat good for beginners?

The Smith Hack Squat is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of quads strength with easier variations first.
