# Resistance Band Seated Bicep Curl

## Quick answer

A biceps curl done sitting down, using a resistance band instead of weights. It works the biceps. Beginner arms exercise using resistance band. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on a bench or chair with your back straight and your feet flat on the floor.
2. Hold the resistance band with your palms facing forward and your arms straight down by your sides.
3. Keep your upper arms still and curl your hands up toward your shoulders.
4. Lower your hands slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Pin your elbows to your sides and do not let them drift forward during the curl.
- Sit on the middle of the band to anchor it and maintain consistent tension throughout.
- Squeeze your biceps hard at the top of each curl and lower for a 2-second eccentric.

## Common mistakes

- Allowing the elbows to move forward during the curl, which shifts the load to the front delts.
- Not anchoring the band properly, which results in uneven resistance between the left and right sides.
- Rushing through reps. Band curls are most effective with a slow, controlled tempo.

## FAQ

### Can I build biceps with resistance bands?

Yes, especially with progressive tension bands. The biceps respond to tension regardless of whether it comes from a band, dumbbell, or cable.

### Are band curls easier or harder than dumbbell curls?

They are easier at the bottom (stretched position) and harder at the top (contracted position) due to how band tension increases with stretch. This different resistance curve is complementary to free weights.

### How do I increase difficulty for band Bicep Curls?

Use a thicker band, double-wrap the band under your feet, or slow down the tempo. You can also add a 3-second pause at the top of each curl.

### What muscles does the Resistance Band Seated Bicep Curl work?

The Resistance Band Seated Bicep Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Resistance Band Seated Bicep Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Resistance Band Seated Bicep Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building biceps strength.
