# Barbell Clean and Press

## Quick answer

A barbell lift from the floor to your shoulders, then a press overhead. It works the quads and trains strength, power and coordination across your whole body. Advanced legs exercise using barbell. Targets quads, also works hamstrings, glutes, shoulders, triceps.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Quads
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Glutes, Shoulders, Triceps
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and a barbell on the floor in front of you.
2. Bend your knees and hips to grip the bar with your palms facing you, hands a little wider than your shoulders.
3. Push through your heels and straighten your hips and knees to lift the bar off the floor, keeping it close to your body.
4. As the bar passes your thighs, drive your hips forward hard, shrug your shoulders and pull the bar up toward your chest.
5. Drop under the bar and catch it at your shoulders, elbows pointing forward and palms facing up.
6. Press the bar overhead until your arms are straight, then lower it back to the floor.

## Pro tips

- Start the clean with the bar over your mid-foot, arms straight, and hips loaded; think of it as a jump, not an arm pull.
- Catch the bar in a front rack position with your elbows high before initiating the press overhead.
- Keep the bar path as close to your body as possible throughout the entire movement for maximum efficiency and safety.

## Common mistakes

- Pulling the bar with the arms instead of driving through the legs and hips, which limits the weight you can handle and increases injury risk.
- Pressing the bar overhead before fully standing up from the clean, which leaks power and compromises form.
- Letting the bar drift forward away from the body during the clean, putting excessive strain on the lower back.

## FAQ

### Is the Barbell Clean and Press good for building muscle?

Yes. It's one of the most comprehensive full-body exercises, hitting the quads, glutes, hamstrings, shoulders, traps, and triceps in a single movement. It also builds explosive power and coordination.

### How heavy should I go on clean and press?

Start with just the bar to learn the technique. Even experienced lifters often use moderate weight (60-70% of their strict press max) because the clean demands proper positioning. Technique should always come before load.

### What's the difference between a clean and press and a clean and jerk?

The press uses strict shoulder pressing to get the bar overhead. The jerk uses a dip-and-drive with the legs to propel the bar up, allowing much heavier loads. The clean portion is the same.

### What muscles does the Barbell Clean and Press work?

The Barbell Clean and Press primarily targets the quads, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, glutes, shoulders, triceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Barbell Clean and Press good for beginners?

The Barbell Clean and Press is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental legs movements first.
