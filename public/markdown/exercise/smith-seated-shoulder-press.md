# Smith Seated Shoulder Press

## Quick answer

A shoulder press done seated in a Smith machine, with the bar moving on fixed rails in front of your face. It works the front of the shoulders, with help from the triceps and upper back. Beginner shoulders exercise using smith machine. Targets shoulders, also works triceps, upper back.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Smith Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Upper Back
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set the seat so the bar is about shoulder height. Sit with your back against the pad and your feet flat on the floor.
2. Grab the bar with your palms facing forward and unhook it, holding it at shoulder level in front of your face.
3. Press the bar up until your arms are straight overhead.
4. Lower it slowly back to shoulder level.

## Pro tips

- Position the bench so the bar lowers to just below chin level, in front of your face, not behind your head.
- Press straight up and finish with the bar directly over your shoulders, not in front of you.
- Keep your core braced and avoid excessive arching of the lower back.

## Common mistakes

- Positioning the bench too far forward or back, causing the bar to track awkwardly relative to your body.
- Arching the lower back excessively to press heavier weight, turning it into an incline press.
- Not lowering the bar far enough; bring it to at least chin level for a full range of motion.

## FAQ

### Is the seated Smith Shoulder Press safe for the shoulders?

The fixed bar path can be an issue if it doesn't match your natural pressing arc. Position the bench so the bar tracks comfortably in front of your face. If it feels forced or painful, switch to dumbbells.

### Should I press in front of or behind my head?

Always press in front. Behind-the-neck pressing places the shoulder in an externally rotated and abducted position under load, which significantly increases impingement and rotator cuff injury risk.

### How does the seated version differ from standing?

The seated version removes the leg drive and core stability component, isolating the shoulders more. It typically allows you to focus on pressing strength without worrying about balance.

### What muscles does the Smith Seated Shoulder Press work?

The Smith Seated Shoulder Press primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the triceps, upper back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Smith Seated Shoulder Press good for beginners?

Yes, the Smith Seated Shoulder Press is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building shoulders strength.
