# Barbell Glute Bridge

## Quick answer

A glute bridge on the floor with a barbell across your hips for extra weight. It works the glutes, with help from the hamstrings and lower back. Intermediate legs exercise using barbell. Targets glutes, also works hamstrings, lower back.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Lower Back
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your back on the floor with your knees bent and your feet flat.
2. Rest a barbell across your hips and hold it in place with both hands.
3. Push your hips up until your body forms a straight line from your knees to your shoulders.
4. Lower your hips slowly back to the floor.

## Pro tips

- Use a thick bar pad or folded towel across your hips to prevent the barbell from digging into your hip bones.
- Set up with your shoulder blades against the ground and your feet about hip-width apart, heels roughly 12 inches from your glutes.
- Drive through your heels and squeeze your glutes at the top until your body forms a straight line from knees to shoulders.

## Common mistakes

- Pushing through the toes instead of the heels, which shifts work from the glutes to the quads.
- Hyperextending the lower back at the top by pushing the hips too high, which compresses the lumbar spine.
- Placing the feet too far from the glutes, which turns it into a hamstring-dominant exercise.

## FAQ

### How heavy should I go on Barbell Glute Bridges?

Most people can eventually work up to 1.5-2 times their bodyweight. Start light to learn the movement pattern and add weight progressively each week.

### Glute bridge vs hip thrust: what is the difference?

A glute bridge is done with your back on the floor, while a hip thrust has your upper back elevated on a bench. The hip thrust provides a greater range of motion.

### How do I get the barbell into position?

Roll the barbell over your legs until it sits in the crease of your hips. Use bumper plates for easier rolling, or have a partner help position it.

### What muscles does the Barbell Glute Bridge work?

The Barbell Glute Bridge primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, lower back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Barbell Glute Bridge good for beginners?

The Barbell Glute Bridge is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of glutes strength with easier variations first.
