# Band Seated Twist

## Quick answer

A seated twist on the floor against a band anchored to your side, turning away from it as you straighten your arms. It works the abs and the obliques along your sides. Beginner core exercise using resistance band. Targets abs, also works obliques.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Obliques
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Anchor a band to one side of you at chest height. Sit on the floor with your legs straight out in front.
2. Hold the handle in both hands, one wrapped over the other, thumbs up. Turn toward the anchor with your hands near your chest.
3. Turn your chest away from the anchor and push your arms straight out until they point over your feet.
4. Return slowly to the start. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Sit with a tall spine and twist from the ribcage, not the hips, to target the obliques.
- Keep the band taut throughout the entire range of motion for constant tension.
- Control the rotation in both directions rather than letting the band pull you back to center.

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the back and slouching as you twist, which reduces oblique engagement and loads the spine poorly.
- Moving only the arms without actually rotating the torso.
- Twisting too far, which can strain the lower back when done under resistance.

## FAQ

### Are seated twists safe for the lower back?

Yes, if done with a tall spine and moderate resistance. The seated position stabilizes the hips so the rotation comes from the thoracic spine where it should.

### How far should you twist?

Rotate only as far as comfortable, typically 45-60 degrees to each side. Do not force the rotation beyond your natural range.

### What is the difference between seated twists and Russian Twists?

Russian Twists add a lean-back component that loads the rectus abdominis more. Seated twists focus purely on rotation with an upright torso.

### What muscles does the Band Seated Twist work?

The Band Seated Twist primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the obliques. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Band Seated Twist good for beginners?

Yes, the Band Seated Twist is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building abs strength.
