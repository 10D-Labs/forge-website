# Stairmaster (Distance)

## Quick answer

Cardio on a stair-climbing machine, stepping up moving stairs while you hold the handrails, with distance usually counted in floors. It works your heart and lungs, plus your legs. Beginner cardio exercise using cardio machine. Targets cardiovascular, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, glutes, calves.

- **Body Part:** Cardio
- **Equipment:** Cardio Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Cardiovascular
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Cardio

---

## How to perform

1. Set the stair machine to a comfortable level.
2. Step onto the stairs and rest your hands on the handrails for support.
3. Climb one step at a time, stepping up with one foot and then the other.
4. Stay upright and keep your abs tight.
5. Keep going for the set distance.

## Pro tips

- Stand upright without leaning on the console or handrails to fully engage your legs and core.
- Drive through each step with your full foot, not just the toes, to engage the glutes and hamstrings.
- Find a sustainable pace that you can maintain consistently throughout the entire distance.

## Common mistakes

- Leaning heavily on the handrails or console, which dramatically reduces the workload on your legs.
- Taking tiny, rapid steps instead of full, controlled steps that engage the posterior chain.
- Hunching forward over the console, which compresses the spine and limits breathing.

## FAQ

### Is the Stairmaster good for building glutes?

It's one of the best cardio machines for glute activation, especially when you take full, deliberate steps and avoid holding the rails. It combines endurance training with glute engagement.

### What level should I set the Stairmaster to?

Start at level 4-6 and adjust based on your fitness level. You should be able to maintain the pace without holding on. If you need the rails, lower the level.

### Stairmaster vs incline treadmill: which is better?

Both are excellent for lower body cardio. The Stairmaster requires slightly more quad and glute strength per step, while the incline treadmill is more sustainable for longer durations.

### What muscles does the Stairmaster (Distance) work?

The Stairmaster (Distance) primarily targets the cardiovascular, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, glutes, calves. It's classified as a cardio exercise for the cardio.

### Is the Stairmaster (Distance) good for beginners?

Yes, the Stairmaster (Distance) is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building cardiovascular strength.
