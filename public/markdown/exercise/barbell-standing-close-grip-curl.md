# Barbell Standing Close Grip Curl

## Quick answer

A standing barbell curl with your hands close together and palms facing forward. It works the biceps, with the forearms helping. Beginner arms exercise using barbell. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand tall with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a barbell with your palms facing forward and your hands close together.
2. Keep your elbows close to your sides and your upper arms still.
3. Curl the bar up to your shoulders.
4. Lower it slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Position your hands 6-8 inches apart on the bar to emphasize the outer (long) head of the biceps.
- Keep your elbows pinned to your sides and your upper body completely still throughout the curl.
- Squeeze the biceps hard at the top and lower slowly for 2-3 seconds to maximize the eccentric stimulus.

## Common mistakes

- Placing the hands too close together (touching), which strains the wrists without additional bicep benefit.
- Swinging the torso to generate momentum as the set gets harder.
- Cutting the range of motion short at the bottom by not fully extending the arms.

## FAQ

### Does close grip change which part of the bicep is worked?

Yes. A close grip (hands inside shoulder width) emphasizes the outer (long) head of the biceps, which contributes more to the bicep peak. A wide grip shifts emphasis to the inner (short) head.

### How close should my hands be for close grip curls?

About 6-8 inches apart works well. Going closer than that stresses the wrists without providing additional bicep stimulus.

### Should I use close grip curls as my main bicep exercise?

It works well as one of multiple bicep exercises. Rotating between close grip, shoulder width, and wide grip curls ensures complete bicep development.

### What muscles does the Barbell Standing Close Grip Curl work?

The Barbell Standing Close Grip Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Barbell Standing Close Grip Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Barbell Standing Close Grip Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building biceps strength.
