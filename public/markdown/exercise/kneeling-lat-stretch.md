# Kneeling Lat Stretch

## Quick answer

A stretch on your hands and knees, sliding one arm forward along the floor and letting your chest sink. It stretches the lats along the side of your back and the shoulder. Beginner back exercise using body weight. Targets lats, also works shoulders, triceps.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Triceps
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Stretching

---

## How to perform

1. Get on your hands and knees with your hands under your shoulders and your knees under your hips.
2. Slide one hand forward along the floor until that forearm rests flat on it. Keep your other hand where it is.
3. Let your chest sink toward the floor until you feel a stretch down that side of your back.
4. Hold for the set time, then slide your hand back slowly and switch sides.

## Pro tips

- Sit your hips back toward your heels as you lean to each side to deepen the lat stretch.
- Breathe deeply into the side being stretched, expanding the ribcage to increase the stretch intensity.
- Hold each side for at least 20 seconds and do not bounce or pulse, just steady pressure.

## Common mistakes

- Leaning forward instead of directly to the side, which stretches the lower back rather than the lats.
- Holding breath during the stretch, which increases muscle tension and reduces effectiveness.
- Rushing through the stretch without holding long enough for the muscle to actually release.

## FAQ

### When should I do the Kneeling Lat Stretch?

It works well as a warm-up before Pullups or rows, between sets of back exercises, or as part of a cooldown routine.

### How long should I hold the Kneeling Lat Stretch?

Hold each side for 20-30 seconds for maintenance, or 45-60 seconds if you are trying to improve lat flexibility.

### Why are tight lats a problem?

Tight lats can limit overhead shoulder mobility, contribute to poor posture (rounded shoulders), and restrict your ability to perform exercises like overhead presses with proper form.

### What muscles does the Kneeling Lat Stretch work?

The Kneeling Lat Stretch primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the shoulders, triceps. It's classified as a stretching exercise for the back.

### Is the Kneeling Lat Stretch good for beginners?

Yes, the Kneeling Lat Stretch is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building lats strength.
