# Kettlebell Single Arm Clean and Jerk

## Quick answer

A one-arm kettlebell lift in two parts: you pull it from between your legs to your shoulder, then use a quick knee dip to drive it overhead. It works the front of the shoulders, plus the triceps and core. Advanced shoulders exercise using kettlebell. Targets shoulders, also works triceps, core.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Core
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a kettlebell in one hand, palm facing your body.
2. Bend your knees and hinge at your hips to lower the kettlebell between your legs.
3. Stand up fast, pulling the kettlebell to your shoulder. Let it rest on the back of your forearm, palm facing in.
4. Dip your knees slightly, then straighten them hard and punch the kettlebell straight up until your arm locks out overhead.
5. Lower it to your shoulder, then back down between your legs.

## Pro tips

- The clean and jerk is two distinct movements: first clean the bell to the rack, then use a dip-and-drive to jerk it overhead.
- During the jerk, dip your knees quickly (4-6 inches), then explode upward while simultaneously punching the bell overhead.
- Lock out the overhead position with your bicep near your ear and your wrist straight before lowering.

## Common mistakes

- Trying to strict press the weight overhead after the clean instead of using the leg dip to generate upward momentum.
- Not fully completing the clean before initiating the jerk, blending the two movements into a sloppy single motion.
- Hyperextending the lower back at lockout instead of squeezing the glutes and bracing the core.

## FAQ

### What's the difference between a clean and jerk and a clean and press?

A clean and press uses strict shoulder strength to push the weight overhead. A clean and jerk uses a quick knee dip and explosive leg drive, allowing you to handle heavier weights and making it more of a power exercise.

### Is the single arm clean and jerk good for fat loss?

Yes. It's one of the most metabolically demanding kettlebell exercises because it involves the entire body through multiple planes of motion in a single complex movement.

### What muscles does the Kettlebell Single Arm Clean and Jerk work?

The Kettlebell Single Arm Clean and Jerk primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the triceps, core. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Kettlebell Single Arm Clean and Jerk good for beginners?

The Kettlebell Single Arm Clean and Jerk is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental shoulders movements first.
