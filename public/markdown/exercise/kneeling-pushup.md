# Kneeling Pushup

## Quick answer

A pushup done from your knees, an easier version of the full pushup. It works the chest, with help from the triceps and shoulders. Beginner chest exercise using body weight. Targets chest, also works triceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Kneel on the floor and lean forward onto your hands, placed flat under your shoulders, fingers pointing forward.
2. Keep your body in a straight line from your head to your knees.
3. Bend your elbows to lower your chest until it nearly touches the floor.
4. Push back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Maintain a straight line from your knees to your head; do not let your hips sag or pike.
- Lower your chest between your hands and keep your elbows at about 45 degrees from your body.
- Fully extend your arms at the top of each rep to complete the range of motion.

## Common mistakes

- Placing the knees too far back, which makes the exercise too easy and reduces chest and tricep activation.
- Letting the hips sag, which strains the lower back instead of engaging the core.
- Only performing half reps by not lowering the chest close to the floor.

## FAQ

### Are Kneeling Pushups effective for building chest strength?

Yes, they target the same muscles as regular Pushups (chest, triceps, shoulders) at reduced difficulty. They are the standard progression toward full Pushups.

### How many Kneeling Pushups should I do before trying regular Pushups?

Once you can do 3 sets of 15-20 Kneeling Pushups with good form, try regular Pushups. You may only get a few reps at first, and that is normal.

### Where should I position my knees for Kneeling Pushups?

Place them far enough back that your body forms a straight line from knees to head, not directly under your hips. A mat or towel under the knees adds comfort.

### What muscles does the Kneeling Pushup work?

The Kneeling Pushup primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Kneeling Pushup good for beginners?

Yes, the Kneeling Pushup is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building chest strength.
