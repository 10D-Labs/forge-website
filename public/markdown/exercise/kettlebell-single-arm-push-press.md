# Kettlebell Single Arm Push Press

## Quick answer

A one-arm kettlebell overhead press that starts with a small knee dip, so your legs help drive the weight up. It works the shoulders, with help from the triceps and abs. Intermediate shoulders exercise using kettlebell. Targets shoulders, also works triceps, core.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Core
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a kettlebell in one hand at shoulder height.
2. Bend your knees slightly and tighten your abs.
3. Straighten your legs hard and use that push to press the kettlebell up until your arm is straight overhead.
4. Lower the kettlebell back to your shoulder, bending your knees slightly as it comes down. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Start with the kettlebell in a solid rack position with your elbow tight to your body and the bell resting against your forearm and upper arm.
- Dip quickly by bending your knees 4-6 inches, then drive hard through your legs to launch the kettlebell upward.
- Time the arm extension to catch the momentum from the leg drive. The arm finishes the press, but the legs initiate the power.

## Common mistakes

- Dipping too deep, which wastes time and energy and breaks the rhythm of the explosive drive.
- Leaning back during the press instead of staying vertical and letting the legs do the initial work.
- Not using any leg drive at all, making it a strict press instead of a push press.

## FAQ

### When should I use a push press instead of a strict press?

Use a push press when you want to move more weight overhead, when training for power, or when your shoulders are fatigued from strict pressing and you want to continue working. It's also better for conditioning workouts.

### Can I use the push press to improve my strict press?

Yes. The push press overloads the lockout position with heavier weight than you can strict press, building strength in the top range. Use push presses with heavy weight and strict presses with moderate weight for best results.

### What muscles does the Kettlebell Single Arm Push Press work?

The Kettlebell Single Arm Push Press primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the triceps, core. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Kettlebell Single Arm Push Press good for beginners?

The Kettlebell Single Arm Push Press is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of shoulders strength with easier variations first.
