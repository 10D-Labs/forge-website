# Smith Narrow Row

## Quick answer

A bent-over row on a Smith machine with your hands close together. It works the upper back, with help from the biceps and the back of the shoulders. Beginner back exercise using smith machine. Targets upper back, also works biceps, rear deltoids.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Smith Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Rear Deltoids
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand facing a Smith machine bar, feet hip-width apart. Grip it with your hands a little closer than shoulder-width, palms facing your legs.
2. Unhook the bar, bend your knees slightly and lean forward from your hips with a flat back, arms hanging straight.
3. Pull the bar up to your stomach, driving your elbows back.
4. Lower it slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Pull the handles toward your lower sternum area and squeeze your shoulder blades together at peak contraction.
- Keep your chest up and maintain a neutral spine throughout the movement.
- Use a 2-second squeeze at the top of each rep to maximize mid-back engagement.

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the upper back and losing the chest-up position during the pull.
- Using too much weight and relying on momentum instead of controlled rowing.
- Pulling with the biceps instead of initiating the movement with the back muscles.

## FAQ

### What does the narrow grip target differently from a wide grip row?

The narrow grip increases the range of motion and shifts emphasis to the lower lats and mid-back thickness. A wider grip targets the upper back and rear delts more.

### Is the Smith Narrow Row good for lat development?

Yes, the narrow grip and extended range of motion make it effective for lat thickness. It complements wide-grip pulling movements like Pullups and lat pulldowns.

### Should I lean forward during Smith Narrow Rows?

A slight forward lean (about 15-20 degrees from vertical) is fine and can increase the range of motion. Avoid excessive leaning, which turns it into a Bent Over Row and loads the lower back.

### What muscles does the Smith Narrow Row work?

The Smith Narrow Row primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the biceps, rear deltoids. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Smith Narrow Row good for beginners?

Yes, the Smith Narrow Row is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building upper back strength.
