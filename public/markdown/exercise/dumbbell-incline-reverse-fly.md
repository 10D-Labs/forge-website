# Dumbbell Incline Reverse Fly

## Quick answer

A reverse fly done lying face down on an incline bench, so the bench holds your body still. It works the rear shoulders and the upper back. Intermediate shoulders exercise using dumbbell. Targets shoulders, also works trapezius, rhomboids.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Trapezius, Rhomboids
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie face down on an incline bench set to about 45 degrees, chest on the pad, legs straight and toes on the floor.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand, arms hanging straight down, palms facing each other.
3. Keep a slight bend in your elbows and raise the dumbbells out to the sides until your arms are level with your shoulders.
4. Lower slowly back to the start.

## Pro tips

- Lie chest down on an incline bench and let the dumbbells hang below your shoulders, then raise them out to the sides in a wide arc.
- Squeeze your shoulder blades together at the top of each rep for one second.
- Keep a slight bend in your elbows throughout, as if you are hugging a large tree.

## Common mistakes

- Using too much arm bend, which turns the fly into a row and shifts the load from the rear delts to the lats.
- Lifting your chest off the bench to swing the weights up.
- Setting the bench too steep, which turns the movement into more of a lateral raise.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between a reverse fly and a rear delt row?

A reverse fly raises the arms out to the sides with nearly straight arms, isolating the rear delts. A row pulls the elbows back with bent arms, using more of the lats, rhomboids and biceps.

### Can I do reverse flyes on a machine instead?

Yes. The reverse pec deck gives steady resistance and makes it easy to isolate the rear delts. Dumbbells on an incline bench give a little more freedom of movement.

### How many sets per week for rear delts?

Most people do well with 8-12 sets per week of direct rear delt work, split across 2-3 sessions.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Incline Reverse Fly work?

The Dumbbell Incline Reverse Fly primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the trapezius, rhomboids. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Dumbbell Incline Reverse Fly good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Incline Reverse Fly is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of shoulders strength with easier variations first.
