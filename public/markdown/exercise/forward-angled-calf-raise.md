# Forward Angled Calf Raise

## Quick answer

A standing calf raise done in a hack squat machine while facing the back pad, with the weight on your shoulders. It works the calves. Beginner legs exercise using machine. Targets calves, also works hamstrings, glutes.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Calves
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Glutes
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Step into a hack squat machine facing the back pad, chest against it and shoulders under the shoulder pads. Hold the handles.
2. Stand with your feet flat on the footplate, hip-width apart, and your legs nearly straight.
3. Raise your heels as high as you can, pushing the pads up with your shoulders.
4. Lower your heels slowly back down.

## Pro tips

- Lean your torso forward slightly against the sled to change the ankle angle and hit the calves differently than a standard calf raise.
- Rise all the way up onto the balls of your feet and hold the peak contraction for 2 seconds.
- Lower your heels slowly below the platform to get a full stretch at the bottom of each rep.

## Common mistakes

- Bouncing at the bottom of the rep instead of pausing in the stretched position.
- Bending the knees during the calf raise, which shifts the load from the gastrocnemius to the soleus.
- Using excessive weight that limits the range of motion to a few inches.

## FAQ

### How is the Forward Angled Calf Raise different from a regular calf raise?

The forward body angle changes the line of force through the ankle, which can shift emphasis slightly and provide variety for the calves. It's also more comfortable for people with lower back sensitivity.

### What rep range works best for calf raises?

Calves are endurance muscles with a high proportion of slow-twitch fibers. Use 15-25 reps per set with a controlled tempo for best results.

### How often should I train calves?

Calves recover quickly and can be trained 3-4 times per week. They respond well to high frequency and high volume since they're used to daily walking.

### What muscles does the Forward Angled Calf Raise work?

The Forward Angled Calf Raise primarily targets the calves, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, glutes. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Forward Angled Calf Raise good for beginners?

Yes, the Forward Angled Calf Raise is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building calves strength.
