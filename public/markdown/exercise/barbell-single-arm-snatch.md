# Barbell Single Arm Snatch

## Quick answer

An explosive one-arm lift that takes a barbell from the floor to straight overhead in one move, catching it in a squat. It works the shoulders, with the legs, hips and upper back driving the pull. Advanced shoulders exercise using barbell. Targets shoulders, also works traps, forearms, core.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Traps, Forearms, Core
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand over a barbell with your feet shoulder-width apart and toes turned slightly out.
2. Squat down with your back straight and chest up, and grip the middle of the bar with one hand, palm facing you.
3. Drive up hard through your hips, knees and ankles, then pull the bar up past your chest, keeping it close to your body.
4. Flip your elbow under the bar and punch your arm straight overhead, dropping into a squat to catch it.
5. Stand up with your arm locked straight, then lower the bar to the floor under control. Do all your reps, then switch arms.

## Pro tips

- Start with the bar in one hand at mid-thigh and use an explosive hip extension to drive it overhead in one fluid motion.
- Keep the bar close to your body throughout the pull and punch your hand toward the ceiling as you receive the bar overhead.
- Lock the bar out overhead with your bicep next to your ear and hold for a full second before lowering.

## Common mistakes

- Pulling with the arm instead of using the hips to generate the initial power, leading to a slow, muscled-up lift.
- Letting the bar swing out in front, which puts the shoulder in a dangerous position and makes the catch unstable.
- Not fully extending the hips before pulling under the bar, which limits the height the bar can reach.

## FAQ

### Is the single arm barbell snatch harder than a dumbbell snatch?

Yes. The barbell is longer and more awkward to control with one hand. It demands more grip strength, wrist stability, and coordination than a dumbbell or kettlebell snatch.

### What muscles does the single arm snatch work?

It's a full-body exercise hitting the quads, glutes, hamstrings, shoulders, traps, core, and forearms. It builds explosive power and coordination across the entire kinetic chain.

### Should I use wrist wraps for this exercise?

Wrist wraps can help stabilize the joint if you're handling meaningful weight. However, start light without wraps to build wrist strength, and add wraps only if needed for heavier loads.

### What muscles does the Barbell Single Arm Snatch work?

The Barbell Single Arm Snatch primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the traps, forearms, core. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Barbell Single Arm Snatch good for beginners?

The Barbell Single Arm Snatch is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental shoulders movements first.
