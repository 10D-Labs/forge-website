# Bodyweight Kneeling Tricep Extension

## Quick answer

A kneeling bodyweight move that presses you up from your forearms onto straight arms. It works the triceps, with help from the shoulders and chest. Intermediate arms exercise using body weight. Targets triceps, also works shoulders, chest.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Chest
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Kneel on the floor and lean forward onto your forearms, elbows under your shoulders, palms flat on the floor in front of your face.
2. Keep your hips above your knees.
3. Press your palms into the floor and straighten your arms, lifting your elbows and chest off the floor.
4. Bend your elbows slowly to lower your forearms back to the floor.

## Pro tips

- Start in a kneeling position with your hands on the ground slightly in front of your head, then extend at the elbows to push your body up.
- Keep your body straight from knees to shoulders; don't let the hips sag or pike up.
- Lower slowly by bending only at the elbows, keeping them pointed forward, not out to the sides.

## Common mistakes

- Turning this into a Pushup by bending at the shoulders instead of isolating the elbow extension.
- Placing the hands too far forward, which reduces the tricep load and increases shoulder involvement.
- Letting the elbows flare out to the sides, which shifts work to the chest and shoulders.

## FAQ

### What is a Bodyweight Kneeling Tricep Extension?

It's essentially a bodyweight skullcrusher from a kneeling position. You lower your head toward the ground by bending at the elbows, then extend back up using only tricep force.

### Is this easier or harder than regular Pushups?

It isolates the triceps more than Pushups, making it harder for the triceps specifically. The kneeling position reduces the overall bodyweight load compared to a full plank position.

### How can I make this exercise harder?

Progress from kneeling to a full plank position (toes on the ground). You can also elevate your feet on a bench or place your hands on a lower surface to increase the load on the triceps.

### What muscles does the Bodyweight Kneeling Tricep Extension work?

The Bodyweight Kneeling Tricep Extension primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the shoulders, chest. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Bodyweight Kneeling Tricep Extension good for beginners?

The Bodyweight Kneeling Tricep Extension is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of triceps strength with easier variations first.
