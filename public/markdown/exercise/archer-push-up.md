# Archer Push Up

## Quick answer

A wide pushup where you lower toward one hand while the other arm stays straight out to the side, so one arm takes most of your weight. It works the chest, triceps and shoulders. Advanced chest exercise using body weight. Targets chest, also works triceps, shoulders, core.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders, Core
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Start in a pushup position with your hands wider than shoulder-width apart.
2. Shift your weight toward one hand and keep the other arm straight out to the side.
3. Bend the elbow of the working arm to lower your chest toward that hand, keeping your back straight.
4. Push back up, then shift to the other side and lower toward the other hand. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Place your hands roughly double shoulder width apart, with the working arm doing most of the pressing.
- Keep the extended arm as straight as possible with fingers pointing outward to stretch the chest.
- Lower your chest toward the working hand, not to the center, to maximize unilateral loading.

## Common mistakes

- Bending the extended arm significantly, which distributes the load too evenly and defeats the purpose.
- Rotating the torso toward the working arm instead of keeping the chest facing the floor.
- Placing hands too close together, which limits the range and makes the movement feel like a standard wide Pushup.

## FAQ

### Are Archer Push Ups a good stepping stone to one-arm Pushups?

Yes. They train unilateral pressing strength while the extended arm provides stability assistance. Gradually reduce reliance on the assist arm to progress.

### Which muscles do Archer Push Ups target most?

The working side chest, triceps, and front delt bear the majority of the load, making it significantly harder than a standard Pushup for those muscles.

### Can Archer Push Ups replace bench press?

They can build substantial pushing strength and muscle, but they lack the easy progressive overload of a bench press. They work best as a complement or when equipment isn't available.

### What muscles does the Archer Push Up work?

The Archer Push Up primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders, core. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Archer Push Up good for beginners?

The Archer Push Up is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental chest movements first.
