# Cable Seated Crunch

## Quick answer

A crunch done sitting with your back to a high cable, curling forward against a rope held behind your head. It works the abs, plus the obliques. Intermediate core exercise using cable. Targets abs, also works obliques.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Obliques
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Attach a rope to a high pulley. Sit on a bench with your back to the machine, feet flat on the floor and knees bent.
2. Hold the rope with both hands behind your head.
3. Tighten your abs and slowly curl your upper body forward, bringing your chest toward your knees.
4. Uncurl slowly back to upright.

## Pro tips

- Sit upright and hold the rope behind your head. Curl your spine by driving your rib cage down toward your pelvis, not by bending at the hips.
- Exhale forcefully at the bottom of the crunch to help contract the rectus abdominis fully.
- Use a slow eccentric (2-3 seconds) back to the upright position to keep the abs under tension longer.

## Common mistakes

- Hinging at the hips instead of flexing the spine, which turns this into a hip flexor exercise rather than an ab exercise.
- Pulling the rope with the arms during the crunch, which engages the lats and takes tension off the abs.
- Using too much weight, which prevents full spinal flexion and limits the range of motion.

## FAQ

### Is the Cable Seated Crunch better than a regular crunch?

The cable version allows you to add progressive resistance over time, which is difficult with bodyweight crunches. This makes it more effective for building ab size and strength long-term.

### Should I use the Cable Seated Crunch for a six-pack?

The Cable Seated Crunch builds the rectus abdominis muscle. However, visible abs also require low body fat. Combine this exercise with a caloric deficit for visible results.

### What muscles does the Cable Seated Crunch work?

The Cable Seated Crunch primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the obliques. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Cable Seated Crunch good for beginners?

The Cable Seated Crunch is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of abs strength with easier variations first.
