# Walking on Incline Treadmill (Time)

## Quick answer

Uphill walking on a treadmill, with the incline raised to make it harder. It raises your heart rate and works the quads, hamstrings and calves. Beginner cardio exercise using treadmill. Targets cardiovascular, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Cardio
- **Equipment:** Treadmill
- **Target Muscle:** Cardiovascular
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Cardio

---

## How to perform

1. Set the treadmill incline to suit how hard you want to work.
2. Stand on the treadmill with your feet shoulder-width apart.
3. Start walking at a comfortable pace, keeping your back straight.
4. Keep going for the set time.

## Pro tips

- Don't hold the handrails; let your arms swing naturally to engage your core and burn more calories.
- Set the incline to at least 10-12% to get meaningful glute and hamstring activation.
- Maintain a brisk walking pace (3.0-3.5 mph) rather than going slow on a steep incline.

## Common mistakes

- Gripping the handrails, which reduces the workload by up to 30% and eliminates the core engagement benefit.
- Leaning backward to counterbalance the incline instead of maintaining an upright or slightly forward-leaning posture.
- Setting the incline too high and the speed too low, which can strain the lower back and Achilles tendons.

## FAQ

### What incline should I start with?

Begin at 5-8% incline and 2.5-3.0 mph. Once comfortable, increase the incline to 10-15%. The popular 12-3-30 method uses 12% incline at 3.0 mph for 30 minutes.

### Is incline walking as good as running for fat loss?

Incline walking can burn comparable calories per hour to light jogging while being much easier on the joints. It's a sustainable, effective approach for fat loss.

### Does incline walking build leg muscle?

It primarily builds muscular endurance in the glutes, hamstrings, and calves. For actual muscle growth, you'll need to supplement with resistance training.

### What muscles does the Walking on Incline Treadmill (Time) work?

The Walking on Incline Treadmill (Time) primarily targets the cardiovascular, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a cardio exercise for the cardio.

### Is the Walking on Incline Treadmill (Time) good for beginners?

Yes, the Walking on Incline Treadmill (Time) is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building cardiovascular strength.
