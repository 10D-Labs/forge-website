# Dumbbell Seated Alternate Press

## Quick answer

A seated overhead press, one dumbbell at a time. It works the front of the shoulders, with the triceps and chest helping. Beginner shoulders exercise using dumbbell. Targets shoulders, also works triceps, chest.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Chest
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on an upright bench with a dumbbell in each hand.
2. Raise the dumbbells to shoulder height, elbows bent and palms facing forward.
3. Press one dumbbell straight overhead until your arm is straight.
4. Lower it back to your shoulder, then press the other one. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Alternate pressing one arm at a time while keeping the other dumbbell at shoulder height, which doubles the time under tension per set.
- Brace your core and press straight up over each shoulder, not toward the center of your body.
- Keep the non-pressing arm engaged at shoulder height rather than resting it, which maintains constant tension.

## Common mistakes

- Resting the non-working arm on the thigh between reps instead of keeping it at shoulder height, which removes constant tension.
- Leaning to one side as you press, which stresses the spine laterally.
- Pressing the dumbbell forward instead of straight overhead, which shifts the load to the front delt.

## FAQ

### Is the alternating press harder than pressing both dumbbells together?

Yes, because each shoulder works for the entire set without rest, and the core must stabilize against the asymmetric load.

### What is the benefit of alternating shoulder press?

It doubles the time under tension per arm, trains core anti-rotation, and helps identify strength imbalances between shoulders.

### Can beginners do alternating dumbbell press?

Yes. Start with light weight and focus on pressing straight overhead with good posture. The seated position provides back support that makes it accessible.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Seated Alternate Press work?

The Dumbbell Seated Alternate Press primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the triceps, chest. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Dumbbell Seated Alternate Press good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Seated Alternate Press is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building shoulders strength.
