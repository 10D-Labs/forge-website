# Band Reverse Wrist Curl

## Quick answer

A wrist curl with your palms facing down, lifting the backs of your hands against a band under your feet. It works the muscles on top of your forearms. Beginner arms exercise using resistance band. Targets forearms, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Forearms
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on a box, bench or chair with your feet flat on the floor and the middle of a resistance band under your feet.
2. Hold the band with your palms facing down, wrapping it around your fingers.
3. Rest your forearms on your thighs so your wrists hang past your knees.
4. Bend your wrists to lift the backs of your hands up.
5. Lower your hands slowly.

## Pro tips

- Rest your forearms on your thighs with wrists hanging off the edge and palms facing down for proper positioning.
- Curl the wrists upward slowly against the band resistance, focusing on the top of the forearm contraction.
- Use a light band because the wrist extensors are small muscles that fatigue quickly.

## Common mistakes

- Lifting the forearms off the thighs during the curl, which recruits the biceps and defeats the isolation purpose.
- Using a band that is too thick, causing compensatory movements and wrist strain.
- Performing the movement too quickly without controlling the eccentric phase.

## FAQ

### What muscles do reverse wrist curls target?

The wrist extensors on the top of the forearm, primarily the extensor carpi radialis and extensor digitorum. These are critical for grip balance and injury prevention.

### Can reverse wrist curls help with tennis elbow?

Yes. Strengthening the wrist extensors is a common rehab protocol for lateral epicondylitis (tennis elbow). Start with very light resistance and high reps.

### How often should you train wrist extensors?

2-3 times per week with 2-3 sets of 15-20 reps. These are small muscles that benefit from higher frequency and lighter loads.

### What muscles does the Band Reverse Wrist Curl work?

The Band Reverse Wrist Curl primarily targets the forearms, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Band Reverse Wrist Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Band Reverse Wrist Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building forearms strength.
