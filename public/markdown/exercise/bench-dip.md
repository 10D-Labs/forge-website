# Bench Dip

## Quick answer

A dip with your hands on a bench behind you and your legs straight out on the floor. It works the triceps, with help from the chest and shoulders. Beginner arms exercise using body weight. Targets triceps, also works chest, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Chest, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on the edge of a flat bench and grip the edge beside your hips, palms down, fingers pointing forward.
2. Walk your feet out until your legs are straight with your heels on the floor, and slide your hips off the bench.
3. Keep your back close to the bench and bend your elbows to lower your hips until your upper arms are about level with the floor.
4. Press through your hands until your arms are straight again.

## Pro tips

- Keep your hands close to your hips on the bench edge with fingers pointing forward to maximize triceps engagement.
- Lower until your upper arms are parallel to the floor, then press back up powerfully through your palms.
- Keep your back close to the bench throughout the movement to keep the load on the triceps rather than the shoulders.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the elbows flare outward instead of keeping them pointing straight back, which shifts stress to the shoulders.
- Sliding the hips too far forward from the bench, which increases shoulder strain and reduces triceps focus.
- Shrugging the shoulders up toward the ears during the dip instead of keeping them depressed.

## FAQ

### Are bench dips safe for the shoulders?

They can stress the anterior shoulder capsule, especially at the bottom of the movement. If you have shoulder issues, limit your depth or choose skull crushers or pushdowns instead.

### How do I make bench dips harder?

Extend your legs further out (straight legs are harder than bent), elevate your feet on another bench, or place a weight plate on your lap.

### What muscles do bench dips work?

Primarily the triceps, with secondary work from the front deltoids and chest. The closer your hands are together, the more the triceps are isolated.

### What muscles does the Bench Dip work?

The Bench Dip primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the chest, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Bench Dip good for beginners?

Yes, the Bench Dip is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building triceps strength.
