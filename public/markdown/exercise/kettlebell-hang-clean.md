# Kettlebell Hang Clean

## Quick answer

A kettlebell swung from between your legs up to rest at your shoulder, powered by a quick drive of the hips. It works the hamstrings, with the glutes, quads and calves helping. Intermediate legs exercise using kettlebell. Targets hamstrings, also works glutes, quadriceps, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Target Muscle:** Hamstrings
- **Secondary Muscles:** Glutes, Quadriceps, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a kettlebell in one hand in front of your thighs.
2. Bend your knees slightly and lean forward from your hips with your back straight, letting the kettlebell swing back between your legs.
3. Straighten your hips and knees quickly to swing the kettlebell up toward your shoulder.
4. Turn your wrist as it reaches your shoulder and catch it resting on your forearm, with your elbow tucked in.
5. Lower the kettlebell and let it swing back down between your legs.

## Pro tips

- Hinge at the hips and let the Kettlebell Swing back between your legs before explosively snapping your hips forward to propel it to the rack position.
- Tame the arc by keeping the kettlebell close to your body. Think of zipping up a jacket rather than swinging in a wide loop.
- Catch the kettlebell softly in the rack position by meeting it with your hand and absorbing the momentum with a slight knee dip.

## Common mistakes

- Using the arm to pull the kettlebell up instead of driving with the hips, which fatigues the biceps and misses the point of the exercise.
- Letting the kettlebell bang against the forearm at the catch because the wrist isn't rotating smoothly.
- Not hinging deeply enough, which reduces the power of the hip snap.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the Kettlebell Hang Clean work?

Primarily the hamstrings and glutes through the hip hinge, with the forearms, traps, and shoulders working during the pull and catch phase. It's also a great conditioning exercise.

### How do I prevent forearm bruising from cleans?

Learn to insert your hand around the bell at the top rather than letting it flip over your wrist. The bell should roll smoothly into the rack position, not crash down onto your forearm.

### What muscles does the Kettlebell Hang Clean work?

The Kettlebell Hang Clean primarily targets the hamstrings, with secondary activation in the glutes, quadriceps, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Kettlebell Hang Clean good for beginners?

The Kettlebell Hang Clean is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of hamstrings strength with easier variations first.
