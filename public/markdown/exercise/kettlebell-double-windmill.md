# Kettlebell Double Windmill

## Quick answer

A windmill with two kettlebells: one held straight overhead while you bend sideways and lower the other toward your foot. It works the abs and obliques, with help from the shoulders and hamstrings. Advanced core exercise using kettlebell. Targets abs, also works shoulders, hamstrings.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Hamstrings
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, a kettlebell in each hand. Press the right one straight overhead and let the left hang at your side.
2. Turn your left foot out about 45 degrees to the left.
3. Bend sideways at the waist to the left, keeping your right arm straight overhead.
4. Lower the left kettlebell down toward your left foot.
5. Push through your right foot to stand back up. Do all your reps, then switch sides.

## Pro tips

- Hold one kettlebell overhead and one in the lower hand. Keep eyes locked on the overhead bell throughout.
- Hinge at the hip by pushing the hip on the overhead side outward, not just bending forward.
- Lower the bottom kettlebell along your inner leg, reaching toward the floor while keeping the overhead arm vertical.

## Common mistakes

- Bending the overhead arm, which destabilizes the shoulder and risks dropping the weight.
- Rotating forward instead of hinging laterally, which loads the lumbar spine unevenly.
- Letting the bottom kettlebell pull you forward, rounding the back.

## FAQ

### Why hold two kettlebells in a windmill?

The lower kettlebell adds resistance to the lateral hinge, increasing the demand on the obliques and hamstrings, while the overhead kettlebell continues to challenge shoulder stability. It's a significant progression from the single-bell version.

### What should I master before attempting a double windmill?

First master the bodyweight windmill, then the single Kettlebell Windmill with a light load. Only progress to the double when you can perform 5 clean reps per side with a single bell.

### What muscles does the Kettlebell Double Windmill work?

The Kettlebell Double Windmill primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the shoulders, hamstrings. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Kettlebell Double Windmill good for beginners?

The Kettlebell Double Windmill is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental core movements first.
