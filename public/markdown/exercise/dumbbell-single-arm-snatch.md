# Dumbbell Single Arm Snatch

## Quick answer

An explosive move that lifts one dumbbell from the floor to overhead in one motion, driven by your hips and legs. It works the glutes, hamstrings and quads, plus the shoulders and abs. Advanced legs exercise using dumbbell. Targets glutes, also works hamstrings, quadriceps, core.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Quadriceps, Core
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a dumbbell in one hand, palm facing your body.
2. Bend your knees slightly and lean forward from your hips, chest up and back straight. Lower the dumbbell toward the floor, close to your body.
3. Straighten your hips, knees and ankles fast, driving the dumbbell straight up close to your body.
4. Turn your hand as the dumbbell passes your shoulder and punch it overhead, catching it with your arm straight and your knees and hips slightly bent.
5. Lower the dumbbell back down under control. Do all your reps on one arm, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Generate power from your hips by snapping them forward, not by pulling with your arm.
- Keep the dumbbell close to your body on the way up, like zipping a jacket from hip to overhead.
- Catch the dumbbell overhead with a slight bend in the knees to absorb the force.

## Common mistakes

- Pulling the weight with the arm from the start instead of using the hip hinge to generate power.
- Letting the dumbbell swing away from the body, which creates a longer lever arm and strains the shoulder.
- Not fully locking out the arm overhead, which means you're not completing the movement safely.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the single-arm snatch work?

It's a full-body exercise hitting the glutes, hamstrings, traps, shoulders, and core. The explosive hip drive targets the posterior chain, while the catch challenges shoulder stability.

### How heavy should the dumbbell be?

Start with 20-30% of your body weight and master the technique before adding load. For most people that's 25-45 lbs.

### Is a dumbbell snatch easier than a barbell snatch?

Technically simpler because you don't need the same wrist flexibility or overhead position as a barbell snatch. But it still demands explosive power and coordination.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Single Arm Snatch work?

The Dumbbell Single Arm Snatch primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, quadriceps, core. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Dumbbell Single Arm Snatch good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Single Arm Snatch is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental legs movements first.
