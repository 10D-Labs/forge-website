# Barbell Deadlift

## Quick answer

A barbell lift from the floor to standing, with your feet about shoulder-width apart. It works the glutes, hamstrings and lower back. Intermediate legs exercise using barbell. Targets glutes, also works hamstrings, lower back.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Lower Back
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and a barbell on the floor in front of you.
2. Bend your knees and push your hips back to reach down. Grip the bar with your palms facing you, hands slightly wider than shoulder-width apart.
3. Keep your back straight and your chest up as you push through your heels and stand up with the bar.
4. Lower the bar back to the floor by bending your hips and knees.

## Pro tips

- Set up with the bar over your mid-foot, shins about one inch from the bar, and pull the slack out of the bar before lifting by engaging your lats.
- Push the floor away with your feet rather than thinking about pulling the bar up; this cue helps you drive with your legs first.
- Keep the bar dragging against your shins and thighs throughout the lift to maintain the most efficient bar path.

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the lower back during the pull, which puts the lumbar discs at risk under heavy load.
- Starting with the hips too high (stiff-leg position) or too low (squat position); your hips should be between your shoulders and knees.
- Jerking the bar off the floor instead of building tension gradually and then driving smoothly.

## FAQ

### How often should I Deadlift?

1-2 times per week is sufficient for most people. Deadlifts are very taxing on the nervous system and lower back. Allow at least 48-72 hours of recovery between sessions.

### Should I use a mixed grip or double overhand for Deadlifts?

Use double overhand as long as possible to build grip strength. Switch to mixed grip or hook grip when your grip limits your Deadlift. Straps are fine for higher rep sets.

### Is Deadlift a back exercise or a leg exercise?

Both. The primary movers are the glutes and hamstrings (hip extension), with the back working isometrically to keep the spine neutral. It's most commonly programmed on back or leg day.

### What muscles does the Barbell Deadlift work?

The Barbell Deadlift primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, lower back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Barbell Deadlift good for beginners?

The Barbell Deadlift is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of glutes strength with easier variations first.
