# Bodyweight Towel Standing Row

## Quick answer

A bodyweight row on a towel looped around a wall bar, leaning back and pulling your chest in with both hands. It works the upper back, with help from the biceps and shoulders. Beginner back exercise using body weight. Targets upper back, also works biceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Loop a towel around a wall bar rung at chest height. Hold one end in each hand, palms facing each other, with your toes near the base.
2. Lean back until your arms are straight, keeping your body in a straight line from head to heels.
3. Pull your chest toward the bars, driving your elbows back past your sides.
4. Lower yourself slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Loop a towel around a sturdy anchor point and grip both ends, then lean back to create tension.
- Pull yourself toward the anchor by squeezing your shoulder blades together and driving your elbows back.
- The unstable grip of the towel forces your forearms and grip to work harder than a standard bar row.

## Common mistakes

- Using a towel that is too short, which limits how far back you can lean and reduces the exercise difficulty.
- Letting the wrists bend excessively, which strains them. Keep your wrists straight and grip firmly.
- Not maintaining body tension, allowing the hips to sag and reducing the core demand.

## FAQ

### Why use a towel for rows?

The towel adds a grip challenge since the soft, flexible material requires more forearm activation to hold. It also makes the exercise accessible anywhere you have a sturdy anchor point.

### Is a towel row as effective as a dumbbell row?

For beginners, towel rows are an effective bodyweight pulling exercise. As you get stronger, you will need external load (dumbbells, cables) to continue progressing.

### What is the best anchor point for towel rows?

A closed, sturdy door with the towel looped around the handle on the opposite side, a power rack upright, or any smooth, solid post that the towel can wrap around.

### What muscles does the Bodyweight Towel Standing Row work?

The Bodyweight Towel Standing Row primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the biceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Bodyweight Towel Standing Row good for beginners?

Yes, the Bodyweight Towel Standing Row is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building upper back strength.
