# Captain's Chair Knee Raise

## Quick answer

A knee raise done propped up on your forearms in an upright station, lifting your knees to hip height. It works the abs and the muscles at the front of your hips. Advanced core exercise using body weight. Targets abs, also works hip flexors.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hip Flexors
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Step into a knee-raise station, back against the pad. Rest your forearms on the arm pads, hold the handles and let your legs hang.
2. Lift your knees up in front of you until your thighs are level with the floor.
3. Lower your legs slowly until they hang straight again.

## Pro tips

- Prop yourself up on your forearms with your back against the pad and your shoulders down away from your ears.
- Lift your knees to hip height, then curl your pelvis up slightly to finish with the abs.
- Lower your legs over at least 2 seconds to keep tension on the abs.

## Common mistakes

- Swinging the legs with momentum instead of lifting them with the abs.
- Letting the shoulders shrug up toward the ears.
- Stopping at hip height without curling the pelvis, which leaves most of the work to the hip flexors.

## FAQ

### What muscles do Captain's Chair Knee Raises work?

The abs and the hip flexors. Curling your pelvis up at the top puts more of the work on the abs.

### How do I progress to leg raises in the captain's chair?

Once you can do 3 sets of 15 controlled knee raises, start straightening your legs a little more each session until they are fully straight.

### Are captain's chair knee raises better than hanging knee raises?

They work the same muscles. The captain's chair supports your arms and back, so it is easier to learn. Hanging versions also train grip and shoulder strength.

### What muscles does the Captain's Chair Knee Raise work?

The Captain's Chair Knee Raise primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the hip flexors. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Captain's Chair Knee Raise good for beginners?

The Captain's Chair Knee Raise is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental core movements first.
