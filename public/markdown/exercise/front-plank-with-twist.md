# Front Plank with Twist

## Quick answer

A forearm plank that turns into a side plank with your top arm reaching up, then back. It works the abs and obliques, with help from the shoulders. Intermediate core exercise using body weight. Targets abs, also works obliques, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Obliques, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Start in a plank on your forearms, elbows under your shoulders and your body in a straight line from head to heels.
2. Turn your body to the right onto your left forearm and reach your right arm up toward the ceiling, keeping your hips up.
3. Lower your arm and turn back to the forearm plank.
4. Twist to the left side next. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Stack your wrist directly under your shoulder on the planted arm as you rotate to protect the joint.
- Rotate from the thoracic spine (mid-back) rather than just reaching your arm up to get a true oblique contraction.
- Keep your hips level and resist the urge to let them sag or pike up during the rotation.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips drop or sag during the twist, which puts stress on the lower back instead of the obliques.
- Rotating too quickly without control, which uses momentum instead of engaging the core muscles.
- Not extending the arm fully toward the ceiling, which reduces the rotational range and oblique activation.

## FAQ

### What muscles does a plank with twist target?

Primarily the obliques and rectus abdominis, with secondary engagement of the shoulders, glutes, and hip stabilizers. The twist adds a rotational element that standard planks don't provide.

### How many reps should I do per side?

Start with 5-8 twists per side for 2-3 sets. Focus on slow, controlled rotations rather than speed.

### What muscles does the Front Plank with Twist work?

The Front Plank with Twist primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the obliques, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Front Plank with Twist good for beginners?

The Front Plank with Twist is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of abs strength with easier variations first.
