# Close Stance Hack Squat

## Quick answer

A hack squat with your feet close together, your back and shoulders braced against pads. It works the glutes and quads, plus the hamstrings and calves. Intermediate legs exercise using machine. Targets quads, also works glutes, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Quads
- **Secondary Muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand on the hack squat platform with your back against the pad, shoulders under the shoulder pads and feet close together.
2. Grip the handles by your shoulders, palms facing forward.
3. Bend your knees to lower yourself until your thighs are about level with the floor.
4. Push through your feet to straighten your legs back to the start.

## Pro tips

- Place your feet close together on the platform to shift more emphasis onto the quads.
- Drive through the full foot, keeping your heels down throughout the movement.
- Keep your back flat against the pad and don't let your hips drift forward at the bottom.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the heels lift at the bottom, which means the weight is too heavy or your ankles need more mobility.
- Letting the knees cave inward as you stand up.
- Not going deep enough; aim for thighs at least parallel to the platform.

## FAQ

### What's the difference between a Close Stance and a regular Hack Squat?

Feet close together bend the knees more and put more work on the quads. A wider stance brings in more of the glutes and inner thighs.

### Is the Close Stance Hack Squat harder on the knees?

The knees bend more, so it can be. If your knees bother you, use a moderate depth and make sure they track over your toes.

### Can I use the Close Stance Hack Squat to build bigger quads?

Yes. The machine removes the balance demand, so you can focus on pushing with the quads.

### What muscles does the Close Stance Hack Squat work?

The Close Stance Hack Squat primarily targets the quads, with secondary activation in the glutes, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Close Stance Hack Squat good for beginners?

The Close Stance Hack Squat is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of quads strength with easier variations first.
