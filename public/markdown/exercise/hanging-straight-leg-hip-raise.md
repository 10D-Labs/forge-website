# Hanging Straight Leg Hip Raise

## Quick answer

A hanging leg raise that goes past level, curling your hips up until your feet reach the bar. It works the abs, with help from the front of the hips. Advanced core exercise using body weight. Targets abs, also works hip flexors.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hip Flexors
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Hang from a pullup bar with your arms straight, hands shoulder-width apart, palms facing away from you.
2. Keep your legs straight and raise them in front of you without swinging.
3. Keep lifting, curling your hips up, until your feet reach the bar.
4. Lower your legs slowly until you're hanging straight again.

## Pro tips

- Keep your legs locked out straight and lift by posteriorly tilting your pelvis, not by using hip flexor momentum.
- Pause at the top position (legs parallel) for 1-2 seconds to eliminate any swing and increase time under tension.
- Grip the bar with your hands shoulder-width apart and actively depress your shoulder blades to create a stable hang.

## Common mistakes

- Allowing the knees to bend as fatigue sets in, which reduces the lever length and shifts work away from the abs.
- Swinging the legs forward and back rather than performing slow, controlled raises.
- Failing to engage the pelvis at the top, which means the hip flexors are doing most of the work.

## FAQ

### What's the difference between a hip raise and a leg raise?

A hip raise specifically involves curling the pelvis upward at the top of the movement, while a leg raise can stop at lifting the legs. The hip raise component is what shifts the work from hip flexors to abs.

### My grip gives out before my abs. What should I do?

Use ab straps that loop around your elbows, or use a captain's chair. You can also build grip endurance with dead hangs performed separately.

### What muscles does the Hanging Straight Leg Hip Raise work?

The Hanging Straight Leg Hip Raise primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the hip flexors. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Hanging Straight Leg Hip Raise good for beginners?

The Hanging Straight Leg Hip Raise is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental core movements first.
