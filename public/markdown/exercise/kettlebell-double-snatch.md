# Kettlebell Double Snatch

## Quick answer

A fast lift that takes two kettlebells from in front of your thighs to straight overhead in one move. It works the shoulders, with the upper back, forearms and abs helping. Advanced shoulders exercise using kettlebell. Targets shoulders, also works trapezius, forearms, core.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Trapezius, Forearms, Core
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a kettlebell in each hand at arm's length in front of your thighs.
2. Bend your knees slightly and lean forward from your hips, keeping your back flat and your chest up.
3. Straighten your hips, knees and ankles in one fast move, pulling the kettlebells up toward your shoulders.
4. Turn your wrists as the kettlebells reach shoulder height and punch them straight overhead until your arms are straight.
5. Lower the kettlebells back down in front of your thighs.

## Pro tips

- Hike both kettlebells back between your legs with a powerful hip hinge, then snap your hips forward to drive them overhead in one fluid motion.
- Punch through at the top to insert your hands into the handles and lock out both arms simultaneously.
- Keep the kettlebells close to your body on the way up, as if zipping up a jacket, to reduce the arc and improve efficiency.

## Common mistakes

- Trying to muscle the kettlebells overhead with arm strength instead of using hip power to project them.
- Letting the kettlebells swing wide in a big arc, which is inefficient and stresses the shoulders.
- Not inserting the hands at the top, causing the bells to crash down onto the forearms.

## FAQ

### Is the double snatch safe?

It's an advanced kettlebell exercise. It's safe when you've mastered the single-arm snatch and double clean first. The increased load demands excellent hip mechanics and shoulder stability.

### What muscles does the double snatch target?

It's a full-body exercise: glutes and hamstrings drive the hike, traps and shoulders finish the pull, and the core stabilizes throughout. It's also highly demanding on the cardiovascular system.

### What muscles does the Kettlebell Double Snatch work?

The Kettlebell Double Snatch primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the trapezius, forearms, core. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Kettlebell Double Snatch good for beginners?

The Kettlebell Double Snatch is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental shoulders movements first.
