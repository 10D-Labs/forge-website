# Dumbbell Shrug

## Quick answer

A shrug with a dumbbell hanging at each side, lifting your shoulders toward your ears. It works the traps, the muscles between your neck and shoulders. Beginner back exercise using dumbbell. Targets traps, also works shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Traps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and a dumbbell hanging at each side, palms facing your body.
2. Keep your arms straight and raise your shoulders as high as you can, as if trying to touch your ears with them.
3. Lower your shoulders slowly back down.

## Pro tips

- Shrug straight up toward your ears as if trying to touch your shoulders to your earlobes, and hold the top for 1-2 seconds.
- Keep your arms completely straight throughout; do not bend the elbows or row the weight.
- Use straps if your grip gives out before your traps, since traps can handle far more weight than your grip strength.

## Common mistakes

- Rolling the shoulders in circles, which adds no benefit and can stress the shoulder joint.
- Bending the elbows and turning the shrug into a partial upright row.
- Using too much weight and only achieving a tiny range of motion, which barely activates the upper traps.

## FAQ

### How heavy should I go on Dumbbell Shrugs?

Traps can handle heavy loads. Use the heaviest dumbbells that still allow you to shrug to full height with a 1-second hold. Most intermediate lifters use 50-80 lbs per hand.

### Are Dumbbell Shrugs better than Barbell Shrugs?

Dumbbells allow a more natural arm position at your sides rather than in front, which can feel more comfortable and reduce hip interference.

### How many reps for shrugs?

Traps respond to both heavy low reps (6-8) and moderate high reps (12-20). Varying the rep range across sessions works well.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Shrug work?

The Dumbbell Shrug primarily targets the traps, with secondary activation in the shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Dumbbell Shrug good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Shrug is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building traps strength.
