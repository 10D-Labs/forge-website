# Dumbbell Incline Hammer Curl

## Quick answer

A hammer curl done sitting back on an incline bench, with your palms facing in the whole time. It works the biceps and the forearms. Intermediate arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit back on an incline bench with your back against the pad and your feet flat on the floor.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand, arms hanging straight, palms facing your body.
3. Keep your upper arms still and your palms facing in (thumbs up) as you curl both dumbbells up to shoulder level.
4. Lower them slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Sit on a 45-60 degree incline bench with dumbbells hanging at arm's length, palms facing each other (neutral grip). The incline stretches the brachialis from the start.
- Curl without rotating your wrists. Maintain the neutral grip throughout to target the brachialis and brachioradialis.
- Keep your elbows pointing straight at the floor and your upper arms motionless. The incline position makes cheating obvious, so use it as a form check.

## Common mistakes

- Swinging the elbows forward to make the curl easier, which eliminates the advantage of the incline position.
- Rotating the wrists to a supinated position, which turns this into an incline Bicep Curl instead of a hammer curl.
- Using too much weight. The combined stretch from the incline and the neutral grip makes this harder than standing hammer curls.

## FAQ

### What does the incline add to hammer curls?

The incline places the brachialis and long head of the bicep in a stretched position at the bottom of each rep. This increases muscle fiber recruitment and provides a stronger growth stimulus compared to standing hammer curls.

### Incline hammer curl vs incline Bicep Curl: what is the difference?

Incline hammer curls use a neutral grip (palms facing each other) to target the brachialis and brachioradialis. Incline Bicep Curls use a supinated grip (palms up) to target the bicep heads. Both benefit from the incline stretch.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Incline Hammer Curl work?

The Dumbbell Incline Hammer Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Incline Hammer Curl good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Incline Hammer Curl is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of biceps strength with easier variations first.
