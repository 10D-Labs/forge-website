# Single Arm Lat Pulldown

## Quick answer

A lat pulldown done one arm at a time with a single cable handle while sitting on a bench. It works the lats, with help from the biceps and upper back. Beginner back exercise using cable. Targets lats, also works biceps, rhomboids.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Rhomboids
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Attach a single handle to a high cable pulley. Sit on the end of a flat bench below it, facing the cable, feet flat.
2. Reach up and grab the handle with one hand, palm facing away from you, arm straight.
3. Pull the handle straight down, bringing your elbow down to your side until your hand is level with your shoulder.
4. Let the handle rise slowly until your arm is straight. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Initiate each pull by depressing the shoulder blade on the working side first, then drive the elbow down and back.
- Lean back only slightly (about 10-15 degrees) to create a better line of pull for the lats.
- Pause at the bottom of each single-arm pull for a full second to eliminate momentum and maximize lat contraction.

## Common mistakes

- Using too much body lean or twisting the torso to help the pull, which takes work away from the lats.
- Gripping too tightly and pulling with the biceps instead of driving with the elbow.
- Rushing the reps without fully contracting the lat at the bottom or letting the arm reach a full stretch at the top.

## FAQ

### What is the benefit of doing lat pulldowns one arm at a time?

Working one arm at a time lets you focus on each lat, helps correct imbalances between sides, and lets the working arm travel through a longer range than a two-handed bar allows.

### Which way should my palm face on Single Arm Lat Pulldowns?

A neutral grip, with your palm facing in, is comfortable for most people and lets you pull the elbow straight down to your side. Use whatever grip lets you feel the lat working without wrist or elbow discomfort.

### Can I use Single Arm Lat Pulldowns as a warmup for Pullups?

Yes. Doing 2-3 light sets is a good way to activate each lat before heavier pulling movements.

### What muscles does the Single Arm Lat Pulldown work?

The Single Arm Lat Pulldown primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the biceps, rhomboids. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Single Arm Lat Pulldown good for beginners?

Yes, the Single Arm Lat Pulldown is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building lats strength.
