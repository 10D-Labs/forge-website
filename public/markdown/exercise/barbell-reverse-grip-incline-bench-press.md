# Barbell Reverse Grip Incline Bench Press

## Quick answer

An incline bench press with your palms facing your head instead of your feet. It works the upper chest, with help from the triceps and shoulders. Advanced chest exercise using barbell. Targets chest, also works triceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set an incline bench to 45 degrees and lie back on it with your feet flat on the floor.
2. Grip the bar with your palms facing your head, hands slightly wider than your shoulders, and lift it off the rack.
3. Lower the bar slowly until it touches your chest, keeping your elbows close to your sides.
4. Press the bar back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Set the incline to 30-45 degrees and grip the bar with palms facing you at about shoulder width.
- Have a spotter help you unrack and keep the bar path from lower chest to lockout above the upper chest.
- Tuck your elbows at about 45 degrees to your torso throughout the movement.

## Common mistakes

- Setting the incline too steep (above 45 degrees), which turns it into a shoulder press and makes the reverse grip unstable.
- Not having a spotter for the unrack, which is the most dangerous moment with a reverse grip.
- Gripping too wide, which weakens grip security and increases the chance of the bar rolling out of the hands.

## FAQ

### What does the reverse grip incline bench press target?

It places extra emphasis on the upper chest (clavicular head of the pec major) and the biceps compared to a standard incline bench press.

### Is this exercise necessary for a good chest workout?

Not necessary, but it provides a unique stimulus. If your upper chest is lagging, it can be a valuable variation to add alongside standard incline presses.

### How does this compare to a regular incline bench press?

EMG studies show higher upper pec activation with a reverse grip. However, the trade-off is less weight and greater need for spotting due to grip challenges.

### What muscles does the Barbell Reverse Grip Incline Bench Press work?

The Barbell Reverse Grip Incline Bench Press primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Barbell Reverse Grip Incline Bench Press good for beginners?

The Barbell Reverse Grip Incline Bench Press is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental chest movements first.
