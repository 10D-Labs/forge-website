# Barbell Rear Delt Row

## Quick answer

A bent-over barbell row with a wide grip, pulling the bar to your chest. It works the backs of the shoulders, plus the upper back and biceps. Intermediate shoulders exercise using barbell. Targets shoulders, also works trapezius, rhomboids, biceps.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Trapezius, Rhomboids, Biceps
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and your knees slightly bent, holding a barbell with your palms facing your thighs and your hands wide apart.
2. Bend forward at the hips, keeping your back straight and your chest up.
3. Pull the bar up to your chest, squeezing your shoulder blades together.
4. Lower the bar slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Row the bar to your upper chest (collarbone level) with your elbows flared out wide to target the rear delts instead of the lats.
- Keep your torso at about a 45-degree angle and don't let your back rise up during the row.
- Use a wider-than-shoulder-width grip to emphasize the rear delts and rhomboids over the biceps and lats.

## Common mistakes

- Pulling the bar to the lower chest or stomach with elbows tucked, which turns it into a standard Bent Over Row targeting the lats.
- Using too much weight and letting the torso rise with each rep to generate momentum.
- Gripping too narrow, which shifts the emphasis to the biceps and lats instead of the rear delts.

## FAQ

### What's the difference between a rear delt row and a regular Bent Over Row?

Elbow position and bar path. Rear delt rows use a wider grip and flared elbows, pulling to the upper chest. Regular rows use a narrower grip with elbows tucked, pulling to the lower chest. Different muscles are emphasized.

### Can rear delt rows fix rounded shoulders?

Yes. Strengthening the rear delts, rhomboids, and mid traps through exercises like this helps pull the shoulders back and counteract the forward-rounded posture common from desk work and too much pressing.

### How many sets per week should I do for rear delts?

6-12 total sets per week targeting the rear delts. Most people underdevelop this area, so erring toward the higher end is usually beneficial.

### What muscles does the Barbell Rear Delt Row work?

The Barbell Rear Delt Row primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the trapezius, rhomboids, biceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Barbell Rear Delt Row good for beginners?

The Barbell Rear Delt Row is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of shoulders strength with easier variations first.
