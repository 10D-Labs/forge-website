# Seated Overhead Lat Stretch

## Quick answer

A seated stretch where you reach one arm straight overhead and lean slightly away from it. It stretches the lats along the side of your back. Beginner back exercise using body weight. Targets lats, also works shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Stretching

---

## How to perform

1. Sit tall on a chair with your back against the backrest and your feet flat on the floor. Let one arm hang at your side.
2. Raise your other arm straight up overhead.
3. Reach that hand higher and slightly over your head, leaning a little to the other side, until you feel a stretch under your raised arm.
4. Hold for the set time, then return slowly to sitting upright and switch arms.

## Pro tips

- Sit tall and reach one arm straight overhead, then lean slightly away from that arm.
- Reach up and over rather than just bending sideways, so you feel the stretch along the side of your back.
- Breathe slowly and let the stretch deepen a little with each exhale.

## Common mistakes

- Collapsing the torso forward instead of leaning to the side.
- Shrugging the reaching shoulder up toward your ear.
- Bouncing in and out of the stretch instead of holding it.

## FAQ

### How long should I hold the Seated Overhead Lat Stretch?

20-30 seconds per side, 2-3 times. It is a good stretch after back workouts or long periods of sitting.

### Where should I feel this stretch?

Along the side of your back, from under the armpit down toward your hip. If you feel it mostly in your shoulder, lean a little less and reach higher.

### Should I do this stretch before or after a workout?

After a workout, when the muscles are warm. Before a workout, use moving warmups like arm circles instead.

### What muscles does the Seated Overhead Lat Stretch work?

The Seated Overhead Lat Stretch primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the shoulders. It's classified as a stretching exercise for the back.

### Is the Seated Overhead Lat Stretch good for beginners?

Yes, the Seated Overhead Lat Stretch is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building lats strength.
