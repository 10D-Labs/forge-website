# Barbell Zercher Squat

## Quick answer

A squat holding the barbell in the bend of your elbows, in front of your body. It works the glutes, plus the quads, hamstrings, calves and abs. Advanced legs exercise using barbell. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves, core.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves, Core
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and your toes turned slightly out.
2. Hold the barbell in the inside bend of your elbows, with your hands gripping the bar to keep it steady.
3. Push your hips back and down to squat, keeping your chest up and your knees in line with your toes.
4. Push through your heels to stand back up.

## Pro tips

- Hold the bar in the crooks of your elbows, using a towel or pad for comfort, and keep your elbows close to your body.
- The front-loaded position forces an extremely upright torso; use this to build incredible core and upper back strength.
- Start light to build tolerance in the elbow crease area before adding significant weight.

## Common mistakes

- Not using padding on the bar, which makes the exercise painfully uncomfortable in the elbow crease and limits how much you can lift.
- Letting the elbows drop and the bar slide down, collapsing the posture.
- Rounding the upper back because the front loading is demanding on the thoracic extensors.

## FAQ

### What are Zercher squats good for?

Zercher squats build tremendous core strength, upper back endurance, and quad and glute development with a very upright torso. They're popular with strongman athletes and anyone who wants to improve front squat position strength.

### Do Zercher squats hurt your arms?

The bar in the elbow crease is uncomfortable, especially at first. Use a bar pad, towel, or Zercher harness. Over time, you'll build tolerance. Start with lighter weight.

### How much weight can I Zercher squat compared to back squat?

Most people Zercher squat 50-70% of their back squat weight. The limiting factor is usually elbow comfort and upper back strength, not leg strength.

### What muscles does the Barbell Zercher Squat work?

The Barbell Zercher Squat primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves, core. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Barbell Zercher Squat good for beginners?

The Barbell Zercher Squat is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental legs movements first.
