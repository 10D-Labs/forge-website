# Barbell Single Arm Bent Over Row

## Quick answer

A one-arm row where one end of the barbell stays on the floor and you row the loaded end up beside your body. It works the upper back, with help from the biceps and forearms. Intermediate back exercise using barbell. Targets upper back, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Load one end of a barbell and leave the other end on the floor. Stand beside the bar, feet hip-width apart, with the loaded end just in front of you.
2. Bend your knees slightly and lean forward from your hips with a flat back. Grab the bar just behind the plates with one hand, palm facing in toward your body.
3. Pull the loaded end up toward your chest, driving your elbow up and back.
4. Lower it slowly until your arm is straight. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Brace your non-working hand on a bench or rack for support and to keep your torso stable at 45-60 degrees.
- Row the bar up toward your hip, driving the elbow past your torso and squeezing the lat at the top.
- Use an overhand grip and keep the bar parallel to the ground throughout the movement.

## Common mistakes

- Rotating the torso toward the working arm to help pull the weight up instead of keeping the hips and shoulders square.
- Using too much weight and jerking the bar up with body momentum rather than a controlled pull.
- Letting the working shoulder droop at the bottom instead of maintaining a neutral shoulder position.

## FAQ

### Why use a barbell for single arm rows instead of a dumbbell?

A barbell allows for a unique loading pattern and grip challenge since you're gripping a thicker bar off-center. It also trains anti-rotation stability differently than a dumbbell.

### Where should I grip the barbell for single arm rows?

Grip the bar at the center for balanced weight distribution. You can also grip near one end with the other end anchored in a corner (landmine setup) for a different angle.

### How many sets should I do per side?

3-4 sets of 8-12 reps per arm. Start with your weaker arm first to ensure balanced development, and match the reps on your stronger side.

### What muscles does the Barbell Single Arm Bent Over Row work?

The Barbell Single Arm Bent Over Row primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Barbell Single Arm Bent Over Row good for beginners?

The Barbell Single Arm Bent Over Row is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of upper back strength with easier variations first.
