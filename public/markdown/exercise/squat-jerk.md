# Squat Jerk

## Quick answer

A fast overhead lift where you drive the bar up from your shoulders and catch it in a deep squat with straight arms. It works the quads, glutes and shoulders and takes speed and balance. Advanced legs exercise using barbell. Targets quads, also works glutes, hamstrings, calves, core.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Quads
- **Secondary Muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings, Calves, Core
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart and a barbell resting on the front of your shoulders. Hold it just outside your shoulders, palms facing forward.
2. Dip by bending your knees a few inches, keeping your chest up and your upper body upright.
3. Drive up hard through your legs and push the bar straight overhead.
4. As the bar rises, drop into a deep squat and catch it overhead with your arms locked straight.
5. Stand up with the bar overhead, then lower it back to your shoulders.

## Pro tips

- Dip straight down with the knees before the drive; any forward lean will send the bar forward.
- Receive the bar in a deep overhead squat position with active shoulders pressing up against the bar.
- Drive the bar off your shoulders with maximum leg power; the stronger the drive, the less you need to press.

## Common mistakes

- Dipping forward instead of straight down, which sends the bar forward and out of position.
- Not committing to the full depth overhead squat catch, resulting in receiving the bar on bent arms.
- Pressing the bar with the arms instead of using the leg drive to get the bar to lockout.

## FAQ

### When should I use the Squat Jerk vs the split jerk?

The Squat Jerk is used when an athlete has excellent overhead squat mobility and strength. The split jerk is more forgiving of slight errors. Most athletes use the split jerk; the Squat Jerk is a specialized technique.

### What are the prerequisites for the Squat Jerk?

Excellent overhead squat mobility, strong overhead position stability, and consistent dip-and-drive mechanics. You should be comfortable overhead squatting your bodyweight before attempting Squat Jerks.

### Why is the Squat Jerk so rare in competition?

It requires exceptional mobility, precision, and timing. Any slight forward or backward deviation in the dip causes a miss. The split jerk is more forgiving, which is why most weightlifters prefer it.

### What muscles does the Squat Jerk work?

The Squat Jerk primarily targets the quads, with secondary activation in the glutes, hamstrings, calves, core. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Squat Jerk good for beginners?

The Squat Jerk is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental legs movements first.
