# Cable Low Seated Row

## Quick answer

A seated cable row from a low pulley, pulling a straight bar to your lower chest. It works the upper back, with help from the biceps and forearms. Beginner back exercise using cable. Targets upper back, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit at the machine with your feet flat on the footrests and your knees slightly bent.
2. Grip the straight bar with your palms facing down.
3. Keep your back straight and lean slightly forward, with a slight bend in your elbows.
4. Pull the bar to your lower chest, squeezing your shoulder blades together.
5. Let the bar go back slowly until your elbows are only slightly bent.

## Pro tips

- Sit upright with a slight forward lean at the start. As you pull, retract your shoulder blades without leaning your torso backward past vertical.
- Drive your elbows straight back, not out to the sides, to target the middle traps and rhomboids along with the lats.
- Fully extend your arms on the return to get a complete stretch in the lats before the next rep.

## Common mistakes

- Excessive torso rocking back and forth to generate momentum, which takes tension off the back muscles and stresses the lower back.
- Pulling with the biceps by not initiating the movement with the shoulder blades. Think about pinching a pencil between your shoulder blades first.
- Rounding the upper back and letting the shoulders roll forward at the extended position.

## FAQ

### What grip should I use for Cable Seated Rows?

A neutral grip (palms facing each other) with a V-bar or close-grip handle targets the middle back. An overhand wide grip shifts emphasis to the upper back and rear delts.

### Should I lean forward during Cable Seated Rows?

A slight forward lean at the stretched position is fine to increase range of motion, but avoid excessive swinging. Your torso should stay mostly upright during the pull phase.

### What muscles does the Cable Low Seated Row work?

The Cable Low Seated Row primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Cable Low Seated Row good for beginners?

Yes, the Cable Low Seated Row is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building upper back strength.
