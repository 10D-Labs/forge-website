# Band Single Arm Twisting Chest Press

## Quick answer

A one-arm band press done sitting on the floor, turning your upper body as you push forward. It works the chest, with help from the shoulders and triceps. Intermediate chest exercise using resistance band. Targets chest, also works shoulders, triceps.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Triceps
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Anchor a band behind you at chest height. Sit on the floor with your legs out in front, knees slightly bent.
2. Hold the handle in one hand beside your chest, palm facing in (thumb up), elbow pointing back. Rest your other hand on your hip.
3. Press the handle straight forward until your arm is straight, letting that shoulder turn forward as you reach.
4. Return slowly to the start. Do all your reps on one arm, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Start with the band at chest height and press forward while rotating your torso away from the anchor point.
- Fully extend the arm at the end of the press and twist through the ribcage, not the hips.
- Control the return to the starting position for a 2-second eccentric to maximize chest and core engagement.

## Common mistakes

- Rotating the hips instead of the thoracic spine, which reduces the chest and oblique engagement.
- Not fully extending the arm, which limits the range of motion and reduces pec contraction.
- Standing too close to the anchor point, which results in insufficient band tension.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the twisting Chest Press work?

It targets the chest, anterior deltoid, and triceps through the pressing motion, while the twist recruits the obliques and serratus anterior.

### Is this a replacement for bench press?

No. It is a functional accessory exercise that trains pressing with rotation. Use it alongside compound pressing movements, not as a substitute.

### How far should you stand from the anchor?

Far enough that there is significant tension at the starting position with your hand at your chest. If the band is slack at the start, step further away.

### What muscles does the Band Single Arm Twisting Chest Press work?

The Band Single Arm Twisting Chest Press primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the shoulders, triceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Band Single Arm Twisting Chest Press good for beginners?

The Band Single Arm Twisting Chest Press is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of chest strength with easier variations first.
