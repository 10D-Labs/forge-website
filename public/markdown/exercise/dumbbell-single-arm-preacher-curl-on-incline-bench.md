# Dumbbell Single Arm Preacher Curl on Incline Bench

## Quick answer

A one-arm curl done standing behind an incline bench, with the back of your upper arm resting on the backrest. It works the biceps and the forearms. Intermediate arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Raise a bench's backrest to about 45 degrees. Stand behind it and lean over the top so the back of one upper arm rests flat on the pad.
2. Hold a dumbbell in that hand with your arm nearly straight down the pad, palm facing up.
3. Curl the dumbbell up toward your shoulder, keeping your upper arm pressed into the pad.
4. Lower it slowly until your arm is nearly straight. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Stand behind an incline bench and rest the back of your upper arm on the backrest, with your armpit at the top of the pad.
- Curl the dumbbell up without lifting your arm off the pad, then lower it until your arm is nearly straight.
- Lower slowly; the bottom of a preacher curl is where the biceps work hardest.

## Common mistakes

- Lifting the elbow off the pad to swing the weight up.
- Dropping the dumbbell at the bottom, which can strain the elbow.
- Leaning your body back to help the curl.

## FAQ

### How is this different from a normal preacher curl?

It uses the backrest of an incline bench instead of a preacher pad, so you can do preacher curls when your gym has no preacher bench.

### What angle should the bench be?

Around 45-60 degrees. A steeper backrest feels more like a standing curl; a lower one makes the bottom of the curl harder.

### Which arm should I start with?

Start with your weaker arm and match the reps on your stronger arm.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Single Arm Preacher Curl on Incline Bench work?

The Dumbbell Single Arm Preacher Curl on Incline Bench primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Single Arm Preacher Curl on Incline Bench good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Single Arm Preacher Curl on Incline Bench is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of biceps strength with easier variations first.
