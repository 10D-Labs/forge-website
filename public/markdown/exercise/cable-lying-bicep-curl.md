# Cable Lying Bicep Curl

## Quick answer

A curl done lying face up on a bench, pulling a cable bar from a low pulley at your feet. It works the biceps, and the cable keeps them under tension the whole way. Intermediate arms exercise using cable. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Attach a straight bar to a low pulley and lie face up on a flat bench, with the pulley at your feet.
2. Put your feet flat on the floor. Hold the bar with your palms facing up, hands shoulder-width apart.
3. Start with your arms straight and your elbows close to your sides.
4. Keep your upper arms still and curl the bar up toward your shoulders.
5. Lower the bar slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Position the bench about 2 feet from the low pulley so the cable pulls at an angle that keeps tension through the full range.
- Pin your upper arms to your sides and keep your elbows pointed at the ceiling throughout the curl.
- Squeeze the biceps hard at the top, then take 3 seconds to lower the bar back down.

## Common mistakes

- Positioning too far from or too close to the pulley, which changes the angle of tension and may reduce bicep loading.
- Lifting the elbows off the bench during the curl, which recruits the shoulders.
- Arching the back off the bench to generate momentum on heavy reps.

## FAQ

### What is the benefit of doing curls while lying down?

Lying down eliminates all possibility of body swing or momentum, making this one of the strictest Bicep Curl variations available. Every rep is pure bicep contraction.

### Is the cable lying curl better than incline dumbbell curls?

They're complementary. The lying Cable Curl provides constant tension, while incline dumbbell curls provide a deeper stretch at the bottom. Both are excellent for bicep hypertrophy.

### What attachment should I use?

A straight bar works best for lying Cable Curls. An EZ bar attachment is also fine if you prefer the angled grip.

### What muscles does the Cable Lying Bicep Curl work?

The Cable Lying Bicep Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Cable Lying Bicep Curl good for beginners?

The Cable Lying Bicep Curl is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of biceps strength with easier variations first.
