# Full Planche Pushup

## Quick answer

An advanced pushup done balancing on your hands with your whole body, feet included, held off the floor. It works the chest, shoulders, triceps and abs. Advanced chest exercise using body weight. Targets chest, also works shoulders, triceps, core.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Triceps, Core
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Get into a pushup position with your hands slightly wider than shoulder-width apart.
2. Lean forward over your hands and lift your feet off the floor, balancing on your hands with your body straight from head to heels.
3. Bend your elbows, keeping them close to your sides, until your chest is just above the floor.
4. Push through your hands to straighten your arms, keeping your feet off the floor.

## Pro tips

- Hold a solid Full Planche for at least 5 seconds before attempting the Pushup, as the bottom position is even more demanding.
- Lower slowly (3 seconds) and press up explosively to manage the extreme shoulder loading.
- Keep your body perfectly straight throughout; any hip pike means you're reducing the difficulty and not training the actual movement.

## Common mistakes

- Piking the hips at the bottom of the Pushup to make the press easier, which isn't a true Full Planche Pushup.
- Performing half reps by not lowering the chest close enough to the ground.
- Training this movement too frequently without adequate rest, leading to wrist, elbow, or shoulder overuse injuries.

## FAQ

### How hard is a Full Planche Pushup?

It's one of the hardest bodyweight exercises in existence. Very few people in the world can perform multiple clean Full Planche Pushups with perfect form.

### What's harder: Full Planche Pushup or one-arm Pushup?

The Full Planche Pushup is significantly harder. It requires holding your entire body horizontal while pressing, which demands far more shoulder and core strength.

### What should I train before attempting planche Pushups?

Master the Full Planche hold (5+ seconds), Straddle Planche Pushups, and tuck planche Pushups first. Each progression builds the specific pressing strength needed.

### What muscles does the Full Planche Pushup work?

The Full Planche Pushup primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the shoulders, triceps, core. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Full Planche Pushup good for beginners?

The Full Planche Pushup is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental chest movements first.
