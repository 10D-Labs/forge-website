# Leg Press

## Quick answer

A machine press done sitting back, pushing a weighted footplate up and away from you on a 45-degree slant. It works the glutes, plus the quads, hamstrings and inner thighs. Beginner legs exercise using machine. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Adjust the seat and footplate of the 45-degree leg press to fit you.
2. Sit with your back against the backrest and your feet shoulder-width apart on the footplate. Hold the handles at your sides.
3. Push the footplate away by straightening your legs, keeping your heels on the plate. Stop just before your knees lock.
4. Bend your knees to lower the footplate slowly back toward you.

## Pro tips

- Position your feet shoulder-width apart in the center of the platform; higher foot placement targets glutes more, lower targets quads more.
- Press through your heels to maximize glute and hamstring activation rather than pushing through the toes.
- Stop lowering when your lower back starts to round off the pad (the 'butt wink') to protect your lumbar spine.

## Common mistakes

- Lowering the sled too far, causing the lower back to round off the pad, which puts the lumbar spine at risk.
- Locking the knees fully at the top of the press, which shifts all the load onto the joint instead of the muscles.
- Placing the feet too low on the platform, which puts excessive stress on the knees.

## FAQ

### Where should I place my feet on the Leg Press?

Feet in the center at shoulder width is the standard position. Higher placement shifts emphasis to glutes and hamstrings. Lower placement increases quad involvement but also knee stress.

### How deep should I go on the Leg Press?

Lower the sled until your thighs are about parallel to the platform or until you feel your lower back start to lift off the pad. Stop before the back rounds.

### Is the Leg Press a good substitute for squats?

It's a solid supplement but not a full replacement. The Leg Press removes the core stability and balance demands of squats. Use both if possible.

### What muscles does the Leg Press work?

The Leg Press primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Leg Press good for beginners?

Yes, the Leg Press is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building glutes strength.
