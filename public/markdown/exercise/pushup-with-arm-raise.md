# Pushup with Arm Raise

## Quick answer

A pushup with a reach at the top: you lift one hand and stretch it forward past your head. It works the chest, with help from the triceps, shoulders and abs. Advanced chest exercise using body weight. Targets chest, also works triceps, shoulders, core.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders, Core
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Get into a pushup position on your toes, hands under your shoulders, arms straight, body straight from head to heels.
2. Bend your elbows to lower your chest until it's just above the floor.
3. Push back up until your arms are straight.
4. Lift one hand and reach it forward and up past your head, keeping your hips level.
5. Put the hand back down and do the next pushup, raising the other arm. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Do a full Pushup, then at the top lift one hand and reach it forward past your head.
- Widen your feet a little to stay steady while one hand is off the floor.
- Brace your abs so your hips stay level as you reach; switch hands each rep.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips twist toward the raised arm, which loses the core work.
- Rushing the reach instead of holding it for a moment.
- Cutting the Pushup short to get to the reach faster.

## FAQ

### What does the arm raise add to a Pushup?

Lifting one hand makes your abs and the supporting shoulder work to keep you steady, so you train the core along with the chest and triceps.

### How do I make it easier?

Do it with your hands on a bench, or with your knees on the floor, until you can keep your hips level during the reach.

### What muscles does the Pushup with Arm Raise work?

The Pushup with Arm Raise primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders, core. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Pushup with Arm Raise good for beginners?

The Pushup with Arm Raise is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental chest movements first.
