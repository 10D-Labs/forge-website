# Hammer Grip Preacher Curl

## Quick answer

A machine preacher curl with handles that keep your palms facing each other, thumbs up. It works the biceps and the forearms. Beginner arms exercise using machine. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Adjust the seat height. Sit at the machine with your chest against the pad and your upper arms resting on it.
2. Hold the handles with your palms facing each other (thumbs up).
3. Keep your upper arms on the pad and curl the handles up toward your forehead.
4. Lower the handles slowly back to the start.

## Pro tips

- Press your chest firmly against the preacher pad to prevent your shoulders from lifting during the curl.
- Keep the neutral (hammer) grip throughout; don't let your wrists rotate at the top or bottom of the movement.
- Lower the handles slowly for a 3-second eccentric to fully load the brachialis, which is the primary target with this grip.

## Common mistakes

- Lifting the elbows off the pad at the top of the curl, which recruits the front deltoids and reduces bicep isolation.
- Using too much weight and swinging the body to complete reps, defeating the purpose of the preacher pad.
- Cutting the range of motion short by not fully extending at the bottom.

## FAQ

### What does the hammer grip do differently than a regular Preacher Curl?

The neutral grip shifts emphasis from the biceps brachii to the brachialis and brachioradialis, building arm thickness when viewed from the front.

### Can the Hammer Grip Preacher Curl help forearm development?

Yes, the brachioradialis is a major forearm muscle, and the neutral grip loads it significantly. This exercise builds forearm size as a secondary benefit.

### How many sets and reps should I do?

Three to four sets of 8-12 reps works well for hypertrophy. Keep the weight moderate and focus on the squeeze at the top.

### What muscles does the Hammer Grip Preacher Curl work?

The Hammer Grip Preacher Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Hammer Grip Preacher Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Hammer Grip Preacher Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building biceps strength.
