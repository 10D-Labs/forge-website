# Smith Machine Clean Grip Front Squat

## Quick answer

A front squat in a Smith machine, with the bar resting across the front of your shoulders and your hands holding it in place. It works the glutes, with help from the quads and hamstrings. Intermediate legs exercise using smith machine. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves, core.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Smith Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves, Core
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set the Smith machine bar at shoulder height and stand facing it, feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Hold the bar with your palms facing away from you, hands a little wider than your shoulders.
3. Rest the bar across the front of your shoulders and collarbones, then step back.
4. Keep your chest up and bend your knees and hips to sit back, as if into a chair.
5. Stop when your thighs are level with the floor or a little below, then push through your heels to stand back up.

## Pro tips

- Rest the bar on your front deltoids with elbows high (upper arms parallel to the floor) to maintain the rack position.
- Keep your torso as upright as possible throughout the squat to prevent the bar from rolling forward.
- Push your knees out over your toes and squat to at least parallel depth for full quad and glute activation.

## Common mistakes

- Dropping the elbows, which causes the bar to roll forward and puts dangerous stress on the wrists.
- Leaning too far forward, turning the front squat into a good-morning-like movement.
- Not squatting deep enough due to the unfamiliar front rack position.

## FAQ

### Why use the Smith machine for front squats?

The fixed bar path lets you focus on staying upright and hitting depth without worrying about balance. It is especially helpful for learning the front squat movement pattern.

### What is the clean grip?

The clean grip means holding the bar with your full hand (fingertips or full grip) on the bar, elbows high. The alternative is a cross-arm grip where you cross your arms over the bar.

### Does the front squat target different muscles than the back squat?

The front squat places more emphasis on the quads and requires more upper back and core engagement to stay upright. The back squat distributes more work to the glutes and hamstrings.

### What muscles does the Smith Machine Clean Grip Front Squat work?

The Smith Machine Clean Grip Front Squat primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves, core. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Smith Machine Clean Grip Front Squat good for beginners?

The Smith Machine Clean Grip Front Squat is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of glutes strength with easier variations first.
