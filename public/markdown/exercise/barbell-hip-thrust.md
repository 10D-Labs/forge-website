# Barbell Hip Thrust

## Quick answer

A hip lift with your upper back on a bench and a barbell across your hips. It works the glutes, with help from the hamstrings and quads. Beginner legs exercise using barbell. Targets glutes, also works hamstrings, quadriceps.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Quadriceps
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on the floor with your upper back against the edge of a flat bench and a loaded barbell across your hips.
2. Plant your feet flat, about shoulder-width apart, knees bent. Hold the bar with your hands just outside your hips, palms facing down.
3. Push through your feet and drive your hips up until your body is level from shoulders to knees.
4. Lower your hips slowly until they are just above the floor.

## Pro tips

- Position the barbell in the crease of your hips (use a bar pad) and drive your hips straight up by squeezing your glutes, not arching your lower back.
- At the top of the movement, your shins should be vertical and your torso parallel to the floor, creating a straight line from shoulders to knees.
- Tuck your chin slightly and look forward (not at the ceiling) to keep a posterior Pelvic Tilt and maximize glute engagement.

## Common mistakes

- Hyperextending the lower back at the top instead of achieving full hip extension through glute contraction.
- Placing the feet too far from or too close to the body; shins should be vertical at the top position.
- Pushing through the toes instead of the heels, which recruits the quads more than the glutes.

## FAQ

### Why is the Barbell Hip Thrust considered the best glute exercise?

It allows heavy loading in the position of peak glute contraction (full hip extension). Unlike squats or Deadlifts, the resistance is highest when the glutes are maximally shortened, making it uniquely effective for glute growth.

### How do I set up for Barbell Hip Thrusts?

Sit on the floor with your upper back against a bench, roll the barbell over your legs into your hip crease, plant your feet flat, and drive your hips up. Use a bar pad or towel for comfort.

### How heavy should I hip thrust?

Most people can hip thrust 1.5-2 times their body weight with training. Start with just the bar to learn the movement, then add weight quickly since the glutes are powerful muscles.

### What muscles does the Barbell Hip Thrust work?

The Barbell Hip Thrust primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, quadriceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Barbell Hip Thrust good for beginners?

Yes, the Barbell Hip Thrust is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building glutes strength.
