# Bent Knee Hip Raise

## Quick answer

A lying ab move where you pull your bent knees in and curl your hips up off the floor. It works the abs. Beginner core exercise using body weight. Targets abs, also works hip flexors.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hip Flexors
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your back with your knees bent and feet flat on the floor. Rest your arms at your sides, palms down.
2. Lift your feet and bring your knees in over your chest.
3. Curl your hips off the floor, pushing your feet up toward the ceiling.
4. Lower your hips, then your feet, slowly back to the floor.

## Pro tips

- Drive through your heels and squeeze your glutes at the top, holding for 2 seconds to maximize muscle activation.
- Keep your core braced throughout the movement to prevent your lower back from hyperextending at the top.
- Position your feet about hip-width apart with toes pointing forward for optimal glute engagement.

## Common mistakes

- Hyperextending the lower back at the top instead of stopping when the body forms a straight line from knees to shoulders.
- Pushing through the toes instead of the heels, which shifts the emphasis from glutes to quads.
- Rushing through reps without pausing at the top, missing the peak contraction.

## FAQ

### How is a Bent Knee Hip Raise different from a glute bridge?

They are essentially the same exercise. Both involve lying on your back and raising your hips by squeezing the glutes. Some variations elevate the shoulders or feet, but the base movement is identical.

### Can I add weight to Bent Knee Hip Raises?

Yes. Place a dumbbell or barbell across your hips, using a pad for comfort. Adding weight is the natural progression once bodyweight becomes easy for 15+ reps.

### What muscles does the Bent Knee Hip Raise work?

The Bent Knee Hip Raise primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the hip flexors. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Bent Knee Hip Raise good for beginners?

Yes, the Bent Knee Hip Raise is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building abs strength.
