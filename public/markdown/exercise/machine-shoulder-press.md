# Machine Shoulder Press

## Quick answer

A seated shoulder press on a machine, pushing the handles up overhead. It works the front of the shoulders, with help from the triceps and upper chest. Beginner shoulders exercise using machine. Targets shoulders, also works triceps, upper chest.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Upper Chest
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Adjust the seat height and sit on the machine with your back against the backrest.
2. Grip the handles with your palms facing forward, hands slightly wider than shoulder-width apart.
3. Push the handles up until your arms are straight, without locking your elbows.
4. Lower the handles slowly back down.

## Pro tips

- Adjust the seat so the handles start at shoulder height and press straight up, finishing with straight arms without locking the elbows hard.
- Keep your back pressed against the pad and avoid arching.
- Lower the handles to the start under control instead of letting the weight stack drop.

## Common mistakes

- Arching the back to push heavier weight, which stresses the lower back.
- Not pressing through the full range of motion. Go from shoulder height to straight arms.
- Setting the seat too low, which makes the start position awkward and strains the shoulders.
