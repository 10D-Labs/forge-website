# EZ Bar Seated Close Grip Concentration Curl

## Quick answer

A seated curl with an EZ bar, hands close together and elbows braced against your inner thighs. Bracing your elbows keeps the work on the biceps. Beginner arms exercise using ez bar. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** EZ Bar
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on a bench with your feet flat on the floor and lean forward.
2. Hold an EZ bar with your palms facing up and your hands close together.
3. Rest your elbows against the insides of your thighs, just above the knees.
4. Curl the bar up toward your shoulders, keeping your upper arms still.
5. Lower the bar slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Rest both elbows against the insides of your thighs to create a solid pivot point and prevent cheating.
- Use the inner narrow grips of the EZ bar and curl the weight slowly to your shoulders.
- Squeeze the biceps hard at the top for a 2-second hold before lowering.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the elbows slide off the thighs during the curl, which allows the shoulders to assist.
- Rounding the back and hunching forward, which can strain the lower back.
- Using too much weight for the concentration position, which forces compensatory body movement.

## FAQ

### What does the close grip concentration curl target?

The close grip and braced elbow position isolates the biceps with an emphasis on the long (outer) head, which builds the bicep peak.

### Can I use a straight bar instead of an EZ bar?

Yes, but the EZ bar is preferred because its angled grips reduce wrist strain in the close-grip position, which can be uncomfortable with a straight bar.

### Is this a good finishing exercise for biceps?

Excellent choice for a finisher. The strict concentration position and close grip create an intense pump and deep fatigue in the biceps.

### What muscles does the EZ Bar Seated Close Grip Concentration Curl work?

The EZ Bar Seated Close Grip Concentration Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the EZ Bar Seated Close Grip Concentration Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the EZ Bar Seated Close Grip Concentration Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building biceps strength.
