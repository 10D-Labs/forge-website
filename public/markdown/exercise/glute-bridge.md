# Glute Bridge

## Quick answer

A floor exercise where you lie on your back and lift your hips with your knees bent. It works the glutes, with help from the hamstrings and lower back. Beginner legs exercise using body weight. Targets glutes, also works hamstrings, lower back.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Lower Back
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your back with your knees bent and feet flat on the floor, about hip-width apart. Rest your arms at your sides, palms facing down.
2. Push through your heels and lift your hips until your body is straight from shoulders to knees.
3. Lower your hips slowly back to the floor.

## Pro tips

- Drive through your heels, not your toes, to work the glutes more than the quads.
- Squeeze your glutes hard at the top and hold for 2 seconds before lowering.
- Keep your chin slightly tucked to keep your neck neutral.

## Common mistakes

- Arching the lower back at the top instead of stopping when your hips line up with your knees and shoulders.
- Pushing through the toes instead of the heels, which shifts the work to the quads and calves.
- Letting the knees cave inward during the movement.

## FAQ

### What's the difference between a bridge and a hip thrust?

A bridge is done from the floor with your back on the ground. A hip thrust has your upper back raised on a bench, which allows more range of motion and usually heavier loads.

### Is the Glute Bridge good for lower back pain?

It often helps. It strengthens the glutes and hamstrings, which support the lower back. Avoid arching at the top, and if it hurts, reduce the range of motion.

### How many reps should I do for glute bridges?

Aim for 15-20 reps with a 2-second hold at the top.

### What muscles does the Glute Bridge work?

The Glute Bridge primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, lower back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Glute Bridge good for beginners?

Yes, the Glute Bridge is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building glutes strength.
