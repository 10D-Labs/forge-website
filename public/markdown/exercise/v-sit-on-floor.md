# V-sit on Floor

## Quick answer

A V-sit done on straight arms: with your hands on the floor beside your hips, you lift your hips and raise straight legs until they point up. It works the abs, with help from the front of the hips. Advanced core exercise using body weight. Targets abs, also works hip flexors.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hip Flexors
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on the floor with your legs straight in front of you. Place your hands flat on the floor beside your hips, arms straight.
2. Press down through your hands to lift your hips just off the floor.
3. At the same time, raise your straight legs until they point up at the ceiling, close to your chest.
4. Lower your legs and hips slowly back to the floor.

## Pro tips

- Lean back slightly and press through your palms to counterbalance the weight of your raised legs.
- Lift your legs as high as possible while keeping them straight; the higher the angle, the harder the compression.
- Engage your hip flexors by pulling your knees toward your face, not just lifting your feet.

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the upper back excessively to compensate for tight hamstrings, which collapses the hold.
- Bending the knees to get the legs higher instead of working on compression flexibility.
- Holding your breath during the hold instead of taking controlled, shallow breaths.

## FAQ

### What's the difference between a V-sit and an L-sit?

In a V-sit, your legs are raised higher than 90 degrees (toward your face), requiring much more hip flexor strength and hamstring flexibility than the 90-degree L-sit.

### How do I progress from an L-sit to a V-sit?

Practice compression drills (seated pike with legs lifting higher and higher) and hamstring stretching. Gradually increase the angle of your L-sit over weeks.

### How long should I hold a V-sit?

Even 5-10 seconds with good form is impressive. Work on building to 15 seconds before adding sets.

### What muscles does the V-sit on Floor work?

The V-sit on Floor primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the hip flexors. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the V-sit on Floor good for beginners?

The V-sit on Floor is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental core movements first.
