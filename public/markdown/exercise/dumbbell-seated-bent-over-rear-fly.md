# Dumbbell Seated Bent Over Rear Fly

## Quick answer

A rear fly done sitting on the end of a bench, leaning forward over your knees. It works the rear shoulders and the upper back. Intermediate shoulders exercise using dumbbell. Targets shoulders, also works trapezius, rhomboids.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Trapezius, Rhomboids
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on the end of a flat bench, feet flat. Lean forward from your hips until your chest is over your knees, back flat.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand, hanging beside your lower legs, palms facing each other.
3. Keep a slight bend in your elbows and raise the dumbbells out to the sides until your arms are level with your shoulders.
4. Lower slowly back to the start.

## Pro tips

- Sit on the end of a bench and lean forward over your knees until your chest is close to your thighs, then raise the dumbbells out to the sides with a slight elbow bend.
- Think about pushing the dumbbells away from each other rather than lifting them, which helps cue the rear delts.
- Keep your chin tucked and neck neutral while you lean forward.

## Common mistakes

- Not leaning forward enough, which turns this into a Lateral Raise and misses the rear delts.
- Bending the elbows too much during the lift, turning the fly into a row that recruits the lats and biceps.
- Sitting up as you lift to swing the weights up.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Seated Bent Over Rear Fly work?

Primarily the rear delts, with help from the rhomboids, middle traps and rotator cuff.

### How far should I lean forward for rear flyes?

Your torso should be close to parallel with the floor. Leaning less shifts the work to the side of the shoulders.

### Should I do rear flyes seated or standing?

Seated limits momentum and keeps the movement strict. Standing allows slightly more weight but makes it easier to cheat.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Seated Bent Over Rear Fly work?

The Dumbbell Seated Bent Over Rear Fly primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the trapezius, rhomboids. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Dumbbell Seated Bent Over Rear Fly good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Seated Bent Over Rear Fly is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of shoulders strength with easier variations first.
