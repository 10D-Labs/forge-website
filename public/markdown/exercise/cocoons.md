# Cocoons

## Quick answer

A full-body crunch that goes from lying stretched out to curled in a ball, hugging your knees. It works the abs and the front of the hips. Beginner core exercise using body weight. Targets abs, also works hip flexors, lower back.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hip Flexors, Lower Back
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your back on the floor with your legs straight and your arms stretched out on the floor past your head.
2. Lift your shoulders and legs off the floor at the same time, pulling your knees in toward your chest.
3. Wrap your arms around your shins so you finish balanced on your butt, curled up in a ball.
4. Uncurl slowly and stretch back out flat.

## Pro tips

- Start lying flat with arms extended overhead and legs straight. Simultaneously bring your knees to your chest and your arms forward, curling into a ball.
- Focus on contracting your abs to pull everything inward, not just swinging your limbs toward each other.
- Slowly extend back to the fully stretched position on each rep. The eccentric phase is where the abs work hardest to resist gravity.

## Common mistakes

- Using momentum by swinging the arms and legs together rather than contracting the abs to curl the body.
- Not fully extending back to the start position, which shortens the range of motion and reduces the stretch on the abs.
- Lifting with the hip flexors instead of the abs. If you feel it in your hip crease more than your midsection, slow down and focus on the spinal curl.

## FAQ

### What muscles do Cocoons work?

Primarily the rectus abdominis (six-pack muscle) through the full range of spinal flexion. Secondary muscles include the hip flexors and lower back as stabilizers.

### Are Cocoons better than regular crunches?

Cocoons use a greater range of motion than crunches since you start from a fully extended position. This creates more total ab work per rep, making them more challenging and effective for ab development.

### What muscles does the Cocoons work?

The Cocoons primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the hip flexors, lower back. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Cocoons good for beginners?

Yes, the Cocoons is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building abs strength.
