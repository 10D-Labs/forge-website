# Finger Curls

## Quick answer

A seated forearm exercise where you roll a barbell from your fingers up into your palms. It works the forearms and grip. Beginner arms exercise using barbell. Targets forearms, also works wrist flexors, grip muscles.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Forearms
- **Secondary Muscles:** Wrist Flexors, Grip Muscles
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on the end of a bench with your feet flat on the floor. Hold a barbell with your palms facing up.
2. Rest your forearms on your thighs so your wrists hang off past your knees. Let the bar roll down into your fingers.
3. Curl your fingers to roll the bar up into your palms.
4. Uncurl slowly, letting the bar roll back down to your fingers.

## Pro tips

- Let the bar roll down to your fingertips at the bottom before curling it back into your palm for a full range of motion.
- Keep your forearms pressed firmly against your thighs throughout the movement to isolate the finger flexors.
- Use a slow, deliberate tempo. This exercise is about grip endurance and forearm development, not heavy loading.

## Common mistakes

- Using the wrists to curl instead of isolating the finger flexors, which turns this into a wrist curl.
- Going too heavy and using a jerky motion, which can strain the finger tendons and reduces the training effect.
- Not allowing the bar to roll far enough down to the fingertips, cutting the range of motion short.

## FAQ

### What's the difference between Finger Curls and wrist curls?

Finger Curls isolate the finger flexor muscles by rolling the bar to the fingertips and curling it back. Wrist curls keep the fingers closed and flex the wrist, targeting the wrist flexors more directly.

### How do Finger Curls help with grip strength?

They directly strengthen the muscles responsible for crushing grip (finger flexors), which transfers to better performance on Deadlifts, Pullups, and any exercise where grip is a limiting factor.

### What muscles does the Finger Curls work?

The Finger Curls primarily targets the forearms, with secondary activation in the wrist flexors, grip muscles. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Finger Curls good for beginners?

Yes, the Finger Curls is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building forearms strength.
