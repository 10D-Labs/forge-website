# Cable Single Arm Tricep Pushdown

## Quick answer

A one-arm triceps pushdown on a high cable with a single handle. It works the triceps, with some help from the forearms. Beginner arms exercise using cable. Targets triceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Attach a single handle to a high pulley and stand facing the machine, feet shoulder-width apart and knees slightly bent.
2. Hold the handle in one hand, palm facing down, with your elbow bent and tucked at your side.
3. Keeping your upper arm still, push the handle down until your arm is straight.
4. Let the handle rise slowly until your elbow is fully bent again. Do all your reps with one arm, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Keep your working elbow pinned to your side and only extend at the elbow joint; your upper arm should not move.
- Squeeze the tricep hard at full extension and hold for a one-count before letting the cable pull your forearm back up.
- Use your free hand to hold onto the machine or your hip for stability.

## Common mistakes

- Pushing down with the entire body by leaning over the cable, which turns it into a pressing motion.
- Letting the elbow drift forward during the extension, which recruits the shoulder.
- Using too much weight and compensating with body lean instead of pure Tricep Extension.

## FAQ

### Why do single-arm pushdowns instead of both arms?

Single-arm work isolates each tricep independently, preventing your stronger arm from dominating. It also lets you focus entirely on the mind-muscle connection of one tricep at a time.

### What handle should I use?

A single D-handle or rope end works well. The D-handle allows a neutral or pronated grip; the rope allows a more natural wrist position.

### Where should the cable be set?

Set the pulley at chest to head height. This gives you enough range of motion to fully extend the arm without the weight stack bottoming out.

### What muscles does the Cable Single Arm Tricep Pushdown work?

The Cable Single Arm Tricep Pushdown primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Cable Single Arm Tricep Pushdown good for beginners?

Yes, the Cable Single Arm Tricep Pushdown is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building triceps strength.
