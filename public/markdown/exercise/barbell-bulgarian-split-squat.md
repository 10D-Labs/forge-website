# Barbell Bulgarian Split Squat

## Quick answer

A split squat with a barbell on your back and your rear foot raised on a bench. It works the quads and glutes of the front leg. Intermediate legs exercise using barbell. Targets quads, also works glutes, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Quads
- **Secondary Muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Rest a barbell across your upper back, hands just wider than your shoulders, palms facing forward.
2. Stand a stride in front of a flat bench, facing away from it. Reach one foot back and rest the top of it on the bench.
3. Bend your front knee and lower straight down until your front thigh is about level with the floor.
4. Push through your front foot to stand back up. Do all your reps on one leg, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Keep your front foot flat and drive through the whole foot; your back leg is for balance, not for pushing.
- Lower until your front thigh is about parallel to the floor, keeping your torso upright with the bar on your back.
- Set your front foot far enough from the bench that your front shin stays close to vertical at the bottom.

## Common mistakes

- Placing the front foot too close to the bench, which pushes the front knee far forward and makes balancing harder.
- Leaning forward excessively, shifting the load to the lower back instead of the front leg.
- Letting the front knee drift inward on the way down, which stresses the knee.

## FAQ

### Is the Barbell Bulgarian Split Squat harder than a regular split squat?

Yes. Raising the rear foot on a bench puts more of your weight on the front leg and increases the range of motion, so most people use less weight than on a split squat with both feet on the floor.

### How much weight should I use for Barbell Bulgarian Split Squats?

Start with the empty bar until your balance is steady, then add weight in small jumps. Most people use well under half of their back squat weight.

### Can Bulgarian Split Squats fix muscle imbalances between legs?

Yes. Single-leg exercises make each leg work on its own, so the stronger leg can't make up for the weaker one. Train the weaker leg first and match the reps on the stronger side.

### What muscles does the Barbell Bulgarian Split Squat work?

The Barbell Bulgarian Split Squat primarily targets the quads, with secondary activation in the glutes, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Barbell Bulgarian Split Squat good for beginners?

The Barbell Bulgarian Split Squat is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of quads strength with easier variations first.
