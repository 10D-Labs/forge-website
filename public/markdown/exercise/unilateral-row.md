# Unilateral Row

## Quick answer

A one-arm cable row done bent forward, with one knee and forearm resting on the machine's pads. It works the upper back, with help from the biceps and forearms. Beginner back exercise using machine. Targets upper back, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand on one leg facing the machine. Rest your other knee on the rear pad and that same side's forearm on the front pad, back flat.
2. With your free hand, hold the low cable handle, arm hanging straight down and palm facing in.
3. Pull the handle up to the side of your waist, driving your elbow back past your body.
4. Lower it slowly until your arm is straight. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Pull the handle toward your lower ribcage, not your shoulder, to maximize upper back and lat activation.
- Keep your torso square to the machine and avoid rotating to cheat the weight up.
- Pause for a full second at peak contraction and actively squeeze your shoulder blade toward your spine.

## Common mistakes

- Using body momentum by rocking the torso back and forth instead of isolating the back muscles.
- Shrugging the shoulder up during the pull instead of keeping it depressed and retracted.
- Rushing the eccentric phase and letting the weight stack slam between reps.

## FAQ

### What is the benefit of doing rows one arm at a time?

Unilateral Rows let you identify and fix imbalances between your left and right sides, and you can typically achieve a greater range of motion than bilateral rows.

### Should I use an overhand or underhand grip?

An overhand grip emphasizes the upper back and rear delts, while an underhand grip recruits more of the lats and biceps. Alternate between both for complete development.

### How do I know if the seat height is correct?

Your chest should be flat against the pad with the handle at roughly mid-torso height when your arm is extended. If you are reaching up or down, adjust the seat.

### What muscles does the Unilateral Row work?

The Unilateral Row primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Unilateral Row good for beginners?

Yes, the Unilateral Row is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building upper back strength.
