# Cable Reverse Single Arm Curl

## Quick answer

A one-arm cable curl done with your palm facing down. It works the biceps and forearms. Beginner arms exercise using cable. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Attach a single handle to a low pulley and stand facing the machine with your feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Hold the handle in one hand with your palm facing down, arm straight and elbow close to your side.
3. Curl the handle up toward your shoulder, keeping your elbow at your side.
4. Lower it slowly until your arm is straight.

## Pro tips

- Keep your palm facing down (pronated grip) throughout the entire curl to target the brachioradialis and forearm extensors.
- Pin your elbow to your side and curl only with the forearm, eliminating any shoulder movement.
- Use a slow, controlled tempo since the brachioradialis is a smaller muscle that fatigues quickly with sloppy form.

## Common mistakes

- Rotating the wrist to a supinated (palm up) position during the curl, which turns it into a standard biceps curl.
- Swinging the elbow forward to help lift the weight instead of keeping it locked at the side.
- Using too much weight and compensating with body lean or shoulder momentum.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the reverse curl target?

It primarily targets the brachioradialis (the large forearm muscle) and the brachialis, with less biceps brachii involvement compared to a standard supinated curl.

### Why use a cable instead of a dumbbell for reverse curls?

The cable provides constant tension throughout the entire range of motion, including at the top where a dumbbell reverse curl loses tension.

### How does this help with forearm development?

The pronated grip places the biceps in a mechanically disadvantaged position, forcing the brachioradialis and forearm muscles to handle more of the load.

### What muscles does the Cable Reverse Single Arm Curl work?

The Cable Reverse Single Arm Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Cable Reverse Single Arm Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Cable Reverse Single Arm Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building biceps strength.
