# Diamond Pushup

## Quick answer

A pushup with your hands together under your chest, thumbs and index fingers touching in a diamond shape. It mainly works the triceps, with help from the chest and shoulders. Intermediate arms exercise using body weight. Targets triceps, also works chest, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Chest, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Start at the top of a pushup with your hands close together under your chest, thumbs and index fingers touching to make a diamond shape.
2. Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels.
3. Lower your chest toward your hands, keeping your elbows close to your body.
4. Push back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Form a diamond shape with your thumbs and index fingers touching directly under your chest. This hand position maximizes tricep engagement.
- Keep your elbows tucked close to your body (pointing backward, not outward) as you lower your chest to your hands.
- Lower slowly until your chest touches the diamond, then press up with an explosive push. If you cannot reach full depth, start with an easier variation.

## Common mistakes

- Placing the hands too far forward (under the face instead of the chest), which puts excessive stress on the wrists and shoulders.
- Flaring the elbows outward, which shifts the load from the triceps to the chest and can strain the shoulders.
- Sagging the hips due to core weakness, which reduces the exercise to a partial range of motion.

## FAQ

### Are Diamond Pushups the best tricep bodyweight exercise?

Yes, EMG research shows that Diamond Pushups produce the highest tricep activation among all bodyweight exercises, even surpassing bench dips and Close Grip Pushups.

### I cannot do Diamond Pushups yet. How do I work up to them?

Start with Close Grip Pushups (hands under shoulders), then gradually narrow the grip over weeks until you reach the diamond position. You can also do Diamond Pushups from your knees to build strength.

### What muscles does the Diamond Pushup work?

The Diamond Pushup primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the chest, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Diamond Pushup good for beginners?

The Diamond Pushup is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of triceps strength with easier variations first.
