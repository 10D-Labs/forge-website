# Cable Straight Back Seated Row

## Quick answer

A seated cable row with a V-shaped handle, pulling it toward your body with your back kept straight. It works the upper back, plus the biceps, forearms and shoulders. Beginner back exercise using cable. Targets upper back, also works biceps, forearms, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit at the cable row machine with your feet flat on the footrests and your knees slightly bent.
2. Hold the V-shaped handle with your palms facing each other. Keep your back straight and lean slightly forward from your hips.
3. Pull the handle toward your body, squeezing your shoulder blades together.
4. Let the handle go back slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Maintain a rigid, upright torso throughout the entire set. The straight back position specifically targets the mid-back muscles and prevents lat dominance.
- Pull the handles to your lower ribs and squeeze your shoulder blades together for a two-second hold.
- Keep your chest lifted and think about pushing your chest forward as you pull back.

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the upper back during the pull, which defeats the purpose of the straight-back variation.
- Swinging the torso to generate momentum, which removes the strict posture requirement of this exercise.
- Shrugging the shoulders up as you pull, which recruits the upper traps and takes tension off the rhomboids.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between a straight back Seated Row and a regular Seated Row?

The straight back version keeps the torso rigidly upright with no forward lean during the eccentric. This reduces lat stretch but increases mid-back and rhomboid engagement. A regular Seated Row allows some torso movement.

### Is the Cable Straight Back Seated Row good for posture?

Yes, by training the rhomboids and mid-traps in a strict upright position, this exercise directly improves the muscles responsible for pulling your shoulders back and maintaining good posture.

### What muscles does the Cable Straight Back Seated Row work?

The Cable Straight Back Seated Row primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Cable Straight Back Seated Row good for beginners?

Yes, the Cable Straight Back Seated Row is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building upper back strength.
