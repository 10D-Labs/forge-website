# Drop Push Up

## Quick answer

A pushup that switches between two benches and the floor, with a hard push to get back up onto the benches. It works the chest, with help from the triceps and shoulders. Beginner chest exercise using body weight. Targets chest, also works triceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set two flat benches end to end, slightly more than shoulder-width apart. Get into a pushup, one hand on each bench, palms down.
2. Lower your chest between the benches, then push back up until your arms are straight.
3. Drop both hands off the benches to the floor in the gap and lower your chest toward the floor.
4. Push up hard so your hands leave the floor, land them back on the benches, and bend your elbows as you land.

## Pro tips

- Start in a high plank, lower your chest toward the ground, and drop your knees to the floor to complete the Pushup.
- Use this as a way to extend a set beyond failure on regular Pushups by switching to the easier knee position.
- Keep your core tight even when transitioning to the knees to maintain spinal alignment.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips sag when dropping the knees, which puts strain on the lower back.
- Dropping the knees too early before reaching a challenging point in the Pushup.
- Not maintaining a straight line from head to knees once the knees touch the ground.

## FAQ

### What is the purpose of a Drop Push Up?

It is a regression technique that lets you continue doing Pushups after muscular failure by dropping to your knees, which reduces the load. It is excellent for building endurance.

### Is this the same as a knee Pushup?

Not quite. A Drop Push Up starts in a full Pushup position and transitions to knees mid-set as a drop set technique. A knee Pushup starts and stays on the knees.

### When should you use Drop Push Ups?

At the end of a set when you cannot complete another full Pushup. Drop your knees and continue for 3-5 more reps to push past failure.

### What muscles does the Drop Push Up work?

The Drop Push Up primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Drop Push Up good for beginners?

Yes, the Drop Push Up is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building chest strength.
