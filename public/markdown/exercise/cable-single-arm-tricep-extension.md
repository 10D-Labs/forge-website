# Cable Single Arm Tricep Extension

## Quick answer

A one-arm triceps extension done overhead, with a low cable pulling from behind you. It works the triceps. Intermediate arms exercise using cable. Targets triceps, also works shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set a cable pulley at the bottom of the machine and attach a single handle. Stand with your back to the machine.
2. Hold the handle in one hand behind your head, palm facing forward, elbow bent and pointing up. Hold that upper arm steady with your other hand.
3. Keep your upper arm still and straighten your elbow to push the handle straight up overhead.
4. Bend your elbow slowly to lower the handle behind your head. Do all your reps with one arm, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Set the pulley low, face away from the machine and hold the handle behind your head with your elbow pointing up.
- Straighten your arm overhead and squeeze the triceps for a second at the top of each rep.
- Keep your elbow pointed up and close to your head; don't let it drift out to the side.

## Common mistakes

- Moving the whole arm instead of bending only at the elbow, which brings in the shoulder.
- Using too much weight and arching the lower back to push the handle up.
- Cutting the stretch short instead of letting your hand travel all the way behind your head.

## FAQ

### Why train one arm at a time?

Working one arm at a time stops the stronger arm from doing extra work and makes it easier to focus on each tricep.

### What attachment should I use for this exercise?

A single D-handle works best. Some people prefer to hold one end of a rope for a freer wrist position.

### Where should I feel overhead cable Tricep Extensions?

Along the back of your upper arm, especially near the armpit, where the overhead position stretches the long head of the triceps. If you feel it in your shoulder or lower back, lighten the weight and keep your elbow still.

### What muscles does the Cable Single Arm Tricep Extension work?

The Cable Single Arm Tricep Extension primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Cable Single Arm Tricep Extension good for beginners?

The Cable Single Arm Tricep Extension is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of triceps strength with easier variations first.
