# Dumbbell Incline Reverse Grip Press

## Quick answer

An incline dumbbell press with a reverse grip, palms facing toward your face. It works the upper chest, with help from the shoulders and triceps. Intermediate chest exercise using dumbbell. Targets chest, also works shoulders, triceps.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders, Triceps
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set an incline bench to 45 degrees. Sit back against it with your feet flat on the floor.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder level, elbows bent, palms facing toward your face (a reverse grip).
3. Press the dumbbells up and away from your body until your arms are straight.
4. Lower them slowly back to shoulder level.

## Pro tips

- Hold the dumbbells with your palms turned toward your face and keep that grip for the whole press.
- Keep your elbows tucked close to your sides as you lower the weights to your upper chest.
- Plant your feet and keep your shoulder blades pulled back against the bench for a stable base.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the elbows flare out wide, which feels awkward with a reverse grip and stresses the shoulders.
- Setting the bench too steep (above 45 degrees), which turns the press into more of a shoulder exercise.
- Starting with your usual incline press weight; the reverse grip is weaker, so begin lighter.

## FAQ

### What does the reverse grip change on an incline press?

Turning your palms toward your face keeps the elbows tucked, which puts more of the work on the upper chest and triceps and less on the front of the shoulders.

### Is the reverse grip incline press easier on the shoulders?

Many people find it more comfortable because the elbows stay close to the body. If it hurts, try a neutral grip or a lighter weight.

### What angle should I set the bench for the reverse grip incline press?

30-45 degrees is ideal. This targets the upper chest without turning it into a shoulder press.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Incline Reverse Grip Press work?

The Dumbbell Incline Reverse Grip Press primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the shoulders, triceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Dumbbell Incline Reverse Grip Press good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Incline Reverse Grip Press is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of chest strength with easier variations first.
