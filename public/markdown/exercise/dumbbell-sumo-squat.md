# Dumbbell Sumo Squat

## Quick answer

A wide-stance squat with your toes turned out, holding one dumbbell between your legs. It works the glutes, quads and inner thighs. Advanced legs exercise using dumbbell. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Plyometrics

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet wide and your toes turned out.
2. Hold one dumbbell upright by its top end, palms facing up under the top plate, arms hanging straight down.
3. Bend your knees and lower your hips until your thighs are about level with the floor, keeping your knees in line with your toes.
4. Push through your feet to stand back up.

## Pro tips

- Stand with your feet wider than shoulder width and your toes turned out, holding one dumbbell between your legs with both hands.
- Push your knees out over your toes as you squat down, keeping your chest up.
- Drive through your whole foot to stand up and squeeze your glutes at the top.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the knees cave inward, which strains the knees and takes work away from the glutes and inner thighs.
- Leaning forward and rounding the back to lower the dumbbell further.
- Setting the stance so wide that you can't reach a good depth.

## FAQ

### What muscles do Dumbbell Sumo Squats work?

The glutes, quads and inner thighs. The wide stance works the inner thighs more than a regular squat.

### How heavy should the dumbbell be?

Start with a weight you can hold for 10-15 controlled reps, then move to a heavier dumbbell or kettlebell as you get stronger.

### How deep should I squat?

As deep as you can while keeping your back flat and your heels down. For most people that is with the thighs about parallel to the floor.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Sumo Squat work?

The Dumbbell Sumo Squat primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a plyometrics exercise for the legs.

### Is the Dumbbell Sumo Squat good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Sumo Squat is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental legs movements first.
