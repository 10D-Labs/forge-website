# Hands Elevated Box Pushup

## Quick answer

A pushup with your hands up on a box and your feet on the floor, which makes it easier than a floor pushup. It works the chest, mainly the lower part, plus the triceps and shoulders. Beginner chest exercise using body weight. Targets chest, also works triceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Put your hands on the edge of a sturdy box, a bit wider than your shoulders. Walk your feet back until your body is straight.
2. Bend your elbows to lower your chest to the edge of the box, keeping your body in a straight line.
3. Push back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Place your hands on a box, slightly wider than your shoulders, with your feet on the floor.
- Keep your body in a straight line from heels to head; don't let your hips sag or pike up.
- Lower your chest to the edge of the box over about 2 seconds, then press back up.

## Common mistakes

- Using a box so low that your form breaks down.
- Placing the hands too wide, which strains the shoulders.
- Letting the lower back arch as you tire.

## FAQ

### Are Hands Elevated Box Pushups easier than regular Pushups?

Yes. With your hands raised, your arms push less of your body weight. The higher the box, the easier it gets.

### How high should the box be?

Start with a height where you can do 10-15 good reps, then use a lower box as you get stronger.

### What muscles do they work?

The chest, mainly the lower part, plus the triceps and shoulders.

### What muscles does the Hands Elevated Box Pushup work?

The Hands Elevated Box Pushup primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Hands Elevated Box Pushup good for beginners?

Yes, the Hands Elevated Box Pushup is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building chest strength.
