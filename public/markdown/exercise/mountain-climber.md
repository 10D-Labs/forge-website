# Mountain Climber

## Quick answer

A running motion done in a high plank, driving your knees toward your chest one at a time. It raises your heart rate and works the abs, shoulders and triceps. Beginner cardio exercise using body weight. Targets cardiovascular, also works core, shoulders, triceps.

- **Body Part:** Cardio
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Cardiovascular
- **Secondary Muscles:** Core, Shoulders, Triceps
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Cardio

---

## How to perform

1. Start in a high plank with your arms straight, hands directly under your shoulders and your body in a straight line.
2. Drive your right knee toward your chest.
3. Switch legs quickly, bringing your left knee toward your chest as your right leg goes back.
4. Keep alternating legs at a steady pace, like running, and keep your hips low.

## Pro tips

- Keep your hips at the same height as your shoulders; don't let them pike up or sag down.
- Drive each knee toward your chest explosively while keeping your hands planted directly under your shoulders.
- Land each foot softly under your hips rather than stomping; this reduces joint impact and maintains rhythm.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips rise into a pike position, which turns it into more of a hip flexor exercise than a full-body cardio movement.
- Bouncing the toes off the ground with short, choppy steps instead of fully driving the knees forward.
- Holding the breath instead of maintaining a steady breathing pattern.

## FAQ

### Are Mountain Climbers cardio or strength?

Primarily cardio. They elevate heart rate quickly while also engaging the core, shoulders, and hip flexors isometrically. They're one of the most efficient bodyweight conditioning exercises.

### How many Mountain Climbers should I do?

For cardio intervals, do 20-30 seconds at high intensity followed by 10-15 seconds rest. For reps, 20-30 per side is a solid target for most fitness levels.

### Do Mountain Climbers burn belly fat?

Mountain Climbers burn calories and can contribute to overall fat loss, but no exercise targets fat loss in a specific area. They do strengthen and tighten the core muscles underneath.

### What muscles does the Mountain Climber work?

The Mountain Climber primarily targets the cardiovascular, with secondary activation in the core, shoulders, triceps. It's classified as a cardio exercise for the cardio.

### Is the Mountain Climber good for beginners?

Yes, the Mountain Climber is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building cardiovascular strength.
