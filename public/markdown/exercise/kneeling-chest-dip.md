# Kneeling Chest Dip

## Quick answer

A dip on an assisted machine, kneeling on a pad that takes some of your weight. Leaning forward makes it work the lower chest. Beginner chest exercise using machine. Targets chest, also works triceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Adjust the machine to fit you, then kneel on the pad and hold the handles with your palms facing down and your arms straight.
2. Lean your upper body forward.
3. Bend your elbows to lower your body until your upper arms are level with the floor.
4. Push back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Lean your torso slightly forward (about 15-20 degrees) to shift emphasis onto the chest rather than the triceps.
- Lower until your upper arms are at least parallel to the floor to get a full stretch across the chest.
- Squeeze your chest hard at the top of the movement, but avoid locking out your elbows completely.

## Common mistakes

- Staying too upright, which turns it into a tricep-dominant exercise rather than a Chest Dip.
- Flaring the elbows excessively wide, which puts unnecessary stress on the shoulder joint.
- Using too much weight on the machine and shortening the range of motion to compensate.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between a Kneeling Chest Dip and a regular dip?

The kneeling version uses a machine with adjustable resistance, making it accessible to beginners who can't support their full bodyweight. The movement pattern and chest engagement are similar.

### How much weight should I use on the Kneeling Chest Dip machine?

Start with about 40-50% of your body weight and focus on full range of motion. Increase weight only when you can complete 12-15 controlled reps without shortening the range.

### Can Kneeling Chest Dips replace bench press?

They complement bench press but don't fully replace it. Dips emphasize the lower chest and involve more shoulder adduction, while bench press allows heavier loading and more balanced chest development.

### What muscles does the Kneeling Chest Dip work?

The Kneeling Chest Dip primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Kneeling Chest Dip good for beginners?

Yes, the Kneeling Chest Dip is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building chest strength.
