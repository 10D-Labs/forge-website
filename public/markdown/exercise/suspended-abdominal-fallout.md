# Suspended Abdominal Fallout

## Quick answer

A standing ab exercise with suspension straps or rings: lean your straight body forward as your arms reach overhead, then pull back up. It works the abs, plus the obliques and the front of the hips. Advanced core exercise using rings. Targets abs, also works obliques, hip flexors.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Rings
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Obliques, Hip Flexors
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Attach a suspension trainer or a pair of rings to a high anchor, with the handles at waist height.
2. Stand with the straps hanging just in front of you, holding the handles in front of your hips with straight arms.
3. Lean your whole body forward in a straight line, letting your arms rise overhead. Go as low as you can while keeping control.
4. Pull your body back up to standing, bringing your arms back down in front of you.

## Pro tips

- Start with the straps at mid-length and only lean forward as far as you can while keeping your lower back flat.
- Keep your arms straight and let the movement happen at the shoulders, not the elbows.
- Squeeze your abs hard to pull yourself back to the starting position rather than pulling with your arms.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the lower back arch as you fall forward, which compresses the lumbar spine under load.
- Bending the elbows during the fallout, which shortens the lever arm and makes the exercise easier but less effective.
- Using straps that are too long, which makes the exercise too difficult and compromises form.

## FAQ

### How is the suspended fallout different from an ab Wheel Rollout?

The suspension trainer adds instability because the handles can move in any direction, increasing core stabilizer demand. The ab wheel rolls on a fixed path. The suspended version is generally harder.

### What strap length should I use for suspended fallouts?

Start with the straps at about waist height. The lower the straps, the more horizontal your body becomes and the harder the exercise. Adjust the length to match your current core strength.

### Is this exercise safe for people with lower back problems?

Only if your core is strong enough to maintain a neutral spine throughout the movement. Start with a very limited range of motion and progress gradually. Stop immediately if you feel lower back strain.

### What muscles does the Suspended Abdominal Fallout work?

The Suspended Abdominal Fallout primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the obliques, hip flexors. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Suspended Abdominal Fallout good for beginners?

The Suspended Abdominal Fallout is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental core movements first.
