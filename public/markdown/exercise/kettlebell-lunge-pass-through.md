# Kettlebell Lunge Pass Through

## Quick answer

A forward lunge where you pass a kettlebell under your front thigh from one hand to the other, switching legs each rep. It works the glutes and quads and tests your balance. Intermediate legs exercise using kettlebell. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a kettlebell in one hand at your side.
2. Step forward into a lunge, lowering your back knee toward the floor.
3. Pass the kettlebell under your front thigh to your other hand.
4. Push off your front foot to stand back up, holding the bell in the new hand.
5. Lunge with the other leg and pass the bell back under that thigh. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Pass the kettlebell under your front thigh at the bottom of the lunge, switching hands smoothly without pausing.
- Keep your torso upright throughout the pass. The transfer happens under the leg, not in front of your body.
- Step with control and ensure your front knee stays behind or over your toes, not past them.

## Common mistakes

- Leaning forward excessively during the pass, which puts stress on the lower back and destabilizes the lunge.
- Taking too short a step, which causes the knee to push past the toes and increases joint stress.
- Rushing the hand-off and fumbling the kettlebell during the pass-through.

## FAQ

### What does the pass-through add to a regular lunge?

The pass-through adds a coordination and core stability challenge. Transferring weight under the thigh forces the core to stabilize against rotation while you're in an already unstable lunge position.

### What weight should I start with?

Start light (8-12 kg) to master the coordination of lunging and passing simultaneously. Increase weight once the pass-through feels automatic.

### What muscles does the Kettlebell Lunge Pass Through work?

The Kettlebell Lunge Pass Through primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Kettlebell Lunge Pass Through good for beginners?

The Kettlebell Lunge Pass Through is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of glutes strength with easier variations first.
