# Smith Wide Grip Decline Bench Press

## Quick answer

A decline bench press on a Smith machine with your hands set wide. It works the lower chest, with help from the triceps and shoulders. Intermediate chest exercise using smith machine. Targets chest, also works triceps, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Chest
- **Equipment:** Smith Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Chest
- **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set a decline bench to the angle you want under a Smith machine bar.
2. Lie on the bench with your feet hooked under the foot pads.
3. Grip the bar wide, hands wider than your shoulders, and unhook it from the rack.
4. Lower the bar slowly until it touches your chest, letting your elbows point out to the sides.
5. Press the bar back up until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Secure your legs firmly under the foot pads before unracking to prevent sliding on the decline.
- Lower the bar to your lower chest or upper abdomen line to match the decline angle and maximize lower pec recruitment.
- Keep your wrists stacked directly over your elbows throughout the movement to avoid wrist strain.

## Common mistakes

- Lowering the bar to the upper chest, which does not match the decline pressing angle and reduces effectiveness.
- Setting the decline too steep (beyond 30 degrees), causing blood to rush to the head and increasing discomfort.
- Failing to re-rack the bar properly by not rotating the hooks fully, creating a dangerous false rack.

## FAQ

### Why use the Smith machine for decline bench instead of free weights?

The fixed path is especially valuable on decline where balancing a barbell is more awkward. It also allows you to safely push to failure alone.

### What decline angle should I set the bench to?

A 15-20 degree decline is optimal. Steeper angles do not add lower chest activation and make the exercise less comfortable.

### Is decline bench press necessary if I already do flat and incline?

Not strictly necessary, but it helps develop the lower chest shelf that gives the pecs a fuller, more complete appearance.

### What muscles does the Smith Wide Grip Decline Bench Press work?

The Smith Wide Grip Decline Bench Press primarily targets the chest, with secondary activation in the triceps, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the chest.

### Is the Smith Wide Grip Decline Bench Press good for beginners?

The Smith Wide Grip Decline Bench Press is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of chest strength with easier variations first.
