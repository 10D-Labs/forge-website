# Smith Sumo Squat

## Quick answer

A wide-stance squat in a Smith machine, with your toes pointing out and the bar across your upper back. It works the glutes, with help from the quads and hamstrings. Beginner legs exercise using smith machine. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Smith Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Step under the Smith machine bar and rest it across your upper back and shoulders.
2. Set your feet wider than shoulder-width apart, toes pointing out.
3. Keep your chest up, push your hips back and bend your knees to lower into a squat.
4. Lower until your thighs are level with the floor, or as low as you comfortably can.
5. Push through your heels to stand back up.

## Pro tips

- Position your feet wider than shoulder width with toes pointed out at about 45 degrees under the Smith machine bar.
- Descend by pushing your knees outward in line with your toes while keeping your torso upright.
- Drive through your heels and squeeze your glutes at the top of each rep.

## Common mistakes

- Standing too far forward or back under the bar, which creates an unnatural squatting path on the fixed Smith bar.
- Letting the knees cave inward during the descent, which strains the knee ligaments and reduces inner thigh activation.
- Not going deep enough. In a sumo squat, aim for thighs parallel to the floor or deeper for maximal glute and adductor activation.

## FAQ

### Is the Smith machine sumo squat effective for glutes?

Yes, the wide stance heavily targets the glutes and inner thighs. The Smith machine provides stability that allows you to focus on depth and mind-muscle connection.

### Is the Smith machine bad for squatting?

The fixed bar path does remove some stabilizer muscle activation. However, for targeted muscle isolation and for lifters with balance limitations, the Smith machine is a valid and effective tool.

### How wide should my stance be for sumo squats?

About 1.5 to 2 times shoulder width. Your toes should point outward at 30-45 degrees, and your knees should track directly over your toes.

### What muscles does the Smith Sumo Squat work?

The Smith Sumo Squat primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Smith Sumo Squat good for beginners?

Yes, the Smith Sumo Squat is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building glutes strength.
