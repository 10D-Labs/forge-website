# Dumbbell Incline Tricep Extension

## Quick answer

An overhead tricep extension done sitting back on an incline bench, holding one dumbbell with both hands. It works the triceps. Beginner arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets triceps, also works shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Triceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set a bench to a steep incline and sit with your back against it, feet flat on the floor.
2. Hold one dumbbell upright with both palms cupped under its top end, and press it straight up above your head.
3. Keep your elbows pointing up, close to your head, and bend them to lower the dumbbell behind your head.
4. Straighten your arms to raise it back above your head.

## Pro tips

- Lie back on an incline bench and extend the dumbbells overhead with palms facing each other.
- Lower the dumbbells by bending at the elbows, bringing them beside your head while keeping your upper arms stationary.
- The incline position adds a stretch to the long head of the triceps that a flat bench doesn't provide.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the elbows flare out wide, which reduces the stretch on the tricep long head.
- Lowering the dumbbells too far behind the head, which puts excessive stress on the shoulder joint.
- Moving the upper arms during the extension instead of keeping them fixed perpendicular to the floor.

## FAQ

### Why do Tricep Extensions on an incline?

The incline angle increases the stretch on the long head of the triceps at the bottom of the movement, which can lead to greater muscle activation and growth.

### What incline angle is best?

A 30-45 degree angle provides the best balance of stretch and comfort. Steeper angles increase shoulder involvement.

### Are incline Tricep Extensions better than flat?

They provide a stronger stretch at the bottom, which may enhance long-head hypertrophy. Flat extensions are better for heavier loading. Use both in your program for complete development.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Incline Tricep Extension work?

The Dumbbell Incline Tricep Extension primarily targets the triceps, with secondary activation in the shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Incline Tricep Extension good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Incline Tricep Extension is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building triceps strength.
