# Barbell Curl

## Quick answer

A standing curl, lifting a barbell from your thighs to your shoulders with your palms facing forward. It works the biceps. Beginner arms exercise using barbell. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand tall with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a barbell in front of your thighs with your palms facing forward.
2. Curl the bar up to shoulder level, keeping your elbows close to your sides.
3. Lower the bar slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Keep your elbows pinned to your sides throughout the entire movement; they should not drift forward as you curl up.
- Lower the bar under control for 2-3 seconds to maximize the eccentric (negative) portion, which is a major driver of bicep growth.
- Squeeze your biceps hard at the top for a one-second hold before lowering.

## Common mistakes

- Swinging the hips and using body momentum to heave the bar up, which steals work from the biceps.
- Letting the elbows drift forward as you curl, which shortens the range of motion and reduces peak contraction.
- Loading too heavy and only performing partial reps in the middle range instead of a full stretch and full contraction.

## FAQ

### Should I use a straight bar or EZ curl bar for Barbell Curls?

An EZ curl bar is easier on the wrists and elbows for most people. A straight bar keeps the biceps in full supination, which can produce a slightly stronger contraction. Use whichever feels more comfortable.

### How much weight should I curl?

Most intermediate lifters curl 60-80 lbs for sets of 8-12. Start lighter and focus on strict form. If you need to swing or lean back, the weight is too heavy.

### How many sets of Barbell Curls should I do per week?

10-20 total sets of bicep work per week is optimal for most people. 3-4 sets of Barbell Curls 2-3 times per week, combined with other bicep exercises, covers this range well.

### What muscles does the Barbell Curl work?

The Barbell Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Barbell Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Barbell Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building biceps strength.
