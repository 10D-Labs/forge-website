# Decline Twisting Situp

## Quick answer

A situp on a decline bench, head lower than your feet, with a twist at the top. It works the abs and the obliques along your sides. Intermediate core exercise using body weight. Targets abs, also works obliques.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Obliques
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie back on a decline bench, head lower than your hips, and hook your feet under the foot pads at the high end.
2. Place your hands behind your head, elbows out to the sides.
3. Sit up, and as you rise, twist your upper body to bring one elbow toward the opposite knee.
4. Lower slowly until your back is on the bench. Do all your reps twisting to one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Hook your feet under the pads of a decline bench and start with a gentle angle. Steeper angles make it much harder.
- Start the twist from your ribcage, not your shoulders, so the obliques drive the rotation.
- Turn toward one knee at the top, then alternate sides each rep.

## Common mistakes

- Using momentum to swing up rather than curling up slowly with your abs.
- Twisting only at the shoulders while the torso stays straight, which leaves the obliques out.
- Pulling on the neck with the hands, straining the neck.

## FAQ

### Are Decline Twisting Situps bad for the spine?

They bend and twist the spine under load, which can bother the lower back. Use a moderate angle, move with control, and skip them if you have disc problems. Cable woodchops train rotation with less spinal bending.

### How do I progress this exercise?

Increase the decline angle, slow down the tempo, hold a weight plate against your chest, or add reps. Keep the form strict as it gets harder.

### What muscles does the Decline Twisting Situp work?

The Decline Twisting Situp primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the obliques. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Decline Twisting Situp good for beginners?

The Decline Twisting Situp is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of abs strength with easier variations first.
