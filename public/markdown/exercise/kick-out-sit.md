# Kick Out Sit

## Quick answer

A wall sit where you kick one leg straight out at a time while holding the low position. It works the quads, with help from the glutes. Beginner legs exercise using body weight. Targets quads, also works glutes.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Quads
- **Secondary Muscles:** Glutes
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lean your back against a wall and slide down until your thighs are level with the floor, feet hip-width apart.
2. Hold your arms straight out to your sides at shoulder height.
3. Straighten one knee to kick that leg out in front of you, level with your hip.
4. Lower the foot back to the floor, then kick out the other leg. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Lean back slightly and use your hamstrings to extend your legs straight out, holding the position for 1-2 seconds.
- Keep your toes pointed forward and legs parallel to the ground at full extension for maximum hamstring engagement.
- Control the return phase by slowly bending the knees rather than letting your feet drop.

## Common mistakes

- Using momentum to kick the legs out rather than engaging the hamstrings with a controlled extension.
- Leaning too far back, which reduces hamstring engagement and shifts the work to the hip flexors.
- Not fully extending the legs at the top, cutting the range of motion short.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the Kick Out Sit target?

Primarily the hamstrings through knee extension resistance, with secondary work from the quadriceps and hip flexors to hold the legs in the extended position.

### Can I do this at home without a bench?

Yes. Sit on the edge of a sturdy chair with your hands gripping the sides for support. The movement is the same as on a bench.

### What muscles does the Kick Out Sit work?

The Kick Out Sit primarily targets the quads, with secondary activation in the glutes. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Kick Out Sit good for beginners?

Yes, the Kick Out Sit is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building quads strength.
