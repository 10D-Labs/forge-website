# Narrow Reverse Grip Inverted Row

## Quick answer

A bodyweight row hanging under a low bar, pulling your chest up with a narrow grip and your palms facing you. It works the upper back, with the biceps and forearms helping. Advanced back exercise using body weight. Targets upper back, also works forearms, shoulders.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms, Shoulders
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set a Smith machine bar at about hip height and lie on your back underneath it.
2. Grip the bar with your hands a little closer than shoulder-width, palms facing toward your face.
3. Straighten your legs with your heels on the floor and hang with your arms straight, body in a line from heels to shoulders.
4. Pull your chest up to the bar, keeping your body straight.
5. Lower slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Set the bar at about waist height and hang under it with your heels on the floor and your body straight from head to heels.
- Grip the bar with your palms facing you and your hands closer than shoulder width, then pull your chest up to the bar.
- Squeeze your shoulder blades together at the top and lower yourself with control for 2-3 seconds.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips sag on the way up, which shortens the pull and strains the lower back.
- Reaching for the bar with your chin instead of bringing your chest to it.
- Stopping short at the bottom instead of straightening your arms fully on each rep.

## FAQ

### What muscles do Narrow Reverse Grip Inverted Rows work?

Mainly the upper back, including the rhomboids and middle traps. The palms-up grip brings in more biceps and forearms than an overhand row.

### How do I make inverted rows easier or harder?

Bend your knees and keep your feet flat, or raise the bar so your body is more upright, to make them easier. Straighten your legs, lower the bar or put your feet on a bench to make them harder.

### Are inverted rows good for building up to Chinups?

Yes. They train the same pulling muscles with less of your body weight, so they are a common step toward your first Chinup or Pullup.

### What muscles does the Narrow Reverse Grip Inverted Row work?

The Narrow Reverse Grip Inverted Row primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the forearms, shoulders. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Narrow Reverse Grip Inverted Row good for beginners?

The Narrow Reverse Grip Inverted Row is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental back movements first.
