# Twisted Leg Raise

## Quick answer

A straight-leg raise on a captain's chair that brings your legs up toward one side. It works the abs, with the twist adding the obliques. Intermediate core exercise using body weight. Targets abs, also works obliques, hip flexors.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Obliques, Hip Flexors
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Step into a captain's chair, the upright station with forearm pads. Rest your forearms on the pads and grip the handles, legs hanging straight.
2. Keep your legs straight and together, turn your hips to one side, and raise your legs up and out toward that side.
3. Stop when your legs are level with your hips.
4. Lower slowly until your legs hang straight. Do all your reps to one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Keep your lower back pressed into the floor by engaging your deep core muscles before lifting your legs.
- Twist from the hips, not the knees, to ensure the obliques are doing the rotational work.
- Lower your legs slowly on each side; the controlled eccentric is where the obliques work hardest.

## Common mistakes

- Arching the lower back off the floor as the legs lower, which puts dangerous stress on the lumbar spine.
- Using momentum to swing the legs from side to side instead of controlling the rotation with the obliques.
- Bending the knees during the raise, which shortens the lever arm and reduces the difficulty and ab engagement.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the Twisted Leg Raise target?

It primarily targets the obliques through the twisting motion, with heavy involvement of the rectus abdominis and hip flexors for the leg raise component.

### Are Twisted Leg Raises safe for the lower back?

They are safe as long as your lower back stays in contact with the floor throughout. If your back arches, reduce the range of motion or bend your knees slightly.

### How do I make Twisted Leg Raises easier?

Bend your knees to shorten the lever arm, or reduce how far you twist to each side. You can also place your hands under your glutes for additional lower back support.

### What muscles does the Twisted Leg Raise work?

The Twisted Leg Raise primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the obliques, hip flexors. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Twisted Leg Raise good for beginners?

The Twisted Leg Raise is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of abs strength with easier variations first.
