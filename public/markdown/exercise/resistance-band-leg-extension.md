# Resistance Band Leg Extension

## Quick answer

A seated leg extension with a loop band around both ankles, straightening one knee at a time against the band. It works the quads on the front of the thigh. Beginner legs exercise using resistance band. Targets quads, also works hamstrings.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Quads
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Loop a band around both ankles. Sit on the edge of a flat bench, feet flat on the floor, hands on the bench beside your hips.
2. Keep one foot on the floor and straighten the other knee, lifting that foot forward against the band until your leg is straight.
3. Bend your knee slowly to lower the foot back to the floor. Do all your reps on one leg, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Anchor the band low and behind you so there is tension through the entire range of the extension.
- Extend your leg fully and squeeze the quad hard at the top for 1-2 seconds.
- Control the return and do not let the band pull your leg back too quickly.

## Common mistakes

- Not anchoring the band at the correct height, which changes the resistance curve and reduces quad activation.
- Leaning the torso to compensate for the resistance instead of keeping the upper body stable.
- Using a band that is too heavy, which limits the range of motion and prevents full knee extension.

## FAQ

### Can Resistance Band Leg Extensions replace the Leg Extension machine?

They are a good alternative, especially for home workouts. The resistance curve is different from a machine (more tension at full extension), but the quad activation is effective.

### Are band Leg Extensions good for knee rehab?

Yes, they provide gentle, progressive resistance that strengthens the quadriceps and supports knee stability. Start with a very light band and focus on pain-free range of motion.

### How many reps should I do for band Leg Extensions?

Aim for 15-20 reps per leg since bands provide less overall resistance than machines. Focus on controlled contractions and full range of motion.

### What muscles does the Resistance Band Leg Extension work?

The Resistance Band Leg Extension primarily targets the quads, with secondary activation in the hamstrings. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Resistance Band Leg Extension good for beginners?

Yes, the Resistance Band Leg Extension is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building quads strength.
