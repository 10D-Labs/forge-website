# Band Assisted Pullup

## Quick answer

A pullup with a resistance band under your knees to take off some of your weight, a way to build up to regular pullups. It works the lats, with help from the biceps and forearms. Beginner back exercise using resistance band. Targets lats, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Attach a resistance band to a pullup bar so it hangs down in a loop.
2. Grab the bar with your palms facing away from you, hands slightly wider than your shoulders, and rest your knees in the loop.
3. Hang with your arms straight and your shoulders pulled down and back.
4. Drive your elbows down toward your hips to pull yourself up until your chin is above the bar.
5. Lower yourself slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Place your knee or foot in the band loop and let it assist you most at the bottom where Pullups are hardest.
- Use the same strict form as an unassisted Pullup: full dead hang at the bottom, chin over bar at the top.
- Progress to thinner bands over time rather than jumping straight to unassisted Pullups.

## Common mistakes

- Relying on the band to do most of the work instead of actively pulling with your back muscles.
- Not achieving full range of motion at the bottom by keeping the arms slightly bent throughout.
- Using a band that is too thick, which makes the exercise too easy and delays progression to unassisted Pullups.

## FAQ

### How do you progress from band-assisted to unassisted Pullups?

Use progressively thinner bands over weeks. Once you can do 3 sets of 8 with the thinnest band, try unassisted Pullups with slow negatives to bridge the gap.

### Where should you place the band for Assisted Pullups?

Under one knee is most common and stable. Under one or both feet provides slightly less assistance. Choose the method that feels most secure.

### What band thickness should a beginner start with?

Start with a heavy band (usually 1.75-2.5 inches wide) and progressively work down to thinner bands as you build strength.

### What muscles does the Band Assisted Pullup work?

The Band Assisted Pullup primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Band Assisted Pullup good for beginners?

Yes, the Band Assisted Pullup is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building lats strength.
