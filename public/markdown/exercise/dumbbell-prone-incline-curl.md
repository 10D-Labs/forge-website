# Dumbbell Prone Incline Curl

## Quick answer

A two-arm curl lying chest-down on an incline bench with your arms hanging straight down. The bench stops you from swinging, so the biceps do the work. Intermediate arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Set a bench to a 45-degree incline and lie face down on it, chest and stomach against the pad.
2. Let your arms hang straight down with a dumbbell in each hand, palms facing forward.
3. Keeping your upper arms still, curl the dumbbells up toward your shoulders.
4. Lower slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Lie face down on the incline bench with your arms hanging straight down, which eliminates any ability to use momentum or body swing.
- Curl the dumbbells up while keeping your upper arms perfectly vertical, squeezing the biceps at the top.
- Use a 3-second lowering phase to fully exploit the stretched position that this prone incline angle creates.

## Common mistakes

- Lifting the chest off the bench to swing the weight up, which defeats the strict isolation purpose of the prone position.
- Not lowering the dumbbells all the way to full arm extension, which shortens the most beneficial part of the range of motion.
- Using the same weight as standing curls; you will need to go lighter due to the strict positioning.

## FAQ

### What is the benefit of prone incline curls vs standing curls?

The face-down incline position completely eliminates body swing and shoulder involvement, providing the strictest possible bicep isolation.

### What angle should the incline bench be for prone curls?

45 degrees is standard. A steeper angle reduces the stretch, while a lower angle increases it.

### Should I use a supinated or hammer grip for prone incline curls?

Supinated (palms up) targets the biceps peak more. Hammer grip shifts work to the brachialis. Both are effective depending on your goals.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Prone Incline Curl work?

The Dumbbell Prone Incline Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Prone Incline Curl good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Prone Incline Curl is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of biceps strength with easier variations first.
