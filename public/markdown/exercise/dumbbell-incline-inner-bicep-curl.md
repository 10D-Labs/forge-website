# Dumbbell Incline Inner Bicep Curl

## Quick answer

A curl done lying back on an incline bench with your arms angled out to the sides, away from your body. It works the biceps, with help from the forearms. Intermediate arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit back on an incline bench with a dumbbell in each hand. Let your arms hang down and angled out to the sides, palms facing forward.
2. Keep your upper arms still and angled out, away from your body, the whole time.
3. Curl the dumbbells up toward your shoulders.
4. Lower them slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Keep your upper arms pinned against the incline bench throughout the curl to isolate the biceps and prevent front delt involvement.
- Use a neutral (hammer) grip at the bottom and supinate as you curl to maximize inner bicep (short head) activation.
- Control the eccentric for at least 2 seconds on each rep to increase time under tension on the incline stretch position.

## Common mistakes

- Lifting the elbows off the bench pad during the curl, which shifts the load from the biceps to the front delts.
- Using momentum by swinging the torso forward on the bench instead of curling with a strict, controlled motion.
- Choosing too heavy a weight, causing incomplete range of motion and losing the deep stretch at the bottom.

## FAQ

### What angle should the bench be set at for incline inner Bicep Curls?

Set the bench to 45-60 degrees. A steeper angle reduces the stretch, while a lower angle increases it but may cause shoulder discomfort.

### How are incline inner Bicep Curls different from regular incline curls?

The palms-facing-each-other (neutral) start position targets the inner (short) head of the biceps more than standard supinated incline curls.

### How heavy should I go on incline inner Bicep Curls?

Use 50-65% of your standard standing curl weight. The stretched position makes this exercise harder, so lighter loads with strict form are more effective.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Incline Inner Bicep Curl work?

The Dumbbell Incline Inner Bicep Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Incline Inner Bicep Curl good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Incline Inner Bicep Curl is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of biceps strength with easier variations first.
