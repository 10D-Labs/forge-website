# Pelvic Tilt Into Bridge

## Quick answer

A bridge that starts by tilting your pelvis, then lifts your hips off the floor while you lie on your back. It works the glutes, plus the hamstrings and abs. Beginner legs exercise using body weight. Targets glutes, also works hamstrings, core.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Core
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your back with your knees bent, feet flat on the floor and arms at your sides, palms facing down.
2. Tighten your glutes and abs, then tilt your pelvis up and lift your hips off the floor into a bridge.
3. Hold for a few seconds, then lower your hips slowly back to the floor.

## Pro tips

- Start with a posterior Pelvic Tilt (flatten your lower back to the floor) before driving your hips up into the bridge.
- Squeeze your glutes at the top and hold for 2-3 seconds before lowering slowly back down.
- Keep your feet about hip-width apart with heels roughly 12 inches from your glutes for optimal glute activation.

## Common mistakes

- Skipping the Pelvic Tilt at the start and just thrusting the hips up, which allows the lower back to hyperextend.
- Pushing through the toes instead of the heels, shifting the work to the quads and calves.
- Letting the hips drop quickly on the way down instead of controlling the descent.

## FAQ

### What is the benefit of adding a Pelvic Tilt before the bridge?

The Pelvic Tilt engages the deep core muscles first, which protects the lower back and ensures the glutes do the work during the bridge rather than the spinal erectors.

### Is this better than a regular glute bridge?

It is a better teaching progression. The Pelvic Tilt cue ensures proper core engagement, making it ideal for beginners or anyone who feels bridges in their lower back instead of their glutes.

### How many reps should I do?

Two to three sets of 12-15 reps is standard. Hold each rep at the top for 2-3 seconds to increase glute time under tension.

### What muscles does the Pelvic Tilt Into Bridge work?

The Pelvic Tilt Into Bridge primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, core. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Pelvic Tilt Into Bridge good for beginners?

Yes, the Pelvic Tilt Into Bridge is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building glutes strength.
