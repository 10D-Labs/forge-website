# Cable Reverse Wrist Curl

## Quick answer

A seated wrist exercise with a low cable, lifting the backs of your hands up with your palms facing down. It works the forearms. Beginner arms exercise using cable. Targets forearms, also works forearms, wrists.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Forearms
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms, Wrists
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Attach a bar to a low pulley and sit on a bench facing the machine.
2. Grip the bar with your palms facing down. Rest your forearms on your thighs with your wrists hanging past your knees.
3. Keep your forearms still and bend your wrists up as far as you can.
4. Lower your hands slowly back down.

## Pro tips

- Rest your forearms on a bench with only your wrists and hands hanging off the edge. This isolates the wrist extensors completely.
- Use very light weight and high reps (15-25) since the wrist extensors are small muscles that respond to volume and endurance training.
- Curl your wrists upward as high as possible and hold the top position for a one-second squeeze.

## Common mistakes

- Using too much weight, which limits the range of motion and can strain the small tendons around the wrist.
- Moving the forearms during the curl instead of keeping them pressed flat against the bench.
- Performing the reps too quickly without a pause at the top, which reduces time under tension for the wrist extensors.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between wrist curls and reverse wrist curls?

Regular wrist curls (palms up) work the wrist flexors on the inner forearm. Reverse wrist curls (palms down) work the wrist extensors on the outer forearm. Both are needed for balanced forearm development.

### How often should I do reverse wrist curls?

2-3 times per week at the end of your workout. The forearm extensors recover quickly and benefit from frequent training. Use 3 sets of 15-25 reps with light weight.

### What muscles does the Cable Reverse Wrist Curl work?

The Cable Reverse Wrist Curl primarily targets the forearms, with secondary activation in the forearms, wrists. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Cable Reverse Wrist Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Cable Reverse Wrist Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building forearms strength.
