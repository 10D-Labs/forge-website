# Seated Leg Press

## Quick answer

A leg press done sitting upright, pushing against a footplate so the seat slides back. It works the quads, with help from the glutes and hamstrings. Beginner legs exercise using machine. Targets quads, also works hamstrings, glutes.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Machine
- **Target Muscle:** Quads
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Glutes
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit in the leg press with your back against the pad and your feet flat on the footplate, hip-width apart, knees bent toward your chest.
2. Hold the handles at your sides.
3. Push through your feet to straighten your legs, sliding the seat back, and stop just before your knees lock.
4. Bend your knees slowly to let the seat slide forward to the start.

## Pro tips

- Sit upright with your back against the pad and your feet flat on the footplate, about shoulder width apart.
- Push through your whole foot until your legs are nearly straight, without locking the knees.
- Let the seat slide back under control until your knees are bent to about 90 degrees.

## Common mistakes

- Locking the knees hard at the top.
- Letting the knees cave inward as you push.
- Lifting the hips off the seat to push more weight.

## FAQ

### What muscles does the Seated Leg Press work?

Mainly the quads, with help from the glutes and hamstrings.

### How is it different from a 45-degree leg press?

On the seated version you sit upright and the seat moves instead of the platform. It usually takes less weight and feels easier on the lower back.

### Where should I place my feet?

Lower on the plate works the quads more; higher brings in more glutes and hamstrings. Start in the middle with your feet about shoulder width.

### What muscles does the Seated Leg Press work?

The Seated Leg Press primarily targets the quads, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, glutes. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Seated Leg Press good for beginners?

Yes, the Seated Leg Press is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building quads strength.
