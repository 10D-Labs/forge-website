# Kettlebell Turkish Get Up

## Quick answer

A Turkish get-up: rise from lying on your back to kneeling, then to standing, with a kettlebell held straight overhead the whole way. It works the glutes, legs, abs and shoulders. Advanced legs exercise using kettlebell. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, core.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Kettlebell
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Core
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your back, left leg straight, right knee bent and right foot flat. Hold a kettlebell straight up above your right shoulder.
2. Roll onto your left side and push up onto your left hand, keeping the kettlebell straight up the whole time.
3. Press through your right foot and left hand to lift your hips off the floor.
4. Sweep your left leg under you and set that knee down, then lift your hand so you kneel upright, bell still overhead.
5. Stand up from the kneel, then reverse the steps back down to the floor. Switch the kettlebell to your left hand to do the other side.

## Pro tips

- Keep your eyes on the kettlebell for the first half of the movement, from the floor to kneeling, to keep the shoulder steady.
- Keep the arm holding the bell straight and locked for the whole get-up.
- Move through each position deliberately. The Turkish get-up is a skill exercise, not a speed exercise.

## Common mistakes

- Rushing through the transitions instead of pausing at each position.
- Bending the overhead arm, which makes the kettlebell unstable and puts the shoulder at risk.
- Skipping the roll to the elbow and the push up to the hand, which are the base of the movement.

## FAQ

### What are the steps of a Turkish get-up?

From lying on your back with the bell overhead: roll to your elbow, push up to your hand, lift your hips, sweep your leg under you to a kneeling position, then stand up. Reverse the steps to lie back down.

### How heavy should I go on Turkish get-ups?

Start with no weight to learn the steps, then use a shoe balanced on your fist, then a light kettlebell (8-12 kg). Most people work up to 16-24 kg over months of practice.

### What muscles does the Kettlebell Turkish Get Up work?

The Kettlebell Turkish Get Up primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, core. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Kettlebell Turkish Get Up good for beginners?

The Kettlebell Turkish Get Up is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental legs movements first.
