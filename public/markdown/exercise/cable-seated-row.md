# Cable Seated Row

## Quick answer

A seated cable row, pulling a bar from a forward lean in to your stomach. It works the upper back, with help from the biceps and forearms. Beginner back exercise using cable. Targets upper back, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Cable
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit at the cable row machine with your feet flat on the footrests and your knees slightly bent.
2. Grip the bar with your palms facing down. Lean forward with your arms straight out, back flat and shoulders relaxed.
3. Sit up tall as you pull the bar to your stomach, squeezing your shoulder blades together.
4. Let the bar go back slowly, leaning forward again until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Pull the handle to your lower sternum while driving your elbows past your torso to fully contract the back muscles.
- Sit tall with a slight forward lean at the start, then pull your torso to upright as you row to engage the full back.
- Squeeze your shoulder blades together at the peak contraction for a one-second hold.

## Common mistakes

- Leaning too far back at the top of the row, turning it into a lower back exercise instead of a back row.
- Pulling primarily with the biceps by not initiating the movement with the shoulder blades.
- Using momentum by rocking the torso back and forth instead of controlling each rep.

## FAQ

### What grip is best for Cable Seated Rows?

A close neutral grip (V-bar) targets the mid-back and lats. A wide overhand grip shifts emphasis to the rear delts and rhomboids. Vary your grips for complete development.

### Should you lean forward during cable rows?

A slight forward lean at the start of each rep allows a greater stretch on the lats, but avoid excessive rocking. Control the movement and return to upright as you pull.

### How heavy should you go on Cable Seated Rows?

Use a weight that allows 8-15 controlled reps with full scapular retraction at the top. If you cannot squeeze your shoulder blades together, reduce the weight.

### What muscles does the Cable Seated Row work?

The Cable Seated Row primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Cable Seated Row good for beginners?

Yes, the Cable Seated Row is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building upper back strength.
