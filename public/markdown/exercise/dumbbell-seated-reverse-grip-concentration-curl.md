# Dumbbell Seated Reverse Grip Concentration Curl

## Quick answer

A seated concentration curl done with your palm turned down instead of up. It works the biceps and the top of the forearm. Beginner arms exercise using dumbbell. Targets biceps, also works forearms.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Biceps
- **Secondary Muscles:** Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Sit on the end of a flat bench with your feet wide. Lean forward and rest your free hand on your knee.
2. Hold a dumbbell in your other hand, the back of that arm against your inner thigh, arm straight, palm facing toward you.
3. Keep your upper arm against your thigh and curl the dumbbell up toward your shoulder, the back of your hand leading.
4. Lower it slowly until your arm is straight. Do all your reps with one arm, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Brace your elbow against your inner thigh and curl the dumbbell toward your shoulder with your palm facing up.
- Turn your head to watch the working arm so you can focus on the mind-muscle connection with the bicep.
- Lower the dumbbell slowly, resisting gravity, to maximize the eccentric loading on the bicep.

## Common mistakes

- Moving the elbow off the thigh during the curl, which allows the shoulder to assist and reduces isolation.
- Swinging the torso to generate momentum on the curl rather than using pure bicep contraction.
- Not fully extending the arm at the bottom, cutting the range of motion short.

## FAQ

### What is the benefit of concentration curls over regular curls?

The elbow bracing against the thigh completely eliminates momentum, making it one of the strictest bicep exercises available.

### Do concentration curls build the bicep peak?

Research suggests they produce high bicep activation, particularly in the short head, which contributes to the peak when flexed.

### Should I do concentration curls heavy or light?

Moderate weight with strict form. If you need to swing the weight or move your elbow off the thigh, go lighter. 8-12 reps per arm is a good range.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Seated Reverse Grip Concentration Curl work?

The Dumbbell Seated Reverse Grip Concentration Curl primarily targets the biceps, with secondary activation in the forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the arms.

### Is the Dumbbell Seated Reverse Grip Concentration Curl good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Seated Reverse Grip Concentration Curl is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building biceps strength.
