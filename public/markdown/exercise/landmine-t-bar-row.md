# Landmine T-Bar Row

## Quick answer

A bent-over row with one end of a barbell fixed to the floor, pulling the loaded end up to your chest. It works the upper back, with help from the biceps and forearms. Intermediate back exercise using barbell. Targets upper back, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Upper Back
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Load one end of a barbell with plates and wedge the other end in a landmine holder or a corner.
2. Straddle the bar, facing the plates. Bend your knees and lean forward from your hips, back flat.
3. Grip the bar just behind the plates with both hands, one in front of the other, palms facing each other.
4. Pull the bar up until the plates nearly touch your chest.
5. Lower slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Straddle the bar, hinge forward until your torso is about 45 degrees from the floor, and keep your back flat with your chest up.
- Pull the loaded end of the bar to your chest, driving your elbows back and squeezing your shoulder blades together.
- Use a V-handle under the bar for a close, neutral grip if your gym has one.

## Common mistakes

- Standing too upright, which turns the row into a shrug and takes work off the upper back.
- Jerking the torso up to heave the weight instead of pulling with the back.
- Rounding the lower back under heavy loads.

## FAQ

### What grip should I use for Landmine T-Bar Rows?

A V-handle gives a close, palms-in grip that most people find comfortable. Holding the bar sleeve with both hands also works.

### How do Landmine T-Bar Rows compare to barbell rows?

The fixed end of the bar guides the path, so it is easier to stay balanced and many people can go heavier. Barbell rows demand more from your lower back and core.

### How do Landmine T-Bar Rows compare to seated cable rows?

T-bar rows demand more core and lower back stability. Cable rows are easier on the lower back and make it easier to focus on the squeeze.

### What muscles does the Landmine T-Bar Row work?

The Landmine T-Bar Row primarily targets the upper back, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Landmine T-Bar Row good for beginners?

The Landmine T-Bar Row is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of upper back strength with easier variations first.
