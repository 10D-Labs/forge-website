# L-pullup

## Quick answer

A pullup done while holding your legs straight out in front of you. It works the lats and biceps, and your abs work hard to hold the legs up. Advanced back exercise using body weight. Targets lats, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Grab a pullup bar with your hands about shoulder-width apart, palms facing away from you, and hang with your arms straight.
2. Lift your legs straight out in front of you until they are level with the floor, making an L shape.
3. Hold the L and pull yourself up until your chin is above the bar.
4. Lower slowly until your arms are straight, keeping your legs up.

## Pro tips

- Hold your legs at a 90-degree angle to your torso throughout the entire pull, not just at the top.
- Point your toes and squeeze your quads to keep your legs locked in the L-position.
- Initiate the pull by depressing your shoulder blades before bending your arms.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the legs drop below 90 degrees during the pulling phase, which reduces core engagement.
- Swinging or kipping to initiate the pull instead of performing a strict Pullup from a dead hang.
- Bending the knees slightly to make the hold easier, which changes the lever arm and reduces difficulty.

## FAQ

### What do L-Pullups work that regular Pullups don't?

The L-hold adds significant core, hip flexor, and quad isometric work. Your abs must contract hard throughout to maintain the leg position, making it a Pullup and core exercise in one.

### I can't hold the L-position during Pullups. What should I do?

Practice L-hangs (hanging with legs at 90 degrees) for timed holds first. Build up to 20-30 seconds before adding the Pullup component.

### Are L-Pullups harder than regular Pullups?

Yes, significantly. The L-position shifts your center of gravity forward and requires your core to work the entire time, increasing the total demand substantially.

### What muscles does the L-pullup work?

The L-pullup primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the L-pullup good for beginners?

The L-pullup is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental back movements first.
