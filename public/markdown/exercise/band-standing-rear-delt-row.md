# Band Standing Rear Delt Row

## Quick answer

A standing band row pulled toward your face with your elbows high and wide. It works the rear shoulders, along with the upper back and biceps. Beginner shoulders exercise using resistance band. Targets shoulders, also works trapezius, rhomboids, biceps.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Resistance Band
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Trapezius, Rhomboids, Biceps
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Anchor a band above head height. Facing it, hold an end in each hand, palms facing down, and step back with one foot ahead, arms straight.
2. Pull the band toward your face, bending your elbows and lifting them out to the sides.
3. Stop when your hands are in front of your shoulders and your upper arms are level with the floor.
4. Return slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Pull the band toward your face or upper chest with elbows flared wide to emphasize the rear deltoids.
- Squeeze the shoulder blades together at the peak contraction for a one-second hold.
- Keep the band at shoulder height throughout the pull to maintain rear delt focus.

## Common mistakes

- Pulling the band too low (toward the waist), which shifts the emphasis from rear delts to the mid-back.
- Letting the elbows drop below shoulder height during the pull.
- Using too thick a band, which causes the upper traps to take over the movement.

## FAQ

### Is this the same as a Face Pull?

Very similar. The rear delt row typically uses a wider grip and more horizontal pull angle, while Face Pulls aim slightly higher toward the forehead. Both target the rear delts.

### How often should you train rear delts?

3-4 times per week is ideal since rear delts recover quickly and are often underdeveloped. Include light band rear delt work in every upper body warmup.

### Can band rear delt rows fix rounded shoulders?

They help by strengthening the posterior shoulder and mid-back muscles that pull the shoulders into a healthier position. Combine with pec stretching for best results.

### What muscles does the Band Standing Rear Delt Row work?

The Band Standing Rear Delt Row primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the trapezius, rhomboids, biceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Band Standing Rear Delt Row good for beginners?

Yes, the Band Standing Rear Delt Row is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building shoulders strength.
