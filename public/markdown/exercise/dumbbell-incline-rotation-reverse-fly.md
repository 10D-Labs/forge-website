# Dumbbell Incline Rotation Reverse Fly

## Quick answer

A reverse fly on an incline bench that turns your hands from palms back to palms down as you lift. It works the rear shoulders and the upper back. Intermediate shoulders exercise using dumbbell. Targets shoulders, also works trapezius, rhomboids.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Trapezius, Rhomboids
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie face down on an incline bench set to about 45 degrees, chest on the pad, legs straight and toes on the floor.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand, arms hanging straight down, palms facing back toward your legs so the dumbbells line up end to end.
3. Raise the dumbbells out to the sides until your arms are level with your shoulders, turning your hands as you lift so your palms end up facing the floor.
4. Lower slowly, turning your palms back toward your legs.

## Pro tips

- Lie chest down on an incline bench and start with your palms facing back, then turn them to face the floor as you raise the dumbbells.
- Make the turn smoothly through the whole raise rather than snapping the wrists at the top.
- Keep your chest on the bench so the rear shoulders do the lifting.

## Common mistakes

- Turning only at the wrist instead of rotating the whole arm.
- Using a weight so heavy that you can't make the turn smoothly, which turns this into a standard reverse fly.
- Lifting the chest off the bench to swing the weights up.

## FAQ

### What does the rotation add to the reverse fly?

Turning the arms as you lift brings in the small rotator cuff muscles alongside the rear delts, so you train both in one movement.

### Is this exercise good for shoulder health?

It can be. Strong rear delts and rotator cuff muscles help balance all the pressing most people do. Keep the weight light and the movement smooth.

### How heavy should I go on rotation reverse flyes?

Lighter than standard reverse flyes, about 30-40% less. The rotation needs a light load to stay smooth and controlled.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Incline Rotation Reverse Fly work?

The Dumbbell Incline Rotation Reverse Fly primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the trapezius, rhomboids. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Dumbbell Incline Rotation Reverse Fly good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Incline Rotation Reverse Fly is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of shoulders strength with easier variations first.
