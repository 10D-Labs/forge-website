# Dumbbell Side Lying Rear Delt Raise

## Quick answer

A one-arm raise done lying on your side, lifting the dumbbell from the floor until your arm points at the ceiling. It works the rear shoulders and upper back. Beginner shoulders exercise using dumbbell. Targets shoulders, also works trapezius, rhomboids.

- **Body Part:** Shoulders
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Shoulders
- **Secondary Muscles:** Trapezius, Rhomboids
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your side on the floor with your head resting on your bottom arm.
2. Hold a dumbbell in your top hand on the floor in front of your face, palm facing down.
3. Keep your arm straight and raise the dumbbell until your arm points straight at the ceiling.
4. Lower slowly back to the floor in front of your face. Do all your reps on one side, then switch.

## Pro tips

- Lie on your side on the floor or a bench, holding the dumbbell in your top hand in front of your body.
- Raise the dumbbell with a slight elbow bend until your arm points at the ceiling.
- Lower it slowly to the start; the side-lying position keeps tension on the rear delt at the bottom.

## Common mistakes

- Rolling your body back as you lift, which uses momentum.
- Bending the elbow a lot, which turns the raise into a row.
- Using a weight so heavy that the traps take over.

## FAQ

### Why do rear delt raises lying on your side?

Lying on your side changes the angle so the rear delt works hardest near the bottom of the lift, where standing raises are easiest. It also stops you from swinging.

### How much weight should I use?

Very light. Most people use much less than for bent-over rear delt raises.

### Can I do this on an incline bench instead?

Yes. Lying on your side on an incline bench gives a slightly longer range of motion.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Side Lying Rear Delt Raise work?

The Dumbbell Side Lying Rear Delt Raise primarily targets the shoulders, with secondary activation in the trapezius, rhomboids. It's classified as a strength exercise for the shoulders.

### Is the Dumbbell Side Lying Rear Delt Raise good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Side Lying Rear Delt Raise is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building shoulders strength.
