# Landmine 180

## Quick answer

A twisting core move with a barbell anchored at one end, swinging the free end in an arc from hip to hip. It works the abs and obliques. Intermediate core exercise using barbell. Targets abs, also works obliques, quadriceps.

- **Body Part:** Core
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Abs
- **Secondary Muscles:** Obliques, Quadriceps
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Wedge one end of a barbell into a landmine or a corner so it stays on the floor.
2. Stand facing the anchor, feet shoulder-width apart and knees slightly bent. Hold the loose end with both hands, arms straight, beside one hip.
3. Turn your hips and shoulders to swing the bar end in an arc across the front of your body to your other hip.
4. Swing it back the other way, keeping your arms straight. Keep alternating sides.

## Pro tips

- Keep your arms mostly straight and rotate the barbell in an arc from hip to hip using your core and hips to drive the rotation.
- Pivot on the balls of your feet as you rotate to protect your knees and allow full hip rotation.
- Control the barbell at the bottom of each side. Don't let gravity slam it down.

## Common mistakes

- Using the arms to swing the barbell rather than rotating from the core and hips.
- Keeping the feet planted and twisting only at the waist, which stresses the lumbar spine.
- Going too heavy and losing control of the barbell at the transition points.

## FAQ

### What muscles do Landmine 180s target?

Primarily the obliques and rectus abdominis through rotational force production. Secondary muscles include the shoulders, hip rotators, and quadriceps for stabilization.

### Can I do Landmine 180s without a landmine attachment?

Yes. Wedge the end of a barbell into a corner or wrap it in a towel and place it in the corner of two walls. This provides the same pivot point as a landmine attachment.

### What muscles does the Landmine 180 work?

The Landmine 180 primarily targets the abs, with secondary activation in the obliques, quadriceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the core.

### Is the Landmine 180 good for beginners?

The Landmine 180 is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of abs strength with easier variations first.
