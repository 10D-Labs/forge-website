# Glute Bridge March

## Quick answer

A glute bridge held at the top while you lift one knee at a time, as if marching. It works the glutes, with help from the hamstrings and quads. Intermediate legs exercise using body weight. Targets glutes, also works hamstrings, quadriceps.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Quadriceps
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Lie on your back with your knees bent and your feet flat on the floor.
2. Lift your hips until your body forms a straight line from your knees to your shoulders.
3. Keep your hips up and lift one foot, bringing that knee toward your chest.
4. Put the foot back down and lift the other knee. Keep alternating legs, like marching.

## Pro tips

- Keep your hips level and completely still as you lift each knee; any hip drop means you need to reduce the tempo.
- Drive through the planted foot's heel to maintain the bridge height while the opposite leg lifts.
- Lift each knee slowly (2 seconds up, 2 seconds down) rather than quickly to maximize glute activation.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the hips drop on the side of the lifting leg, which means the stabilizing glute isn't firing properly.
- Lifting the knee too quickly and using momentum instead of controlled hip flexion.
- Placing the feet too far from the body, which shifts the load from the glutes to the hamstrings.

## FAQ

### What does the marching component add to a glute bridge?

The alternating leg lift forces each glute to stabilize independently while maintaining the bridge, turning a bilateral exercise into a unilateral stability challenge.

### Are Glute Bridge Marches good for runners?

Excellent. They build single-leg glute stability, which is exactly what's needed during the stance phase of running. They help fix hip drop and knee caving during running.

### How many reps should I do?

10-15 marches per leg (20-30 total) for 2-3 sets. Focus on keeping the hips perfectly level rather than rushing through reps.

### What muscles does the Glute Bridge March work?

The Glute Bridge March primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, quadriceps. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Glute Bridge March good for beginners?

The Glute Bridge March is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of glutes strength with easier variations first.
