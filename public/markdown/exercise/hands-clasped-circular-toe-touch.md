# Hands Clasped Circular Toe Touch

## Quick answer

A standing toe touch with your hands clasped: fold down to your toes, rise back up, then circle your upper body around with your arms overhead. It works the glutes and the backs of the legs. Beginner legs exercise using body weight. Targets glutes, also works hamstrings, quadriceps, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Quadriceps, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Mobility

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and your arms straight overhead, hands clasped together.
2. Bend your knees slightly and fold forward at your hips, bringing your clasped hands down toward your toes as far as you can without straining.
3. Sweep your arms back up in front of you until they are overhead, and lean back slightly.
4. Keep your arms overhead and circle your upper body around: over to one side, then forward, then back up to standing tall.

## Pro tips

- Clasp your hands in front of your chest and use the circular reaching motion to progressively increase the stretch at each angle.
- Hinge at the hips with a flat back rather than rounding your spine to reach lower.
- Move through the circular path slowly, spending 2-3 seconds at each position in the arc.

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the spine to touch the toes instead of hinging from the hips, which strains the lower back.
- Keeping the legs completely locked straight, which limits the range of motion for people with tight hamstrings.
- Performing the circular motion too quickly, bypassing the stretch benefit at each position.

## FAQ

### What does the Hands Clasped Circular Toe Touch improve?

It improves hamstring flexibility, hip hinge mobility, and thoracic rotation when the circular motion includes a twisting component.

### Should I do this as a warm-up or cool-down?

It works for both. As a warm-up, use it dynamically with flowing reps. As a cool-down, move slowly and hold the deepest positions for 15-20 seconds.

### What if I cannot reach my toes?

Bend your knees slightly and reach as far as comfortable. Flexibility will improve with consistent practice over 2-4 weeks.

### What muscles does the Hands Clasped Circular Toe Touch work?

The Hands Clasped Circular Toe Touch primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, quadriceps, calves. It's classified as a mobility exercise for the legs.

### Is the Hands Clasped Circular Toe Touch good for beginners?

Yes, the Hands Clasped Circular Toe Touch is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building glutes strength.
