# Dumbbell Clean

## Quick answer

A fast, full-body lift that pulls two dumbbells from near the floor to your shoulders and catches them in a squat. It works the glutes, plus the hamstrings, quads and calves. Intermediate legs exercise using dumbbell. Targets glutes, also works hamstrings, quadriceps, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Hamstrings, Quadriceps, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and a dumbbell in each hand, palms facing you.
2. Squat down, lowering the dumbbells toward the floor, with your back straight and chest up.
3. Straighten your hips and knees fast, driving through your heels.
4. Shrug and pull the dumbbells up close to your body. Catch them at your shoulders in a squat, elbows forward and palms up.
5. Lower the dumbbells back down to the floor beside your feet.

## Pro tips

- Start in a squat position with the dumbbells on the floor. Explode upward through your hips and knees, using the lower body power to drive the dumbbells up.
- Shrug your shoulders forcefully as you jump, then quickly rotate your elbows under the dumbbells to catch them at shoulder height.
- Land softly with bent knees in a partial squat to absorb the catch. This is a power movement that requires coordination between the lower and upper body.

## Common mistakes

- Using the arms to curl the dumbbells up instead of generating force from the hip and knee extension.
- Not rotating the elbows under the dumbbells fast enough, which causes you to catch them awkwardly or drop them.
- Landing with straight legs, which sends the impact through the joints instead of absorbing it with the muscles.

## FAQ

### What is the benefit of Dumbbell Cleans over barbell cleans?

Dumbbell Cleans are easier to learn, require less mobility, and allow each arm to work independently. They are excellent for building explosive power without the technical complexity of barbell cleans.

### Can Dumbbell Cleans replace Power Cleans?

Dumbbell Cleans develop similar explosive hip extension and coordination but with lighter loads. They are a great alternative for those without access to a barbell or who have not mastered barbell clean technique.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Clean work?

The Dumbbell Clean primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the hamstrings, quadriceps, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Dumbbell Clean good for beginners?

The Dumbbell Clean is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of glutes strength with easier variations first.
