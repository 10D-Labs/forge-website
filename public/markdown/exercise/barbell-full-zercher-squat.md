# Barbell Full Zercher Squat

## Quick answer

A deep squat with the barbell held in the bends of your elbows instead of on your back. It works the glutes and quads, and your abs work hard to keep you upright. Advanced legs exercise using barbell. Targets glutes, also works quadriceps, hamstrings, calves, core.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Barbell
- **Target Muscle:** Glutes
- **Secondary Muscles:** Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves, Core
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, toes turned out slightly.
2. Hold a barbell in the bends of your elbows, with your hands gripping the bar to keep it steady.
3. Keep your chest up and sit your hips back and down, keeping your knees in line with your toes.
4. Lower until your thighs are level with the floor, or deeper if you can do it comfortably.
5. Drive through your heels to stand back up.

## Pro tips

- Wrap the bar with a towel or use a squat pad in the elbow crook to reduce discomfort on the forearms.
- Brace your core as if taking a punch before descending; the front-loaded position demands extreme trunk stability.
- Drive your elbows up and forward as you stand to prevent the bar from sliding down your arms.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the elbows drop as you descend, which shifts the bar forward and pulls you off balance.
- Using too much weight before mastering the movement pattern; the Zercher position limits how much you can safely hold.
- Rounding the upper back under load, which puts dangerous stress on the thoracic spine.

## FAQ

### What makes the Zercher squat different from a front squat?

The barbell sits in the crooks of your elbows rather than on your shoulders. This forces more core and upper back engagement and allows a deeper squat for many people.

### Is the Zercher squat safe for the lower back?

The front-loaded position actually encourages a more upright torso than back squats, which can reduce lower back stress. However, poor bracing or too much weight negates this advantage.

### How much weight can I Zercher squat compared to a back squat?

Most people Zercher squat around 50-65% of their back squat. The limiting factor is usually forearm comfort and upper back strength, not leg strength.

### What muscles does the Barbell Full Zercher Squat work?

The Barbell Full Zercher Squat primarily targets the glutes, with secondary activation in the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves, core. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Barbell Full Zercher Squat good for beginners?

The Barbell Full Zercher Squat is an advanced exercise. Beginners should master fundamental legs movements first.
