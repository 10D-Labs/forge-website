# Dumbbell Goblet Squat

## Quick answer

A squat holding one dumbbell upright against your chest. It works the quads, with help from the glutes and hamstrings. Beginner legs exercise using dumbbell. Targets quads, also works glutes, hamstrings, calves.

- **Body Part:** Legs
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell
- **Target Muscle:** Quads
- **Secondary Muscles:** Glutes, Hamstrings, Calves
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding one dumbbell upright against your chest with both hands.
2. Keep your chest up, push your hips back and bend your knees to lower into a squat.
3. Lower until your thighs are level with the floor, or as low as you comfortably can.
4. Push through your heels to stand back up.

## Pro tips

- Hold the dumbbell vertically by cupping the top end with both hands against your chest, elbows pointing down.
- Push your knees out over your toes and sit between your hips, using the dumbbell as a counterbalance.
- Keep the dumbbell tight against your chest throughout; don't let it drift forward.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the dumbbell pull forward and away from the chest, which rounds the upper back.
- Not squatting deep enough; the goblet squat is designed for full-depth squatting to maximize glute engagement.
- Placing the feet too narrow, which limits hip mobility and depth.

## FAQ

### What makes the goblet squat good for beginners?

The front-loaded position naturally encourages an upright torso, making it easier to maintain good form. The weight counterbalances your body, actually making it easier to squat deeper.

### How heavy should the dumbbell be for goblet squats?

Start with 20-30 pounds and progress gradually. Most intermediate lifters use 40-70 pounds. The limiting factor is usually grip and shoulder endurance, not leg strength.

### Can goblet squats build leg size?

They build size effectively for beginners and intermediates. Advanced lifters may find the weight limitation (you can only hold so much at your chest) insufficient for continued leg growth.

### What muscles does the Dumbbell Goblet Squat work?

The Dumbbell Goblet Squat primarily targets the quads, with secondary activation in the glutes, hamstrings, calves. It's classified as a strength exercise for the legs.

### Is the Dumbbell Goblet Squat good for beginners?

Yes, the Dumbbell Goblet Squat is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building quads strength.
