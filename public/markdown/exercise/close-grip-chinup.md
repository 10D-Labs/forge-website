# Close Grip Chinup

## Quick answer

A chinup with your hands close together and your palms facing you. It works the lats, with a lot of help from the biceps and forearms. Intermediate back exercise using body weight. Targets lats, also works biceps, forearms.

- **Body Part:** Back
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Lats
- **Secondary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Category:** Strength

---

## How to perform

1. Grab a pullup bar with your palms facing you and your hands close together.
2. Hang with your arms straight and your feet off the floor.
3. Pull yourself up, keeping your elbows close to your body, until your chin is above the bar.
4. Lower yourself slowly until your arms are straight.

## Pro tips

- Use a grip with hands about 6-8 inches apart, roughly at the inner edges of your shoulders.
- Focus on pulling your chest to the bar rather than just getting your chin over it for a deeper back contraction.
- Control the descent for a full 2-3 seconds to maximize the eccentric stimulus on the lats and biceps.

## Common mistakes

- Placing the hands too close together (touching), which puts excessive strain on the wrists and limits pulling power.
- Relying almost entirely on biceps by not engaging the lats at the start of the pull.
- Swinging the legs forward to generate momentum instead of pulling with a still lower body.

## FAQ

### Does a closer grip work the biceps more?

Yes. The closer grip increases biceps contribution compared to a Wide Grip Pullup, while still heavily engaging the lats through a longer range of motion.

### Is the Close Grip Chinup easier or harder than a standard Chinup?

Most people find it roughly the same difficulty. The biceps work harder, but the lats move through a greater range of motion, so the difficulty balances out.

### Can I use a neutral grip instead of supinated?

A neutral grip (palms facing each other) on parallel handles is similar and can be easier on the wrists. It shifts slightly more emphasis to the brachialis and brachioradialis.

### What muscles does the Close Grip Chinup work?

The Close Grip Chinup primarily targets the lats, with secondary activation in the biceps, forearms. It's classified as a strength exercise for the back.

### Is the Close Grip Chinup good for beginners?

The Close Grip Chinup is rated as intermediate. Beginners should build a base of lats strength with easier variations first.
