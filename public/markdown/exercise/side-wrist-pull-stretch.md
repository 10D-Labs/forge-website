# Side Wrist Pull Stretch

## Quick answer

A standing stretch where you hold one arm across your chest and pull its fingers back with the other hand. It stretches the forearm and wrist. Beginner arms exercise using body weight. Targets forearms, also works wrists, hands.

- **Body Part:** Arms
- **Equipment:** Body Weight
- **Target Muscle:** Forearms
- **Secondary Muscles:** Wrists, Hands
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Category:** Stretching

---

## How to perform

1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Raise one arm straight across the front of your chest at shoulder height.
3. With your other hand, take hold of its fingers and gently pull them back toward you until you feel a stretch in your forearm.
4. Hold for the set time, then release and switch arms.

## Pro tips

- Keep the stretching arm straight and at shoulder height for the most effective forearm stretch.
- Pull gently and steadily; the forearm muscles and wrist extensors are small and easy to overstretch.
- Vary the wrist angle (palm up vs palm down) to target different forearm muscles.

## Common mistakes

- Yanking the wrist aggressively instead of applying gentle, sustained pressure.
- Bending the stretching arm at the elbow, which reduces the stretch on the forearm muscles.
- Rushing through the stretch and not holding long enough for tissue lengthening.

## FAQ

### How often should I stretch my wrists and forearms?

Daily stretching is ideal, especially if you type, lift weights, or do manual labor. Hold each stretch for 15-30 seconds and repeat 2-3 times per side.

### Does this stretch help with wrist pain from typing?

It can help relieve tension in the forearm extensors, which are overworked during typing. Pair it with wrist flexor stretches and regular breaks for the most relief.

### Should I stretch my wrists before or after lifting?

Light wrist stretches before lifting can improve mobility for exercises like front squats and cleans. Deeper stretching is best saved for after your workout or on rest days.

### What muscles does the Side Wrist Pull Stretch work?

The Side Wrist Pull Stretch primarily targets the forearms, with secondary activation in the wrists, hands. It's classified as a stretching exercise for the arms.

### Is the Side Wrist Pull Stretch good for beginners?

Yes, the Side Wrist Pull Stretch is rated as a beginner-level exercise. It's a great starting point for building forearms strength.
