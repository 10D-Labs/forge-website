- **Strong:** Minimalist workout tracker. PRO costs $4.99 a month, $29.99 a year, or $99.99 for the lifetime Forever plan, under a free tier with unlimited logging and no ads. iOS and Android, plus a standalone Apple Watch app.
- **Forge:** AI personal trainer with four coaching personalities and a plan that adapts to your progress. $6.99 a week, $19.99 a month, $59.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial. iPhone and Android.

## The Short Version

Strong has one job and a decade of practice at it. You log a set in about two taps, glance at your history, and get back under the bar. Lifters on 5/3/1 or a PPL split treat it as the fastest paper-notebook replacement in the gym, and 108,000 ratings at 4.9 stars back that up. The standalone Apple Watch app is the best in its class, and the free tier skips ads entirely.

What Strong will never do is tell you what to lift. There is no programming anywhere in the product, so a beginner who downloads it gets an empty notebook and a search engine. Charts and cloud sync sit behind the paywall, and the 2020 switch from a one-time purchase to a subscription still burns in the reviews. Forge was built for the person Strong abandons, the lifter who does not already know what to do in the gym. Your trainer writes the plan and adjusts it as you progress, and the 24/7 chat handles every why along the way.

On price Strong wins without argument. Its annual plan is $29.99 against Forge's $59.99, and the $99.99 Forever option ends the billing for good. If your programming comes from a coach or a proven template and you need a flawless log, buy Strong and keep the change. Forge was priced for the lifter who wants the plan written for them, adapted week to week, with a personality pushing through the hard sessions.

## FAQ

### Is Strong free?
Partly. The free tier logs unlimited workouts with no ads, capped at three custom routines, with charts and cloud sync locked. PRO costs $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year, and the PRO Forever lifetime plan is $99.99.

### Is Forge or Strong cheaper?
Strong. Its annual plan is $29.99 against Forge's $59.99, and the $99.99 lifetime option ends the billing entirely. Forge costs more because programming and coaching are the product. Strong sells you the notebook alone.

### Does Strong create workout plans?
No, and it never has. Strong assumes you arrive with a program from a coach, a book, or a template like 5/3/1. If you need the plan itself, Forge's trainers build it and keep adjusting it as your numbers move.

### Does Strong work on Apple Watch?
Yes, and the watch app is a standout. It runs fully standalone, so you can leave the phone in a locker. Forge has no watch app on any platform, so if logging from your wrist matters, Strong wins that row easily.

### Why do people still mention Strong's 2020 pricing change?
Strong sold a one-time purchase, then moved to subscriptions in 2020 and paywalled features some users had bought. Old buyers kept their access, but the resentment lives on in reviews. The current pricing is at least simple, with a free tier, $29.99 a year, or $99.99 forever.

### Which is better for beginners, Forge or Strong?
Strong hands a beginner an empty logbook and no guidance, which is why its fans are lifters who already follow a program. Forge starts a beginner with a plan and a trainer who explains every choice along the way.
