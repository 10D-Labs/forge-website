## The Short Version

JuggernautAI comes from Juggernaut Training Systems and Chad Wesley Smith, one of the most credible names in powerlifting, and the programming shows that pedigree. Block periodization, peaking, meet prep with attempt selection, and daily readiness checks that adjust the session before you touch the bar. Users report real PRs and meet wins off these plans. The app backs them with 300-plus technique videos, warm-up planners, RPE tools, and a weekly live Q&A with Juggernaut coaches.

The scope is the first catch. Squat, bench, deadlift, and their close variations fill the plan, with a Powerbuilding mode covering hypertrophy on the side. Cardio, mobility, and general fitness sit outside the app's interest. Beginners face a wall of RPE charts and programming terms the app assumes you already know, and some users report the auto-programming repeating exercises or picking odd variations. Forge covers the wider job: any goal, a gentler on-ramp, and a trainer who answers questions at 11pm. No one at Forge will plan your meet attempts, and that is the honest trade.

The price is the bigger catch. $34.99 a month or $349.99 a year, verified on JuggernautAI's own pricing page, against Forge's $19.99 monthly and $59.99 yearly. A year of JuggernautAI costs nearly six years of Forge. For a competitive powerlifter in meet prep, that math can still work, because the programming depth has no real rival in an app. Everyone else pays a powerlifting tax for features they will never open.

## FAQ

### Is JuggernautAI worth $34.99 a month?
For a competitive powerlifter, often yes. The block periodization, peaking, and meet prep attempt selection have no real rival in an app, and users report real PRs and meet wins. For anyone chasing general fitness, the same money buys a lot of specialization they will never use, which is where the steep-price reviews come from.

### Is Forge or JuggernautAI cheaper?
Forge by a wide margin. $19.99 monthly against $34.99, and $59.99 yearly against $349.99. A year of JuggernautAI costs nearly six years of Forge, and Forge adds a $6.99 weekly plan. JuggernautAI's 14-day trial runs longer than Forge's 7-day one.

### Is JuggernautAI good for beginners?
No. The app assumes you know RPE, programming terms, and your way around a barbell, and beginners report confusion in the first weeks. Forge builds the on-ramp into the product. Tell your trainer what you have done and what hurts, and the plan meets you there.

### Does JuggernautAI have a coach you can talk to?
Yes, on a weekly schedule. Juggernaut coaches run a live Q&A for subscribers, a human touch most apps skip. Between those sessions the app itself is a programming engine. Forge's trainers answer in character around the clock, though they are AI, and Juggernaut's Q&A is with humans.

### Does JuggernautAI work on Apple Watch?
No, and neither does Forge. Both apps run on iPhone and Android only.

### Can JuggernautAI train me for goals beyond powerlifting?
The Powerbuilding mode covers hypertrophy alongside strength, which widens the scope some. Cardio, mobility, and general fitness sit outside the app's interest. Forge builds plans for any goal and adjusts them from your feedback.
